\section{The Embodied Exergy Paradox of Legacy Machinery (\texttt{D7}, \texttt{D8})}
\label{sec:l2-stage2-embodied-exergy}

Why do early-stage sovereign settlements not simply scrap their Stage~I Scavenged Layer~7 appliances as soon as the first potted controller board fails? The answer lies in the Second Law of Thermodynamics and Layer~3's \textbf{Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$)} accounting \parencite{georgescu1971,odum1971}.

Consider a commercial three-deck bakery oven ($420\,\mathrm{kg}$ of refractory stone, aluminized steel, and rockwool insulation), an industrial horizontal washing machine ($180\,\mathrm{kg}$ of \texttt{304} stainless steel drum and cast-iron counterweights), or a $1{,}200\,\mathrm{kg}$ manual-turned-CNC machine lathe. Over $98\%$ of the embodied exergy of these machines resides in their heavy metallurgical frames, copper motor windings, and precision-ground ways:
\begin{equation}
    E_{\mathrm{embodied,chassis}} \approx 18{,}000{,}000{,}000{,}000\text{--}65{,}000{,}000{,}000{,}000\,\mu\mathrm{J} \quad (18\text{--}65\,\mathrm{GJ}).
    \label{eq:l2-chassis-embodied-exergy}
\end{equation}
 mechanically, a cast-iron lathe bed or a refractory deck oven can serve a neighborhood for a hundred years. Yet in the Layer~7 economy, the manufacturer renders the entire $420\,\mathrm{kg}$ machine inoperable after seven years by locking its temperature regulation and safety relays inside a $60\,\mathrm{g}$ proprietary printed circuit board potted in black polyurethane resin, paired with serialized replacement sensors and discontinued firmware support.

If a settlement discards that $420\,\mathrm{kg}$ chassis because its proprietary brain died, Layer~3 records a massive embodied-exergy write-off ($\Delta \lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$ spike). Instead, as codified in Dialogue~7 and Dialogue~8 (\texttt{CCI-6}), communities execute a \textbf{Stage~II Right-to-Repair Hybrid Retrofit}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Retain the Heavy Iron and Copper:} Keep the high-exergy mechanical chassis, refractory chambers, Induction/BLDC motor windings, and stainless vessels intact.
    \item \textbf{Excise the Hostile Silicon:} Unbolt and recycle the proprietary DRM-locked controller PCB, cloud-tethered Wi-Fi module, and non-standard thermistor harness.
    \item \textbf{Install an Open-Spec Layer~2 Retrofit Brain (\texttt{QUALITY\_RETROFIT\_HYBRID = 0x08}):} Drop in a standardized open-hardware Layer~2 controller board (\texttt{ESP32-S3}, \texttt{STM32H743}, or open \texttt{RISC-V} \texttt{RV32IMAC}) running Zephyr RTOS, wired directly to internal Class-A 4-wire PT100 RTDs, Type-K thermocouples, internal Hall/CT current shunts, and zero-crossing solid-state contactors, mounted via 3D-printable or CNC-milled metric (\texttt{M6}/\texttt{M8}) adapter plates.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Eliminating the Parasitic Shim Tax and Shrinking Diagnostic Uncertainty}

The instant a Stage~I appliance is upgraded with a Stage~II Hybrid Retrofit Kit and passes its hardware-in-the-loop verification sweep, its Layer~2 register profile undergoes four measurable physical improvements:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Provenance Promotion (\texttt{0x04} $\to$ \texttt{0x08}):} \texttt{col-telemetryd} updates the device's quality byte from \texttt{QUALITY\_SCAVENGED\_L7\_SHIM = 0x04} to \texttt{QUALITY\_RETROFIT\_HYBRID = 0x08} (\texttt{HardwareFleetTier::kRightToRepairHybrid}).
    \item \textbf{Elimination of Parasitic Shim Power ($\mathtt{parasitic\_shim\_overhead\_mw} \to 0$):} Because the always-on proprietary display transformer, vendor Wi-Fi radio, and external cloud-spoofing proxy are physically removed, idle standby power drops from $45{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$ to $< 1{,}200\,\mathrm{mW}$ (or $10\,\mu\mathrm{A}$ in Zephyr deep sleep).
    \item \textbf{Direct Internal Transduction ($\pm 1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp} \to \pm 350\,\mathrm{bp}$):} Because the 4-wire PT100 RTD or thermocouple now sits inside the geometric center of the oven chamber or cold-room plenum rather than taped to the exterior sheet-metal skin, the $4\text{-minute}$ thermal diffusion lag ($\tau_{\mathrm{lag}}$ in Equation~\eqref{eq:l2-shim-thermal-lag}) vanishes. Diagnostic uncertainty shrinks from $\pm 1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($\pm 15.0\%$) to $\pm 350\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($\pm 3.5\%$).
    \item \textbf{7-Fold Reduction in Unscheduled Mid-Batch Hard-Blocks:} Because the open controller monitors heater coil resistance $R_{\mathrm{coil}}(t) = V_{\mathrm{bus}}(t)/I_{\mathrm{shunt}}(t)$ and motor phase current directly, Kalman innovation residuals catch degrading relays before mid-batch failure, dropping $\mathtt{unscheduled\_fault\_prob\_bp}$ from $350\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($3.50\%$) down to $45\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($0.45\%$).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The Three-Way Cryptographic Verification Conjunction}

As established in Part~III (\S3.3) of the Council Guidance (\texttt{CCI-2}), operational reputation $R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t)$ in the unified voting weight equation $V_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t) = 10{,}000 + R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t)$ can never be self-minted through synthetic commits or busywork. \begin{equation}

    \label{eq:l2-retrofit-stewardship-conjunction}
\end{equation}

Specifically, during the final step of the Layer~4 retrofit workflow (\texttt{wf.alpha.right\_to\_repair\_retrofit}), \texttt{col-telemetryd} executes an automated 300-second \textbf{Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Burn-In Handshake}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The new open-spec Tier~2 controller signs a cryptographic identity challenge using its onboard \texttt{ATECC608A} secure element ($sk_{\mathrm{hw}}$).
    \item \texttt{col-actuated} sweeps the retrofitted actuator across three calibration setpoints while \texttt{col-sensord} verifies that the internal transducer response tracks the physical plant model within the Stage~II uncertainty bound ($\le 350\,\mathrm{bp}$) and that parasitic standby draw has dropped below the Stage~II threshold.
    \item Upon passing burn-in, \texttt{col-telemetryd} emits a signed \texttt{RetrofitBurnInAttestationV50} frame over \path{/run/collective/ipc/l2_l3.sock} to \texttt{col-storaged}, which commits the \texttt{ProofOfStewardshipRetrofitRecordV50} (Listing~\ref{lst:l2-stage2-retrofit-record}).

\begin{theorem}[Thermodynamic Proportionality of Retrofit Stewardship Minting]
\label{thm:l2-retrofit-stewardship}
Let $E_{\mathrm{rescued\_uj}}$ denote the embodied exergy of the heavy machine chassis rescued from premature obsolescence, $\Delta \lambda_{\mathrm{ERC\_uj}} = \lambda_{\mathrm{ERC,StageI\_uj}} - \lambda_{\mathrm{ERC,StageII\_uj}} > 0$ denote the verified per-cycle Ecological Replacement Cost reduction over expected post-retrofit lifetime cycles $N_{\mathrm{life}}$, and $\Delta \Phi_{\mathrm{tribute\_cents}}$ denote the eliminated annual Layer~7 spare-part tribute. \begin{equation}
    \Delta R_{i,\mathrm{bp}} = \min\!\left( R_{\mathrm{cap\_bp}},\; \left\lfloor \frac{\kappa_{\mathrm{steward}} \cdot \bigl(E_{\mathrm{rescued\_uj}} + N_{\mathrm{life}} \cdot \Delta \lambda_{\mathrm{ERC\_uj}}\bigr)}{E_{\mathrm{ref\_uj}}} \right\rfloor \right),

\end{equation}
where $E_{\mathrm{ref\_uj}} = 3{,}600{,}000{,}000{,}000\,\mu\mathrm{J}$ ($3.6\,\mathrm{MJ}$ reference exergy quantum). If a subsequent Layer~2 telemetry epoch detects a safety regression or ERC spike ($\Delta \lambda_{\mathrm{ERC\_uj}} \le 0$), zero stewardship bonus is minted.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Solving the Cold-Start Apprentice Paradox via Stage II Dual-Key Retrofits (\texttt{D6})}

Stage~II Right-to-Repair retrofits also provide the canonical physical onboarding ladder for \textbf{Pillar~II (\texttt{collective:WorkCapabilityMatrix})} of Layer~5 governance (\texttt{Scenario Kappa: Apprenticeship}). How does a newcomer arriving at a node with zero operational reputation ($R_2(0) = 0$, holding only their non-decaying civic baseline $B = 1 \equiv 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$) earn initial technical stewardship if high-voltage industrial tools require safety certification?

As wired at Layer~2, every Stage~II retrofit bench incorporates a \textbf{Dual-Key Mentor-Apprentice Hardware Interlock} (\texttt{require\_dual\_key\_kappa = 1} in \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1}):
\begin{itemize}
    \item The apprentice ($R_2(0) = 0$) strips the dead potted PCB from a scavenged refrigerator or CNC router, prints the \texttt{M6} metric mounting bracket, crimps the 4-wire PT100 harness, and flashes the Zephyr RTOS image under a Tier-1 Co-Signed Apprentice BPMN task.
    \item Before live $240\,\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{AC}}$ or $48\,\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{DC}}$ bus power can energize the retrofitted chassis for the 300-second HIL burn-in test, Layer~2's physical interlock requires two concurrent hardware signatures: the apprentice's local NFC/Ed25519 key token \textbf{and} a certified mentor's ($R_1 > 0$) physical key-turn on the bench LOTO selector switch.
    \item When the burn-in succeeds and Layer~2 signs \texttt{RetrofitBurnInAttestationV50}, the apprentice earns full co-stewardship credit $\Delta R_{2,\mathrm{bp}} > 0$ from zero---building real technical voice in the guild while rescuing physical infrastructure for the commons.
\end{itemize}
