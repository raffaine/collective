\section{The Completed Arc of the Cyber-Physical Membrane}
\label{sec:l2-conclusion-arc}

Across these fourteen chapters, we have specified the complete hardware, metrological, cryptographic, and cybernetic architecture of \textbf{Layer~2: The Cyber-Physical Membrane and Edge Digital Twin} at Horizon Council Version~3.2.

We began by overturning the two foundational pathologies of twenty-first-century industrial computing: the corporate IoT panopticon that points cameras, microphones, and biometric wearables at human workers, and the planned-obsolescence appliance monopoly that locks heavy physical machinery inside potted epoxy circuit boards and cloud-tethered DRM.     \item \textbf{The Non-Panoptic Hardware Boundary (\texttt{CCI-1}, \texttt{CCI-2}):} Layer~2 sensors monitor machines, material stock lots, and architectural partition boundaries---never human bodies, faces, speech, or exertion signatures. \section{Zero-Stat Liberation: Returning Boundless Time to Human Life}
\label{sec:l2-conclusion-boundless-time}

Why does an embedded systems monograph care so deeply about whether a bearing failure is predicted forty operating hours in advance by a fixed-point piezoelectric FFT, or whether a refrigerator load cell throttles a bakery demand lease silently when a citizen walks into the mountains?

Because the ultimate product of The Sovereign Stack is not megajoules, kilowatts, or Merkle trees. \textbf{The ultimate product of The Sovereign Stack is unbound human time.}

In an extractive economy, machines are loud, needy, and brittle by design: they interrupt dinners with shrill alarms, break down mid-batch to extract emergency repair rents, tether artisans to proprietary cloud subscriptions, and force neighbors into endless administrative meetings over shared resources. When Layer~2 reaches Stage~III Native Open-Spec closure, the machines grow quiet. A wearing bearing announces itself forty hours ahead of time and is swapped in five calm minutes with a single \texttt{M6} hex wrench; a burst pipe preempts a neighboring room's lease in milliseconds without an argument; a weekend hike requires no cancellation forms; and a finished piece of software sleeps undisturbed for years.

The pins have been sampled across $2.5\,\mathrm{kV}_{\mathrm{RMS}}$ of silica glass. The noise has been filtered; the raw speech waveforms have been zeroized in SRAM; the \texttt{ATECC608A} secure element has signed the 1-Hz \texttt{BLAKE2b} Epoch Merkle root; and the 64-byte frame has crossed \path{/run/collective/ipc/l2_l3.sock} into the Thermodynamic Ledger. Having spoken the physical truth of the earth and the workshop in exact 64-bit SI integers, the Cyber-Physical Membrane steps back into the periphery---leaving Dr\_Sofia and her neighbors free to bake, build, rest, and compose a life of their own choosing.
