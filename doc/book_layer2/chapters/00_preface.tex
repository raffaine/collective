Every social order builds sensors in its own image. In the final decades of extractive platform capitalism, the ``Internet of Things'' (\texttt{IoT}) and industrial supervisory control (\texttt{SCADA}) evolved into a dual apparatus of human surveillance and material hostage-taking \parencite{zuboff2019,han2015}. On the warehouse floor and inside the domestic home, cameras, biometric wearables, and cloud-tethered microphones were deployed to track every pause in human motion, converting bodily exhaustion into algorithmic productivity scores. Simultaneously, ordinary appliances---refrigerators, induction ranges, agricultural tractors, solar inverters, and machine tools---were sealed inside glued housings and potted epoxy boards, cryptographically locked by Digital Rights Management (\texttt{DRM}) so that communities could neither read their own physical telemetry nor replace a worn two-dollar bearing seal without paying recurring tribute to a distant corporate cloud \parencite{illich1973}.

The Sovereign Stack begins from the opposite constitutional premise. In Volume~1 of this series, we introduced the seven-layer cybernetic architecture of The Collective, demonstrating how unmediated physical reality (Layer~1) connects through a selectively permeable hardware boundary (Layer~2) to a multi-dimensional thermodynamic ledger (Layer~3), a deterministic WebAssembly and BPMN~2.0 workflow orchestrator (Layer~4), a polycentric constitutional kernel (Layer~5), an HTTP-like local semantic protocol daemon serving specialized human application lenses (Layer~6), and a cooperative legal carapace interfacing with legacy external markets (Layer~7).

This companion volume provides the complete v3.2 engineering specification, embedded signal-processing mathematics, three-tier hardware fleet progression, domain-blind spatial boundary sensing architecture, and 25-scenario operational reference for \textbf{Layer~2: The Cyber-Physical Membrane and Edge Digital Twin}, implemented across bare-metal Zephyr RTOS microcontrollers and the POSIX daemons \texttt{col-telemetryd} (incorporating \texttt{col-membraned}, \texttt{col-sensord}, and \texttt{col-actuated}) and \texttt{col-meshd}.

\section*{The Eight Architectural Understandings of Boundless Time (\texttt{D1--D8})}

Revised under the Horizon Council's v3.2 synthesis of \textit{The Architecture of Boundless Time} (\texttt{D1} through \texttt{D8}), this volume retires early prototype assumptions---including any notion of ``Cryptographic Proof of Labor'' worker monitoring, scalar ``Value Tokens,'' floating-point telemetry packets, or bidirectional live telemetry ingestion into the Oasis simulation engine---and anchors Layer~2 in six cross-cutting architectural laws:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Non-Panoptic Membrane and Strict Outward-Only SITL Airgap (\texttt{CCI-1}, \texttt{CCI-2}):} Layer~2 sensors monitor \textit{machines, material stocks, and spatial partition boundaries}---never human faces, gait, heart rates, chest respiration, or personal movements. Boundary microphones compute root-mean-square sound pressure levels in integer centibels (\texttt{cB}) inside microcontroller SRAM and immediately destroy the raw audio waveform before any packet can cross a bus. Conversely, the Oasis simulation proving ground (\texttt{Layer 1b}) operates strictly as an outward-only producer of synthetic telemetry frames tagged \texttt{QUALITY\_SIMULATED = 0x01}; inside \texttt{col-telemetryd}, the \texttt{HardwareAirgapFilter} mathematically blocks any \texttt{0x01} frame from actuating physical relays.
    \item \textbf{The Canonical 64-Bit Fixed-Point SI Metrological Core (\texttt{CCI-6}, \texttt{D6}, \texttt{D8}):} To prevent floating-point rounding drift across heterogeneous CPU architectures (\texttt{x86\_64}, \texttt{aarch64}, \texttt{riscv64}, \texttt{Xtensa}) and eliminate dimensional conversion failures, Layers~2, 3, and~4 execute exclusively in 64-bit fixed-point International System (SI) integers: microjoules (\texttt{uJ}), milliwatts (\texttt{mW}, where $1\,\mathrm{mW} \times 1\,\mathrm{ms} = 1\,\mu\mathrm{J}$ exact), milligrams (\texttt{mg}), millimeters and micrometers (\texttt{mm}, \texttt{um}), milliliters (\texttt{mL}), millikelvins (\texttt{mK}), centibels (\texttt{cB}), milliseconds (\texttt{ms}), and basis points (\texttt{bp}). Cultural and craft units (baker's hydration percentages, inches, degrees Fahrenheit or Celsius, cups) live strictly at the Layer~6 human application lens edge.
    \item \textbf{Three-Tier Hardware Fleet Progression Without Fake RPG Tech Trees (\texttt{CCI-6}, \texttt{D7}, \texttt{D8}):} Real communities do not boot in a pristine sci-fi cleanroom; they bootstrap out of a Stage~1A $\$20{,}000$ (\texttt{2,000,000 cents}) reserve using \textbf{Stage~I Scavenged Layer~7 Appliances} wrapped in external \textit{Parasitic Layer~2 Shims} (\texttt{QUALITY\_SCAVENGED\_L7\_SHIM = 0x04}). This volume models the four mechanical penalties of shimmed black-box hardware---parasitic standby watt drain and coarse state inference ($\pm 1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$), unannounced blind mid-batch hard-blocks, Layer~7 fiat spare-part tribute escalation, and Layer~3 Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$) spikes---and traces how settlements progress to \textbf{Stage~II Right-to-Repair Hybrid Retrofit Kits} (\texttt{0x08}, open RISC-V/STM32/ESP32-S3 control boards that rescue heavy cast-iron chassis and mint first-class Layer~3 Proof of Stewardship $\Delta R_i$) and \textbf{Stage~III Native Open-Spec Collective Twins} (\texttt{0x02}, continuous acoustic-harmonic bearing wear and winding telemetry enabling 5-minute predictive Layer~4 metric swaps and \textit{Zero-Stat Liberation}).
    \item \textbf{Domain-Blind Enveloping Node Partition Sensing and Two-Speed Jurisprudence (\texttt{CCI-4}, \texttt{D4}, \texttt{D6}):} When multiple autonomous Layer~5 nodes ($N=1$ Solo Studio, $N=1$ Maker Bench, $N=4$ Household Hearth, or $K$ guilds in a coworking floorplate) share one physical building, they are wrapped by a fractal \textbf{Domain-Blind Enveloping Node Hypervisor} (\textit{Room/House $\to$ Street/Microgrid $\to$ Bioregional Watershed}). Layer~2 provides the domain-blind partition sensors---wall centibels (\texttt{cB}), branch breaker milliwatts (\texttt{mW}), differential thermal millikelvins (\texttt{mK}), particulate micrograms (\texttt{ug}), and SPAD Time-of-Flight threshold curtains (\texttt{mm})---that enforce capability leases and trigger millisecond \textbf{Fast-Path Hazard Preemption} (such as a burst-pipe wet-vac overriding a recording studio quiet lease) alongside \textbf{Slow-Path Semantic Diff Precedent Compilation}.
    \item \textbf{Perishable Entropy (\textit{The Pantry}) vs.\ Durable Cycles (\textit{The Workshop}) vs.\ \textit{Dual-Loop Bakery} Validation (\texttt{CCI-3}, \texttt{D1}, \texttt{D4}, \texttt{D5}):} Layer~2 distinguishes continuous temperature-time Arrhenius shelf-life integration ($\mathrm{mK \cdot ms}$) and gravimetric shelf drawdown sensors for Layer~6's \textit{The Pantry} lens---allowing spontaneous weekend detours to throttle outbound bakery demand leases with zero calendar or GPS surveillance---from calendar-agnostic spindle-revolution and wear counters for \textit{The Workshop} lens, and pickup-shelf clearance load cells validating the 85\% staple / 15\% compositional \textit{montage} split in \textit{The Bakery / Hearth} lens.
    \item \textbf{Substrate Drift and the 3-Tier BPMN Hardware Adaptation Cascade (\texttt{CCI-5}, \texttt{D2}, \texttt{D5}, \texttt{D8}):} Layer~2 Digital Twin capability registers (\texttt{ToolTwinCapabilityV50}) feed Layer~4's 3-Tier BPMN Hardware Adaptation Cascade (\textit{Tier~1: SI Parameter Scaling $\to$ Tier~2: Macro-Activity Substitution $\to$ Tier~3: True-Constraint Hard-Block}). Furthermore, when physical silicon revisions or transducer tolerances shift beneath a mature software artifact resting in \textbf{Dormant Equilibrium (``The \TeX\ Asymptote'')}, Layer~2 frame schema mismatch triggers an upward Layer~4 $\to$ Layer~5 $\to$ Layer~6 \textbf{Semantic Diff} that wakes the Scenario~Zero working group on demand.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Structure of This Companion Monograph}

\textbf{Part~I (Chapters~1--9)} develops the core hardware, metrological, and cybernetic architecture of Layer~2:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Chapter~1} defines the Non-Panoptic Membrane, the Three-Tier Microcontroller Hierarchy (\texttt{STM32H743}, \texttt{ESP32-S3}, \texttt{RP2040/RP2350}) under Zephyr RTOS, $2.5\,\mathrm{kV}_{\mathrm{RMS}}$ galvanic isolation, the \texttt{HardwareAirgapFilter} enforcing outward-only \texttt{0x01} SITL isolation, and constitutional hardware air-gaps against person-tracking.

    \item \textbf{Chapter~3} examines Stage~I Hardware Reality: engineering parasitic Layer~2 shims (CT clamps, taped thermistors, RP2040 PIO optical fault-code readers, local cloud-spoofing shims) onto scavenged Layer~7 appliances on Day~0, and quantifying the four structural penalties of the shim tax.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~4} details Stage~II Hardware Evolution: Right-to-Repair Hybrid Retrofit Kits that replace potted DRM boards with open RISC-V/STM32/ESP32-S3 controllers while rescuing heavy cast-iron frames, minting first-class Layer~3 Proof of Stewardship ($\Delta R_i$).
    \item \textbf{Chapter~5} presents Stage~III Hardware Maturity: Native Open-Spec Collective Twins with continuous acoustic-harmonic bearing FFT, winding resistance drift, and thermal gradient telemetry enabling 5-minute predictive Layer~4 metric swaps, the 3-Tier BPMN Hardware Adaptation Cascade, and Substrate Drift wake-ups from Dormant Equilibrium.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~6} specifies Enveloping Node Partition Sensing: the domain-blind boundary microphone (\texttt{cB}), solid-state breaker CT (\texttt{mW}), differential thermal plenum (\texttt{mK}), particulate (\texttt{ug}), and SPAD ToF threshold (\texttt{mm}) arrays powering the fractal Spatial Hypervisor.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~7} formalizes Fast-Path Physical Hazard Interlocks, the 64-byte \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1} and its five mathematical predicates $\mathbf{\Phi}$, Joukowsky water-hammer slew limits, back-calculation anti-windup PID stability, Two-Speed Jurisprudence preemption, and the Red Lever Rule.
