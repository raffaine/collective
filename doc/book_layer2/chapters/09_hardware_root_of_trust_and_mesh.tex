\section{The Oracle Problem and the Microchip \texttt{ATECC608A} Hardware Root of Trust}
\label{sec:l2-atecc608a-root-of-trust}

While Layer~3 (\texttt{col-storaged}) is protected by content-addressed Merkle-DAGs and Layer~1 physics obeys strict conservation laws, Layer~2 sits at the physical oracle threshold between them. If an adversary or defective driver could inject falsified exergy claims (e.g., spoofing $100{,}000{,}000{,}000\,\mu\mathrm{J}$ of solar generation or falsifying a Stage~II Right-to-Repair burn-in attestation), the settlement's thermodynamic accounting and Proof of Stewardship ($R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t)$) would be corrupted.

To anchor physical transduction in unforgeable mathematics, every Tier~1 and Tier~2 Layer~2 node embeds a \textbf{Microchip \texttt{ATECC608A} Cryptographic Co-Processor} \parencite{microchip_atecc608a} isolated within \textbf{Zephyr MPU Domain~0} over a dedicated $400\,\mathrm{kHz}$ I$^2$C bus:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Internal Thermal-Avalanche Key Genesis (\texttt{Scenario Tau}):} During Day-0 JTAG/SWD provisioning, the node's private hardware signing key ($sk_{\mathrm{hw}}$) is minted internally inside hardware Slot~0 using the \texttt{ATECC608A}'s NIST SP~800-90A/B/C True Random Number Generator (\texttt{TRNG}) driven by thermal diode avalanche noise. Slot~0 is permanently write-locked and configured as non-exportable; $sk_{\mathrm{hw}}$ can never be read across the I$^2$C pins.
    \item \textbf{Deterministic \texttt{Ed25519} Sensor Claim Signing:} Every conditioned fixed-point SI observation $i$ is bound to the device's 72-bit silicon Unique Identifier (\texttt{UID}), a non-volatile monotonic hardware counter $c_{\mathrm{mono}}$ (rendering replay attacks impossible), the Hybrid Logical Clock timestamp $\tau_{\mathrm{hlc\_ms}}$ \parencite{kulkarni2014logical}, the canonical SI unit tag, the Kalman state estimate $\hat{x}_{i,\mathrm{si}}$, and the 3-tier provenance byte (\texttt{0x02}, \texttt{0x04}, \texttt{0x08}) via an \texttt{Ed25519} signature \parencite{bernstein2012ed25519}:
    \begin{equation}
        \sigma_i = \mathrm{Sign}_{\mathrm{Ed25519}}\!\Bigl(sk_{\mathrm{hw}},\; \mathrm{BLAKE2b}\bigl(\mathrm{UID} \parallel c_{\mathrm{mono}} \parallel \tau_{\mathrm{hlc\_ms}} \parallel \mathrm{ChanID} \parallel \mathrm{SIUnit} \parallel \hat{x}_{i,\mathrm{si}} \parallel \sigma_{\hat{x}_i,\mathrm{bp}} \parallel \mathrm{QualFlag}\bigr)\Bigr).
        \label{eq:l2-ed25519-claim}
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Active Serpentine Shield and Physical Anti-Tamper Zeroization (\texttt{Scenario Rho}):} The \texttt{ATECC608A} die is protected by an active metal serpentine capacitance mesh; focused ion beam (\texttt{FIB}) decapping immediately zeroes cryptographic EEPROM. At the enclosure boundary, normally-closed (\texttt{NC}) chassis microswitches monitor the junction box lid. If an adversary forces open an outdoor field pod to attach a logic analyzer or JTAG probe, the switch break triggers a non-maskable hardware interrupt that actively zeroes Domain~0 SRAM session keys within $< 10\,\mu\mathrm{s}$, logs a \texttt{TAMPER\_SEAL\_BREACH} frame, and quarantines the node's DID in Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}) until re-provisioned by physical 2-of-3 steward quorum. Once Genesis keys are provisioned into TrustZone/MPU flash, the external JTAG/SWD debug fuse is blown.
\end{enumerate}

\section{1-Hz \texttt{BLAKE2b} Epoch Merkle Batching Across \texttt{l2\_l3.sock}}
\label{sec:l2-epoch-merkle-batching}

Sampling microgrid shunts, acoustic partition boundaries, and CNC spindle encoders at $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$ to $1{,}000\,\mathrm{Hz}$ produces tens of thousands of observations per minute. Writing individual 64-byte signatures directly to Layer~3's RocksDB write-ahead log (\texttt{WAL}) would induce severe flash write amplification and compaction stalls on salvaged 20-year storage media.

Layer~2 (\texttt{col-sensord} and \texttt{col-membraned}) eliminates write amplification through \textbf{1-Hz \texttt{BLAKE2b} Epoch Merkle Batching} \parencite{saarinen2013blake2}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Over each $T_{\mathrm{epoch}} = 1{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}$ window, the aggregator collects $N = 2^h$ discrete fixed-point SI sensor claims and hashes each leaf with domain-separation prefix \texttt{0x00}:
    \begin{equation}
        L_i = \mathrm{BLAKE2b\text{-}256}\bigl(\mathtt{0x00} \parallel \mathrm{Encode}(\mathrm{SensorClaim}_i)\bigr), \qquad i \in \{0,\dots,N-1\}.
        \label{eq:l2-merkle-leaf-hash}
    \end{equation}
    \item Internal binary nodes are folded recursively with prefix \texttt{0x01}:
    \begin{equation}
        I_{j,d} = \mathrm{BLAKE2b\text{-}256}\bigl(\mathtt{0x01} \parallel I_{2j,d-1} \parallel I_{2j+1,d-1}\bigr), \qquad \mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{epoch}} = I_{0,h}.
        \label{eq:l2-merkle-internal-hash}
    \end{equation}
    \item The \texttt{ATECC608A} executes a single hardware signature over the compact \textbf{Epoch Manifest} $M_{\mathrm{batch}}$ containing the Merkle root $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{epoch}}$ and exact integer epoch summary statistics $\mathbf{\Sigma}_{\mathrm{stats}} = \langle x_{\min,\mathrm{si}},\, x_{\max,\mathrm{si}},\, \bar{x}_{\mathrm{si}},\, \Delta E_{\mathrm{epoch\_uj}} \rangle$:
    \begin{equation}
        M_{\mathrm{batch}} = \mathrm{UID} \parallel c_{\mathrm{epoch}} \parallel \tau_{\mathrm{start\_ms}} \parallel \tau_{\mathrm{end\_ms}} \parallel \mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{epoch}} \parallel N \parallel \mathbf{\Sigma}_{\mathrm{stats}}, \qquad \sigma_{\mathrm{batch}} = \mathrm{Sign}_{\mathrm{Ed25519}}\bigl(sk_{\mathrm{hw}},\, \mathrm{BLAKE2b}(M_{\mathrm{batch}})\bigr).
        \label{eq:l2-epoch-manifest-sig}
    \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}

Only $(M_{\mathrm{batch}}, \sigma_{\mathrm{batch}})$ crosses \path{/run/collective/ipc/l2_l3.sock} into \texttt{col-storaged} once per second. Whenever Layer~4 or a peer audit challenges a specific sub-second sample $k$, \texttt{col-telemetryd} serves the $O(\log_2 N)$ Merkle inclusion branch $\Pi_k = \{\mathrm{sibling}_1,\dots,\mathrm{sibling}_h\}$ from its local circular DRAM cache.

\section{\texttt{col-meshd}: Low-Power Field Networking, RFID Locality, and Sovereign Library Gossip}
\label{sec:l2-col-meshd-networking}

Because a Sovereign Node cannot rely on corporate Internet Service Providers (\texttt{Scenario Omega: Decoupling} and \texttt{Scenario Upsilon: Shock}), Layer~2 includes a dedicated mesh transport daemon, \textbf{\texttt{col-meshd}}, which bridges local field transducers and inter-node Layer~3 CRDT / HTLC traffic over physical radio, optical fiber, and vehicular sneakernet links (\texttt{Scenario Beta} Artery~5).

\subsection{1. LoRa Chirp Spread Spectrum and the Ban on Bloated Wire JSON}

For long-range watershed and agroforestry pods (\texttt{Scenario Eta}, \texttt{Scenario Mu}, \texttt{Scenario Omega}), \texttt{col-meshd} drives Semtech \texttt{SX1262} transceivers in the sub-GHz ISM band ($915\,\mathrm{MHz}$ / $868\,\mathrm{MHz}$) under the Reticulum Network Stack. Utilizing Chirp Spread Spectrum (\texttt{CSS}) modulation, LoRa demodulates signals up to $20\,\mathrm{dB}$ ($200\,\mathrm{cB}$) below the thermal noise floor, bounded by the Shannon-Hartley channel capacity theorem \parencite{shannon1949communication}:
\begin{equation}
    C_{\mathrm{bps}} = B_{\mathrm{hz}} \log_2\bigl(1 + \mathrm{SNR}\bigr).
    \label{eq:l2-shannon-hartley}
\end{equation}
Adjusting the Spreading Factor ($\mathrm{SF} \in \{7,\dots,12\}$) trades bit rate for physical range. When an acute hazard or shock occurs (\texttt{Scenario Chi} / \texttt{Scenario Upsilon}), transceivers shift automatically to $\mathrm{SF12}$, where packet Time-on-Air (\texttt{ToA}) scales exponentially:
\begin{equation}
    T_{\mathrm{packet\_ms}} = \frac{2^{\mathrm{SF}}\cdot 1{,}000}{\mathrm{BW}_{\mathrm{hz}}} \times N_{\mathrm{symbols}}.
    \label{eq:l2-lora-toa}
\end{equation}
Because $\mathrm{SF12}$ at $\mathrm{BW} = 125\,\mathrm{kHz}$ requires $32.768\,\mathrm{ms}$ per symbol, \textbf{Layer~2 strictly forbids verbose text JSON payloads over radio or serial links}. All fieldbus and LoRa frames are serialized as 64-byte fixed-point binary structs or deterministic \textbf{Canonical CBOR (RFC~8949)} \parencite{rfc8949} with integer map keys framed via Consistent Overhead Byte Stuffing (\texttt{COBS}) \parencite{cheshire1999cobs}.

\subsection{2. Passive UHF RFID Backscatter and $R^{-4}$ Spatial Locality (\texttt{Scenario Nu}, \texttt{Gamma})}

To track textile provenance (\texttt{Scenario Nu}), reusable stainless tiffin containers (\texttt{Scenario Gamma}), and tool-crib checkouts (\textit{The Workshop}) without battery-powered trackers, Layer~2 uses passive Ultra-High Frequency (\texttt{UHF}) RFID tags operating via backscatter impedance modulation. Received power $P_r$ at the reader obeys the bistatic radar equation \parencite{dobkin2012rf}:
\begin{equation}
    P_r = \frac{P_t\, G_t\, G_r\, \lambda^2\, \sigma_{\mathrm{rcs}}}{(4\pi)^3\, R^4}.
    \label{eq:l2-rfid-bistatic}
\end{equation}
Because received backscatter power decays with the fourth power of distance ($R^{-4}$), passive UHF RFID enforces strict \textbf{cryptographic spatial locality}: an interrogator can energize and read a garment, tool, or tiffin tag only within $R \le 3{,}000\,\mathrm{mm}$ ($3.0\,\mathrm{meters}$), physically preventing remote spoofing of custody handoffs.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Purging \texttt{double rate\_per\_tick} from \texttt{FlowContract}:} Replace the legacy IEEE-754 floating-point flow rate with canonical 64-bit integer microjoules per tick (\texttt{int64\_t rate\_uj\_per\_tick}) and milligrams/milliliters per tick.
    \item \textbf{DTN Gossip Propagation of the Three Sovereign Libraries and \texttt{thermodynamicProof}:} Across OS2 optical fiber, $802.11\mathrm{ah}$ Wi-Fi HaLow, LoRa, and Scenario~Beta cargo e-trike Delay-Tolerant Networking (\texttt{DTN}) sneakernet bricks \parencite{fall2003,shapiro2011crdt}, \texttt{col-meshd} replicates content-addressed deltas for the three \textbf{Sovereign Libraries} (\textit{Ontological Graph}, \textit{Orchestration Registry}, and \textit{Physics Baseline} \texttt{collective:thermodynamicProof} records) alongside federated Tier~3 True-Constraint Hard-Block capability queries for \texttt{col-swarmd}.
\end{enumerate}
