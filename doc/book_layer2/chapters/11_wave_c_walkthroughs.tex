\section{Scenario Delta: Perinatal Sanctuary, Nap-Hour Acoustic Shields, and Localized BLE AoA (\texttt{SCN-DELTA-CHILDCARE} / \texttt{delta\_childcare})}
\label{sec:l2-wave-c-delta}

Scenario~Delta ($N_s = 4 \to 18$ care stewards and parents) upholds constitutional non-commensurability (\texttt{AttemptTokenizeBaselineCare() == false}): childcare is never priced, metered for wages, or subjected to camera surveillance. At Layer~2, Scenario~Delta relies on three specialized hardware protections:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Priority \texttt{950} Nursery Acoustic Sleep-Shield (\texttt{4,000 cB}) and Thermal Floor (\texttt{294,150 mK}):} Inside the building's Enveloping Node Hypervisor, the Nursery Wing holds a priority-\texttt{950} Care Shield lease enforcing an indoor thermal comfort band of $294{,}150 \pm 1{,}000\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($21.0 \pm 1.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$) and a nap-window partition acoustic ceiling of $\mathtt{max\_acoustic\_cb} = 4{,}000\,\mathrm{cB}$ ($40.0\,\mathrm{dBA}$). If an adjacent Maker Bench or courtyard workflow causes the nursery partition's PDM MEMS microphone (with waveform zeroization, Equation~\eqref{eq:l2-centibel-spl}) to approach $4{,}000\,\mathrm{cB}$ during an active nap lease, \texttt{col-execd} and \texttt{col-actuated} automatically throttle or pause the adjacent tier-\texttt{100} high-decibel machine lease in hardware time.
    \item \textbf{\textit{The Pantry} Donor-Milk and Formula Cold-Chain (\texttt{275,150..277,150 mK}):} Dedicated medical-grade breastmilk and infant formula refrigerators log continuous 4-wire PT100 Arrhenius thermal-time (\texttt{PantryPerishableBatchV50}) backed by Tier~1 $\mathrm{LiFePO_4}$ uninterruptible battery reserve ($\Theta_{\mathrm{enter}} = 0\,\mathrm{bp}$). Pasteurized donor milk bottles carry passive NFC tags verified against Bigelow thermal hold records ($\ge 335{,}650\,\mathrm{mK}$ [$62.5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$] Holder pasteurization for $1{,}800\,\mathrm{s}$).
    \item \textbf{Hyper-Localized BLE Direction Finding (Angle-of-Arrival Geofencing):} To alert stewards if a toddler wanders toward an exterior gate or workshop threshold without deploying optical cameras or cloud GPS trackers, perimeter doorways mount multi-antenna Bluetooth Low Energy (\texttt{BLE~5.1}) Angle-of-Arrival (\texttt{AoA}) arrays. Measuring carrier phase difference $\Delta\phi$ across antenna elements separated by distance $d$ yields spatial bearing angle $\theta$:
    \begin{equation}
        \theta = \arccos\!\left(\frac{\lambda_{\mathrm{rf}}\, \Delta\phi}{2\pi d}\right),
        \label{eq:l2-ble-aoa}
    \end{equation}
    providing sub-meter threshold alerts strictly inside the local Domain~1 Zephyr mesh while zero location packets ever cross outside the nursery subnet.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Scenario Kappa: Pillar II Dual-Key Apprenticeship Interlocks (\texttt{SCN-KAPPA-APPRENTICESHIP} / \texttt{kappa\_apprenticeship})}
\label{sec:l2-wave-c-kappa}

Scenario~Kappa ($N_s = 2 \to 20$ mentors and apprentices) is the constitutional showcase for \textbf{Scale Regime~II (Dyadic Mentor-Apprentice Asymmetry)} under $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$ and \textbf{Pillar~II (\texttt{collective:WorkCapabilityMatrix})} (\texttt{D3}, \texttt{D6}).

Early October 2026 drafts of Layer~2 mistakenly proposed verifying apprentice hours via ``Cryptographic Proof of Labor''---using tool Hall-effect sensors and 6-DoF IMUs to score worker motion signatures. As established in Chapter~\ref{ch:non-panoptic-membrane}, that worker-surveillance model is permanently abolished. Instead, Layer~2 implements \textbf{Pillar~II Dual-Key Hardware Interlocks} inside the Training Bay Envelope ($\le 7{,}500\,\mathrm{cB}$):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Tier-0 Unsupervised Safe Prep Tasks ($R_{\mathrm{apprentice}}(0) = 0$):} An inducted newcomer starts with $R_2(0) = 0$ and non-decaying civic baseline $B = 1$ ($V_2(0) = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$). Layer~2 NFC tool-crib latches allow any member with $B=1$ to check out low-hazard Tier-0 hand tools, run non-energized PCB assembly, or execute cold calibration steps unsupervised, earning initial $\Delta R_2 > 0$ when Layer~2 test fixtures verify the completed artifact.
    \item \textbf{Tier-1 Co-Signed Dual-Key High-Exergy Tasks (\texttt{require\_dual\_key\_kappa = 1}):} When the apprentice advances to operating a $4{,}500{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$ 3-axis CNC mill, a TIG welder, or a live $240\,\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{AC}}$ Stage~II Right-to-Repair appliance burn-in bench, \texttt{col-actuated} enforces a physical \textbf{Dual-Key Hardware AND-Gate} on the Class-T contactor holding coil:
    \begin{equation}
        \Phi_{\mathrm{kappa\_dual}} \iff \mathrm{Verify}_{\mathrm{BBS+}}\bigl(\pi_{\mathrm{apprentice\_tier1}}\bigr) \;\land\; \mathrm{Verify}_{\mathrm{Ed25519}}\bigl(\sigma_{\mathrm{mentor\_key\_switch}},\, R_{\mathrm{mentor\_bp}} \ge R_{\min,\mathrm{cert}}\bigr).
        \label{eq:l2-kappa-dual-key}
    \end{equation}
    Both the apprentice's capability token and the supervising mentor's physical rotary key-switch on the machine console must be engaged concurrently. Upon verified completion of the machined part or Stage~II retrofit kit (\texttt{RetrofitBurnInAttestationV50}), Layer~3 credits full stewardship $\Delta R_{2,\mathrm{bp}}$ to the apprentice without ever monitoring their bodily movements.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Scenario Zeta: The Building Enveloping Hypervisor and Cryptographic Hospitality (\texttt{SCN-ZETA-LODGING} / \texttt{zeta\_lodging})}
\label{sec:l2-wave-c-zeta}

Scenario~Zeta ($N_s = 20 \to 120$ building residents, each holding non-decaying $B = 1 \equiv 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ civic standing) operates the \textbf{Primary Building and Block Enveloping Node Hypervisor} wrapping all indoor child nodes (residential suites, ground-floor Hearth, basement Maker Bench, and Acoustic Studio):

\subsection{1. Offline-First Capability-Leased Smart Locks with Mechanical Fail-Safe Egress}

When a federation guest or resident unlocks a suite door, their local device presents an anonymous \texttt{BBS+} or \texttt{Ed25519} capability lease token over NFC / BLE to the door's Tier~2 \texttt{ESP32-C6} controller. Upon signature verification against the cached Layer~5 root inside Domain~0, the microcontroller drives a MOSFET energizing the deadbolt solenoid coil. By Ampere's and Faraday's laws, the magnetic traction force $F_{\mathrm{mag}}$ across air gap $g$ is \parencite{griffiths2005introduction}:
\begin{equation}
    F_{\mathrm{mag}} = \frac{\mu_0\, N_{\mathrm{turns}}^2\, I^2\, A_{\mathrm{core}}}{2 g^2},
    \label{eq:l2-solenoid-force}
\end{equation}
scaled in millinewtons (\texttt{mN}) from coil current $I_{\mathrm{ma}}$ and gap $g_{\mathrm{um}}$. In strict compliance with the \textbf{Red Lever Rule} and life-safety fire codes, the interior door handle is mechanically coupled directly to the deadbolt retraction cam: an occupant can always exit with one hand even if building power and silicon fail completely, and the door controller never logs identity-linked entry/exit timestamps to Layer~3.

\subsection{2. Building-Scale Two-Speed Jurisprudence Execution}

Scenario~Zeta's Layer~2 partition network (\texttt{EnvelopingPartitionTelemetryFrameV50} pods on every stairwell, riser, and party wall) executes the building's \textbf{Two-Speed Jurisprudence} engine (Chapter~\ref{ch:fast-path-hazard-interlocks}):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Fast Path:} Sub-panel solid-state CTs enforce the building's diurnal power envelope; partition MEMS microphones (\texttt{cB}) enforce nighttime quiet hours (\texttt{22:00--07:00}: $\le 4{,}000\,\mathrm{cB}$); and utility-chase water-ingress / smoke sensors trigger instant priority-\texttt{950} hazard preemption (shutting main water solenoids under Joukowsky slew limits and overriding studio quiet leases for emergency wet-vac extraction).
    \item \textbf{Slow Path:} Whenever two child nodes collide on an unmapped courtyard or elevator lease, \texttt{col-telemetryd} supplies the signed objective physical trace (\texttt{cB}, \texttt{mW}, \texttt{mK}, \texttt{ms}) for upward decompilation into a Layer~6 Semantic Diff ballot whose ratified precedent compiles directly back into the building's Layer~4 and Layer~2 guard tables.
\end{itemize}
