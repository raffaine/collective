\section{The Day-Zero Bootstrap Reality: Scavenged Appliances and the $\$20{,}000$ Reserve (\texttt{D7}, \texttt{D8})}
\label{sec:l2-stage1-boot-reality}

A persistent fallacy in utopian systems engineering is assuming that a new community boots inside a gleaming sci-fi cleanroom populated from Day~0 with custom open-source silicon, precision-cast modular machinery, and native fieldbus sensors. In physical reality---and inside the Oasis simulation proving ground (\texttt{Scenario Tau: Genesis} on Lot~402)---communities bootstrap out of the ruins and surplus of Layer~7.

When a Stage~1A Genesis Node launches with its initial $\$20{,}000$ fiat reserve ($\Phi_0 = 2{,}000{,}000\,\text{\texttt{cents}}$), stewards do not spend $\$18{,}000$ commissioning a custom industrial autoclave or a bespoke open-frame induction hearth. They buy secondhand commercial refrigerators off Craigslist, salvage three-phase bakery deck ovens from closed restaurants, pick up used consumer washing machines, repurpose CAN-locked cargo e-bikes, and inherit desktop CNC routers or 3D printers whose controller boards are encased in black epoxy potting compound and locked behind vendor Digital Rights Management (\texttt{DRM}).

In Dialogue~7 and Dialogue~8 (\texttt{CCI-6}), the Horizon Council codified a foundational design law governing how the Sovereign Stack models this hardware evolution:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Prohibition of Fake RPG Tech Trees (\texttt{CCI-6}):} Neither Oasis nor the Sovereign Stack monographs may ever invent an artificial video-game ``Tech Tree'' (e.g., \textit{``Spend 500 Science Points to unlock Tier~2 Ovens with $+25\%$ Cooking Speed''}). A $2{,}200{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$ heating element obeys the same First Law of Thermodynamics whether it is controlled by a proprietary corporate PCB or an open-source RISC-V board. Every advantage of progressing from Stage~I Scavenged Layer~7 appliances to Stage~II Right-to-Repair Hybrid Retrofits and Stage~III Native Open-Spec Collective Twins emerges \textbf{purely and mechanically from eliminating four structural friction penalties (``the shim tax'') across Layers~2, 3, 4, and~7}.
\end{quote}

\subsection{The Five Hardware Channels of a Stage I Parasitic Shim}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Split-Core Non-Invasive Current Transformer (CT) State Inference:} Because the appliance's internal power relays cannot be queried, a split-core ferromagnetic CT clamp (e.g., \texttt{SCT-013-030}) is clipped around the external AC supply conductor. By sampling the $50/60\,\mathrm{Hz}$ current envelope at $2{,}400\,\mathrm{Hz}$, the shim infers coarse operational states from quantized power plateaus:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item $P_{\mathrm{mw}} \in [2{,}000,\; 12{,}000]\,\mathrm{mW}$: Idle standby phantom draw (proprietary Wi-Fi board + display transformer).
        \item $P_{\mathrm{mw}} \in [120{,}000,\; 350{,}000]\,\mathrm{mW}$: Circulation pump or agitation motor active.
        \item $P_{\mathrm{mw}} \in [1{,}800{,}000,\; 3{,}200{,}000]\,\mathrm{mW}$: Primary resistive heating element or compressor active.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Exterior Taped Thermistor Thermal Lag and Coarse Uncertainty ($\pm 1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$):} When a sealed fridge or commercial oven hides its internal RTD probe on a proprietary bus, stewards tape an external surface thermocouple or NTC thermistor to the outer wall or exhaust flue. Because heat must diffuse through the thermal resistance $R_{\mathrm{wall}}$ and capacitance $C_{\mathrm{wall}}$ of the chassis insulation:
    \begin{equation}
        \tau_{\mathrm{lag}} = R_{\mathrm{wall}} C_{\mathrm{wall}} \approx 180{,}000\text{--}420{,}000\,\mathrm{ms} \quad (3\text{--}7\,\text{minutes}),
        \label{eq:l2-shim-thermal-lag}
    \end{equation}
    the inferred internal core temperature $\hat{T}_{\mathrm{core\_mk}}(t) = T_{\mathrm{skin\_mk}}(t) + \alpha_{\mathrm{cal}}\bigl(T_{\mathrm{skin\_mk}}(t) - T_{\mathrm{amb\_mk}}(t)\bigr)$ suffers severe phase lag and a $\pm 1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($\pm 15\%$) uncertainty band. Consequently, Layer~4 (\texttt{col-execd}) must pad Bigelow pasteurization hold windows and cold-chain safety margins on Stage~I gear to compensate for sensor lag.
    \item \textbf{RP2040 PIO Photodiode 7-Segment Fault-Code Scraper:} Many commercial appliances expose fault states exclusively by blinking a multiplexed 7-segment LED display (\texttt{E04}, \texttt{F21}). Rather than pointing a power-hungry camera at the machine (which would risk violating \texttt{INV\_ANTI\_PANOPTICON}), stewards adhere a 3D-printed light-proof shroud containing 14 miniature photodiodes directly over the 7-segment digits. An \texttt{RP2040} Programmable I/O (\texttt{PIO}) state machine locks onto the $250\,\mathrm{Hz}$ cathode-multiplexing strobe in deterministic hardware cycles and translates segment bitmasks into integer fault codes (\texttt{SCAVENGED\_FAULT\_CODE\_E04}) with zero optical imaging.
    \item \textbf{Inline Solid-State Relay (SSR) and Optocoupler Button Actuation:} To start or halt a Stage~I appliance without internal bus access, the shim solders reed-relay optocouplers (\texttt{PC817}) across the front-panel membrane pushbuttons (or uses a mechanical micro-servo finger) paired with a main AC line zero-crossing SSR contactor for hard emergency cutoff.
    \item \textbf{Local VLAN-Isolated Cloud-Spoofing Responder:} Certain late-capitalist appliances (e.g., cloud-tethered solar inverters, filament printers, or smart heat pumps) refuse to boot unless they complete a TLS handshake with a vendor telemetry server every 24 hours. Layer~2 cages the appliance's Ethernet/Wi-Fi MAC address inside a dead-end local bridge where a lightweight responder emulates the vendor's \texttt{200 OK} keep-alive JSON heartbeat locally while blocking 100\% of outbound WAN traffic.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Mathematical Model of Stage I Shim Overhead and Blind Hard-Blocks}
\label{sec:l2-stage1-math-model}

In \path{src/stack/shared_stack_runtime.hpp}, \path{src/daemons/col-telemetryd/main.cpp}, and \path{src/daemons/col-adversaryd/adversary_engine.hpp}, Stage~I hardware friction is governed by two deterministic C++20 structures: the parasitic exergy tax and the blind mid-batch hard-block generator (Listing~\ref{lst:l2-stage1-shim-tribute}).

Given a batch workflow of duration $\Delta t_{\mathrm{batch\_ms}}$ running on a Stage~I machine with active process power $P_{\mathrm{active\_mw}}$, parasitic shim and standby power $P_{\mathrm{shim\_mw}} = \mathtt{parasitic\_shim\_overhead\_mw}$, and high Stage~I Ecological Replacement Cost $\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC,StageI\_uj}}$, total batch exergy charged in Layer~3 is:
\begin{equation}
    E_{\mathrm{total,StageI\_uj}} = \bigl(P_{\mathrm{active\_mw}} + P_{\mathrm{shim\_mw}}\bigr)\cdot \Delta t_{\mathrm{batch\_ms}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{ERC,StageI\_uj}}.
    \label{eq:l2-stage1-total-exergy}
\end{equation}
Even when idle between batches ($\Delta t_{\mathrm{idle\_ms}}$), a Stage~I appliance keeping its proprietary transformer and cloud-spoofing shim alive drains continuous standby exergy $E_{\mathrm{standby\_uj}} = P_{\mathrm{shim\_mw}} \cdot \Delta t_{\mathrm{idle\_ms}}$.

Worse, because the sealed PCB hides internal component degradation ($\mathcal{M}_i(t)$ in Equation~\eqref{eq:l2-mahalanobis-anomaly} cannot observe internal motor windings or bearing harmonics), the appliance exhibits a per-cycle unscheduled fault probability $p_{\mathrm{fault\_bp}} = \mathtt{unscheduled\_fault\_prob\_bp} \approx 350\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($3.50\%$ per heavy cycle). When a blind fault strikes mid-batch:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Layer~2 detects an abrupt current drop ($P_{\mathrm{mw}} \to P_{\mathrm{shim\_mw}}$) and a 7-segment fault strobe (\texttt{ERR\_42}), emitting \texttt{UpwardYieldPacketV31} with \texttt{YieldReason::SCAVENGED\_L7\_BLIND\_HARDBLOCK}.
    \item Layer~4 halts the active BPMN token and triggers a Regime~2 Forward Physical Salvage Saga (routing half-baked dough or cooling broth to compost/BSFL before bacterial danger-zone limits expire).
    \item Because no open CAD or PCB schematic exists yet for the Stage~I unit, the fault escalates through Layer~5 and Layer~6 to Layer~7 (\texttt{col-adversaryd}), emitting a \texttt{ScavengedHardwareTributeEvent} that deducts external fiat spare-part tribute (\texttt{fiat\_tribute\_cents}) from $\Phi_t$ and locks the station for $\mathtt{courier\_lead\_time\_ticks}$ ($48\text{--}120\,\mathrm{hours}$).
\end{enumerate}
