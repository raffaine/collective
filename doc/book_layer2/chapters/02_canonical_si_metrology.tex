\section{Why Market Fragmentation Dissolves Into Canonical SI Uniformity (\texttt{D6}, \texttt{D8})}
\label{sec:l2-two-zone-metrology}

In legacy Layer~7 markets, metrological and mechanical fragmentation is deliberately engineered. Appliance conglomerates proliferate fifteen incompatible battery sleds, proprietary imperial and fractional fastener heads, bespoke thermistor Beta curves, and non-standard tray dimensions to impose switching costs and capture monopoly spare-part rents. Inside software stacks, legacy industrial controllers compound this fragmentation by transmitting untyped IEEE-754 floating-point values across network boundaries---precipitating unit-conversion failures whenever one subsystem interprets a float as gallons per minute or degrees Fahrenheit while another expects liters per second or kelvins.

In Dialogue~6 and Dialogue~8 (\texttt{CCI-6}), the Horizon Council resolved how the Sovereign Stack eliminates both floating-point non-determinism and metrological fragmentation without erecting an authoritarian central planning bureaucracy:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Thermodynamic Gradient Descent via $\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$:} Once Layer~3 (\texttt{col-storaged}) computes the lifecycle \textbf{Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$)} of every physical component in canonical microjoules (\texttt{uJ}), proprietary non-standard parts expose themselves as pure thermodynamic waste (low reuse cycles $N_{\mathrm{reuse\_cycles}}$ and high amortized retooling exergy). Independent nodes minimizing total workflow path exergy $\sum (E_{\mathrm{direct}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}})$ voluntarily converge on open modular standards: \textbf{Gastronorm GN~1/1 pans ($530 \times 325\,\mathrm{mm}$)}, \textbf{ISO $600 \times 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ Euro-totes}, and \textbf{ISO metric \texttt{M6} / \texttt{M8} fasteners}.
    \item \textbf{The Two-Zone Metrological Invariant (\texttt{CCI-6}):} Internally, \textbf{Layers~2, 3, and~4 enforce a single canonical 64-bit fixed-point International System (SI) metrological core} (\texttt{int64\_t}, with \texttt{floating\_point\_opcodes == 0}), while \textbf{Layer~6 acts as a human localization lens} that translates familiar craft or cultural idioms (baker's hydration percentages, imperial inches, degrees Fahrenheit or Celsius, cups, A-weighted decibels) at the human edge prior to Stage~4 Deterministic Read-Back.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{invariant}[Exact Microjoule Integration Identity and the $10^{12}$ Megajoule Scaling Law]
\label{inv:l2-microjoule-identity}
In every Layer~2 ADC power integrator (\texttt{col-sensord}), Layer~3 ledger record (\texttt{col-storaged}), and Layer~4 workflow step (\texttt{col-execd}), electrical or thermal power $P_{\mathrm{mw}} \in \mathbb{Z}$ (in milliwatts, $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{W}$) integrated over discrete sampling interval $\Delta t_{\mathrm{ms}} \in \mathbb{Z}$ (in milliseconds, $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s}$) yields exact integer microjoules $\Delta E_{\mathrm{uj}} \in \mathbb{Z}$ ($10^{-6}\,\mathrm{J}$) with zero division and zero truncation error:
\begin{equation}
    \Delta E_{\mathrm{uj}} = P_{\mathrm{mw}} \times \Delta t_{\mathrm{ms}} \qquad \Bigl(10^{-3}\,\mathrm{W} \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s} = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{J} = 1\,\mu\mathrm{J}\Bigr).
    \label{eq:l2-exact-uj}
\end{equation}
Furthermore, because $1\,\mathrm{J} = 10^6\,\mu\mathrm{J}$ and $1\,\mathrm{MJ} = 10^6\,\mathrm{J} = 10^{12}\,\mu\mathrm{J}$, any upward diagnostic projection from microjoules to megajoules must divide by \texttt{1'000'000'000'000LL} ($10^{12}$), never $10^9$ ($1\,\mathrm{kJ}$).
\end{invariant}

\section{Edge Transducer Physics and Direct Integer Quantization}
\label{sec:l2-adc-transducer-physics}

Every analog phenomenon entering a Tier~1 (\texttt{STM32H743}) or Tier~2 (\texttt{ESP32-S3}) microcontroller is converted directly from raw ADC counts into its canonical 64-bit SI integer representation inside Domain~1 of the Zephyr MPU using integer polynomial calibration and Euclidean carry-save remainder accumulators.

\subsection{High-Side Current Shunt and Exergy Metering (\texttt{uJ}, \texttt{mW})}

For server racks (\texttt{Scenario Zero}), $48\,\mathrm{V}$ $\mathrm{LiFePO_4}$ battery banks (\texttt{Scenario Alpha}), and branch circuit breakers (\texttt{Scenario Zeta}), high-side current-sense amplifiers (Texas Instruments \texttt{INA226} / \texttt{INA228}) measure bus potential $V_{\mathrm{bus}}$ and the differential drop $V_{\mathrm{shunt}}$ across a precision manganin shunt resistor $R_{\mathrm{shunt}}$ (typically $500\,\mu\Omega$ with $< 15\,\mathrm{ppm/K}$ thermal drift). The instantaneous active power in milliwatts is:
\begin{equation}
    P_{\mathrm{mw}}(k) = \left\lfloor \frac{V_{\mathrm{bus\_mv}}(k) \cdot I_{\mathrm{shunt\_ma}}(k) + r_{\mathrm{p}}(k-1)}{1{,}000} \right\rfloor, \qquad I_{\mathrm{shunt\_ma}}(k) = \frac{V_{\mathrm{shunt\_uv}}(k) \cdot 1{,}000}{R_{\mathrm{shunt\_uohm}}},
    \label{eq:l2-shunt-power}
\end{equation}
where $r_{\mathrm{p}}(k) = \bigl(V_{\mathrm{bus\_mv}}(k) \cdot I_{\mathrm{shunt\_ma}}(k) + r_{\mathrm{p}}(k-1)\bigr) \bmod 1{,}000$ is the carry-save sub-milliwatt remainder accumulator ensuring zero long-term energy drift. For an $N$-bit Delta-Sigma or SAR ADC ($N = 16$ to $24$), the theoretical Signal-to-Quantization-Noise Ratio is $\mathrm{SNR}_{\mathrm{dB}} = 6.02 N + 1.76\,\mathrm{dB}$ ($98.08\,\mathrm{dB}$ at 16 bits; $146.24\,\mathrm{dB}$ at 24 bits) \parencite{kester2005data}. Raw ADC sample $q(k)$ is clamped against input transient saturation via:
\begin{equation}
    q(k) = \mathrm{clamp}\!\left( \left\lfloor \frac{V(k T_s) - V_{\min}}{\Delta V} + \frac{1}{2} \right\rfloor,\; 0,\; 2^N - 1 \right), \qquad \Delta V = \frac{V_{\max} - V_{\min}}{2^N - 1}.
    \label{eq:l2-adc-clamp}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Seebeck Thermocouples and 4-Wire PT100 RTDs (\texttt{mK})}

In communal soup cauldrons (\texttt{culinary\_vessel\_v1} in \texttt{Scenario Gamma}), clinical autoclaves (\texttt{Scenario Chi}), and metallurgy induction furnaces (\texttt{Scenario Theta}), temperature is digitized in millikelvins (\texttt{mK}, where $0.00\,^\circ\mathrm{C} = 273{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$).
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{4-Wire Class-A PT100 RTDs ($200{,}000\text{--}673{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$):} A precision constant-current source ($I_{\mathrm{ex}} = 1{,}000\,\mu\mathrm{A}$) drives two outer leads while a 24-bit ratiometric ADC (\texttt{MAX31865}) measures potential across the two inner sense leads, cancelling lead-wire resistance completely. Callendar-Van Dusen linearization is evaluated in 64-bit integer arithmetic via a horner-factored fixed-point polynomial.
    \item \textbf{Type-K and Type-S Thermocouples ($273{,}150\text{--}1{,}973{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$):} Operating on the Seebeck effect $V_{\mathrm{tc}} = \int_{T_{\mathrm{cold}}}^{T_{\mathrm{hot}}} S(T)\,\mathrm{d}T$ \parencite{rowe1995crc}, isolated \texttt{MAX31855}/\texttt{MAX31856} digitizers perform internal cold-junction compensation and emit signed millikelvin integers directly over SPI.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Wheatstone Bridge Load Cells (\texttt{mg}), FDR Soil Dielectric (\texttt{bp}), and NDIR Gas (\texttt{ppm})}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Gravimetric Load Cells (\texttt{mg}):} In \textit{The Pantry} shelves, \textit{Dual-Loop Bakery} pickup racks, and compost scales, four strain gauges wired in a Wheatstone bridge yield differential output $\Delta V = V_{\mathrm{ex}}\bigl(\frac{R_1}{R_1+R_2} - \frac{R_3}{R_3+R_4}\bigr)$ \parencite{fraden2010handbook}. Digitized by a 24-bit bridge ADC (\texttt{HX711} or \texttt{ADS1232}) and compensated for ambient temperature (\texttt{mK}), net mass is reported in exact milligrams (\texttt{mg}).
    \item \textbf{FDR Multiparameter Rhizosphere Probes (\texttt{soil\_probe\_v1}, \texttt{bp}):} Rather than DC resistive probes that electrolyze and corrode within weeks, Layer~2 soil probes emit a $70\text{--}150\,\mathrm{MHz}$ Frequency Domain Reflectometry (FDR) oscillating electric field into the root zone. Because liquid water has relative permittivity $\varepsilon_r \approx 80$ compared to dry mineral soil ($\varepsilon_r \approx 3\text{--}5$), the resonant dielectric permittivity maps via integer Topp polynomial lookup directly to Volumetric Water Content in basis points ($\mathtt{moisture\_vwc\_bp} \in [0, 10{,}000]$), paired with $\mathtt{soil\_temp\_mk}$, $\mathtt{nitrogen\_ppm}$, $\mathtt{phosphorus\_ppm}$, $\mathtt{potassium\_ppm}$, $\mathtt{organic\_carbon\_bp}$, and $\mathtt{ph\_centi}$ (see \path{core/2_membrane/schemas/soil_probe_v1.json}).
    \item \textbf{Non-Dispersive Infrared (NDIR) Biogas \& Indoor Air Quality (\texttt{ppm}):} In anaerobic digesters (\texttt{Scenario Mu}) and coworking halls (\texttt{Scenario Epsilon}), gas concentration $c_{\mathrm{ppm}}$ ($\mathrm{CO_2}$ at $\lambda = 4.26\,\mu\mathrm{m}$, $\mathrm{CH_4}$ at $\lambda = 3.31\,\mu\mathrm{m}$) is derived from the Beer-Lambert optical absorption law $I = I_0 e^{-\varepsilon c l}$ \parencite{hodgkinson2013non}, completely isolating optical windows from corrosive $\mathrm{H_2S}$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Ashby's Law and the Five-Stage Afferent Variety Attenuation Pipeline}
\label{sec:l2-variety-attenuation}

By W.~Ross Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety \parencite{ashby1956}, a regulator's capacity is bounded by $\log_2 |V_R| \ge \log_2 |V_E| - \log_2 |V_O|$. Because Layer~1 environmental variety is continuous and uncountably infinite ($V_E = \aleph_1$) while Layer~3's RocksDB/Automerge ledger and Layer~4's $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$ BPMN engine are discrete and finite ($V_{\mathrm{stack}} \in \mathbb{Z}^k$), Layer~2 must avoid two opposite failure modes:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Entropic Saturation:} Forwarding unconditioned kilohertz ADC noise upward floods Unix sockets, bloats RocksDB SSTables, and causes CRDT merge storms.
    \item \textbf{Catastrophic Variety Collapse:} Naively averaging analog readings crushes high-frequency diagnostic precursors---such as rising battery internal resistance variance or bearing harmonic rattle---blinding the settlement until a machine suffers a catastrophic mid-batch breakdown.
\end{itemize}

Layer~2 resolves this tension through a deterministic \textbf{Five-Stage Afferent Variety Attenuation Pipeline}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Stage 1 --- Active 4th-Order Sallen-Key Anti-Aliasing:} Analog signals pass through hardware Butterworth low-pass filters with cutoff frequency $f_c \le \frac{1}{2} f_s$ satisfying the Nyquist-Shannon sampling theorem \parencite{nyquist1928,shannon1948}, rejecting $16\text{--}100\,\mathrm{kHz}$ inverter PWM switching harmonics before the ADC pin.
    \item \textbf{Stage 2 --- Saturation-Clamped Fixed-Point Quantization:} Conversion into canonical 64-bit SI integers (Table~\ref{tab:l2-canonical-si-core}) via Equation~\eqref{eq:l2-adc-clamp}.
    \item \textbf{Stage 3 --- Fixed-Point Discrete Kalman State Estimation:} Every Tier~1/Tier~2 channel executes a fixed-point Discrete Kalman Filter \parencite{kalman1960,grewal2014kalman} scaled in Q32.32 integer arithmetic:
    \begin{align}
        \hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k} &= \hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k-1} + \mathbf{K}_k \bigl(\mathbf{z}_k - \mathbf{H}_k \hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k-1}\bigr), \label{eq:l2-kalman-update}\\
        \mathbf{P}_{k|k} &= \bigl(\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{K}_k \mathbf{H}_k\bigr)\mathbf{P}_{k|k-1}, \label{eq:l2-kalman-cov}
    \end{align}
    separating true thermodynamic trajectory $\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k}$ from zero-mean Gaussian transducer noise.
    \item \textbf{Stage 4 --- Normalized Innovation Residual Anomaly Detection:} Rather than discarding the innovation residual $\tilde{\mathbf{y}}_k = \mathbf{z}_k - \mathbf{H}_k \hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k-1}$ and innovation covariance $\mathbf{S}_k = \mathbf{H}_k \mathbf{P}_{k|k-1} \mathbf{H}_k^T + \mathbf{R}_k$, Layer~2 integrates the standardized Mahalanobis innovation variance across $N_{\mathrm{eff}} = T_{\mathrm{detect}} \cdot f_s$ samples:
    \begin{equation}
        \mathcal{M}_i(t) = \frac{1}{N_{\mathrm{eff}}} \sum_{m=0}^{N_{\mathrm{eff}}-1} \frac{\tilde{y}_{i,k-m}^2}{\sigma_i^2} > 1 + \frac{3}{\sqrt{N_{\mathrm{eff}}}},
        \label{eq:l2-mahalanobis-anomaly}
    \end{equation}
    or, in square-root-free integer L1 folded-normal form ($\mathbb{E}[|\tilde{y}_i|] = \sigma_i\sqrt{2/\pi} \approx 7{,}979\,\mathrm{bp}\cdot\sigma_i$):
    \begin{equation}
        \frac{1}{N_{\mathrm{eff}}} \sum_{m=0}^{N_{\mathrm{eff}}-1} \Bigl(10{,}000\cdot|\tilde{y}_{i,k-m}| - 7{,}979\cdot\sigma_i\Bigr) > \frac{18{,}083\cdot\sigma_i}{\sqrt{N_{\mathrm{eff}}}}.
        \label{eq:l2-folded-normal-integer}
    \end{equation}
    Breaching Equation~\eqref{eq:l2-folded-normal-integer} flags incipient hardware degradation (e.g., subterranean pipe leakage, PV string bypass-diode failure, or spindle bearing wear) weeks before mean process values cross an alarm limit.
    \item \textbf{Stage 5 --- Significance-Triggered Deadband Delta Emission:} Upward frames are emitted over \path{l2_l3.sock} only when $|\hat{x}(t) - \hat{x}(t_{\mathrm{last}})| \ge \delta_{\mathrm{sig}}$ or $(t - t_{\mathrm{last}}) \ge \tau_{\mathrm{heartbeat}}$, cutting quiescent ledger write traffic by $> 90\%$ while preserving sub-millisecond transient capture.
\end{enumerate}
