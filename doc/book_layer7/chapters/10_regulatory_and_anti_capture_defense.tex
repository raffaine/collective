\section{Systemic Blinding: Cellular Independence and the Mundane Semantic Camouflage Filter}
\label{sec:l7-systemic-blinding}

Drawing directly on James C. Scott's analysis of administrative legibility \parencite{scott1998}, the Sovereign Stack refuses all umbrella corporate hierarchies. There is no ``Global Collective Inc.,'' no central foundation holding IP rights, and no shared bank account across nodes.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Army of Unremarkable Cooperatives:} To county assessors, tax auditors, and commercial banks, each settlement cell manifests as an ordinary, hyper-local entity: a neighborhood bakery cooperative (NAICS \texttt{311811} / CNAE \texttt{1091-1/02}), a community tool library and repair shop (NAICS \texttt{811310}), an urban market garden (NAICS \texttt{111419}), or a limited-equity housing cooperative (NAICS \texttt{531110}).
    \item \textbf{Asymmetric Legal Heterogeneity:} Neighboring nodes deliberately incorporate under distinct statutory vehicles (e.g., a Washington SPC + PPT, a California Consumer Cooperative, a Colorado LCA, a Brazilian \textit{Associa{\c{c}}{\~a}o Civil} paired with a \textit{Cooperativa de Trabalho}) represented by independent legal counsel, ensuring that no single judicial ruling or statutory amendment can sweep the federation.
    \item \textbf{The Mundane Semantic Camouflage Filter (\texttt{col-commonsd} $\to$ \texttt{col-adversaryd}):} Whenever Layer~6 compiles a ratified instruction across \texttt{l6\_l7.sock}, the \textbf{Mundane Semantic Camouflage Filter} translates internal cybernetic terminology into standard, boring statutory accounting and commercial prose. Internal terms such as \textit{Proof of Stewardship}, \textit{Enveloping Node Hypervisor}, \textit{Bioregional Path-Vector Protocol}, or \textit{The \TeX{} Asymptote} never appear on external invoices, tax returns (IRS Form~1120-C / Brazilian ECF), or bills of lading.
    \item \textbf{Five Corporate Veil \& Anti-Consolidation Firewalls:} To defeat ``alter ego'' or ``single business enterprise'' veil-piercing and Sherman Act \S~1 antitrust claims (alongside Capper-Volstead Act, 7~U.S.C.~\S\S~291--292 exemptions \parencite{cappervolstead1922}), nodes enforce: (i) zero overlapping board directors or bank signatories across nodes; (ii) distinct EIN/CNPJ registrations and separate SQLCipher general ledgers; (iii) absolute prohibition of cross-collateralization or debt guarantees; (iv) arm's-length Subchapter~T SSA / BMAA bills of lading referencing opaque \texttt{Treaty\_CID} exhibits; and (v) explicit contractual disclaimers of partnership or agency.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Subpoena Immunity via Data Non-Existence and \textit{NAACP v.\ Alabama}}
\label{sec:l7-data-nonexistence}

In \textit{NAACP v. State of Alabama ex rel. Patterson}, 357 U.S. 449 (1958) \parencite{naacp1958}, the U.S. Supreme Court established that the First Amendment shields an association's internal membership rosters from compelled state disclosure (a guarantee paralleled in Brazil by \texttt{Art.~5{\textordmasculine}, XVII--XXI CF/88} and the data-minimization mandate of \texttt{LGPD Lei 13.709/2018, Art.~6{\textordmasculine}, III} \parencite{brazil_cf88,brazil_lgpd2018}).

Beyond constitutional privilege, Layer~7 enforces a stronger physical guarantee: the \textbf{Doctrine of Data Non-Existence}. Because \texttt{col-adversaryd} resides on VLAN~40 DMZ with zero read paths to Layers~1--5 (\texttt{CCI-1}), and because Layer~5 strips personal DIDs from \textbf{\textit{Pantry}} appetite vectors via zero-knowledge \texttt{BBS+} proofs before writing 64-byte \texttt{AnonymousAppetiteLeaseV50} records to Layer~3 (\texttt{CCI-2}), the Layer~7 daemon physically possesses:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Zero} pairwise decentralized identifiers (\texttt{did:peer}) or Web-of-Trust social graphs;
    \item \textbf{Zero} Layer~5 assembly ballot tallies, liquid delegations, or stewardship scores $R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t)$;
    \item \textbf{Zero} household \textbf{\textit{Pantry}} consumption histories or weekend-detour schedules; and
    \item \textbf{Zero} unencrypted mesh topology maps.
\end{itemize}
When served with a broad civil subpoena or regulatory document demand (\texttt{REGULATORY\_DOCUMENT\_DEMAND}), the cooperative truthfully and completely produces its audited corporate tax returns, W-2 export payroll logs, vendor invoices, and property deeds. It cannot be held in contempt or accused of spoliation for failing to produce internal surveillance records that were never created or held in the first place.

\section{Defending Dormant Equilibrium Nodes Against Hostile Capture via $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$ (\texttt{D2}, \texttt{D3}, \texttt{CCI-2})}
\label{sec:l7-dormant-capture-defense}

A subtle external capture vector identified in Dialogues~2 and~3 (\texttt{D2}, \texttt{D3}) targets mature epistemic or physical commons (\texttt{Scenario Zero}, \texttt{Scenario Iota}, \texttt{Scenario Alpha}). When a software artifact or tool specification reaches \textbf{Dormant Equilibrium (``The \TeX{} Asymptote'')} \parencite{knuth1984tex}, servicing incidents drop to zero and every founding member's operational reputation $R_i(t)$ decays exponentially via reputational entropy $\lambda$ ($\tau_{1/2} = \ln 2 / \lambda$):
\begin{equation}
R_i(t) = R_i(t_0)\,e^{-\lambda(t - t_0)} \xrightarrow{t \to \infty} 0 \qquad \forall i \in \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{node}}
\label{eq:ch10-entropy-decay}
\end{equation}

Under a naive pure-meritocracy DAO ($V_i(t) = R_i(t)$ with $B = 0$), a corporate adversary could wait for a 50-member node to fall asleep ($\sum_j R_j(t) \to 0$) and instruct an infiltrator to submit one minor hardware-drift patch ($\Delta R_{\mathrm{attacker}} = 5{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$), instantly capturing $100\%$ of voting weight to re-license the commons or liquidate its assets.

Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}) and Layer~7's corporate bylaws make \textbf{Dormant-Node Single-Maintainer Hijack} mathematically impossible by pairing decaying operational reputation $R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t)$ with the \textbf{non-decaying Universal Civic Baseline $B_{\mathrm{bp}} = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($B = 1$)} scoped strictly to \textbf{Node Membership ($N_s$)}:
\begin{equation}
\frac{V_{\mathrm{attacker}}(t)}{\sum_{j=1}^{N_s} V_j(t)} = \frac{10{,}000 + \Delta R_{\mathrm{attacker,bp}}}{N_s \cdot 10{,}000 + \Delta R_{\mathrm{attacker,bp}}} < \frac{1}{2} \quad \forall N_s > \frac{\Delta R_{\mathrm{attacker,bp}}}{10{,}000} + 2
\label{eq:ch10-anti-capture-bound}
\end{equation}
For $N_s = 50$ members in total dormancy and $\Delta R_{\mathrm{attacker,bp}} = 5{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($0.5\,B$), the attacker acquires only $\frac{15{,}000}{505{,}000} = 2.97\%$ ($297\,\mathrm{bp}$) of the vote---nowhere near the $6{,}667\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($66.67\%$) constitutional supermajority required to alter the node's Layer~5 charter or instruct Layer~7 to encumber cooperative assets.
