\chapter{Physical Grid Leeching}

\section{The Fragility of the Macro-Grid}
The legacy nation-state relies on massive, centralized power grids and ISP monopolies. These systems are highly efficient during times of stability but catastrophically fragile during ecological shocks (Scenario Upsilon) or adversarial attacks (Scenario Rho). The Sovereign Stack treats the macro-grid purely as a temporary, volatile resource.

\section{Intelligent Grid Parasitism}
Layer 2 hardware (smart inverters and local battery banks) interfaces directly with the state power grid. Governed by Layer 7 bridging policies, the system engages in \textit{Intelligent Parasitism}. When macro-grid power is cheap and abundant, the mesh draws heavily, charging internal local batteries and running compute-intensive WASM tasks. The system mathematically arbitrages the state's infrastructure to subsidize its own bootstrapping.

\section{The Instant Islanding Protocol}
However, the bridge is designed to be severed. The moment Layer 2 sensors detect voltage sag, frequency instability, or a complete blackout on the macro-grid, the Layer 7 physical relays execute an \textit{Instant Islanding Protocol}. The physical connection to the state grid is severed in milliseconds. The community microgrid immediately shifts to internal solar, geothermal, and stored battery reserves, dynamically degrading Layer 6 UIs and throttling Layer 4 non-critical tasks to ensure perfect thermodynamic homeostasis while the legacy world goes dark.
