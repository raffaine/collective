\chapter{Residual Export, the Exergy Shield, and Fair-Trade Clearing}
\label{ch:l7-export-surplus-clearing}

\epigraph{The market must be an accessory feature of an institutional setting controlled and regulated more than ever by social authority.}{---\textcite{polanyi1944great}, \textit{The Great Transformation}}

During Stages~1B and~2, before the federated mesh achieves full material closure across semiconductors, specialty alloys, and clinical reagents, a settlement must generate modest sums of legacy fiat currency (\texttt{cents}) to fund its ratified Needs List ($\Phi_t$) and capitalize its permanent Municipal Property Tax Escrow Endowment. It does so by exporting high-craft guild outputs---such as artisan sourdough and fermented preserves (\texttt{Scenario Gamma}), handcrafted acoustic instruments (\texttt{Scenario Lambda}), custom open-spec Stage~II Right-to-Repair retrofit PCBs (\texttt{Scenario Pi}), or precision CNC components (\texttt{Scenario Alpha})---into the surrounding legacy economy.

Yet history warns that commercial export is the primary vector through which egalitarian cooperatives degenerate into capitalist firms \parencite{cornforth1995}. If export revenue is allowed to dictate internal schedules, or if stewards who bring in dollars are rewarded with extra voting power, the commons collapses from within. This chapter codifies the six constitutional governors that keep Layer~7 export strictly subordinate to human and ecological thriving: \textbf{Export as a Thermodynamic Residual ($R_t, L_t$)}, the \textbf{Exergy Shield ($V \le \kappa E$)}, the \textbf{Progressive Siphon ($S(V_s)$)}, the \textbf{Prohibited Export Register}, the \textbf{Export Ethics Charter}, and \textbf{Zero Reputational Premium for Fiat ($\Delta R = 0$)}.

\section{Export as a Strict Residual: The Ceiling Equations ($R_t, L_t$)}
\label{sec:l7-residual-equations}

In Layer~4 (\texttt{col-execd}), outward commercial export ranks strictly last (Tier~5) behind the four internal constitutional reservations:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Ecological Regeneration Floors ($F_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}}$):} Mandatory watershed, soil-carbon, and coppice-regeneration exergy budgets (\texttt{Scenario Eta}, \texttt{Scenario Mu}).
    \item \textbf{Unconditional Human Survival Baseline ($B_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}}$):} The inviolable $B_{\mathrm{baseline}} = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ floor guaranteeing nutrition, potable water, thermal shelter ($293{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$), sanitation, and clinical care to every resident and Ring~$R_6$ arrival (\texttt{Scenario Tau}, \texttt{Scenario Gamma}, \texttt{Scenario Zeta}, \texttt{Scenario Chi}).
    \item \textbf{Standing Commons \& Care Commitments ($C_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}}$):} Perinatal childcare pods (\texttt{Scenario Delta}), elder sanctuaries (\texttt{Scenario Phi}), and apprenticeship labs (\texttt{Scenario Kappa}).
    \item \textbf{Ordinary Internal Workflows \& 15\% Montage Reserve ($O_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}}$):} Day-to-day settlement production plus Pillar~I's $1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($15\%$) experimental \textit{montage} reserve (\texttt{D4}).
\end{enumerate}

Let $A_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}}$ be total available exergy metered and forecast by Layer~3 for cycle~$t$, and let $M_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}}$ be the ratified safety margin. The \textbf{Exergy Export Residual ($R_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}}$)} and \textbf{Labor Export Residual ($L_{t,\mathrm{ms}}$)} are:
\begin{align}
R_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}} &= A_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}} - F_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}} - B_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}} - C_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}} - O_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}} - M_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}} \label{eq:ch8-exergy-residual} \\
X_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}} &\le \max\bigl(0,\; R_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}}\bigr), \qquad L_{t,\mathrm{ms}} \le \max\bigl(0,\; H_{t,\mathrm{ms}} - H^{\mathrm{int}}_{t,\mathrm{ms}}\bigr) \label{eq:ch8-export-bounds}
\end{align}
where $H_{t,\mathrm{ms}}$ is voluntary self-reported human stewardship capacity (protected by \texttt{Scenario Sigma} rest shields) and $H^{\mathrm{int}}_{t,\mathrm{ms}}$ is the time required for internal commons care and maintenance. If drought, cloud cover, or human illness causes $R_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}} \le 0$ or $L_{t,\mathrm{ms}} \le 0$, Layer~4 curtails export automatically. Every external commercial contract drafted by Layer~7 contains an explicit \textbf{Interruptible Delivery Clause} excluding liquidated damages or continuous SLAs.

\section{The Exergy Shield ($V \le \kappa E$) and the Progressive Siphon ($S(V_s)$)}
\label{sec:l7-exergy-shield-siphon}

To prevent the cooperative shell from sliding into rentier speculation, financial arbitrage, or wage stratification, \texttt{col-adversaryd} enforces two mathematical governors in 64-bit fixed-point integer arithmetic:

\subsection{1. The Exergy Shield}
Total gross fiat receipts $V_{t,\mathrm{cents}}$ accepted by the cooperative in cycle~$t$ are upper-bounded by the verified physical exergy $E_{\mathrm{export},\mu\mathrm{J}}$ (including full embodied exergy and Layer~3 $\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$) delivered in exported craft batches:
\begin{equation}
V_{t,\mathrm{cents}} \le \left\lfloor \frac{\kappa_{\mathrm{cents\_per\_mj}} \cdot E_{\mathrm{export},\mu\mathrm{J}}}{10^{12}} \right\rfloor
\label{eq:ch8-exergy-shield}
\end{equation}
Here $\kappa_{\mathrm{cents\_per\_mj}}$ is an internal anti-rentier audit ceiling ratified by Layer~5---\textbf{never} a currency exchange rate (\S\ref{sec:l7-value-incommensurability}). It mathematically prohibits the legal entity from collecting passive financial interest, real-estate rent, patent royalties, or speculative markup detached from delivered physical craft.

\subsection{2. The Progressive Siphon}
Stewards who perform W-2 / cooperative export labor receive personal fiat disbursements up to a ratified \textbf{Living Obligation Threshold ($\tau_{\mathrm{live,cents}}$)} calibrated to cover their remaining external legacy liabilities (student loans, child support, medical prescriptions, or family remittances). If a steward earns independent legacy consulting income $V_{\mathrm{ext,cents}} > \tau_{\mathrm{live,cents}}$ and voluntarily routes it through the guild commons (\texttt{Scenario Pi}), the surplus $V_{s,\mathrm{cents}} = \max(0, V_{\mathrm{ext,cents}} - \tau_{\mathrm{live,cents}})$ follows the progressive contribution curve:
\begin{equation}
S(V_s) = V_s \cdot \bigl(1 - e^{-\lambda_{\mathrm{siphon}} V_s}\bigr)
\label{eq:ch8-progressive-siphon}
\end{equation}
evaluated in fixed-point integer basis points inside \texttt{col-adversaryd} and applied directly toward zeroing the cycle's Needs List target $\Phi_{t,\mathrm{cents}}$.

\section{The Prohibited Export Register and the Export Ethics Charter}
\label{sec:l7-prohibited-exports}

Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}) and Layer~6 (\texttt{col-commonsd}) enforce an inviolable \textbf{Prohibited Export Register}. Regardless of how much fiat currency an external buyer offers, the stack will reject any instruction attempting to export:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Human and Epistemic Privacy Data:} Zero member telemetry, zero \textbf{\textit{Pantry}} appetite profiles, zero biometric or health records, zero internal governance transcripts, and zero behavioral logs (\texttt{member\_data\_export\_allowed == false}).
    \item \textbf{Ecological Foundation Stock:} Living topsoil, humus, biochar needed for local soil fertility, groundwater beyond natural watershed replenishment, or biomass drawn below ecological floor $F_t$.
    \item \textbf{Irreplaceable Seeds and Single-Point Tooling:} Heritage seed genetics lacking surplus vault reserves (\texttt{Scenario Gamma}, \texttt{Scenario Eta}) or single-point workshop machine tools lacking local redundancy.
\end{enumerate}

Simultaneously, the \textbf{Export Ethics Charter} and \textbf{Customer Exclusion List} bar \texttt{col-adversaryd} from transacting with weapons manufacturers, mass-surveillance contractors, fossil-fuel extraction firms, or speculative real-estate developers, while prioritizing fair-trade reciprocity with neighboring family farms, municipal schools (such as Brazil's \textbf{PNAE / PAA}, \texttt{Lei 11.947/2009}, which mandates that at least $30\%$ of municipal school meal funds procure directly from local family and cooperative agroecology \parencite{brazil_pnae2009}), and regional worker cooperatives.

\section{Protecting the People in the Membrane: Zero Reputational Premium for Fiat}
\label{sec:l7-people-in-membrane}

Interfacing with legacy bureaucracy and commercial buyers carries high cognitive friction. Layer~7 protects the stewards standing in the membrane through four constitutional guarantees:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Zero Reputational Wealth Premium for Fiat ($\Delta R_{\mathrm{fiat\_bonus}} \equiv 0$):} In Layer~5's voting formula $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$, a steward who generates $\$50{,}000$ in external export sales earns \textbf{zero} extra stewardship weight per dollar over a steward who repairs a neighborhood cargo trike or tends the compost retort. $\Delta R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}$ is minted strictly per verified Layer~4 task completion and thermodynamic stewardship, preventing the emergence of a commercial "rainmaker" oligarchy.
    \item \textbf{Mandatory Rotational Carapace:} Corporate officer seats, bank signatory duties, tax liaison roles, and customer account stewardship rotate on fixed annual terms. All client relationships belong to the cooperative carapace, never to a permanent managerial caste.
    \item \textbf{Unconditional Protocol of Refusal and Care Preemption:} Any steward may invoke conscientious refusal or activate a \texttt{Scenario Sigma} rest shield / \texttt{Scenario Delta} care leave at any moment with zero penalty. When human wellbeing collides with an external delivery deadline, the commercial contract's interruptible curtailment clause fires automatically; \textbf{the contract degrades, never the human}.
    \item \textbf{Decompression Circles:} Stewards rotating out of external Layer~7 liaison terms participate in peer decompression rounds (\texttt{Scenario Psi}) to shed market urgency before returning to the unhurried rhythm of the internal commons.
\end{enumerate}
