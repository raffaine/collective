\section{Dual-Mode Layer 7 Reach and Cooperative Legal Carapace}
Scenario Nu (\texttt{docs/scenarios/nu\_fashion/}, Wave~D, Stage~1A $\to$ 3) replaces fast-fashion synthetic microplastic waste with a bioregional fiber shed (bast hemp/flax, wool, organic cotton), \textit{Sashiko} visible mending, and Goodyear-welted resoleable footwear.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Layer~7 Reach Mode:} \textit{Dual-Mode (Transitional Natural Yarn/Needle Procurement \& Residual High-Craft Garment/Boot Export)}. Prohibits all PFAS/fluoropolymer coatings and petrochemical polyester; exports residual tailored workwear and resoleable leather/cork boots under the Exergy Shield ($V \le \kappa E$) until $\Phi_t = 0$.
    \item \textbf{US / Common-Law Carapace:} IRC Subchapter~T Textile and Footwear Artisans' Cooperative paired with Capper-Volstead regional wool/flax processing pools and FTC Wool/Textile Fiber Products Identification Act compliance.
    \item \textbf{Brazilian / Lusophone Carapace (\texttt{bra/README.md}):} \textit{Cooperativa T{\^e}xtil e Cal{\c{c}}adista de Economia Solid{\'a}ria} (\texttt{Lei 12.690/2012} and \texttt{Ato Cooperativo Lei 5.764/1971}) releasing all parametric garment patterns under \textbf{CC-BY-SA-4.0 / CERN-OHL-S-2.0}.
\end{itemize}
