\section{Engineering Anatomy of a Stage II Right-to-Repair Hybrid Retrofit}
\label{sec:l7-stage2-anatomy}

In almost every legacy commercial or domestic appliance, the physical structure that embodies $95\%$ of the thermodynamic manufacturing exergy---the cast-iron hearth deck, the stainless-steel wash drum, the induction motor windings, the precision-ground cast-iron lathe bed, or the insulated refrigeration cabinet---remains mechanically sound for 30 to 80 years. What fails or locks the steward out in Year~4 is the $250\,\mathrm{g}$ proprietary control board and cheap plastic linkage.

A \textbf{Right-to-Repair Hybrid Retrofit Kit} is a content-addressed Layer~6 engineering artifact (\texttt{collective:HardwareRetrofitBlueprint}, licensed under \texttt{CERN-OHL-S-2.0} and \texttt{AGPL-3.0}) published into the \textbf{Ontological Graph} and \textbf{Orchestration Registry} by federated hardware guilds (\texttt{Scenario Alpha} and \texttt{Scenario Pi}). Every retrofit kit comprises three standardized layers:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Open Layer~2 Replacement Controller PCB:} An open-hardware microcontroller carrier board based on the Sovereign Stack's 3-tier Zephyr RTOS MCU hierarchy (\texttt{STM32H7} for deterministic real-time motion/inverter control, \texttt{ESP32-S3} for local room telemetry, or \texttt{RP2040/RP2350} for PIO state-machine interfacing), protected by $2.5\,\mathrm{kV}_{\mathrm{RMS}}$ galvanic opto-isolation and an onboard \texttt{ATECC608A} cryptographic root-of-trust chip.
    \item \textbf{Direct Internal Transducer Harness:} The retrofit crew strips out the legacy potted PCB and parasitic outer clamps, wiring internal K-type thermocouples, Hall-effect motor phase current sensors, optical spindle encoders, and piezoelectric bearing accelerometers directly into the open Layer~2 ADC channels.
    \item \textbf{3D-Printable / CNC-Machinable Metric Mechanical Adapters:} Parametric STEP/OpenSCAD files for mounting brackets, shaft couplers, and seal glands that convert proprietary plastic clips and non-standard bushings to standard ISO metric \texttt{M6}/\texttt{M8} fasteners and off-the-shelf industrial bearings (e.g., standard \texttt{608-2RS} or \texttt{6204-ZZ} bearings).
\end{enumerate}

When the retrofit BPMN~2.0 workflow completes and passes its Layer~2/4 safety interlock test, \texttt{col-telemetryd} upgrades the tool twin's provenance register from \texttt{QUALITY\_SCAVENGED\_L7\_SHIM = 0x04} to \texttt{QUALITY\_RETROFIT\_HYBRID = 0x08}. As quantified in Table~\ref{tab:ch2-fleet-penalty-comparison}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Parasitic standby overhead drops by $>92\%$ (from $45{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$ to $3{,}500\,\mathrm{mW}$);
    \item Telemetry uncertainty narrows from $\pm 1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ to $\pm 350\,\mathrm{bp}$;

    \item Annualized Layer~7 OEM spare-part fiat tribute drops by $>90\%$ (from $48{,}000\,\text{cents/yr}$ to $4{,}500\,\text{cents/yr}$ for generic commodity relays or bearings).
\end{itemize}
