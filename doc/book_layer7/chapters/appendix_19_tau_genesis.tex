\section{Dual-Mode Layer 7 Reach and Cooperative Legal Carapace}
Scenario Tau (\texttt{docs/scenarios/tau\_genesis/}, Wave~A, Stage~1A $\to$ 3) governs how an initial household or small founding crew ($N_s = 1 \to 4$) boots a sovereign node on an ordinary suburban lot starting from the finite $\$20{,}000$ (\texttt{2,000,000 cents}) fiat reserve $\Phi_0$, and how newcomers, neighbors, and Ring~$R_6$ migrants onboard across four inclusive pathways without financial buy-ins.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Layer~7 Reach Mode:} \textit{Dual-Mode (Transitional $\Phi_0$ Stage~I Appliance Bootstrap \& Purely Defensive UUNAA \S~6 / ADU Carapace)}. Allocates $\Phi_0$ across secondhand Stage~I appliances and keyline garden stock to establish immediate caloric, thermal, and sanitation sufficiency ($B_{\mathrm{baseline}} = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$), while strictly prohibiting any fiat membership fee or property buy-in for new stewards.
    \item \textbf{US / Common-Law Carapace:} Unincorporated Nonprofit Association (UNA) under UUNAA~\S~6 (Phase~1) utilizing ministerial State ADU preemption (SB~9/10, HB~1110/1337) and residential backyard gardening/home-workshop exemptions.
    \item \textbf{Brazilian / Lusophone Carapace (\texttt{bra/README.md}):} \textit{N{\'u}cleo Dom{\'e}stico e Associa{\c{c}}{\~a}o Civil de Ajuda M{\'u}tua} (\texttt{Art.~5{\textordmasculine}, XVII--XXI CF/88}; \texttt{Art.~53 CC}) combined with urban agriculture incentives and strict prohibition of pecuniary admission barriers.
\end{itemize}
