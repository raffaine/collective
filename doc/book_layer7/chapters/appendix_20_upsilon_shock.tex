Civ. Code~\S~1714.5) shielding the Open-Hearth emergency shelter and potable water distribution point.
    \item \textbf{Brazilian / Lusophone Carapace (\texttt{bra/README.md}):} \textit{Microgerac{\~a}o Distribu{\'\i}da com Ilhamento Seguro} (ANEEL PRODIST M{\'o}dulo~3 \& \texttt{Lei 14.300/2022}) combined with Civil Defense Mutual-Aid Refuge immunity (\texttt{Sistema Nacional de Prote{\c{c}}{\~a}o e Defesa Civil --- Lei 12.608/2012} and \texttt{Art.~5{\textordmasculine} XVIII CF/88}).
\end{itemize}
