\chapter{Wave C and Wave D Assimilation Walkthroughs: Non-Commensurable Care, Decommodified CLT Shelter, and Closing the Bioregional Material Loop}
\label{ch:l7-waves-c-and-d}

\epigraph{Land, labor, and care are not commodities produced for sale; the fiction that they are is the root of our ecological and social dislocation.}{---\textcite{polanyi1944great}, \textit{The Great Transformation}}

Once a neighborhood pod stabilizes its daily caloric, fabrication, and logistics baseline in Waves~A and~B, it expands along two complementary axes: \textbf{Wave~C (Care, Kinship, Apprenticeship \& Decommodified Shelter: Scenarios Delta, Omicron, Kappa, and Zeta)} and \textbf{Wave~D (Regenerative Land, Extraction, Materials \& Architecture: Scenarios Eta, Mu, Theta, Nu, and Xi)}. This chapter details how Layer~7 insulates human care and housing from market commodification while closing the heavy bioregional material loop that starves external Layer~7 supply chains of recurring tribute.

\section{Wave C: Care, Kinship, Apprenticeship, and Decommodified Shelter (Stage 2)}
\label{sec:l7-wave-c}

\subsection{1. Scenario Delta (\texttt{SCN-DELTA-CHILDCARE}): Camera-Free Perinatal Sanctuaries and Priority 950 Enveloping Shields}
Under Layer~7 capitalism, early childhood care is squeezed between extortionate private tuition and panoptic cloud-camera daycares that monetize family anxiety. In \textbf{Scenario Delta} ($N_s = 4 \to 18$ care stewards and parents), childcare and 90-day postpartum cocoons are unconditionally non-commensurable (\texttt{domestic\_care\_token\_minting == 0}):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Purely Defensive Layer~7 Cooperative Co-op Carapace:} Organized as a parent-steward cooperative under state cooperative childcare exemptions (or a Brazilian \textit{Associa{\c{c}}{\~a}o de Pais e Mestres / Creche Comunit{\'a}ria} under \texttt{LDB Lei 9.394/96} and \texttt{ECA Lei 8.069/90}), Scenario Delta holds zero commercial customer accounts and exports zero data.
    \item \textbf{\textit{The Pantry} Formula/Milk Arrhenius Lens \& Priority 950 Acoustic Shield:} Inside the building's \textbf{Enveloping Node Hypervisor} (\texttt{CCI-4}), the Nursery Wing holds a priority-\texttt{950} Fast-Path boundary lease enforcing a strict $4{,}000\,\mathrm{cB}$ ($40.0\,\mathrm{dBA}$) nap-hour quiet shield and $294{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($21.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$) thermal floor, automatically throttling adjacent workshop spindle leases during nap windows. Stage~I bottle sterilizers and breast-milk freezers are retrofitted with Stage~II open thermistor loggers integrated directly with \textbf{\textit{The Pantry}} lens.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{2. Scenario Omicron (\texttt{SCN-OMICRON-KINSHIP}): Chosen Kinship Covenants and $<500\,\mathrm{ms}$ Unilateral Safe-Harbor Exit}
\textbf{Scenario Omicron} ($N_s = 2 \to 15$ multi-generational household members) decouples material survival and hospital visitation rights from patriarchal nuclear-family property traps:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Layer~7 Durable Healthcare POA \& Cohabitation Carapace:} When stewards form or amend a multi-suite kinship pod in Layer~6, \texttt{col-adversaryd} compiles statutory Durable Healthcare Powers of Attorney, HIPAA medical release authorizations, and CLT cohabitation riders (or Brazilian \textit{Escritura Declarat{\'o}ria de Uni{\~a}o Est{\'a}vel / Condom{\'\i}nio Multipropriedade} and \textit{Diretivas Antecipadas de Vontade} under CFM Res.~1.995/2012).
    \item \textbf{Unilateral $<500\,\mathrm{ms}$ Safe-Harbor Exit:} Because every resident holds an individual non-decaying $B = 1$ ($10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$) baseline and unconditional $B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$ housing/food entitlement independent of their partner's fiat income, any member can trigger a $<500\,\mathrm{ms}$ unilateral exit in their Layer~6 lens, immediately rotating door keys, severing shared \textbf{\textit{Pantry}} and Enveloping Node leases, and allocating a private refuge suite in \texttt{Scenario Zeta} with zero financial destitution.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{3. Scenario Kappa (\texttt{SCN-KAPPA-APPRENTICESHIP}): Solving the $R_i = 0$ Cold-Start Paradox via Pillar II Dual-Key Retrofits}
\textbf{Scenario Kappa} ($N_s = 2 \to 20$ mentors and apprentices) replaces tuition debt with debt-free polymathic mastery:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Canonical Showcase of Pillar~II (\texttt{collective:WorkCapabilityMatrix}, \texttt{D6}, \texttt{CCI-4}):} An apprentice enters with $R_{\mathrm{apprentice}}(0) = 0$ and full civic floor $B = 1$ ($V_{\mathrm{apprentice}}(0) = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$). Under Pillar~II, they begin on Tier-0 unsupervised prep tasks and Tier-1 mentor-co-signed \textbf{Stage~II Right-to-Repair Hybrid Retrofit tasks} (e.g., wiring an open STM32H7 controller into a scavenged kiln under a $\le 2{,}000\,\mathrm{mm}$ BLE dead-man tether), earning verified $\Delta R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}$ upon Layer~2/3 validation while their Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) learning curve remains $100\%$ private in Ring~$R_0$.
    \item \textbf{Layer~7 Vocational Licensing Bridge:} When municipal law requires a state-licensed electrician, plumber, or PE (\texttt{Qualifying Individual}) to stamp permits, Layer~7 funds the state exam fees and registers the cooperative as an accredited apprenticeship sponsor (or Brazilian SENAI / PRONATEC / Cooperativa Escola equivalency under \texttt{Lei 12.690/12}) without ever exposing internal $R_0$ learning logs.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{4. Scenario Zeta (\texttt{SCN-ZETA-LODGING}): The Primary Building Enveloping Hypervisor and Dual-Chassis CLT}
\textbf{Scenario Zeta} ($N_s = 20 \to 120$ residents) is the canonical home of the \textbf{Primary Building and Block Enveloping Node Hypervisor (\texttt{CCI-4})} and the \textbf{Dual-Chassis Community Land Trust (CLT)}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Eliminating Speculative Rent \& Partition Suits:} Bare land title is locked permanently inside the CLT (or Brazilian \textbf{Termo de Territorialidade Coletiva / TTC, \texttt{Lei 13.465/2017}} \parencite{brazil_ttc2017}) paired with a 99-year Housing Cooperative Ground Lease and an Irrevocable Municipal Property Tax Escrow Endowment.
    \item \textbf{Two-Speed Jurisprudence in Action:} Internally, every resident holds $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$ in the Building Enveloping Node. Fast-Path L2 hazard preemption (e.g., a burst pipe or greywater pump alarm) overrides quiet leases in milliseconds, while Slow-Path courtyard or quiet-hour adjustments compile from Layer~6 Semantic Diff ballots into permanent Layer~4 guard predicates.
\end{itemize}

\section{Wave D: Regenerative Land, Extraction, Materials, and Architecture (Stage 2 $\to$ 3)}
\label{sec:l7-wave-d}

\subsection{1. Scenario Eta (\texttt{SCN-ETA-COPPICE}): Rotational Agroforestry, Conservation Easements, and Solar Kilns}
\textbf{Scenario Eta} ($N_s = 4 \to 16$ foresters) wraps watershed woodlands inside a \textbf{Bioregional Forestry Enveloping Sub-Node} ($\le 2{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ annual canopy harvest cap enforced by the Mother-Tree Veto) and a \textbf{UCEA Conservation Easement / Brazilian RPPN (\texttt{Lei 9.985/2000})} that slashes rural property taxes by $80\text{--}100\%$. Scavenged portable sawmills and solar timber kilns are retrofitted with Stage~II open moisture/pyrometry controllers, feeding seasoned structural timber (\texttt{mm} scaled via Tier~1 cascade) directly to \texttt{Scenario Xi}.

\subsection{2. Scenario Mu (\texttt{SCN-MU-EXTRACTION}): Five-Stream Urban Unbuilding and $\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$ Feedstock Recovery}
\textbf{Scenario Mu} ($N_s = 6 \to 25$ deconstruction and nutrient stewards) is the primary engine driving down regional \textbf{Layer~3 Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$)}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Operating under municipal deconstruction permits and EPA/CONAMA composting exemptions within an Extraction Enveloping Lease ($9{,}000\,\mathrm{cB}$ daytime window, strict particulate $\mu\mathrm{g}$ and runoff $\mathrm{mL/s}$ interlocks), Scenario Mu unbuilds obsolete Layer~7 structures and discarded Stage~I appliances into five pure streams: structural timber, XRF-sorted alloy scrap (\texttt{Scenario Theta}), 72-hour thermophilic compost ($\ge 333{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$), TLUD pyrolysis biochar, and precipitated struvite fertilizer.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{3. Scenario Theta (\texttt{SCN-THETA-FOUNDRY}): Secondary Scrap Metallurgy and Casting the Stage III Native Fleet}
\textbf{Scenario Theta} ($N_s = 8 \to 35$ metallurgists) operates solar-scheduled induction furnaces and CNC finishing cells inside a Heavy Foundry Enveloping Lease ($9{,}200\,\mathrm{cB}$, megawatt \texttt{mW} solar-peak power leases, $\le 250\,\mathrm{ms}$ two-person dead-man pour interlocks). When a sintering task exceeds local kiln limits, Layer~4 triggers a \textbf{Tier~3 True-Constraint Hard-Block} (\texttt{temp\_deficit\_mk > 0}) and routes the melt across \texttt{col-swarmd} to the regional foundry node under a Subchapter~T SSA. Crucially, Scenario Theta casts and machines the open-spec housings, Gastronorm pans, and \texttt{M6}/\texttt{M8} hardware that birth the \textbf{Stage~III Native Open-Spec Collective Fleet}.

\subsection{4. Scenario Nu (\texttt{SCN-NU-FASHION}) and Scenario Xi (\texttt{SCN-XI-ARCHITECTURE}): Zero-PFAS Textiles and Two-Track \textit{Kigumi} Open Building}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Scenario Nu ($N_s = 1 \to 12$ weavers and cobblers):} Retrofits DRM-locked commercial embroidery and knitting machines with Stage~II open stepper controllers (\texttt{0x08}), uses Tier~1 SI bolt-width scaling (\texttt{mm}) and Tier~2 stitch/fastener macro-substitution, reserves $15\%$ capacity for garment \textit{montage}, and purges personal body-measurement scans from RAM immediately after pattern cutting (\texttt{OP\_PURGE\_R0\_LINEAGE}).
    \item \textbf{Scenario Xi ($N_s = 10 \to 45$ builders):} Uses \textbf{Open-Source Franchising (\texttt{D7}, \texttt{CCI-5})} to compile Two-Track Parametric \textit{Kigumi} (nail-free reversible timber joinery \parencite{habraken1972supports,brand1994buildings}) wall and floor templates pre-engineered for Enveloping Node acoustic ($5{,}500\,\mathrm{cB}$ isolation) and thermal ($R\text{-}40$) boundaries, ministerially permitted under \textbf{IRC Appendices AQ, AR, and AS} (or Brazilian ABNT NBR~7190 / NBR~15575 stamped via cooperative CREA/CAU responsibility).
\end{itemize}
