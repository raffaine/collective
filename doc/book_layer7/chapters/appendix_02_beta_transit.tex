\section{Dual-Mode Layer 7 Reach and Cooperative Legal Carapace}
Scenario Beta (\texttt{docs/scenarios/beta\_transit/}, Wave~B, Stage~1A $\to$ 3) coordinates the five physical arteries of settlement logistics: consolidated cargo-trike and light-EV milk-runs ($\eta_{\mathrm{route}} \ge 6{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$), $48\,\mathrm{V}/380\,\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{DC}}$ microgrid interties, potable/greywater flumes, Reticulum fiber/LoRa conduits, and the constitutional \textbf{\texttt{No-Transit} Principle} (eliminating daily commuting through localized polycentric workspaces).
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Layer~7 Reach Mode:} \textit{Dual-Mode (Tripartite Public-Corridor Shield \& IEEE~1547 Utility Tariff Arbitrage)}. Shields inter-node cargo traversing municipal streets from UCC Article~2 sales tax and DOT commercial common-carrier licensing while managing lawful IEEE~1547-2018 grid-tie net-metering until $\Phi_{\mathrm{utility},t} = 0$.
    \item \textbf{US / Common-Law Carapace:} Tripartite Legal Chassis (\S\ref{sec:l7-tripartite-chassis}): (1) IRC Subchapter~T Shared Services Agreements (SSAs) for at-cost inter-cooperative cargo manifests referencing opaque Ricardian \texttt{Treaty\_CID} hashes; (2) UCEA Conservation and Utility Easements for cross-parcel DC cables, water pipes, and cargo trails; and (3) IEEE~1547 / PURPA interconnection agreements.
    \item \textbf{Brazilian / Lusophone Carapace (\texttt{bra/README.md}):} \textit{Transporte Cooperativo Sem Fins Lucrativos sob Ato Cooperativo} (\texttt{Lei 5.764/1971, Art.~79}, exempt from ICMS commercial merchandise circulation tax on internal cooperative transfers), \textit{Servid{\~a}o Ambiental e de Passagem} (\texttt{Art.~1.378 CC} \& \texttt{Lei 12.651/2012}), and ANEEL \texttt{Lei 14.300/2022} distributed generation credits.
\end{itemize}
