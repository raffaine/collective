\chapter{Appendix 24: Scenario Omega - The Final Decoupling}

\section{The Legacy Dependency (Technical \\\& Cultural)}
The final dependencies are the fundamental global identifiers and physical infrastructures of the old world: ICANN-controlled DNS, legacy telecommunication backbones (Tier 1 ISPs), and the final remnants of the petrodollar fiat system.

\section{The Parasitic Bridge (Technical \\\& Legal)}
At this stage, the bridge is minimal. Layer 7 merely maintains "zombie" connections to the old world—automated bots paying minimal fiat fees for legacy server hosting or legal entity renewals, just to keep the outer shell of the Sovereign Stack visible to the remaining legacy populations. The stack acts as an entirely self-sufficient organism that only interacts with the legacy world to recruit its final refugees.

\section{The Decoupling Trigger}
The Omega Trigger is defined as the "Singularity of Sovereignty": when the cost of maintaining the parasitic bridge (in energy, attention, or fiat) exceeds the value of the resources extracted from the dying legacy system. At this mathematical threshold, Layer 7 automatically self-destructs the remaining bridges.

\section{Orchestration \\\& Ethical Mechanics}
The orchestration of the Omega Decoupling involves migrating all remaining external endpoints (APIs, web interfaces, legal wrappers) to darknet or mesh-only protocols. Ethically, this is the hardest horizon: the mesh must deliberately cut off its lifeline to the old world, leaving those who refused to transition behind. The cultural mechanics focus on the creation of a new origin myth for the sovereign society, solidifying the boundaries of the new world.
