\chapter{Appendix Zeta: The Lodging Scenario}

\section{The Legacy Dependency (Technical \\\& Cultural)}
Zeta Nodes depend on legacy hospitality infrastructure. This includes short-term rental regulations, municipal occupancy taxes, legacy booking platforms (Web2 hospitality APIs), and traditional cleaning/maintenance labor markets. Culturally, it depends on consumer expectations of standardized, frictionless lodging services and traditional guest-host dynamics.

\section{The Parasitic Bridge (Technical \\\& Legal)}
The node uses API Parasitism to list availability on legacy Web2 platforms, extracting fiat revenue from non-sovereign tourists. A cooperative trust acts as the legal entity handling municipal occupancy taxes and fiat compliance. Internally, a Layer 5 resource-allocation protocol schedules lodging, distributing sovereign reputation tokens and access rights to internal mesh members, subsidizing their stay through the extracted tourist fiat.

\section{The Decoupling Trigger}
The reliance on legacy Web2 booking APIs is severed when the internal Sovereign Network's demand for lodging (from nomadic mesh members) reaches 85\% of total capacity. At this threshold, the API bridge is closed, and the node operates exclusively on Layer 4/5 internal scheduling protocols.

\section{Orchestration \\\& Ethical Mechanics}
Cryptographic oracles monitor municipal tax requirements and automate compliance via the Fiat-Crypto Bridge. Ethically, the node avoids exacerbating local housing crises by strictly repurposing underutilized commercial hospitality zones rather than cannibalizing long-term residential stock. Labor for maintenance is transitioned from gig-economy exploitation to vested DAO participation.
