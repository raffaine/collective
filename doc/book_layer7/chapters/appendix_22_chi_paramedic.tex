\chapter{Appendix 22: Scenario Chi - Autonomous Paramedic \\\& Dispatch}

\section{The Legacy Dependency (Technical \\\& Cultural)}
Emergency medical response is tightly monopolized by state 911 dispatch systems, legacy hospital emergency rooms, and municipal ambulance monopolies. Culturally, citizens are conditioned to dial a central state number for life-threatening emergencies.

\section{The Parasitic Bridge (Technical \\\& Legal)}
Layer 7 integrates a read-only listener on legacy municipal dispatch radio frequencies and APIs where available, allowing sovereign paramedics (Layer 6) to respond to emergencies in mesh-covered zones often faster than legacy services. Legally, sovereign paramedics operate under "Good Samaritan" laws and decentralized mutual aid legal wrappers, shielding them from the liability structures that constrain municipal providers.

\section{The Decoupling Trigger}
The state 911 dependency is severed when the local Sovereign Stack's internal emergency dispatch routing (Layer 5) demonstrates a verifiable average response time that is 50\% faster than the legacy municipal service, and local mesh-affiliated clinics achieve Level 3 trauma stabilization capabilities.

\section{Orchestration \\\& Ethical Mechanics}
The orchestration uses cryptographic zero-knowledge proofs to verify the medical qualifications of sovereign paramedics without exposing them to state licensing boards that may revoke credentials for participating in the mesh. Ethically, the mesh must decide when to hand a patient over to a legacy hospital (parasiting their advanced surgical capacity) versus treating them entirely within the mesh.
