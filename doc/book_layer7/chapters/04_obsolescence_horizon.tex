\chapter{The Obsolescence Horizon}

\section{The Cybernetic Sunset}
A parasitic bridge is only successful if it eventually kills its own necessity. Layer 7 is explicitly designed to be temporary. The architecture requires a mathematically defined \textit{Obsolescence Horizon} for every legacy dependency, preventing the Sovereign Stack from permanently tethering itself to the dying world.

\section{Triggering Decoupling}
The Layer 4 Orchestrator continuously monitors the internal density of the mesh against its legacy reliance. Decoupling triggers are hardcoded into the Layer 5 Policy matrix:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Energy Decoupling:} When the community's internal solar and geothermal generation exceeds 120\% of peak thermodynamic load for three consecutive months, the system recommends physically severing the state grid connection (Scenario Omega).
    \item \textbf{Data Decoupling:} When local sensor density reaches sufficient coverage to predict micro-climates natively, the Edge Oracles automatically terminate their API calls to municipal weather servers, reclaiming the network bandwidth.
    \item \textbf{Financial Decoupling:} As the internal circular economy establishes itself and Trust Rings begin producing their own food and fabrication materials, the Fiat-Crypto bridge is intentionally throttled, gradually reducing the community's exposure to fiat inflation.
\end{itemize}

\section{The Autonomy Threshold}
When all major legacy bridges cross their Obsolescence Horizons, the community crosses the Autonomy Threshold. Layer 7 effectively goes dormant, having successfully extracted the necessary capital and data to bootstrap a fully sovereign, self-sustaining mesh.
