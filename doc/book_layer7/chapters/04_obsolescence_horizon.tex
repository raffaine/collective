\chapter{The Obsolescence Horizon}
\label{ch:obsolescence_horizon}

The Obsolescence Horizon represents the definitive thermodynamic and computational tipping point at which legacy institutional structures become strictly inferior to Layer-7 protocols in terms of informational exergy. This horizon is not merely a conceptual metaphor; it is a mathematically rigorous threshold integrated directly into the protocol's core logic, dictating the automated severing of legacy dependencies.

\section{Informational Exergy and Threshold Triggers}

We model the efficiency of systemic state transitions using the framework of informational exergy ($E_x$). For any given computational, legal, or bureaucratic process, the exergy difference between the legacy state ($S_{legacy}$) and the decentralized Layer-7 state ($S_{layer7}$) is defined as:

\begin{equation}
    \Delta E_x = T_0 \sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( \mathcal{H}(S_{i}^{legacy}) - \mathcal{H}(S_{i}^{layer7}) \right)
\end{equation}

where $T_0$ is the ambient friction (or entropy cost) of the surrounding economic environment, and $\mathcal{H}$ denotes the Shannon entropy of the process execution graph. When legacy systems bloat with regulatory compliance, middle-management overhead, and rent-seeking intermediaries, $\mathcal{H}(S_{i}^{legacy})$ approaches criticality.

\section{The Severance Protocol}

The Layer-7 protocol enforces an automated severance policy when the cumulative frictional decay of the legacy system surpasses the energetic cost of migrating and operating purely on-chain. We define the Horizon Trigger condition, $\Phi_{trigger}$, as:

\begin{equation}
    \Phi_{trigger} = \lim_{\Delta t \to \infty} \int_{t_0}^{t_0 + \Delta t} \left[ \mathcal{C}_{legacy}(\tau) - \mathcal{C}_{layer7}(\tau) \right] d\tau > \mathcal{K}_{severance}
\end{equation}

where $\mathcal{C}(\tau)$ represents the transaction cost function over time, and $\mathcal{K}_{severance}$ is the activation energy required to port the dependency entirely to the decentralized network. 

Once the inequality evaluates to true ($\Phi_{trigger} > 0$), smart contracts autonomously execute a deterministic, irreversible detachment from the legacy API. This mechanism strands the obsolete institutional apparatus on the far side of the horizon, ensuring that Layer-7 remains unencumbered by the collapsing energetic architecture of the previous era.
