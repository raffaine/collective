\section{Dual-Mode Layer 7 Reach and Cooperative Legal Carapace}
Scenario Eta (\texttt{docs/scenarios/eta\_coppice/}, Wave~D, Stage~1A $\to$ 3) stewards multi-decade rotational chestnut/hazel/locust coppice forestry, keyline watershed retention swales, and a dual-assay botanical apothecary.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Layer~7 Reach Mode:} \textit{Dual-Mode (Defensive Conservation Easement Tax Shield \& Transitional Surplus Timber/Botanical Export)}. Locks forest parcels against commercial clear-cutting while drastically reducing rural property taxes (\texttt{80--100\%} exemption); exports residual seasoned timber or assayed botanicals only when canopy regeneration exceeds the ecological floor $F_t$.
    \item \textbf{US / Common-Law Carapace:} \textbf{Uniform Conservation Easement Act (UCEA)} perpetual forestry servitudes \parencite{ucea1981} paired with State Current-Use Timber/Open-Space Tax Classifications and Capper-Volstead (7~U.S.C.~\S\S~291--292) agroforestry cooperative marketing insulation.
    \item \textbf{Brazilian / Lusophone Carapace (\texttt{bra/README.md}):} \textbf{Reserva Particular do Patrim{\^o}nio Natural (RPPN, \texttt{Lei 9.985/2000, Art.~21})} granting $100\%$ ITR rural land tax exemption, \textbf{Servid{\~a}o Ambiental (\texttt{Lei 12.651/2012, Art.~9{\textordmasculine}-A})}, and municipal \textbf{SUS / Farm{\'a}cias Vivas (\texttt{Portaria MS 886/2010})} phytotherapeutic covenants.
\end{itemize}
