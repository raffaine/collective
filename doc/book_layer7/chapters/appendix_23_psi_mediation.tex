\chapter{Appendix 23: Scenario Psi - Distributed Mediation \\\& Justice}

\section{The Legacy Dependency (Technical \\\& Cultural)}
Societal conflict resolution relies entirely on legacy state courts, law enforcement, and a fiat-based penal system. Citizens depend on state monopolies of violence and legal arbitration to enforce contracts and resolve disputes.

\section{The Parasitic Bridge (Technical \\\& Legal)}
Layer 7 utilizes traditional legal arbitration clauses embedded in fiat legal contracts (LLCs, NDAs) as a bridge. The mesh dictates that all internal disputes must be settled via the Sovereign Stack's Layer 4 decentralized arbitration protocols. The state court is only used as a parasitic enforcement mechanism if a party attempts to flee the mesh's jurisdiction, leveraging the state's own arbitration enforcement laws against it.

\section{The Decoupling Trigger}
Complete decoupling from the legacy justice system occurs when the local mesh economy is fully closed-loop—meaning the threat of cryptographic exile (loss of access to mesh energy, food, and communication) is a stronger deterrent and enforcement mechanism than the threat of state violence or fiat fines.

\section{Orchestration \\\& Ethical Mechanics}
The orchestration relies on multi-signature escrows and reputation tokens to disincentivize bad behavior natively. Ethically, the mesh must navigate the transition carefully to avoid "mob rule." The bridging mechanics ensure that while state courts are deprecated, the fundamental rights of individuals are protected by algorithmic transparency and appeal processes that are culturally translated to feel legitimate to participants.
