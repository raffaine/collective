In compliance with \texttt{docs/BOOK\_TEAM\_DRIFT\_REPORT.md} and the Horizon Council's v3.2 directive (\S 1.4 and \S 5.1/5.8), all legacy references to ``Physical Grid Leeching'' and ``Macroscopic Infrastructure Leeching'' (such as clamping unauthorized inductive taps onto $12\,\mathrm{kV}$ utility drops or tapping municipal water mains with illegal saddle valves) are permanently excised from the Sovereign Stack architecture. Unlawful utility tampering is physically hazardous, violates electrical and plumbing safety codes, and hands utility monopolies an immediate criminal and civil injunction (\texttt{UTILITY\_DISCONNECT\_ORDER} and \texttt{REGULATORY\_CIVIL\_INJUNCTION} in \texttt{col-adversaryd}) to raid and disconnect the settlement.

Instead, a Sovereign Stack node interfaces with municipal electrical, water, and telecommunications grids through \textbf{Lawful Municipal Utility Interconnection}, \textbf{Thermodynamic Tariff Arbitrage}, \textbf{Sub-Cycle IEEE~1547-2018 Islanding}, and \textbf{Enveloping Node Feeder Leases (\texttt{CCI-4})}.

\section{IEEE 1547-2018 Grid-Tie Architecture and Phase-Locked Loop Synchronization}
\label{sec:l7-ieee1547-pll}

During Stages~1A, 1B, and~2, most suburban and urban nodes retain a physical point of common coupling (PCC) with the legacy municipal AC utility grid ($120/240\,\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{AC}}$ split-phase in North America; $127/220\,\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{AC}}$ or $220/380\,\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{AC}}$ three-phase under ANEEL PRODIST Module~3 in Brazil) while building out their internal $48\,\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{DC}}$ (house/lot) and $380\,\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{DC}}$ (neighborhood block) solar-plus-storage microgrids (\texttt{Scenario Beta}, \texttt{Scenario Upsilon}).

At the PCC, the bi-directional hybrid inverter controlled by Layer~2 (\texttt{col-telemetryd}) and bounded by Layer~4 (\texttt{col-execd}) maintains strict compliance with \textbf{IEEE Std 1547-2018} (and California Rule~21 / UL~1741~SA):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Synchronous Phase-Locked Loop (PLL) Tracking:} When the grid tie contactor is closed, the inverter's STM32H7 DSP tracks the municipal grid voltage waveform $v_g(t) = V_m \sin(\omega_g t + \theta_g)$, matching frequency ($\omega_g \approx 2\pi \times 60\,\mathrm{Hz}$), phase angle ($\theta_g$), and voltage amplitude within $\pm 50\,\mathrm{bp}$ before closing the solid-state interconnect relay.
    \item \textbf{Galvanic and Cybernetic Airgap at the PCC:} The utility's revenue smart meter sits strictly outside the node's internal bus on the legacy side of the PCC. Internal branch-circuit current transformers (CTs), battery State-of-Charge (SoC) registers, and room-level Enveloping Node sensors never share a data bus with the utility meter.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Time-of-Use Tariff Arbitrage and Bill-Zeroing ($\Phi_{\mathrm{utility,t}} \to 0$)}
\label{sec:l7-tariff-arbitrage}

Legacy investor-owned utilities (modeled in \texttt{col-adversaryd} as \texttt{"AmeriGrid"} and \texttt{"MetroPower"}) impose volatile Time-of-Use (TOU) tariff schedules, peak-demand charges, and surprise rate adjustments (\texttt{UTILITY\_SURPRISE\_RATE\_ADJUSTMENT}, scaling up to $300\%$ under \texttt{LEVEL\_2\_HOSTILE}).

Rather than fighting the utility unlawfully, Layer~4 (\texttt{col-execd}) and Layer~7 (\texttt{col-adversaryd}) execute deterministic \textbf{Thermodynamic Tariff Arbitrage} within the bounds of the node's net-metering or PURPA Qualifying Facility interconnection agreement (or Brazilian ANEEL Lei~14.300/2022 \textit{Micro e Minigera{\c{c}}{\~a}o Distribu{\'\i}da} net-metering credits):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Off-Peak / Midday Solar Absorption:} When local rooftop photovoltaic generation exceeds immediate baseload consumption---or during super-off-peak municipal wind/hydro surplus windows---Layer~4 charges the node's $\mathrm{LiFePO_4}$ / sodium-ion battery banks ($48{,}000\,\mathrm{mV}$) and schedules high-exergy thermal batches (heating domestic hot-water tanks in \texttt{Scenario Zeta}, pre-heating bakery deck mass in \texttt{Scenario Gamma}, or running induction melts in \texttt{Scenario Theta}).
    \item \textbf{Peak-Tariff Self-Consumption and Residual Net-Metering Export:} During peak evening tariff windows (\texttt{16:00--21:00}), the PCC inverter zeroes grid import ($P_{\mathrm{import,mW}} = 0$) and, if Layer~3 verifies a positive exergy residual $R_t > 0$ after all five internal priority tiers ($F_t, B_t, C_t, O_t, M_t$) are satisfied, exports bounded surplus power ($X_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}} \le R_{t,\mu\mathrm{J}}$) solely up to the amount required to zero out the cooperative's annual utility standing charges and water/property bills ($\Phi_{\mathrm{utility},t} \to 0\,\text{cents}$).
\end{itemize}

\section{DMZ Telemetry Obfuscation Against Smart-Meter Surveillance}
\label{sec:l7-telemetry-obfuscation}

High-frequency utility smart meters are notorious instruments of \textbf{Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring (NILM)}: external data brokers analyze $1\,\mathrm{Hz}$ edge power transients to infer when residents wake, shower, cook, or run specific workshop machinery. Layer~7 neutralizes NILM surveillance physically at the PCC: because the node's local $\mathrm{LiFePO_4}$ battery bank and DC bus act as a low-pass thermodynamic buffer between internal child nodes and the utility meter, the external utility grid sees only a flat, scheduled constant-wattage charging plateau or a smooth IEEE~1547 net-metering curve. All internal switching transients terminate inside Layer~2, revealing zero pattern-of-life information to the outside world.
