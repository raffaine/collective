\section{The Dual-Membrane Doctrine}
\label{sec:l7-dual-membrane}

The Dual-Membrane Doctrine rejects two symmetrical historical failures:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Extraterritorial Secession Fallacy:} Early cypherpunk projects and naive intentional communities frequently imagined they could declare unilateral immunity from municipal zoning codes, tax authorities, or labor regulators, or rely on adversarial ``parasitism'' and ``grid leeching.'' In practice, the territorial state holds a monopoly on coercive enforcement; unlawful wire taps, tax evasion, or unpermitted occupancy hand hostile authorities an immediate, unassailable pretext to seize land and dismantle the settlement.
    \item \textbf{The Commercial Assimilation Fallacy:} Conversely, traditional worker cooperatives and eco-villages that allow external market prices, equity investors, or speculative token bridges to enter their internal accounting gradually subordinate domestic care and ecological regeneration to commercial revenue maximization---the classic cooperative degeneration crisis documented by \textcite{cornforth1995}.
\end{enumerate}

To defeat both failure modes simultaneously, the Sovereign Stack erects two concentric, semi-permeable operational membranes:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Membrane~1 --- The External Cooperative Legal Carapace (Layer~7):} A lawful, substantively compliant corporate and trust shell facing municipal building inspectors, tax agencies, utility monopolies, banking rails, and hardware vendors. Membrane~1 holds fee-simple land title in trust, files GAAP/statutory tax returns, maintains workers' compensation and general liability policies, and settles external invoices in integer fiat \texttt{cents}.
    \item \textbf{Membrane~2 --- The Internal Sovereign Core (Layers~1--6):} The non-monetary, thermodynamic, and epistemic commons inside the settlement. Membrane~2 operates on 64-bit fixed-point SI vector accounting (\texttt{uJ}, \texttt{mW}, \texttt{mg}, \texttt{mm}, \texttt{mL}, \texttt{mK}, \texttt{cB}, \texttt{ms}, \texttt{bp}), pairwise decentralized identifiers (\texttt{did:peer}), zero-knowledge \texttt{BBS+} capability proofs, unconditional baseline existence ($B_{\mathrm{baseline}} = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$), and node-membership governance ($V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$).
\end{itemize}

\section{Strict 7-Layer Adjacency and the VLAN 40 DMZ Boundary (\texttt{CCI-1})}
\label{sec:l7-daemon-isolation}

\begin{invariant}[Layer~7 DMZ Containment and Zero Lower-Layer Coupling]
\label{inv:l7-dmz-containment}
The Layer~7 daemon \texttt{col-adversaryd} executes inside an unprivileged Linux/POSIX network and mount namespace assigned to \textbf{VLAN~40 (DMZ)} under dedicated user \texttt{uid\_col\_adversaryd}. It satisfies five hardware- and kernel-enforced isolation properties:

    \item \textbf{Single Inward Socket (\texttt{l6\_l7.sock}):} \texttt{col-adversaryd} binds and connects inward exclusively to \texttt{/run/collective/ipc/l6\_l7.sock} (\texttt{AF\_UNIX}, \texttt{SOCK\_SEQPACKET}) verified via kernel \texttt{SO\_PEERCRED} against \texttt{uid\_col\_commonsd}. \section{Two Value Systems, Zero Exchange Rate (\texttt{CCI-6})}
\label{sec:l7-value-incommensurability}

The most dangerous architectural error in legacy ``Web3'' and ``crypto-cooperative'' designs is the construction of a two-way financial bridge or Automated Market Maker (AMM) that establishes a floating exchange rate between external fiat currency and internal accounting units. The moment an internal thermodynamic unit can be bought with dollars or redeemed for dollars:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Wealthy external actors can purchase internal governance influence or pre-empt workshop and housing leases;
    \item Internal stewards are incentivized to hoard credits for fiat cash-out rather than circulating capacity locally; and
    \item Tax authorities (such as the IRS under 26~U.S.C.~\S~6045 or the Brazilian Receita Federal) reclassify the internal ledger as a taxable virtual-currency or commercial barter exchange.
\end{itemize}

\begin{theorem}[Thermodynamic-Fiat Incommensurability]
\label{thm:l7-incommensurability}
Let $\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{L3}} = (\mathbb{Z}_{64})^{\text{dim}}$ denote the multi-dimensional 64-bit fixed-point SI physical vector space conserved by Layer~3 (\texttt{col-storaged}) across microjoules (\texttt{uJ}), milligrams (\texttt{mg}), milliliters (\texttt{mL}), millimeters (\texttt{mm}), millikelvins (\texttt{mK}), centibels (\texttt{cB}), milliseconds (\texttt{ms}), and basis points (\texttt{bp}), and let $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{L7}} = \mathbb{Z}_{64}$ denote the scalar fiat treasury balance tracked by Layer~7 (\texttt{col-adversaryd}) in integer \texttt{cents}. There exists no protocol homomorphism, exchange-rate oracle, or conversion functor $f : \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{L7}} \leftrightarrow \mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{L3}}$ anywhere in the Sovereign Stack:
\begin{equation}
\texttt{AttemptConvertFiatToExergyCredits()} \equiv \texttt{false}, \qquad \texttt{AttemptTokenizeBaselineCare()} \equiv \texttt{false}
\label{eq:ch1-no-exchange-rate}
\end{equation}
What crosses the physical boundary of the settlement is strictly \textbf{matter and energy}: physical goods or IEEE~1547 electrical energy ($E_{\mathrm{export},\mu\mathrm{J}}$) leave the node to settle external invoices in \texttt{cents}, and physical raw materials, tools, or replacement components enter the node to be inventoried by Layer~2 and Layer~3 strictly according to their physical mass (\texttt{mg}), volume (\texttt{mL}), and lifecycle Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$ in \texttt{uJ}).
\end{theorem}
