\epigraph{The purpose of a system is what it does. If a system is to remain viable, it must adapt to the variety of its environment without surrendering its internal cohesion.}{---\textcite{beer1974}, \textit{Designing Freedom}}

Every autonomous settlement in The Collective begins its physical life inside a territory claimed by a legacy nation-state, wired to a legacy utility grid, governed by municipal zoning ordinances, and equipped with secondhand appliances manufactured by legacy industrial supply chains. No post-capitalist commonwealth materializes out of thin air inside a sterile cleanroom stocked with native open-spec silicon and debt-free land. A community that confronts the surrounding state and market apparatus with theatrical polemics or unlawful infrastructure tampering invites immediate administrative suppression before its first garden harvest; a community that uncritically internalizes market price signals, equity shares, or proprietary hardware dependencies inevitably degenerates back into the extractive capitalism it set out to transcend \parencite{polanyi1944great,cornforth1995}.

\textbf{Layer~7: The Layer~7 Carapace and Assimilation Bridge} is the outermost membrane of the seven-layer Sovereign Stack and the only layer engineered with a built-in imperative to shrink toward its own obsolescence. Realized in software by the Demilitarized Zone (DMZ) boundary daemon \texttt{col-adversaryd} (isolated on VLAN~40 and speaking strictly inward to Layer~6 \texttt{col-commonsd} over \texttt{/run/collective/ipc/l6\_l7.sock}) and in law by a progressive succession of cooperative, land-trust, and purpose-trust entities, Layer~7 performs two strictly bounded functions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Protective Cooperative Legal Carapace (Membrane~1):} It absorbs external tax, zoning, building-code, labor-law, and commercial liability friction through impeccably lawful, unremarkable local entities---shielding the internal thermodynamic, epistemic, and domestic commons (Membrane~2: Layers~1 through~6) from partition lawsuits, hostile buyouts, regulatory consolidation, and panoptic state legibility \parencite{scott1998,ostrom1990}.
    \item \textbf{The Transitional Assimilation Bridge and Hardware Obsolescence Horizon:} It manages the settlement's finite external fiat reserves (beginning with the Stage~1A $\$20{,}000$ / \texttt{2,000,000 cents} Genesis reserve $\Phi_0$), governs residual fair-trade export ($R_t, L_t$) to fund ratified non-local needs ($\Phi_t$), and orchestrates the physical transition of the settlement's machinery from \textbf{Stage~I Scavenged Layer~7 Hardware} through \textbf{Stage~II Right-to-Repair Hybrid Retrofits} to \textbf{Stage~III Native Open-Spec Collective Hardware} \parencite{perzanowski2022right,illich1973}.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Retiring Adversarial Metaphors and Harmonizing with the Horizon Council (v3.2)}

Earlier preliminary notes for this volume used adversarial cypherpunk tropes---speaking of ``strategic parasitism,'' ``API parasitism,'' ``physical grid leeching,'' ``bypassing AML/KYC,'' and ``fiat-crypto exchange bridges.'' In ratification of the Horizon Council's \textit{Architecture of Boundless Time} synthesis (Dialogues~D1--D8 and Cross-Cutting Invariants \texttt{CCI-1} through \texttt{CCI-6}), those tropes are permanently retired:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Two Value Systems, Zero Exchange Rate (\texttt{CCI-6}):} There is no ``fiat-crypto bridge'' or automated market maker converting dollars into internal Layer~3 credits. Layer~3 (\texttt{col-storaged}) maintains a multi-dimensional vector ledger in canonical 64-bit fixed-point SI integers (\texttt{uJ}, \texttt{mW}, \texttt{mg}, \texttt{mm}, \texttt{mL}, \texttt{mK}, \texttt{cB}, \texttt{ms}, \texttt{bp}), whereas Layer~7 tracks external statutory accounts in integer \texttt{cents}. Domestic care and baseline existence ($B_{\mathrm{baseline}} = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$) are unconditionally non-commensurable (\texttt{AttemptTokenizeBaselineCare() == false}).
    \item \textbf{Anatomy of Scavenged Layer~7 Hardware Friction (\texttt{D7}, \texttt{D8}, \texttt{CCI-6}):} Under Layer~7 venture capitalism, neither software nor hardware is ever permitted to \textit{be finished} because recurring rent extraction requires planned obsolescence, proprietary fastener fragmentation, potted PCBs, and DRM locks. Across the Sovereign Stack, bootstrapping on scavenged Layer~7 appliances imposes four concrete physical and cybernetic penalties (``the shim tax''): (1) \textit{Parasitic Layer~2 Shims} (\texttt{0x04}), (2) \textit{Blind Layer~4 Mid-Batch Hard-Blocks}, (3) \textit{Layer~7 Fiat Tribute Escalation}, and (4) \textit{Layer~3 Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$) Penalties}.
    \item \textbf{Right-to-Repair Hybrid Retrofits as First-Class Proof of Stewardship (\texttt{D8}, \texttt{CCI-6}):} Rather than inventing fake RPG ``tech trees,'' communities liberate boundless human time (\textbf{Zero-Stat Liberation}) by reverse-engineering ubiquitous consumer appliances---publishing open ESP32-S3 / STM32H7 / RISC-V Layer~2 controller PCBs and 3D-printable metric adapters as Layer~6 retrofit artifacts. Executing a Stage~I $\to$ Stage~II retrofit rescues embodied chassis exergy from premature scrap and is recognized as a first-class Layer~3 Proof of Stewardship ($\Delta V$ and Layer~5 $\Delta R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}$) event.
    \item \textbf{Protecting Internal $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$ Democracy and Enveloping Node Hypervisors (\texttt{D2}--\texttt{D6}, \texttt{CCI-2}--\texttt{CCI-4}):} The external legal carapace is engineered so that internal governance never leaks into shareholder equity. Inside Membrane~2, every Layer~5 node evaluates voting weight strictly across \textbf{Node Membership ($N_s$)} as $V_i(t) = B + R_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$ ($10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp} + R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t)$), protecting mature commons in \textbf{Dormant Equilibrium (``The \TeX{} Asymptote'')} from single-maintainer hijack while external demand ($N_p$) flows via zero-surveillance \textbf{Pantry} appetite vectors and auto-expiring pickup leases. Co-located polycentric nodes ($N=1$ Studio, $N=1$ Maker Bench, $N=4$ Hearth) are wrapped internally by a domain-blind \textbf{Enveloping Layer~5 Node Hypervisor} and externally by a unified \textbf{Community Land Trust (CLT) / Cooperative Ground Lease Carapace}.
    \item \textbf{Open-Source Franchising Without the Rent (\texttt{D5}, \texttt{D7}, \texttt{CCI-5}):} Proven node blueprints stored across the three \textbf{Sovereign Libraries} (\textit{Ontological Graph}, \textit{Orchestration Registry}, and \textit{Physics Baseline}) are exported to sister settlements under reciprocal open licenses (\texttt{AGPLv3}, \texttt{CERN-OHL-S v2}, \texttt{DPL 1.1}) and adapted to heterogeneous local hardware via Layer~4's \textbf{3-Tier BPMN Hardware Adaptation Cascade}.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Structure of This Companion Monograph}

Part~I (Chapters~1--10) establishes the foundational engineering, legal, and thermodynamic architecture of Layer~7:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Chapter~1} formalizes the \textbf{Dual-Membrane Doctrine} and the bi-directional carapace (\texttt{col-adversaryd} on VLAN~40 DMZ over \texttt{l6\_l7.sock} paired with \texttt{col-gatewayd}), proving why internal 64-bit SI vector accounting and external fiat \texttt{cents} never share an exchange rate.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~2} presents a rigorous engineering dissection of \textbf{Scavenged Layer~7 Hardware Friction} (``the shim tax''): parasitic Layer~2 shims (\texttt{0x04}), blind Layer~4 mid-batch hard-blocks, Layer~7 fiat tribute escalation, and Layer~3 Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$) spikes.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~3} specifies the \textbf{3-Tier Hardware Fleet Progression} and \textbf{Right-to-Repair Hybrid Retrofit Kits}, detailing how replacing proprietary potted PCBs with open RISC-V/STM32/ESP32-S3 controllers severs vendor tribute, mints first-class Layer~3 Proof of Stewardship (\texttt{ProofOfStewardshipRetrofitRecordV50}), and leverages statutory Right-to-Repair shields.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~4} demonstrates how Layer~3 $\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$ and the \textbf{Canonical 64-Bit Fixed-Point SI Core} dissolve artificial market fragmentation into open modular geometry (\textbf{Gastronorm GN~1/1}, \textbf{ISO $600 \times 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ totes}, \textbf{M6/M8 metric fasteners}) while Layer~6 localizes human craft idioms at the edge.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~5} details the \textbf{Cooperative Legal Carapace, Community Land Trust (CLT), and Tripartite Inter-Node Chassis} (UUNAA \S~6 BMAAs, IRC Subchapter~T SSAs, UCEA Easements, Perpetual Purpose Trusts, \textit{Alamo} labor bifurcation, and Brazilian/Lusophone statutory equivalents) wrapping polycentric \textbf{Enveloping Node Hypervisors}.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~6} formalizes \textbf{Purpose-Bound Procurement}, the 3-tier recursive BOM resolver, Layer~4's \textbf{3-Tier BPMN Hardware Adaptation Cascade}, the ratified Needs List ($\Phi_t$), and the \textbf{Automatic $\Phi_t = 0$ Bill-Zeroing Shutoff}.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~7} specifies \textbf{Lawful Municipal Utility Interconnection, Tariff Arbitrage, and IEEE~1547-2018 Islanding}, replacing adversarial ``grid leeching'' with compliant net-metering, time-of-use battery buffering, sub-cycle SiC MOSFET islanding, and DMZ telemetry obfuscation.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~8} develops the mathematics and human safeguards of \textbf{Residual Export ($R_t, L_t$)}, the \textbf{Exergy Shield ($V \le \kappa E$)}, the \textbf{Progressive Siphon ($S(V_s)$)}, the Prohibited Export Register, and rotational membrane protections ($\Delta R = 0$ for fiat revenue).
    \item \textbf{Chapter~9} codifies \textbf{Open-Source Franchise Export (``The Speedee Service System Without the Rent'')} across the three \textbf{Sovereign Libraries} (\textit{Ontological Graph}, \textit{Orchestration Registry}, \textit{Physics Baseline}) and their four instantiation modes.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~10} specifies \textbf{Systemic Blinding, Regulatory Defense, and Anti-Capture Immunity}: structural illegibility, mundane semantic camouflage, \textit{NAACP v.~Alabama} data non-existence, the \texttt{col-adversaryd} Discrete Event Simulation (DES) engine, Ring $R_6$ migrant assimilation, and defending Dormant Equilibrium nodes via $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$.
\end{itemize}

Part~II (Chapters~11--14) walks through the Layer~7 assimilation and hardware transition mechanics across \textbf{Horizontal Waves~A through~F} (all 25 Sovereign Scenarios) and concludes with the \textbf{Severance Protocol ($D_t \to 0$)}, a line-by-line C++20 reference audit of \texttt{col-adversaryd}, a Six-Specialist Layer~7 Council Review, and the terminal dissolution of the commercial carapace into \textbf{Zero-Stat Liberation}.

Part~III (Appendices~00--24) provides an exhaustive, scenario-by-scenario engineering and legal reference for all twenty-five Sovereign Scenarios (\texttt{Scenario Zero} through \texttt{Scenario Omega}), tabulating each domain's Dual-Mode Layer~7 Reach, Stage~I $\to$ Stage~II $\to$ Stage~III hardware progression, Right-to-Repair Retrofit Kit BOM, and external fiat tribute elimination curve.
