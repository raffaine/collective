Part~II of this monograph traces how the Layer~7 legal carapace, the 3-Tier Hardware Fleet Progression (Stage~I Scavenged $\to$ Stage~II Right-to-Repair Hybrid $\to$ Stage~III Native Open-Spec), and the automatic $\Phi_t = 0$ bill-zeroing bridge operate across the \textbf{Six Horizontal Waves (Waves~A through~F)} and \textbf{25 Sovereign Scenarios} defined in \texttt{docs/03\_SCENARIOS.md}. \section{Wave A: Meta, Onboarding, and Homeostasis Core (Stage 1A Foundation)}
\label{sec:l7-wave-a}

\subsection{1. Scenario Zero (\texttt{SCN-ZERO-SOFTWARE}): Khronos/OpenGL Working-Group Carapace and Dormant Equilibrium}
Under Layer~7 venture capitalism, software is trapped in perpetual subscription churn: SaaS vendors push constant UI mutations and proprietary API breaking changes to justify monthly recurring revenue (\texttt{MRR}), turning software maintainers into burned-out wage servants.

In \textbf{Scenario Zero} (\texttt{D1}, \texttt{D2}, \texttt{CCI-1}--\texttt{CCI-3}), software---including the Sovereign Stack and the Oasis (\texttt{Layer~1b}) simulation specification itself---is governed as an immutable, content-addressed \textbf{Layer~6 Software Artifact} stewarded by a dedicated \textbf{Layer~5 Working Group} ($N_s = 1 \to 12$), analogous to the \textbf{Khronos Group} stewarding the \textbf{OpenGL} specification \parencite{khronos_opengl}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Mild Layer~1/2 Physical Footprint vs.\ Full Layer~4--6 Lifecycle:} Inside Oasis (\texttt{Layer~1b}), Scenario Zero never emulates guest operating systems or inner binaries. At Layers~1 and~2, software is simply workstation and 4-SBC server rack hardware drawing electrical power ($450{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$ inside a Habitat Enveloping Node closet lease capped at $4{,}200\,\mathrm{cB}$ and $+2{,}000\,\mathrm{mK}$ exhaust delta, recovering $\ge 8{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ of waste heat into domestic hot water). Simultaneously, it traverses its full 5-phase Khronos lifecycle across Layers~4--6: (1) \textit{Friction to RFC}, (2) \textit{Working Group Charter}, (3) \textit{Inception and 3-Arch Reproducible Build} (\texttt{x86\_64}, \texttt{aarch64}, \texttt{riscv64}), (4) \textit{Spec Ratification} with a plain-language \texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1} nutrition label, and (5) \textit{Servicing and Operations}.
    \item \textbf{Layer~7 Dual-Mode Reach \& Legacy Git Forge Detox:} During Stage~1A/1B, Scenario Zero operates an air-gapped \textbf{Detox Quarantine Diode} across VLAN~40: external open-source tarballs (Linux kernel, GCC/Clang, Zephyr RTOS, Wasmtime) are mirrored inward through \texttt{col-adversaryd}, stripped of telemetry phone-home binaries, and compiled hermetically on salvaged 20-year-old hardware ($\le 512\,\mathrm{MiB}$ RAM budget, \texttt{external\_network\_calls == 0}). All outbound artifacts carry an \texttt{AGPL-3.0} / \texttt{CERN-OHL-S-2.0} copyleft shield under a UUNAA~\S~6 / Brazilian \texttt{Art.~5{\textordmasculine} XVIII CF/88} unincorporated working-group charter.
    \item \textbf{Dormant Equilibrium (``The \TeX{} Asymptote'') and Substrate Drift Wake-Up:} When a Scenario Zero release reaches stability \parencite{knuth1984tex}, incident tickets drop to zero and working-group reputation $R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t) \to 0$. The non-decaying civic baseline $B_{\mathrm{bp}} = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ in $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$ prevents single-maintainer capture (\S\ref{sec:l7-dormant-capture-defense}). When a new Layer~2 sensor revision (\texttt{ESP32-S3} probe v1.4) later triggers a \textbf{Substrate Drift} hard-block (\texttt{SUBSTRATE\_DRIFT\_ADAPTER\_REQUIRED}), \texttt{col-commonsd} decompiles it into a Layer~6 Semantic Diff Card in the \textbf{\textit{Scenario Zero Forge Studio}} lens, waking the working group just long enough to ratify the adapter before returning to quiet dormancy.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{2. Scenario Tau (\texttt{SCN-TAU-GENESIS}): The $\$20{,}000$ Reserve ($\Phi_0$) and UUNAA \S~6 Genesis Carapace}
\textbf{Scenario Tau} governs Day-0 bring-up on a single suburban lot ($N_s = 1 \to 4$) starting with the finite $\$20{,}000$ (\texttt{2,000,000 cents}) fiat reserve $\Phi_0$:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Legal Carapace (Purely Defensive):} The household organizes immediately as an Unincorporated Nonprofit Association under UUNAA~\S~6 (or a Brazilian \textit{Associa{\c{c}}{\~a}o Civil} under \texttt{Art.~5{\textordmasculine} XVIII CF/88}), utilizing ministerial State ADU preemption and home-occupation exemptions while keeping its Lot/House Enveloping Node boundary within $5{,}500\,\mathrm{cB}$ ($55\,\mathrm{dBA}$) daytime, $3{,}800\,\mathrm{cB}$ ($38\,\mathrm{dBA}$) nighttime, and the $9{,}600{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$ ($9.6\,\mathrm{kW}$) residential main breaker cap.
    \item \textbf{Turnkey Day-0 Instantiation on Stage~I Scavenged Hardware:} Using Mode~1 Turnkey Instantiation (\texttt{D7}), the founders pull the Lot~402 Genesis blueprint and allocate $\Phi_0$ across secondhand Stage~I appliances (Craigslist chest freezer, induction stockpot, 400W balcony solar kit, raised-bed timber) wrapped in parasitic Layer~2 CT/thermistor shims (\texttt{0x04}) to guarantee the unconditional \textbf{$B_{\mathrm{baseline}} = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ Right to Exist} from Day~1.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{3. Scenario Sigma (\texttt{SCN-SIGMA-BURNOUT}): Somatic Homeostasis and Zero-Stat Liberation}
\textbf{Scenario Sigma} defends human stewards ($N_s = 1 \to 6$) against the chronic exhaustion of both legacy wage labor and founder overwork:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Purely Defensive Layer~7 Shield:} Layer~7 enforces the constitutional rule that \textbf{people precede contracts}: when a steward activates a \texttt{RestShield} widget or flips their physical brass sanctuary door latch (enforcing a $3{,}800\,\mathrm{cB}$ acoustic ceiling and zero non-hazard alerts), \texttt{col-execd} and \texttt{col-adversaryd} automatically invoke the interruptible curtailment clause on any affected Tier~5 export task.
    \item \textbf{Hardware Progression Eliminating Burnout:} Scenario Sigma dramatizes why Stage~I Scavenged Hardware causes founder stress: random mid-batch blind hard-blocks ($350\,\mathrm{bp}$) interrupt evening rest. Upgrading key household appliances to Stage~II Right-to-Repair Hybrids and Stage~III Native Open-Spec twins converts erratic emergency repairs into predictable 5-minute scheduled swaps, realizing \textbf{Zero-Stat Liberation}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Wave B: Suburban Survival, Energy, Fabrication, and Transit Seed (Stage 1B Pod)}

\subsection{1. Scenario Gamma (\texttt{SCN-GAMMA-KITCHEN}): The Dual-Loop Bakery, \textit{The Pantry} Lens, and PNAE / Commissary Carapace}
\textbf{Scenario Gamma} replaces commercial grocery bills ($D_{\mathrm{food}} \to 0$) across a 5-to-12 lot neighborhood pod and scales into a Stage~2 commissary bakery ($N_s = 5$ active bakers serving $N_p = 500$ neighbors):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Thermodynamic Separation of \textit{The Pantry} vs.\ \textit{The Bakery / Hearth} Lenses (\texttt{D4}, \texttt{D5}, \texttt{CCI-3}):} On each household node, \textbf{\textit{The Pantry}} lens tracks continuous biological Arrhenius TTL decay curves ($275{,}150\text{--}277{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ cold chain) and absorbs \textbf{Spontaneous Weekend Detours} automatically: when a resident heads to the mountains, Layer~2 shelf drawdowns pause and short-lived 64-byte \texttt{AnonymousAppetiteLeaseV50} pickup leases expire silently at \texttt{expiry\_hlc\_ms} with zero calendar surveillance. At the communal commissary, \textbf{\textit{The Bakery / Hearth}} lens governs only the $N_s = 5$ member-bakers via $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$, scheduling \textbf{$8{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($85\%$) verified staples} and **$1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($15\%$) Dr\_Sofia's culinary \textit{montage} experiments**.
    \item \textbf{3-Tier BPMN Cascade \& Gastronorm ERC Convergence:} When a turnkey 20-kg sourdough blueprint meets a scavenged 10-kg deck oven, Tier~1 SI Parameter Scaling splits the bake into $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{kg}$ waves across standard \textbf{Gastronorm \texttt{GN 1/1} ($530 \times 325\,\mathrm{mm}$)} pans. Replacing the oven's potted controller with a Stage~II open STM32H7 PID + dual K-type thermocouple retrofit eliminates $\$620/\mathrm{yr}$ in OEM board tribute and verifies Bigelow pasteurization ($F_0 \ge 347{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$).
    \item \textbf{Layer~7 Carapace:} Stage~1B porch tiffin sharing operates under UUNAA~\S~6 BMAAs and Cottage Food exemptions; Stage~2 wraps the commissary in an IRC Subchapter~T Cooperative (or Brazilian \texttt{Lei 5.764/71} Agroecological Cooperative selling residual surplus to municipal schools under \textbf{PNAE \texttt{Lei 11.947/2009}} \parencite{brazil_pnae2009}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{2. Scenario Alpha (\texttt{SCN-ALPHA-FAB}): \textit{The Workshop} Lens, Tier 2 Macro-Substitution, and CNC Right-to-Repair}
\textbf{Scenario Alpha} governs the neighborhood tool library and fabrication shop ($N_s = 1 \to 6$ machinists inside an Industrial Bay Enveloping Node capped at $8{,}500\,\mathrm{cB}$ daytime / $5{,}000\,\mathrm{cB}$ nighttime and $4{,}500{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Durable Cycle Telemetry in \textit{The Workshop} Lens (\texttt{D5}, \texttt{CCI-3}):} Unlike \textit{The Pantry}, \textbf{\textit{The Workshop}} lens ignores calendar expiration for drills, end-mills, and \texttt{M6}/\texttt{M8} fasteners, tracking discrete spindle usage hours (\texttt{cumulativeUseMs}), torque cycles, calibration intervals, and hardware-interlocked custody leases (\texttt{Scenario Kappa} \texttt{BBS+} VCs).
    \item \textbf{Tier~2 Macro-Activity Substitution \& Stage~II CNC Retrofit:} When an imported blueprint calls for a laser seam weld unavailable locally, Layer~4's Tier~2 cascade splices in \texttt{macro:FlangedM6BoltedJointWithGasket}. When a scavenged DRM-locked 3-axis router suffers a proprietary motion-card fault, the guild executes a Stage~II Right-to-Repair retrofit using an open RISC-V / STM32H7 LinuxCNC/GRBL controller board, minting first-class Layer~3 Proof of Stewardship (\texttt{ProofOfStewardshipRetrofitRecordV50}) and severing $\$850/\mathrm{yr}$ in OEM maintenance fees.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{3. Scenario Beta (\texttt{SCN-BETA-TRANSIT}): Five-Artery Milk-Runs, ISO Totes, and Open BMS Retrofits}
\textbf{Scenario Beta} coordinates consolidated cargo-trike and light-EV milk-runs ($\eta_{\mathrm{route}} \ge 6{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$, $N_s = 6 \to 25$ mechanics and couriers where $N_s > R_{\max} + 2$ guarantees community anti-capture):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Consuming Scenario Zero Releases \& Tier~1 Payload Splitting:} Scenario Beta consumes deterministic CVRP routing releases ratified by Scenario Zero's working group and splits cargo payloads across standardized \textbf{ISO $600 \times 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ Euro-totes} via Layer~4 Tier~1 scaling.
    \item \textbf{Stage~I $\to$ Stage~II Open BMS Retrofit \& Tripartite Highway Shield:} Scavenged commercial e-bike batteries with CAN-bus DRM locks are retrofitted with open-hardware Smart BMS boards ($48{,}000\,\mathrm{mV}$ standard bus). Inter-node cargo crossing public streets is shielded from DOT commercial carrier taxation by Subchapter~T Shared Services Agreements (SSAs) and UCEA trail easements (or Brazilian \textit{Ato Cooperativo} transport manifests under \texttt{Lei 5.764/71}).
\end{itemize}
