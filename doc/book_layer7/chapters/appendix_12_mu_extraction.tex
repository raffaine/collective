\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Layer~7 Reach Mode:} \textit{Dual-Mode (Transitional Municipal Deconstruction Salvage \& Defensive EPA/CONAMA Biosafety Shield)}. Bids on municipal building deconstruction and appliance-takeback contracts (often receiving tipping-fee credits in \texttt{cents} while harvesting massive embodied exergy in structural timber, copper, steel, and glass).
    \item \textbf{US / Common-Law Carapace:} IRC Subchapter~T Deconstruction and Resource Recovery Cooperative licensed under state solid-waste diversion exemptions and 40~CFR~Part~503 Class~A biosolids/thermophilic compost standards ($\ge 333{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ for $\ge 259{,}200{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}$ [72 hours]).

\end{itemize}
