### 1. Beyond Cages, Lawsuits, Call-Out Mobs, and Commune "Peace-Washing"
Every human community eventually faces conflict: two neighbors clash over late-night workshop noise, a guild member cuts a safety corner on a lathe, someone breaks a treaty commitment, or two factions develop irreconcilable visions for the village.

In Layer 7—and in naive intentional communities—conflict resolution fails in four predictable ways:
1. **Carceral Vengeance & Prisons:** State courts equate "justice" with locking human beings in cages—traumatizing people, bankrupting families, leaving victims with zero material restitution, and never fixing the root cause.
2. **Adversarial Lawsuits:** Civil disputes drag on for years until corporate lawyers eat up the entire value of the cooperative.
3. **Algorithmic Call-Out Mobs & Scarlet Letters:** Online platforms and toxic activist spaces replace due process with viral public shaming walls and permanent digital stigmas.
4. **Forced "Peace-Washing" of Abuse:** Conversely, informal communes without clear boundaries sometimes pressure a survivor of domestic intimidation to "sit in a healing circle" with their abuser, or trap dissenting groups inside one deadlocked assembly until it explodes.

**Scenario `Psi`** replaces all four failures with **Transformative Root-Cause Justice and the Constitutional Right of Graceful Fission**.
