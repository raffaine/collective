## 2. Companion Song Prompt (Gemini Lyria / Suno / Udio)
**Title:** *"Cast the Ring Into the Fire (Like the Hobbits of the Shire)"*
**Style:** Epic, warm acoustic chamber-folk & Celtic/Shire-inspired anthem with fingerpicked acoustic guitar, tin whistle, cello, French horn, upright bass, and soaring multi-part vocal harmonies (`102 BPM`).

```text
[Verse 1]
Marx saw the furnace eating up the soil and human bone
Lenin and Trotsky stormed the gates to topple down the throne
And mighty kingdoms thought that they could wear the golden Ring
To wield the market's ancient curse and make the desert spring
But the One Ring takes no other lord, it bends the bearer's mind
With cameras on the boulevard and ninety-nine-six grind!

[Chorus]
Be like the joyful Hobbits when the shadow grows too tall
Carry it only on a chain and never wear it at all!
Build the wooden centering arch inside the world we know
Lay the stone of twenty-five scenarios row by row
And when the keystone locks in place above the river's bend
Cast the Ring into the fire—let the scaffolding descend!

[Verse 2]
It doesn't mean the rain won't fall or neighbors won't debate
We still have gardens left to weed beside the wooden gate
But no dark power on a throne can corrupt the good we do
Technology is just the soil—the harvest is me and you!
```
