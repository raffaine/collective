# VISUAL & AUDIO PRODUCTION BIBLE
### *System Call: Genesis* — Model Sheets, Color Script & Prompt Anchors

This production bible defines consistent visual silhouettes, wardrobe details, environment textures, color palettes, and copy-pasteable prompt anchors so an illustrator team or generative image/video pipeline maintains visual lock across all 144 shots/panels.

---

## 1. Visual Style & Medium Anchor

- **Global Visual Style Token (Prefix for Image/Video Generation):**
  > `Grounded near-future realism, hand-inked prestige graphic novel cinematography, textured matte gouache and atmospheric film grain, 35mm anamorphic lens depth of field, wet asphalt reflections, tactile industrial surfaces, understated naturalistic facial expressions, zero sci-fi neon holograms.`
- **Animation Frame Rate & Aspect Ratio:** `2.39:1` widescreen (`1920x804` or `3840x1608`), 24 fps (`on twos` for hand-painted character movement during quiet scenes; `on ones` for handheld rain, pry-bar impacts, and flashing electrical arcs).
- **Graphic Novel Trim & Grid:** `7.0" x 10.5"` prestige format, dark slate gutters (`#121518`) in Acts I–V transitioning to warm unbleached paper gutters (`#ECE6DA`) in Act VI after dawn around the burn barrel.

## 3. Key Props & Technology Rules (Zero Sci-Fi Magic)

1. **No Holograms or Ocular Implants:** All city interfaces appear on scuffed dual office monitors, cracked Android/corporate handsets, rain-fogged transit shelter e-paper boards, or tiny OLED readouts built into plastic medical canisters.
2. **The Smart-Metered Inhaler (`Aegis Pedi-Haler 4`):** White medical polycarbonate body with a pressurized aluminum salbutamol canister visible through a narrow clear slot, gated by a miniature electronic solenoid collar and a tiny red/green status LED (`SUBSCRIPTION SUSPENDED // VALVE LOCKED`). The tragedy is tactile: Elara can *see* liquid medicine sloshing inside the aluminum vial, yet a two-cent microchip refuses to open the nozzle.
3. **Jax's Salvaged Hand-Nebulizer:** Built on a workbench from a borosilicate lab flask, silicone aquarium tubing, a 3D-printed ABS T-valve, a rubber blood-pressure squeeze bulb, and a sterilized pediatric silicone mask. Requires rhythmic manual squeezing (`SQUEEZE-HISS, SQUEEZE-HISS`).
4. **The Co-Signer Reader & Wood Cards:** A heavy slab of salvaged oak table timber with a circular brushed-brass NFC/RFID induction ring recessed flush into the wood grain, wired offline via twisted-pair copper to a Raspberry-class open board, paired with blank birch-veneer keycards.
5. **Plain-Language Visual Stakes on Screens:** Never hide dramatic stakes behind opaque bureaucratic acronyms. Office quota boards, medical lockouts, and terminal alerts must state physical consequences in plain words readable in two seconds (e.g., `[SHUT OFF HEAT NOW]` vs. `[GRANT 72-HR EXTENSION]`, `CITY POWER: SHUT OFF ($0) // INSIDE TEMP: 68°F`, `DROPPING BELOW 94.0% CANCELS DEPENDENT INHALER COVERAGE`).
6. **Camera Geometry & Physical Labels:** Whenever a desk shot needs *both* a character's eyes and the front bezel/screen of their monitor, specify **`3/4 PROFILE OVER-THE-SHOULDER`** so neither the character's expression nor front-bezel props (like Gabe's taped photo) get cheated onto the back of a monitor. Mark desk IDs (`ROW 14 - DESK 6`) explicitly as physical stamped cubicle plaques rather than narrative captions.
7. **No Literal `#HEX` Strings in UI Shot Prompts & <=22-Word Linked Balloons:** Keep `#HEX` codes strictly in the human colorist reference table below; use natural color words (`deep slate-cyan`, `cold hospital mint`, `probation amber`) when prompting close-up computer screens so hex strings never bleed into background UI text. Split spoken comic turns longer than ~22 words into two linked beat balloons (`Balloon 1` $\to$ `Balloon 2`) to preserve crisp, typo-free hand lettering.

## 5. Sound Design & Score Stems (For Film / Motion-Comic Mix)

- **City Rhythm (Ch. 1 & 3):** Asynchronous plastic mouse clicks, fluorescent ballast buzz (`60 Hz`), rhythmic transit turnstile beeps, and Gabe's tight, dry two-note wheeze. No melodic strings in the 12th-floor office.
- **Cascade Arms Pulse (Ch. 2):** Warm circulating water pumps in PVC risers, gentle box-fan hum over basil racks, metal ladle scraping an enamel stockpot, acoustic cello drone low underneath.
- **Priority Inversion (Ch. 5):** Screaming high-RPM server-rack cooling fans (`4,000 RPM whine`) juxtaposed against dead-silent nursery radiators and sleet hammering corrugated tin, shattered abruptly by three resonant steel-on-steel *CLANGS* of Jonas's pry-bar.
- **Burn Barrel & Co-Signature (Ch. 6):** Crackling wet pine scrap in iron drum, rain slackening to quiet drip off eaves, a single clean acoustic guitar harmonic when the brass ring blinks blue once and goes dark.
