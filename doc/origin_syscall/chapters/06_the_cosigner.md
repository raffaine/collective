### PAGE 47 (3 Panels) — EXT. FOOTHILL MILL COURTYARD — PRE-DAWN `[24:10–24:45]`
*Palette: Burn-barrel ember orange against wet pewter dawn and mud-stained wool; comic page gutters shift from dark slate to warm unbleached cream.*  
*Audio Stem: Crackling dry pine slats in a 55-gallon steel drum; quiet creek roar; sleet eased to rare cold drops.*

**SHOT 123 | PANEL 47.1 — [WIDE / FIFTY-TWO PEOPLE AROUND THE BURN BARREL, 6.0s]**
- **Visual:** 5:45 AM. Pale grey light seeps over the eastern fir ridge. In the muddy center of the mill yard, flames leap bright orange out of a rusted 55-gallon steel drum, warming a wide, silent circle of fifty-two exhausted city refugees wrapped in mismatched coats and damp blankets. Three canvas duffel bags sit packed by the gate where two families stand half-ready to quit and walk down County Road Nine. Beside the fire on hot coals sits a blackened cast-iron stockpot of rice and pinto beans.

**SHOT 124 | PANEL 47.2 — [MEDIUM / KAEL, ELARA, JAX & JONAS AT THE DRUM, 5.0s]**
- **Visual:** Nobody stands on a stage or holds a megaphone. Kael stands at eye level in mud-caked boots beside a salvaged oak workbench dragged out under the porch eaves. Carved and painted in plain black stencil beside a circular brushed-brass card ring on the oak plank are the words: `2 NEIGHBOR CARDS REQUIRED TO UNLOCK HIGH-VOLTAGE SHOP`.

**SHOT 125 | PANEL 47.3 — [CLOSE-UP / JAX HOLDING THE RATION-SCORING DRIVE, 4.5s]**
- **Visual:** Jax steps up to the rim of the 55-gallon burn barrel. In his flux-stained fingers sits the silver flash drive pulled from the smashed server rack, labeled in black marker: `RATION & CHORE SCORING CODE`. He holds it out to Kael without excuse.
- **Dialogue (Jax, Balloon 1):** "Wiped the ration-scoring code and every chore-debt table off the backup too."
- **Dialogue (Jax, Balloon 2):** "Keep the spare-parts list if you want. Rest of it belongs in the fire."

### PAGE 48 (3 Panels) — EXT. BURN BARREL — CONTINUOUS `[24:45–25:25]`
*Palette: Sparks wheeling upward into cold blue-grey mountain air.*

**SHOT 126 | PANEL 48.1 — [MACRO INSERT / THE DRIVE DROPPING INTO THE COALS, 4.0s]**
- **Visual:** Kael takes the flash drive from Jax and drops it straight into the white-hot pine coals at the heart of the barrel. Green silicon solder blisters and curls black in flame as orange sparks spiral upward past fifty watching faces.
- **SFX:** *FOOMP-CRACKLE.*

**SHOT 127 | PANEL 48.2 — [MEDIUM / KAEL ADDRESSING THE CIRCLE, NO SPEECHIFYING, 6.5s]**
- **Visual:** Kael warms both chapped hands briefly at the iron drum, looking straight across the fire at Jonas (holding sleeping Mina wrapped warm against his chest) and the two families standing beside their packed duffels.
- **Dialogue (Kael, Balloon 1):** "We dragged city fear up the mountain in our pockets. Thought a cleaner computer meant we didn't have to trust strangers."
- **Dialogue (Kael, Balloon 2):** "Nearly froze Jonas's little girl learning better."

**SHOT 128 | PANEL 48.3 — [CLOSE-UP / KAEL AT THE OAK TABLE, 6.0s]**
- **Visual:** Kael rests one palm flat on the heavy salvaged oak plank beside the brass reader ring.
- **Dialogue (Kael, Balloon 1):** "From this morning on, two plain rules run this valley. First: roofs, blankets, medicine, and the soup pot are unconditional."
- **Dialogue (Kael, Balloon 2):** "No computer, no vote, and no clerk ever puts a meter between a neighbor and winter heat again."

### PAGE 49 (4 Panels) — EXT. MILL PORCH OAK TABLE — CONTINUOUS `[25:25–26:10]`

- **Dialogue (Mechanic):** "And if the guy you vouched for steals copper wire or hurts somebody?"
- **Dialogue (Elara, Balloon 1):** "Then your own shop key freezes right alongside theirs while twelve neighbors drawn by lot sort it out."
- **Dialogue (Elara, Balloon 2):** "Nobody hides behind a screen anymore. Your word has skin on it."

**SHOT 131 | PANEL 49.3 — [MEDIUM / KAEL LADLING THE BOTTOM OF THE IRON POT, 5.0s]**
- **Visual:** Kael lifts the lid off the blackened cast-iron pot beside the fire, scrapes the wooden ladle deep across the bottom for the thick last scoop of rice and pinto beans, and fills a dented tin bowl, handing it across the firelight to Elara first.

**SHOT 132 | PANEL 49.4 — [MACRO INSERT / THE VERY FIRST CO-SIGNATURE, 5.0s]**
- **Visual:** Elara sets the hot tin bowl on the oak plank. Kael places his blank birch-veneer keycard against the brushed-brass induction ring; Elara lays her blank birch card directly beside his, her flannel-bandaged knuckle brushing his copper-mended coat cuff. The tiny local LED on the brass rim blinks clean **BLUE** once and goes dark: offline, local, witnessed by fifty pairs of human eyes.
- **SFX:** Single quiet acoustic harmonic; rain stops completely on the tin eaves.

*Palette: First cold shaft of pale gold sun cresting the Cascade ridge, lighting steam rising off fifty bowls.*

**SHOT 133 | PANEL 50.1 — [MEDIUM / JONAS & JAX AT THE OAK TABLE, 5.5s]**

**SHOT 134 | PANEL 50.2 — [CLOSE-UP / JAX PRESSING HIS CARD BESIDE JONAS'S, 4.0s]**
- **Visual:** Jax swallows hard once without a speech or an excuse, and presses his birch card flat beside Jonas's chapped knuckles. The brass ring blinks **BLUE** once.

**SHOT 135 | PANEL 50.3 — [MEDIUM CLOSE / ELARA & GABE SHARING THE TIN BOWL, 5.0s]**
- **Visual:** Sitting on an upturned cedar beam beside the warm drum, Elara blows steam off a spoonful of rice and beans and hands the tin spoon to 8-year-old Gabe first. Gabe eats, breathing silent and deep in crisp 20-degree mountain dawn air without a plastic canister in sight.
