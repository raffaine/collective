# SYSTEM CALL: GENESIS
### *An Origin Cine-Script & Graphic Novella in the Collective Universe*

**Format:** Dual-Adaptation Hybrid Cine-Script (Animated Featurette + Prestige Graphic Novella)  
**Target Scale:** 54 Graphic Novel Pages | 144 Numbered Shots / Panels | ~28:30 Animated Runtime  
**Timeline:** 10 to 7 years prior to [*Fault Tolerance*](../book_fault_tolerance/main.tex)  
**Literary Standard:** Governed strictly by [`literary_style_guide.md`](../book_fault_tolerance/editorial_board/literary_style_guide.md) (show through framing and physical action; zero narrative em-dashes; zero melodramatic prose clichés; zero textbook protocol jargon).

---

## 1. Purpose & Dual-Pipeline Architecture

*System Call: Genesis* reimagines the early concepts of [*System Call*](../book_syscall/main.tex) from the ground up so they align seamlessly with the grounded realism and character history of [*Fault Tolerance*](../book_fault_tolerance/main.tex). Gone are sci-fi bone-bonded ocular implants, holographic villains, and instant global revolutions. In their place is a gritty, rain-soaked origin story about municipal complicity, maternal terror, salvaged hardware, and the nearly fatal winter bug that taught fifty frightened city refugees why clean math can never replace human trust.

Every beat in the script is engineered to map **1:1** across two visual mediums simultaneously:

| Script Unit | Animated / AI-Generated Film Pipeline | Graphic Novel / Comic Pipeline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`### PAGE XX (N Panels) [MM:SS–MM:SS]`** | A 25-to-40-second edited sequence with unified location, lighting palette, and continuous audio stem. | One full 6.625" × 10.25" (or European *bande dessinée* 8.5" × 11") comic page with explicit panel count and layout tier. |
| **`SHOT XXX \| PANEL X.Y [FRAMING, MOTION, DUR]`** | A single camera setup / AI-video generation clip with explicit lens framing (`WIDE`, `MEDIUM`, `CLOSE`, `MACRO`), camera movement (`STATIC`, `HANDHELD`, `SLOW PUSH`), and target duration in seconds. | A single comic panel with explicit composition, focal foreground/background staging, and panel weight (`SPLASH`, `WIDE TIER`, `HALF-TIER`, `INSERT`). |
| **`Visual`** | Concrete physical staging, character posture, lighting key, and prop state (directly usable with the prompt anchors in [`visual_production_bible.md`](./visual_production_bible.md)). | Pencil/ink staging and colorist lighting cue without abstract internal monologue. |
| **`Audio / SFX`** | Foley, room tone, score stem, and sound-bridge cues for sound mixing or AI audio generation. | Lettered sound effects (`SFX:`) and visual silence beats. |
| **`Dialogue / UI`** | Spoken lines with concise physical delivery cues + diegetic screen/signage inserts. | Speech balloons, radio/phone jagged tails, and rectangular screen-text boxes (zero omniscient narrator captions unless strictly time/place stamps). |

## 3. Canonical Continuity Locks with *Fault Tolerance*

- **Cluster 442-B Flashback ([`12_ignition_and_echoes.tex`](../book_fault_tolerance/chapters/12_ignition_and_echoes.tex)):** Rendered verbatim on screen in Chapter 1 (12th-floor City Utility Enforcement, `94.0%` shutoff quota, the grainy 30-second hardship appeal of the mother holding a coughing two-year-old in a parka, and Elara's plastic mouse click).
- **4th Avenue Rainy Transit Shelter ([`18_the_brink_of_severance.tex`](../book_fault_tolerance/chapters/18_the_brink_of_severance.tex)):** Kael (dark-bearded, ten years younger) handing 32-year-old Elara the encrypted thumb drive in downtown rain.
- **The First Winter Server-Rack Crisis ([`02_genesis.tex`](../book_fault_tolerance/chapters/02_genesis.tex)):** Dramatized in full across Chapters 5 and 6, including Jax's automated ration ledger, the 3:00 AM heating priority bug freezing the nursery cribs, carpenter Jonas smashing the server cabinet with a steel pry-bar, Elara splicing torn cable in icy mud outside the kitchen, and Kael signing beside Elara over a tin bowl of rice and beans around a burn barrel before dawn.
- **Character De-Collision:** The corporate division director from early *System Call* is renamed **Director Mercer** so the name **Vane** remains unique to the kiln stoker in *Fault Tolerance* Chapters 15–20.
- **Priya's Origin:** Seeded in Chapters 2 and 4 as a twenty-one-year-old bike courier who stays behind to run the legally shielded urban soup-and-filter cell in Grey Seattle.
