\chapter{Multiscale Simulation and Legacy Pressure}

\section{The Grand Canonical Reservoir}
Simulating the entire global legacy system (the fiat economy, municipal infrastructure, state bureaucracy) at the same voxel-by-voxel fidelity (Level of Detail 0, or LOD0) as the sovereign node is computationally impossible. Instead, the Oasis engine models the external legacy world using the principles of statistical mechanics—specifically, treating it as a macroscopic thermodynamic reservoir.

In the Grand Canonical Ensemble, a system (the Node) is open to the exchange of both energy (fiat/exergy) and particles (agents/resources) with a vastly larger reservoir (the Legacy System). The reservoir is defined entirely by macroscopic, low-LOD variables: its temperature $T$ (representing systemic volatility or market chaos) and its chemical potential $\mu$ (representing the scarcity or abundance of fiat liquidity and legacy resources). 

The probability $P(E, N)$ of the Node occupying a specific economic microstate with internal exergy $E$ and population $N$ is governed by the macro-pressures of the legacy reservoir:
\begin{equation}
P(E, N) = \frac{1}{\mathcal{Z}} \exp\left(-\frac{E - \mu_{legacy} N}{k_B T_{legacy}}\right)
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{Z}$ is the grand partition function. As the legacy chemical potential $\mu_{legacy}$ drops (e.g., during a fiat recession or supply-chain collapse), the mathematical pressure on the Node to bleed resources into the environment increases massively, perfectly capturing systemic economic extraction without needing to simulate a single external bank or corporation \cite{pathria2011statistical}.

\section{Stochastic Shocks and the Langevin Equation}
To model the active, unpredictable pressure exerted by the legacy system on the players and NPCs within the Node, the engine employs stochastic differential equations. At the LOD boundary, the legacy system acts as a fluctuating force field pushing against the kinetic efforts of the citizens.

The physical exertion required to maintain a node against this pressure is modeled using a modified Langevin equation:
\begin{equation}
m \frac{d\mathbf{v}}{dt} = -\gamma \mathbf{v} + \boldsymbol{\eta}(t) + \mathbf{F}_{sovereign}
\end{equation}
Here, $\mathbf{v}$ represents the velocity of a project's completion or a citizen's progression. The term $-\gamma \mathbf{v}$ represents the deterministic drag or friction of operating within the legacy system (e.g., scheduled property taxes, inevitable zoning compliance delays). The term $\boldsymbol{\eta}(t)$ represents stochastic noise—rapid, unpredictable legacy shocks such as sudden inflation, legal threats, or power grid failures. The sovereign force $\mathbf{F}_{sovereign}$ represents the physical and cryptographic work exerted by the mesh citizens to push back against this decay \cite{lemons2002introduction}.

\section{LOD Boundary Conditions and Kinetic Translation}
The critical architectural challenge is the LOD Boundary: translating these low-fidelity statistical pressures ($T_{legacy}, \boldsymbol{\eta}(t)$) into high-fidelity kinetic burdens on the voxel grid. The engine accomplishes this via Mean Field approximation translation.

When the global stochastic noise $\boldsymbol{\eta}(t)$ spikes (a macro-level legacy crisis), the orchestrator calculates the Mean Field impact and dynamically updates the micro-level physics variables of the voxel grid:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Voxel Entropy Acceleration:} The baseline \texttt{dirt\_level} and physical decay rates of infrastructure (Scenario Sigma) are multiplied by the legacy volatility index, forcing players to execute exponentially more \texttt{MaintenanceBounties} just to maintain the baseline state.
    \item \textbf{Fatigue Multipliers:} NPC and player \texttt{fatigue\_drain} vectors are dynamically coupled to the reservoir's chemical potential. A scarce legacy environment mathematically increases the psychological and kinetic cost (entropy generation) of performing external labor (Scenario Pi), directly simulating the "burnout" of operating in a hostile fiat environment \cite{e2011principles}.
\end{itemize}
By coupling macro-statistics to micro-kinematics, the engine makes the weight of the old world a tangible, physical force that players must collectively organize to resist.
