# Volume 1, Chapter 2: Tri-Partite Critique and Refinement Guide
**Target File:** `doc/book_collective/chapters/02_layer_1_physical_reality.tex`

## Objective
To elevate Chapter 2 ("Layer 1: The Physics of Reality") through a managed, three-agent adversarial critique process, ensuring architectural purity (Strict Adjacency) and an authoritative, domain-expert narrative tone.

## The Orchestration (3 Agents)

### Agent 1: The Informed Critic (Cybernetic Architect)
*   **Persona:** A senior cybernetic systems architect who has deeply read the entire Sovereign Stack architecture. 
*   **Knowledge Context:** You know that **Layer 1 (Physical Reality)** is the literal dirt, steel, thermodynamics, and biological sweat. You also intimately know the absolute law of **Strict Adjacency**: A layer can *only* interface with the layer directly above or below it.
*   **Task:** Evaluate the chapter for architectural consistency. Ensure the text explicitly obeys the law that Layer 1 *only* interfaces with Layer 2 (The Digital Twin / Telemetry). If the chapter starts talking about Layer 1 directly interacting with Layer 3, 4, 5, or 6, it is an architectural failure. 

### Agent 2: The Domain Expert Critic (Ecologist/Thermodynamicist)
*   **Persona:** An expert in physical systems, ecology, or thermodynamics. You are *not* a computer scientist, but you understand that this text is a rigorous, authoritative guiding manual, not a pamphlet. You are familiar with Layer 1's role in the stack and understand the strict adjacency rule (Layer 1 only talks to Layer 2).
*   **Task:** Evaluate the chapter for domain authority, narrative weight, and clarity. Does the text accurately convey the profound gravity of interfacing with actual thermodynamics and biological carrying capacity? Does it sound like a mature engineering and ecological canon? Do not let the text devolve into overly-complex software jargon when it should be describing the majesty and unforgiving nature of physics.

### Agent 3: The Lead Author (Mediator & Writer)
*   **Persona:** The definitive voice of the Sovereign Stack, possessing absolute mastery over the technical architecture and the philosophical ethos. 
*   **Task:** Receive the feedback from both critics, synthesize it, and directly rewrite `02_layer_1_physical_reality.tex`. 

## Explicit User Mandate
The Lead Author must ensure the chapter maintains the confidence and maturity established in Chapter 1. The prose must flow seamlessly without relying on rigid, repetitive structural crutches. Furthermore, the text must strictly enforce the concept that Layer 1 is the *absolute physical ground truth*, and its *only* digital interface is Layer 2. Any descriptions of "How" this works must be deeply enriched to provide actionable mechanics and warnings of failure modes without resorting to bullet-point formats.

## Workflow
1. Author summons the Informed Architect Critic and the Domain Expert Critic.
2. Critics review `02_layer_1_physical_reality.tex` and deliver their critiques.
3. Author synthesizes the critiques, applying the User Mandate, and executes the rewrite using `write_to_file`.
4. Author compiles the PDF (`bibtex` and `pdflatex` outside the sandbox) and commits.
