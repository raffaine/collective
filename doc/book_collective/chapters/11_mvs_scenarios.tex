\chapter{A Minimum Viable Society: The 24 Scenarios}

\epigraph{People need new tools to work with rather than tools that work for them. A convivial society should be designed to allow all its members the most autonomous action by means of tools least controlled by others.}{\textit{Ivan Illich}, \textnormal{Tools for Conviviality} \parencite{illich1973}}

In orthodox software engineering, an enterprise initiates development by defining a Minimum Viable Product (MVP)---the smallest collection of digital features required to extract capital, capture attention, or validate market demand. In the cybernetic, physical architecture of The Sovereign Stack, we reject this logic at its root. We do not manufacture consumer products, nor do we assemble disposable digital applications. We cultivate durable, autopoietic human settlements capable of weathering ecological collapse, resource depletion, and political entropy. Therefore, we define a \textit{Minimum Viable Society (MVS)}.

The Minimum Viable Society represents the baseline ensemble of thermodynamic, ecological, and sociological workflows that an autonomous settlement must master to secure collective human autonomy. If a community cannot feed its members without industrial monoculture supply chains, cannot repair its tools without proprietary manufacturer licenses, cannot arbitrate disputes without state carceral violence, cannot coordinate trade across watersheds without central banks or speculative blockchains, and cannot care for its young and old without predatory financial extraction, it is not sovereign. It remains an enclave of consumers masquerading as a community, entirely vulnerable to the first disruption of the legacy grid.

Yet true sovereignty can never be attained by an isolated, atomized eco-commune. A solitary homestead or single village cannot smelt its own copper, weave its own sailcloth, compound its own antibiotics, and manufacture its own photovoltaic inverters. Bunker survivalism and pastoral escapism are fragile illusions. Real civilizational resilience emerges only when sovereign nodes form a \textit{horizontal, polycentric bioregional federation}---sharing specialized tooling, seasonal food surpluses, structural timber, pyrometallurgical casting capacity, traveling apprentices, and emergency life support across watershed and river basin corridors without relying on fiat currency, centralized clearinghouses, or corporate supply chains.

To systematically conquer these survival imperatives, The Collective has formulated twenty-four canonical scenarios, designated from Alpha ($\alpha$) through Omega ($\omega$). Each scenario represents an empirical stress-test of the seven-layer architecture within an active federated mesh. Rather than presenting an arbitrary catalogue of technological features, this chapter organizes these twenty-four scenarios into five developmental acts that trace the organic maturation of a federated settlement:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Act I: The Metabolism of Everyday Life} (Scenarios $\alpha$ through $\varepsilon$): Securing the baseline metabolic foundation---mechanical repair, shared mobility, collective nourishment, mutual aid childcare, and non-commercial third places.
    \item \textbf{Act II: Ecological Guilds \& Bioregional Commons} (Scenarios $\zeta$ through $\kappa$): Expanding horizons through regenerative silviculture, secondary metallurgy, open-source science, somatic craft transmission, and reciprocal hospitality waystations.
    \item \textbf{Act III: Cultural Resilience \& Collective Governance} (Scenarios $\lambda$ through $o$): Achieving material closure and social immunity---celebratory acoustics, thermophilic pyrolysis, circular textiles, reciprocal timber framing, and the kinship web of trust.
    \item \textbf{Act IV: Specialized \& Crisis Coordination} (Scenarios $\pi$ through $\upsilon$): Navigating vulnerability, acute stress, and collective defense---trade guild solidarity, adversarial shielding, caregiver burnout defense, newcomer genesis onboarding, and sub-16ms microgrid islanding.
    \item \textbf{Act V: Systemic Decoupling \& Transition Horizons} (Scenarios $\phi$ through $\omega$): Maturing into full autopoiesis---intergenerational elder dignity, paramedic trauma mesh, restorative justice circles, and terminal post-fiat corporate dissolution.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The Philosophy of Scenario-Driven Development}
\label{sec:scdd_philosophy}

Civilizations cannot be ``launched'' from a whiteboard; they must be lived and practiced. Historically, intentional communities and utopian experiments have foundered not on the poverty of their ideals, but on the unyielding friction of administrative weariness: who sweeps the kitchen, whose turn it is to split the cordwood, how much water remains in the cistern, how to clear trade balances with neighboring valleys without cash, and how to resolve a bitter dispute between founding neighbors without calling armed agents of the state \parencite{kanter1972}.

\subsection{The Shadow Simulator: Rehearsing Reality in Oasis}
To bridge the chasm between social philosophy and thermodynamic reality, The Sovereign Stack employs \textit{Scenario-Driven Development (ScDD)}. ScDD adapts the disciplines of Test-Driven Development \parencite{beck2002test} and Behavior-Driven Development \parencite{north2006introducing} to the physical laws of nature. Before a single foundation trench is dug, before timber is felled, and before kilowatt-hours are allocated, each scenario is subjected to exhaustive empirical simulation within \textit{Oasis}---the discrete-event, data-oriented C++20 simulation engine developed alongside this architecture.

Oasis functions as the \textit{Shadow Simulator}. Operating upon a $32^3$ spatial voxel substrate populated by memory-buffered human agents governed by psychological drives, stress accumulations, and social affinities, Oasis rehearses the failure modes of human togetherness under strict physical constraints. Crucially, Oasis models not merely intra-node agent interactions, but also inter-node network meshing over the Reticulum Network Stack (RNS) and LoRa packet radios. It stress-tests Bioregional Path-Vector Protocol (BPVP) route propagation across river basins, channel liquidity exhaustion in Pairwise Bilateral Clearing Channels (BCC), credit window deadlocks under seasonal droughts, and multi-hop transport delays over rugged topography.

Yet Oasis is not a video game, nor does it issue top-down commands to living humans. The simulation serves strictly as a rehearsal space. When a scenario passes its six acceptance gates (G1 through G6) in the digital twin, the underlying Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN 2.0) state machines, W3C JSON-LD semantic ontologies, and safety envelopes are compiled directly to bare-metal POSIX daemons (\texttt{col-telemetryd}, \texttt{col-storaged}, \texttt{col-execd}, \texttt{col-kmsd}, \texttt{col-commonsd}, and \texttt{col-adversaryd}) running on local edge nodes embedded in the physical settlement. The simulation designs the harness; human hands do the work.

\subsection{The Three Core Invariants: Adjacency, Sanctuary, and Horizontal Federation}
Across all twenty-four scenarios, the implementation of the Sovereign Stack is governed by three immutable architectural laws established in Chapters 1, 4, and 6:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Strict Layer Adjacency and Encapsulation:} A layer communicates exclusively with its directly adjacent neighbors over authenticated Unix domain stream sockets under \texttt{/run/collective/ipc/}:
    \[
    \mathrm{L1} \longleftrightarrow \mathrm{L2} \longleftrightarrow \mathrm{L3} \longleftrightarrow \mathrm{L4} \longleftrightarrow \mathrm{L5} \longleftrightarrow \mathrm{L6} \longleftrightarrow \mathrm{L7}
    \]
    There are no shortcuts, backdoors, or administrative bypasses. Physical nature in Layer~1 is sovereign: it consists purely of physical matter, tools, mass, and energy. It contains \textbf{strictly zero software, zero firmware, zero microcontrollers, zero cryptographic keys, and zero network routing}. Layer~2 executes the cybernetic membrane on real-time microcontrollers (Zephyr RTOS), housing all analog-to-digital converters, bus transceivers, and hardware secure elements (Microchip ATECC608A / OPTIGA Trust M). The Thermodynamic Ledger in Layer~3 knows only Joules, kilograms, and physical demurrage; it holds no fiat currency, runs no speculative trading pairs, and never executes arbitrary user-facing business logic. The Deterministic Orchestrator in Layer~4 schedules workflows within metered Wasm fuel limits over \texttt{l3\_l4.sock} and \texttt{l4\_l5.sock}. Layer~5 manages polycentric governance without touching physical relays. Layer~6 translates human intent into W3C JSON-LD semantics over \texttt{l5\_l6.sock}. Finally, Layer~7 operates as an outward-facing legal and commercial carapace over \texttt{l6\_l7.sock}, interfacing with the legacy state while keeping internal mesh state completely blinded.

    \item \textbf{The Convivial Boundary and Non-Steward Sanctuary:} Technology must never become a barrier to human dignity \parencite{illich1973}. A sovereign node is not an insular guild of computer scientists. Elders, young children, individuals recovering from illness or burnout, and refugees from the legacy economy are full citizens endowed with equal standing and an unconditional claim on survival. No resident is ever required to manage private cryptographic keys, execute terminal commands, or interact with a glowing screen to receive nourishment, warmth, medical attention, or shelter. The cybernetic system functions as a calm, ambient substrate \parencite{weiser1996calm}---absorbing administrative friction so that human beings can encounter one another face-to-face.

    \item \textbf{Horizontal Federation and Zero-Sum Conservation:} Sovereign settlements never trade through centralized clearinghouses, commercial banking corridors, or planetary speculative blockchains. Inter-node trade executes strictly across \textbf{Pairwise Bilateral Clearing Channels (BCC)} established between peering nodes $u$ and $v$, governed by the zero-sum conservation invariant:
    \[
    B_{u \to v}(t) + B_{v \to u}(t) = 0
    \]
    bounded by negotiated mutual aid credit windows $[-C_{u \to v}, +C_{v \to u}]$. Multi-hop logistics across river basins route value atomically through chained Hashed Timelock Contracts (HTLCs), where value clears in a cascading backward wave the instant the recipient's hardware confirms physical delivery via a cryptographic delivery receipt ($\sigma_{\mathrm{delivery}}$). Local capability discovery propagates via the Bioregional Path-Vector Protocol (BPVP), enforcing loop-freedom and the Export Surplus Bound ($Q_{\mathrm{avail}}$) to guarantee that local survival baselines ($B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$) are fully satisfied before resources leave the watershed.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The Living Repository vs. The Printed Page}
The twenty-four scenario specifications codified in the project's living code repositories encompass hundreds of kilobytes of formal C++ ECS component definitions, W3C JSON-LD schema contexts, Protobuf wire formats, and STRIDE threat matrices. It would be a profound category error to reduce this volume to a static printout of rapidly evolving code. 

Instead, this chapter serves as the authoritative architectural, sociological, and narrative blueprint. For each scenario, we dissect the human dilemma it resolves, describe the lived sensory experience of the stewards within the node and across the bioregional federation, and trace the unbroken seven-layer traversal executed by the cybernetic substrate. Readers seeking raw code schemas, unit test suites, and hardware bills-of-materials are directed to the project's living Git and IPLD repositories, where the specifications are continuously maintained and debated by the international community of builders.

\subsection{Scenario Alpha (\texorpdfstring{$\alpha$}{Alpha}): The Fabrication Commons}

\paragraph{Movement 1: The Human Dilemma \& Legacy Friction.}
On any suburban street in the industrialized world, fifty closed garage doors conceal fifty identical plastic carrying cases, each housing an 18-volt cordless power drill. Over its entire mechanical lifespan, each drill will be operated for an average of barely twelve minutes. This is the quiet thermodynamic tragedy of atomized consumerism: millions of tons of high-grade copper, neodymium, and impact-resistant polymers are extracted, refined, shipped across oceans, and locked behind private deadbolts, while the households that purchased them work overtime at alienating jobs to service the mortgage debt on the garages that house them. When an everyday household appliance breaks---a plastic pump impeller in a washing machine or a nylon bracket on a bicycle---the manufacturer withholds the technical schematic, obsoletes the part, and forces another landfill deposit and another credit-card charge. Atomized ownership does not grant freedom; it enforces collective helplessness.

\paragraph{Movement 2: What the Steward Sees \& Experiences.}
At the corner of the block, a reclaimed municipal utility barn has been transformed into the Fabrication Commons. The rider drops off a wooden crate of extruded recycled PETG filament pellets in exchange for a precision-milled carbide cutter sharpened in the workshop. The exchange is logged by a quiet near-field chime. At no point does the neighbor confront a command-line interface or manage cryptographic tokens. For passive retrieval of finished parts, a simple wooden key fob containing an embedded passive NFC coil suffices: she taps it against locker bay B-03, a brass latch clicks open, and she retrieves a freshly printed, food-grade impeller cured overnight during the solar generation peak. If she operates the three-axis CNC mill herself, she does so under \textit{Apprentice Mode}: the machinist slots his mentor card into the console alongside hers, dual-energizing the machine relays. She leaves with a functioning appliance, having incurred zero fiat debt and experienced the dignity of collaborative repair.
