# Volume 1, Chapter 3: 5-Agent Multi-Disciplinary Critique Guide
**Target File:** `doc/book_collective/chapters/03_layer_2_digital_twin.tex`

## Objective
To subject Chapter 3 ("Layer 2: The Digital Twin") to a massive, multi-disciplinary stress test. Because Layer 2 is the exact threshold where physical reality (dirt, flesh, electricity) converts into cybernetic data (telemetry, actuation), it requires rigorous critique from multiple distinct domains to ensure it doesn't fail technically, ecologically, or psychologically.

## The Orchestration (5 Agents)

### Agent 1: Natural Sciences Expert (Physics & Electronics)
*   **Persona:** A master of physical instrumentation, thermodynamics, and salvage electronics.
*   **Task:** Critique the physical mechanics of the sensors and actuators. Are the descriptions of thermocouples, current shunts, and solenoids grounded in reality? Is the distinction between Deterministic and Inferred sensoring scientifically sound? Defend the physical integrity of the hardware layer against abstract software assumptions.

### Agent 2: Human Sciences Expert (Medicine, Psychology & Sociology)
*   **Persona:** A physician and sociologist focused on the human experience within cybernetic systems.
*   **Task:** Critique the human element of Layer 2. How does the system handle "bio-metric sensing"? What is the psychological impact on a citizen who acts as an "actuator" (doing the physical labor the machine cannot do)? Ensure the text fiercely defends human dignity and algorithmically prevents burnout and surveillance trauma.

### Agent 3: Sovereign Stack Architect
*   **Persona:** The absolute enforcer of the Sovereign Stack architecture.
*   **Task:** Critique the structural routing. Defend the **Strict Adjacency** rule: Layer 2 can *only* interface downwards to Layer 1 (Physical Reality) and upwards to Layer 3 (The Ledger). If the text suggests Layer 2 is talking directly to the Semantic Interface (Layer 6) or the Orchestrator (Layer 4) without passing through the Ledger, flag it as a critical failure.

### Agent 4: C++ and Game Architect (Oasis Engine)
*   **Persona:** A performance-obsessed C++ engine architect.
*   **Task:** Critique how the Oasis Engine *simulates* Layer 2. The game does not ingest telemetry; it *produces* it. Players install virtual sensors and actuators in their voxel landscape, and the engine generates massive streams of simulated Layer 2 telemetry. This simulated data is plugged directly into the real Layer 3 protocols (tagged as simulation data) to safely stress-test the actual network. Ensure the text accurately reflects this outward flow of virtual telemetry from the C++ engine into the broader architecture.

### Agent 5: The Lead Author (Mediator & Writer)
*   **Persona:** The definitive voice of the Sovereign Stack.
*   **Task:** Synthesize the 4 distinct expert critiques. Remove repetitive bullet points and rigid structures, weaving the mechanics into mature, flowing, authoritative prose. Execute the final rewrite of `03_layer_2_digital_twin.tex`.

## Explicit User Mandate
The text must maturely handle the intersection of these four domains. It cannot sound like a disjointed collection of facts. The transition from physical dirt (Expert 1) to human psychology (Expert 2), routed through strict architecture (Expert 3), and finally rendered into the digital twin (Expert 4), must feel like a unified, inevitable engineering canon.
