# Volume 1, Chapter 1: Drift Review Council Guide
**Target File:** `doc/book_collective/chapters/01_anatomy_of_a_node.tex`
**Reference chapters (settled):** `chapters/02_*` through `chapters/08_layer_7_legacy.tex`

## Objective
Chapter 1 was written before Chapters 2–8 went through their expert councils, and parts of it have drifted from what those chapters now establish. This review has three aims:
1. Bring Ch1 back into agreement with the settled architecture.
2. Keep it **narrative**. Ch1 is the one place in Volume 1 where a narrative, persuasive voice belongs. It should invite and orient the reader, but never promise what later chapters withdraw.
3. Preserve its depth and its intellectual lineage.

## Register for Ch1 (different from Ch2–8)
- Narrative and persuasive prose is welcome: story, image, argument, a human voice.
- No overclaiming. Every promise made here must be honoured by the later chapters.
- Prefer flowing prose over spec lists. The seven-layer overview may stay compact, but should not read as a datasheet.
- No bold pseudo-headings. Use real (sub)sections.
- Cite only real, verifiable sources. Epigraphs and attributed quotes must be genuine or clearly marked as paraphrase.

## Settled decisions Ch1 must not contradict (from Ch2–8)
- **Strict Adjacency:** each layer talks only to its neighbours.
- **L2:** measurement and actuation; actuation happens only within ratified envelopes.
- **L3:**
  - Exergy Credits are minted only by L3 under Proof-of-Thermodynamic-Work and are subject to demurrage.
  - There is no exchange rate with fiat.
  - The hardware root of trust and the physical web of trust are part of L3.
  - Check Ch4 for its exact consensus/CRDT and ERC claims.
- **L4:**
  - Executes ratified workflows inside actuation envelopes. Never mints credits.
  - Priority order: floors and baseline → standing commitments → ordinary workflows → enhancements → export, which is last and curtailed first.
  - Biological time; the unconditional survival baseline; ecological floors.
- **L5:**
  - Meta-recursive governance with two chambers and a Genesis Compact.
  - Three rule tiers.
  - Private, verifiable voting; pairwise identifiers. **No surveillance graph of who trusts whom.**
  - Restorative-first sanctions; the Protocol of Refusal; hearings for non-members.
  - L5 enforces nothing itself.
  - Reputational Wealth cannot be transferred.
- **L6:**
  - Draft → deterministic read-back → human signs. The machine never signs.
  - Safety alerts use templates only.
  - Calm technology; screenless access; unmediated spaces.
  - Separate intake and drafting models. Legacy input is untrusted.
  - "The only interface humanity needs" is withdrawn.
- **L7:**
  - Dual role of protection and extraction. **Export is a residual, never a target.**
  - Fiat is raised only against a ratified needs list, with stop rules.
  - Success means declining dependence on the legacy economy.
  - A lawful cooperative entity. The "mask" and "trick" framing is retired, and "parasitic", "toxic" and "Trojan Horse" are retired.
  - "Leeching" is kept but defined precisely: the Node declines the logic of accumulation and takes nothing dishonestly.
  - Designed to shrink (Severance Protocol).
- **Oasis:**
  - A shared interface with distinct modes.
  - It *produces* simulated telemetry, tagged as simulation. It does not ingest live telemetry.
  - Real and simulated contexts are unmistakably distinct.
  - The "game that becomes the Orchestrator" framing needs checking against Ch7.
- **Humans stay in the loop.** The Stack supports judgement; it does not replace it.

  - "absorb this interpersonal friction", "un-gameable protocols", "zero cognitive overhead".
  - "the citizens… simply watch the water flow".
  - "delegating the mechanics of survival to a… deeply ethical intelligence".

  - "localized biometrics".

  - "capitalist mask to trick", "actively parasitic", "dressing those intents up".
