## Objective
Stress-test Chapter 8 ("Layer 7: The Legacy Proxy"). Layer 7 is the outermost layer and the only one that faces the legacy world. It has a **dual role**:
1. **Protection (ablative shield):** legal wrappers for the Node and for individual Stewards, regulatory and certification parallels, and insulation of internal records and processes from legacy pressure.
2. **Extraction (leeching):** selling services into the legacy market to obtain the fiat the Node cannot yet do without (hardware, medical equipment, legacy debts).

## The User's Governing Principle (non-negotiable)
**Export is a residual, never a target.** The amount of exergy the community is willing to export is dictated by the layers below, never by Layer 7 or by market demand:
- physical availability (L3)
- minus ecological floors, the unconditional survival baseline, standing commitments, and the human capacity ceiling (L4/L5, per Ch5–6)
- = an **export ceiling**, ratified in L5, which reaches L7 only as instructions via L6.

Fiat is raised only against a ratified list of needs and stops when those needs are met. Success is measured by **declining dependence** on the legacy economy, not by revenue. Extraction must structurally be prevented from becoming an end in itself.
