# Worldbuilding Continuity Report

## 1. The Polycentric Mesh vs. Isolated Commune
In Chapter 4, a major worldbuilding shift establishes that the Collective is part of a sprawling, deeply networked Polycentric Bioregional Commonwealth (e.g., relying on high-altitude cooperatives and industrial syndicates). However, Chapters 1-3 mistakenly portray the settlement as a completely isolated, lone commune. 

**Inconsistencies to Rewrite in Chapters 1-3:**
*   **Chapter 1:** Portrays the community as an isolated "autarkic experiment." The narrative focuses strictly on the Genesis SPC node standing alone against the legacy world. The text heavily emphasizes local self-reliance without acknowledging the broader supply chains of the Commonwealth.
*   **Chapter 2:** Focuses on the "Genesis Node" and its immediate perimeter. The integration of newcomers is framed entirely around the local node's survival, with no mention of the broader bioregional network. 
*   **Chapter 3:** Silas and Elara's dialogue heavily reinforces the isolated commune trope. Silas says, "we just summon it from the dirt," and "We aren't outsourcing the entropy to someone else's backyard." They describe themselves as a single "pocket of density," failing to acknowledge the massive, redundant network of other hubs that Chapter 4 relies upon.

**Recommendation:** Inject references to the Polycentric Bioregional Commonwealth into the early chapters. Show (rather than hide until Chapter 4) that the local node is constantly routing resources, information, and mutual aid with other specialized hubs across the bioregion. 

## 2. The Sovereign Stack (Layer 1-7 Consistency)
The treatment of the Sovereign Stack in the early chapters is mostly consistent with the expanded architecture in Chapters 5+. 
*   **Chapter 1:** Mentions an "L5 policy gate" dealing with "trust ring attestations." This perfectly aligns with Layer 5 functioning as the meta-governance/trust layer. Layer 7 is accurately portrayed as the bureaucratic, legal interface with the legacy world ("Layer 7 defense seminars").
*   **Chapter 3:** Describes a "Layer 7 breach attempt" (municipal code-enforcement sweep), which remains consistent with Layer 7 as the external legal/presentation layer. 
*   **Recommendation:** No major structural rewrites needed for the Stack in Chapters 1-4, as the conceptual architecture holds up.

## 3. Explicit Greek Letters and Systemic Underscores
The directive explicitly forbids the use of Greek letters (e.g., "Scenario Alpha") and systemic underscores (e.g., "Layer_5"). 

**Inconsistencies to Rewrite (Greek Letters):**
*   **Chapter 1:** "Gamma kitchen"
*   **Chapter 2:** "Alpha Fabrication shop", "Nu Textiles", "Eta Coppice woods", "Zeta micro-lodgings"
*   **Chapter 3:** "Mu biochar bins", "Alpha extrusion hoppers"

*(Note: Greek letters were also found in later chapters during the audit, including "Nu textile commons" in Chapter 10, "Alpha-7" in Chapter 11, and "Scenario Omega" in Chapter 17. These will also need to be scrubbed).*

**Systemic Underscores:** 
*   A scan of the chapters confirms that no systemic underscores (like "Layer_5") slipped into the text. They are correctly formatted as "Layer 7" or "L5".
