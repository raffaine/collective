# Character Continuity Report

## Overview
This report details continuity issues regarding character arcs, timelines, and relationships across the manuscript of *Fault Tolerance*. Specifically, it highlights discrepancies found in Chapters 1-4 that need to be rewritten to align with the events and character states in the later chapters.

## 1. Gabe, Maya, & Clara
- **Current State in Later Chapters**: In Chapter 16, Gabe (still age 16) and his partner Maya welcome their new baby daughter, Clara. Gabe mentions that they "bonded last year."
- **Inconsistencies in Chapters 1-4**: While Gabe's age is consistent (he is introduced as 16 in Chapter 1), Maya is completely absent from the first four chapters. There is no mention of their relationship or the pregnancy. *(Note: The structural outline originally pegged Clara as a baby sister, but Chapter 16 explicitly writes her as Gabe and Maya's daughter).*
- **Required Rewrite (Ch 1-4)**: Maya must be introduced in the early chapters as Gabe's partner. Their bond and the impending birth of Clara need to be seeded early to make the events of Chapter 16 continuous and believable.

## 2. Elara & Silas
- **Current State in Later Chapters**: The structural outline dictates that Elara and Silas simmer in "lukewarm water" for the first 11 chapters, maintaining guarded, trauma-bonded personalities before their romance finally ignites in Chapter 12.
- **Inconsistencies in Chapters 1-4**: The romantic tension is far too overt early on. In Chapters 3 and 4, they are already displaying deep romantic gravity and physical intimacy (e.g., holding hands, Silas brushing his lips against her temple).
- **Required Rewrite (Ch 1-4)**: The physical affection and overt romance in Chapters 3 and 4 must be significantly toned down. Their early interactions should be rewritten to reflect professional, guarded mutual respect and slow-burn tension, saving the romantic breakthrough for Chapter 12.

## 3. Kael & Jax
- **Current State in Later Chapters**: Kael and Jax (consistently male) arrive in Chapter 17 from their nomadic travels, having spread the mesh across the continent.
- **Inconsistencies in Chapters 1-4**: While Kael is briefly mentioned in Elara's Chapter 2 flashback, Jax is entirely missing from the early narrative. Furthermore, their ongoing mythic nomadic travels are not seeded at all in the first four chapters.
- **Required Rewrite (Ch 1-4)**: Jax must be established alongside Kael in the early text. The narrative should explicitly seed their mythic status and their current nomadic journey to properly set up their urgent return in Chapter 17.

## 4. Bram
- **Current State in Later Chapters**: The authorial intent is for Bram to be a wise elder whose arc ends in death and composting. 
- **Inconsistencies in Chapters 1-4**: Bram is completely absent from the first four chapters, meaning his presence and wisdom as an elder are not established. *(Note: The manuscript has major identity continuity issues with Bram beyond Chapter 4. In Chapter 5, Bram is introduced as a female delivery driver. In Chapter 14, the elder who passes away and is composted is named "Olander." In Chapters 16 and 17, Bram is alive as a male recovering addict turning compost.)*
- **Required Rewrite (Ch 1-4)**: Bram must be introduced in Chapters 1-4 as a respected community elder. His wisdom and presence need to be established early to give emotional weight to his intended arc (and his character identity/name needs to be standardized across the rest of the manuscript, replacing "Olander" in Ch 14).
