\epigraph{A community is tested not when everyone agrees at the harvest feast, but at midnight when a survivor needs an immediate locked door of her own, when a dissenter refuses the majority's gaze, or when harm must be healed without building a prison.}{---Adapted from \textcite{hirschman1970} and \textcite{zehr1990}}

High-trust intentional communities face an intimate hazard that corporate platforms never confront:
when people share kitchens, workshops, child pods, and land trusts, informal social pressure can
quietly curdle into moralistic conformity, intrusive gossip, trapped domestic partnerships, or
vindictive ostracism \parencite{freeman1972,mansbridge1980}. Conversely, technological systems that
attempt to ``automate trust'' through ubiquitous cameras, LiDAR scans, and algorithmic slashing
recreate \textcite{zuboff2019}'s surveillance panopticon inside the very sanctuary meant to
replace it.

Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}) defends personal autonomy and communal peace simultaneously through
four constitutional institutions: (1)~the \textbf{\texttt{Protocol of Refusal} Asymmetric Ratchet},
the \textbf{``Custom Shirt Rule,''} and the \textbf{Constitutional Anti-Moralism Firewall};
(2)~unilateral sub-$500\,\mathrm{ms}$ \textbf{\texttt{SafeHarborSeverance}} with zero-knowledge
spatial co-scheduling blind-spots; (3)~\textbf{Transformative Justice Peace Circles}
\parencite{zehr1990} paired with systemic root-cause inquiry; and (4)~\textbf{Autopoietic Immune
Defense} replacing punitive ``social slashing'' with non-carceral \textbf{Passive Quarantine} and
hardware-enforced $72$-hour emergency sunset timers.

\section{Intimate Sanctuary, the Protocol of Refusal, and Anti-Moralism}
\label{sec:ch7-protocol-of-refusal}

In the Sovereign Stack, legibility is strictly graduated across trust rings: public Layer~3
accounting illuminates \textit{shared} guild tools and watershed flows while keeping the $R_0$
intimate hearth completely dark to telemetry. Four structural mechanisms enforce this boundary
(Table~\ref{tab:ch7-sanctuary-firewalls}):

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The \texttt{Protocol of Refusal} Asymmetric Ratchet:} Enacted by an ordinary
    majority ($> 5{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$) of the affected hearth, pod, or indigenous stewardship circle
    yet requiring a Meta-Rule supermajority ($6{,}600\,\mathrm{bp}$) plus a $30$-day cooling-off
    window to lift, the \texttt{Protocol of Refusal} physically de-energizes microphones/cameras
    via hardware relays and excises protected domains from Layer~3 ledgers, Oasis \texttt{L1b}
    digital twins, and BPVP advertisements. Protected domains include $R_0$ bedrooms and living
    hearths ($\mathtt{interior\_surveillance\_sensors} = 0$, $\mathtt{r0\_lidar\_scans} = 0$ in
    Scenarios~Zeta and~Xi), sacred grief vigils and ceremonies (Scenario~Lambda), indigenous
    ecological knowledge and medicinal seeds \parencite{kimmerer2013} (Scenario~Iota), and
    old-growth Mother Trees (Scenario~Eta).
    \item \textbf{The ``Custom Shirt Rule'' (Scenario~Nu):} Layer~5 bifurcates $R_2$ guild assets
    (looms, CNC mills, insulated PPE) from $R_0$ personal belongings (tailored garments, boots,
    journals, musical instruments). The instant a personal item is handed off to a resident,
    \texttt{col-kmsd} marks $\mathtt{non\_requisitionable} = \mathsf{true}$ and executes a
    cryptographic purge of its public Layer~3 inventory record ($\mathtt{retained\_body\_scan\_bytes} = 0$,
    $\mathtt{rfid\_garment\_tags} = 0$). No assembly may ever requisition a person's coat or violin.
    \item \textbf{Autonomous \texttt{No-Transit} Spatial Polygons (Scenario~Beta):} Constituent
    spatial polities draw signed polygon boundaries over \texttt{Delta} nurseries, quiet \texttt{Zeta}
    courtyards, \texttt{Iota} optical labs, and \texttt{Eta} wildlife corridors. \texttt{col-kmsd}
    blocks all autonomous rover and heavy-freight routing through these polygons, permitting entry
    \textit{solely} under manual human-steered \texttt{Chi}/\texttt{Upsilon} life-safety overrides.
    \item \textbf{The Constitutional Anti-Moralism Firewall (Scenario~Omicron):} Any governance
    proposal attempting to police consensual adult romantic, queer, trans, polyamorous, or chosen
    kinship structures---or private $R_0/R_1$ contemplative botanical exploration---is rejected at
    the Layer~5 schema membrane with \texttt{SCHEMA\_VIOLATION\_MORALISTIC\_OVERREACH}. Only the
    objective \textbf{Three-Rule Sustainable Social Agreement} is enforceable: (i)~acoustic curfews
    ($\le 450\,\mathrm{cB}$ night quiet), (ii)~\texttt{Iota} HPTLC chemical purity verification
    ($\mathtt{toxic\_adulterant\_mass\_mg} = 0$), and (iii)~strict sobriety interlocks before
    operating \texttt{Kappa} high-energy machinery or clinical shifts.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Unilateral \texttt{SafeHarborSeverance} and Survivor Protection}
\label{sec:ch7-safe-harbor}

\textcite{hirschman1970} demonstrated that ``Voice'' inside any institution becomes hollow coercion
unless ``Exit'' is materially costless and physically safe. In historical communes, leaving a
marriage, a polycule, or a household often meant losing one's roof, food security, or community
standing---or worse, being pressured into ``couples mediation'' with an abusive partner.

Layer~5 (\texttt{SCN-OMICRON-KIN} and \texttt{SCN-PSI-MEDIATION}) codifies an absolute
constitutional prohibition against mandatory mediation in any case involving domestic coercion,
stalking, or intimate harm, providing instead a unilateral, no-fault \textbf{\texttt{SafeHarborSeverance}}
opcode executed in $< 500\,\mathrm{ms}$ from a single hardware key tap:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Sub-$500\,\mathrm{ms}$ Cryptographic Door \& Key Rotation:} \texttt{col-kmsd}
    immediately revokes shared smart-lock HMAC keys on the severed threshold and provisions an
    independent private \texttt{Zeta} sanctuary suite with autonomous keys.
    \item \textbf{Zero-Knowledge Spatial Routing Blind-Spot:} Rather than publishing a humiliating
    gossip notice explaining \textit{why} two residents separated, \texttt{col-kmsd} installs a
    pairwise blind-spot constraint inside its \texttt{0x0503 L5\_GRANT\_CLAIM\_HANDLE} filter
    (Figure~\ref{fig:ch7-safe-harbor-justice}). Whenever Steward~$A$ holds an active claim in
    workshop bay or kitchen shift $Z$ during window $[t_1, t_2]$, overlapping shift offers in $Z$
    are silently omitted from Steward~$B$'s eligible feed (and vice versa), guaranteeing zero
    forced physical co-location without leaking private history to \texttt{col-execd} or neighbors.
    \item \textbf{Unconditional $B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$ and Zero Slashing:} Both parties retain
    $10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($100\%$) of their nutritional, thermal, clinical, and housing baseline,
    $100\%$ of their $R_0$ personal belongings, and $100\%$ of their earned domain credentials.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{theorem}[Zero Forced Co-Location and Zero Gossip Leakage Under ZK Spatial Severance]
\label{thm:ch7-zk-severance}
Let $(u, v)$ be a pair of stewards bound by an active \texttt{SafeHarborSeverance} record inside
\texttt{col-kmsd}'s encrypted SQLCipher vault, and let $\mathcal{S}$ be the set of spatial task
offers generated by \texttt{col-execd}. Under \texttt{col-kmsd}'s pairwise exclusion filter over
\texttt{0x0503} and \texttt{0x0606}: (1)~for every physical zone $z$ and time interval $[t_a, t_b]$,
$\mathrm{Claimed}(u, z, [t_a, t_b]) \land \mathrm{Claimed}(v, z, [t_a, t_b]) = \mathsf{false}$; and
(2)~\texttt{col-execd} observes only unlinkable 24-byte \texttt{ClaimHandle} PRF tokens and learns
zero bits regarding the existence of $(u, v)$'s severance record.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every voluntary task claim traverses \texttt{col-kmsd} serially on its single-threaded governance
state machine before issuing a signed \texttt{0x0503 L5\_GRANT\_CLAIM\_HANDLE} frame. When steward
$u$ acquires a claim on $(z, [t_a, t_b])$, \texttt{col-kmsd} records $(z, [t_a, t_b])$ against $u$'s
vault-internal index. Any subsequent eligibility projection (\texttt{0x0606}) or claim attempt by
$v$ on an overlapping $(z, [t_c, t_d])$ with $\max(t_a, t_c) < \min(t_b, t_d)$ fails the internal
disjointness predicate prior to token minting, establishing~(1). Because \texttt{col-execd} never
receives rejected local pull candidates and sees accepted claims solely as pseudorandom tokens
$\mathrm{PRF}_{K_{L5}}(\mathtt{did} \parallel \mathtt{task\_id} \parallel \mathtt{nonce})$
indistinguishable from uniform 192-bit strings, \texttt{col-execd}'s view is computationally
independent of the severance table, proving~(2).
\end{proof}

\section{Transformative Justice Peace Circles and Systemic Root-Cause Inquiry}
\label{sec:ch7-transformative-justice}

When non-intimate harm or ecological negligence occurs---for instance, an apprentice bypasses a
scrubber interlock or greywater overflows into a creek---punitive states respond by caging bodies
or levying fines, while naive reputation protocols broadcast permanent digital scarlet letters.
Layer~5 (\texttt{SCN-PSI-MEDIATION}) abolishes carceral cages, survival-floor fines, and public
shaming pillories ($\mathtt{retributive\_vengeance\_or\_cages\_count} \equiv 0$) in favor of
\textcite{zehr1990}'s \textbf{Transformative Justice}:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Systemic Root-Cause Inquiry Before Individual Blame:} Before drafting any
    restitution covenant, the Peace Circle queries Oasis \texttt{L1b} operational context to ask
    \textit{how the system set the human up to fail}: Was the steward past Scenario~Sigma's
    $144{,}000\,\mathrm{s}$ ($40\,\mathrm{h}$) fatigue ceiling? Did Scenario~Kappa fail to provide
    a clear checklist? Was a Scenario~Alpha latch poorly designed? Fixing the underlying workflow
    defect takes precedence over blaming the individual.
    \item \textbf{Rights of the Living Watershed \& 3-of-3 NFC Covenants:} When soil, water, or
    forest commons suffer harm, a VRF-selected ecological guardian represents the watershed in the
    Peace Circle alongside the affected parties and facilitator. Restorative covenants (for example,
    replanting riparian willow stakes or completing supervised \texttt{L0} safety refresher hours)
    activate via a 3-of-3 physical NFC tap and auto-seal upon completion.
    \item \textbf{Defensive Federal Arbitration Shield (\texttt{9 USC 3}):} Ratified Peace Circle
    covenants bind a private-law arbitration clause under the Federal Arbitration Act (\texttt{9 U.S.C.\ \S\,3}),
    obligating external courts to stay opportunistic civil litigation while strictly maintaining
    \textit{zero} outbound calls to armed state police ($\mathtt{sheriff\_enforcement\_calls} = 0$).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Autopoietic Immunity Without Autoimmune Paranoia}
\label{sec:ch7-autopoietic-immunity}

Early drafts of decentralized governance protocols often obsess over ``Sybil slashing'' until the
remedy becomes worse than the disease: a corroded thermocouple or drifting flowmeter gets misread
as ``Byzantine fraud,'' automatically slashing an honest neighbor's reputation and cutting off
their heat. Scenario~Rho (\texttt{SCN-RHO-ADVERSARY}) cures this autoimmune pathology through four
constitutional immune guardrails:

\begin{invariant}[Passive Quarantine and ``Fault Before Malice'' Physics Cross-Check]
\label{inv:ch7-passive-quarantine}
Recursive punitive ``Social Slashing'' is permanently abolished across Layer~5. When a telemetry
anomaly arises, \texttt{col-kmsd} enforces \textbf{Fault Before Malice}: an anomaly is classified
as a benign hardware maintenance ticket (Scenario~Alpha) unless independent multi-sensor conservation
laws fail simultaneously ($\|P_{\mathrm{elec\_mw}} - \dot{Q}_{\mathrm{thermal\_mw}}\| > \epsilon_{\mathrm{fault}}$
across distinct Enclave-signed transducers). Even under confirmed external malice or Sybil cartel
attack, \texttt{col-kmsd} executes \textbf{Passive Quarantine}---severing discretionary BCC credit
extensions and high-hazard actuation while preserving $10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($100\%$) of $R_0$
survival baselines ($\mathtt{r0\_survival\_baseline\_severed} \equiv \mathsf{false}$).
\end{invariant}

Finally, to guarantee that an acute wildfire, siege, or grid collapse (\texttt{Upsilon}/\texttt{Rho})
never mutates into a permanent dictatorship, every emergency posture in \texttt{col-kmsd} is
governed by a hardcoded \textbf{$259{,}200\,\mathrm{s}$ ($72$-hour) Hardware Sunset Timer}. Unless
re-ratified by a physical live quorum of the Commons Chamber after $72$~hours, emergency overrides
expire deterministically in silicon, restoring ordinary bicameral law.
