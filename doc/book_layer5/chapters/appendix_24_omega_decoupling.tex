\chapter{Appendix 24: Scenario Omega - Terminal Decoupling}

\section{The Legacy Paradigm \& Its Failures}
During the Genesis phase, Layer 7 proxy corporations are necessary compromises. However, as long as a Node relies on a state-sanctioned entity to hold land, pay fiat taxes, or shield members, it remains subservient to the legacy state. Furthermore, legacy systems treat the end of a citizen's life as an extraction event via probate courts, privatizing generational knowledge.

\section{The Incremental Automation Pathway}
Phase 1 relies heavily on the Social Purpose Corporation (SPC) for legal existence. Phase 2 involves the gradual conversion of fiat treasuries into hard thermodynamic physical assets. Phase 3 achieves full sovereignty: complete legal dissolution of the SPC, deprecation of Layer 7, and a transition to pure inter-node sovereign federation.

\section{The Human Boundary (Limits of Automation)}
The system automates fiat liquidation and server decoupling, but humans must collectively agree when the ``Sovereignty Threshold'' is reached. The physical diplomacy required to forge inter-node federations and trade physical assets remains a human endeavor.

\section{Orchestration Mechanics (The ``How'')}
A \texttt{SovereignFederationIntent} triggers the liquidation of all legacy fiat into physical hardware. The SPC is legally dissolved via a Trust Ring multi-sig transaction, transitioning assets to pure cryptographic stewardship. API connections to legacy banks are permanently severed. The Ledger abandons fiat pegs entirely, pegging Value Tokens strictly to localized exergy (Joules) and biomass. Layer 5 transitions from internal governance to inter-node diplomacy, federating the Web of Trust with neighboring Nodes via LoRaWAN and dark fiber.
