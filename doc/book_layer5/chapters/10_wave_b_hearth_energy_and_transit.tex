\section{Scenario Gamma (\texttt{SCN-GAMMA-KITCHEN}): The Sovereign Hearth}
\label{sec:waveb-gamma}

Scenario~Gamma transforms nutrition from an opaque industrial supply chain into a decommodified
$B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$ commons spanning backyard mirepoix gardens, rotational silvopasture,
precision fermentation, and neighborhood tiffin hearths \parencite{bigelow1921thermal}.

\subsection{Four Constitutional Hearth Gates and Zero-Knowledge Allergen Shields}

\texttt{col-kmsd} governs hundreds of daily culinary and agricultural workflows through four
deterministic Layer~5 gates:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Trophic Non-Competition \& Rotational Animal Welfare:} Livestock and poultry
    forage strictly on crop residues, food scraps, insect larvae, and rotational silvopasture
    ($\mathtt{human\_edible\_grain\_fed\_mg} \equiv 0$, $\mathtt{pasture\_rest\_days} \ge 30$),
    ensuring animals never compete with human stomachs for arable grain.
    \item \textbf{Bioprocess \& Heavy-Metal Purity:} Precision fermentation and pressure-retort
    vessels require food-grade \texttt{316L} stainless Clean-in-Place (\texttt{CIP}) cycles with
    zero single-use plastic liners ($\mathtt{single\_use\_plastic\_flag} = 0$) and verified Bigelow
    thermal hold $\ge 347{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($74.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$) for $\ge 120\,\mathrm{s}$
    (or $394{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ [$121.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$] for low-acid canning).
    \item \textbf{Cryptographic Anaphylactic \& Dietary Charter Shield:} Rather than storing a
    central database of residents' medical allergies or religious diets (Halal, Kosher, Jain, vegan),
    each diner's smartcard presents a \texttt{BBS+} zero-knowledge proof \parencite{camenisch2004signature}
    at the insulated tiffin latch verifying $(\mathtt{batch\_allergen\_ethics\_mask} \mathbin{\&}
    \mathtt{diner\_zk\_exclusion\_mask}) == 0$ locally in $O(1)$ bitmask operations.
    \item \textbf{Law~3 Surplus \& $\Phi_t$ Auto-Shutoff:} Transitional cottage-food sales under
    \texttt{policy:commons\_plus\_l7\_zero\_bill} execute strictly above $F_{\mathrm{eco}} + B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$
    and shut off automatically the instant monthly municipal/tax bills hit \texttt{0 cents}.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{invariant}[Zero-Knowledge Anaphylactic Latch Safety and Trophic Non-Competition]
\label{inv:waveb-gamma-safety}
No smart-tiffin latch may unseal if $(\mathtt{batch\_allergen\_ethics\_mask} \mathbin{\&}
\mathtt{diner\_zk\_exclusion\_mask}) \ne 0$, and no animal husbandry workflow may draw human-edible
grain ($\mathtt{human\_edible\_grain\_fed\_mg} \equiv 0$).
\end{invariant}
