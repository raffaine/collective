\section*{1. The Legacy Paradigm \& Its Failures}
The legacy ``Third Place'' has been heavily financialized. Remote workers face isolation at home or exorbitant fiat subscriptions to centralized corporate coworking spaces that extract massive profit margins. Accountability is managed by centralized human front-desk staff or opaque corporate policies.

\section*{2. The Incremental Automation Pathway}
\textbf{Phase 1 (Manual Trust):} A local community leases a space; members hold physical keys and use a white-board for scheduling.
\textbf{Phase 2 (AI-Assisted Policy):} A centralized app manages bookings; human stewards review complaints about etiquette violations and manually restrict access.
\textbf{Phase 3 (Autonomous Cryptographic Enforcement):} The space uses BLE proximity locks. The Layer 5 policy engine autonomously checks DID reputation and graph distance, locking Value Token collateral based on historical acoustic or cleanliness violations without a human intermediary.

\section*{3. The Human Boundary (Limits of Automation)}
The orchestrator cannot physically clean a desk or judge the nuanced social context of a noise violation. If an acoustic sensor flags a violation, the AI does not immediately ban the user; it flags the DID for review by local Stewards (human oversight) or demands higher collateral for future bookings until trust is regained.

\section*{4. Orchestration Mechanics (The ``How'')}
Access control relies on a local Layer 5 policy engine parsing the \texttt{WorkspaceIntent}. When a user requests entry, their DID is queried against the local Web of Trust graph. 
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Dynamic Collateral Locking:} If the user is unknown or has a history of \texttt{Etiquette\_Violation} attestations, the smart contract demands a dynamic multiplier on their Value Token escrow.
    \item \textbf{Hardware Reality:} Keys are stored in the user's mobile Secure Enclave. The BLE handshake occurs entirely offline via a localized MQTT broker to prevent lockouts during legacy ISP outages.
\end{itemize}
