\chapter{Appendix 23: Scenario Psi - Restorative Justice}

\section{The Legacy Paradigm \& Its Failures}
The legacy justice system is punitive and adversarial, extracting fiat fines and relying on a monopoly on violence. By focusing on assigning blame rather than repairing relationships, legacy courts fundamentally destroy the human social graph, which is catastrophic for a highly interdependent, decentralized mesh node.

\section{The Incremental Automation Pathway}
Phase 1 utilizes informal neighborhood dispute resolution. Phase 2 implements standardized mediation agreements and centralized arbitration. Phase 3 employs algorithmic selection of mathematically neutral mediators and thermodynamic labor restitution to physically repair the Trust Ring.

\section{The Human Boundary (Limits of Automation)}
The system can find a neutral mediator and temporarily escrow disputed tokens, but resolving deep interpersonal conflict requires emotional labor, active listening, and physical presence at the Council Fire. No algorithm can simulate true restorative justice and emotional de-escalation.

\section{Orchestration Mechanics (The ``How'')}
Upon receiving a \texttt{MediationIntent}, Layer 5 executes a bidirectional Breadth-First Search (BFS) on the Trust Ring graph to find a mediator with mathematically equal graph distance and vouch weight from both conflicting parties. Disputed Value Tokens are temporarily frozen via CRDT multi-sig locks. Resolution requires a 3-of-3 NFC signature payload (Party A, Party B, Mediator) to unlock funds or trigger thermodynamic restitution routing (e.g., assigning Maintenance Bounties to the offender).
