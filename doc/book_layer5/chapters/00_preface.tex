Every technical architecture eventually reveals whom it trusts to decide and how it compels
obedience. Across the twentieth and early twenty-first centuries, modern societies alternated
between three deeply flawed models of institutional authority. State and corporate
bureaucracies concentrated permission inside hierarchical access-control tables, credentialed
ministries, and coercive courts, reducing living neighborhoods to passive subjects of distant
administrative decrees \parencite{scott1998,hart1961}. Later, surveillance platforms replaced
explicit legal deliberation with opaque behavioral scoring, biometric identity capture, and
terms-of-service panopticons that could revoke a household's livelihood overnight without due
process \parencite{zuboff2019}. Most recently, speculative ``Web3'' governance experiments
promised decentralization while encoding plutocracy directly into protocol law: coin-weighted
voting, financialized escrow, permanent public scarlet letters marketed as ``Soulbound
Tokens,'' and automated slashing scripts that mistook ruthless capital liquidation for social
trust.

Meanwhile, generations of well-intentioned intentional communities and cooperatives discovered
the opposite failure mode. Rejecting formal rules altogether did not abolish power; as
\textcite{freeman1972} diagnosed, structurelessness merely masked informal cliques, charismatic
dominance, and exhausting, endless committee meetings that burned out working parents and
caregivers while leaving critical infrastructure unmaintained \parencite{michels1911,han2015}.

\textbf{Layer~5: Polycentric Governance and Trust}---realized in POSIX silicon as the hardened
C++20 daemon \texttt{col-kmsd}---exists to transcend all four failure modes. Positioned in the
Sovereign Stack directly above the keyless, deterministic orchestrator (\texttt{col-execd},
Layer~4) and directly below the natural-language semantic Agora (\texttt{col-commonsd} and
\texttt{col-gatewayd}, Layer~6), Layer~5 is the constitutional immune system and normative
closure of a Sovereign Node. Its core engineering thesis is exacting: \textit{a free community
can govern hundreds of concurrent physical workflows across a living watershed without rulers,
without surveillance rosters, without financialized tokens, and without drowning human life in
perpetual meetings} \parencite{beer1974,illich1973,weiser1996calm}.

\section*{Why Governance Must Be Deterministic, Key-Isolated, and Non-Financialized}

In Volume~1 of this series and its sister monograph on Layer~4, we established that any daemon
whose outputs govern physical relays, microgrids, water manifolds, clinical autoclaves, and
food-pasteurization vessels must obey deterministic mathematical invariants. If Layer~5
evaluated trust weights, ecological regeneration floors, or credit ceilings in IEEE-754
floating-point arithmetic, subtle rounding divergences across \texttt{x86\_64},
\texttt{aarch64}, and \texttt{riscv64} nodes would fracture bilateral treaty verification and
Sparse Merkle Tree state roots. Worse, if stochastic language models in Layer~6 were permitted
to ratify policies or sign capability tokens directly, a prompt injection or confabulated
parameter could bypass safety clearances or breach watershed extraction limits.

Consequently, \texttt{col-kmsd} enforces three unyielding architectural boundaries:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Pure-Integer Fixed-Point Policy Arithmetic:} Every trust attenuation edge,
        anchored EigenTrust iteration \parencite{kamvar2003}, Bayesian Knowledge Tracing
        mastery score \parencite{corbett1995bkt}, Reputational Wealth decay curve, and export
        surplus bound executes strictly in 64-bit integer International System (SI) and
        basis-point (\texttt{bp}, where $10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp} = 100\%$) units with zero
        floating-point opcodes.
\item \textbf{Hardware-Rooted Cryptographic Custody with Downward Blindness:} Citizen
        Decentralized Identifiers (\texttt{did:peer}), threshold Schnorr signing shares
        (\texttt{FROST}) \parencite{komlo2020frost}, and zero-knowledge selective-disclosure
        Verifiable Credentials (\texttt{BBS+}) \parencite{camenisch2004signature,w3c2022vc}
        are anchored inside non-exportable hardware secure elements (\texttt{ATECC608A})
        \parencite{microchip_atecc608a} and pinned local vault memory (\texttt{mlockall}).
        Plaintext citizen identities \textit{never} cross downward into Layer~4 or Layer~3;
        \texttt{col-kmsd} authorizes human task execution solely through unlinkable 24-byte
        \texttt{ClaimHandle} tokens.
\item \textbf{Constitutional Non-Commensurability:} Nourishment, shelter, thermal comfort,
        sanitation, acute healthcare ($B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$), perinatal care, eldercare, and
        apprenticeship mentorship are inviolable rights of existence---never tokenized,
        auctioned, or revoked as punishment \parencite{raworth2017,zehr1990}.
\end{enumerate}
