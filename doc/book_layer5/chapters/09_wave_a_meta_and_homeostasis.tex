\section{Scenario Zero (\texttt{SCN-ZERO-SOFTWARE}): The Sovereign Software Forge}
\label{sec:wavea-zero}

In corporate cloud computing, pushing a commit to a central repository silently overwrites the
software governing thousands of passive users. In a Sovereign Node, software \textit{is} operational
constitution \parencite{saltzer1984end}: an unverified update to \texttt{col-kmsd} or \texttt{col-execd}
could alter survival floors or leak private keys. Scenario~Zero governs the autopoietic compilation,
canary verification, and bicameral deployment of the Sovereign Stack itself.

\subsection{Bicameral Release Topology, Reproducible Builds, and Studio Conscience}

Ordinary bug fixes and Layer~4 workflow optimizations within ratified bounds require a \textbf{2-of-3
Domain Maintainer} threshold signature in the Stewardship Chamber ($R_0\text{--}R_2$ local deploy,
mirrored across $R_3\text{--}R_6$ git/CAS bundles). However, whenever a commit modifies Layer~3
thermodynamic ledger equations or Layer~5 constitutional trust/policy gates, \texttt{col-kmsd}
enforces full \textbf{Bicameral Ratification}: both the 2-of-3 Maintainer quorum \textit{and} a
Commons Chamber 1-person-1-vote supermajority must co-sign \texttt{SoftwareRelease\_CID}.

Simultaneously, Scenario~Zero codifies the \textbf{\texttt{Protocol of Refusal} Studio Conscience Veto}
and strict licensing hygiene: contributors hold an unassailable right to refuse building surveillance
trackers, DRM locks, dark-pattern notifications, or weaponized firmware, while all Sovereign Stack
artifacts ship under \texttt{AGPLv3} (software), \texttt{CERN-OHL-S v2} (hardware), and the
\texttt{Defensive Patent License (DPL v1)} prior-art diode.

\begin{invariant}[Tri-Architecture Determinism, Oasis Canary Soak, and Hardware Break-Glass]
\label{inv:wavea-zero-release}
\texttt{col-kmsd} refuses to emit \texttt{0x0501 L5\_DEPLOY\_WORKFLOW\_CID} or authorize A/B daemon
promotion unless: (1)~independent reproducible builds across \texttt{x86\_64}, \texttt{aarch64}, and
\texttt{riscv64} yield bit-identical BLAKE3 digests ($\mathtt{arch\_consensus\_mask} = \mathtt{0x07}$);
(2)~static ELF/Wasm binary audit confirms $\mathtt{float\_opcodes\_l2\_l5} = 0$ and
$\mathtt{layer\_skip\_imports} = 0$; and (3)~the candidate release completes $\ge 10{,}000$
deterministic shadow ticks inside Oasis (\texttt{Layer 1b}) with zero invariant regressions. Even
post-promotion, a physical toggle switch wired to the node chassis executes a $< 500\,\mathrm{ms}$
hardware \textbf{Break-Glass Rollback} to \texttt{prev\_verified\_cid} without requiring network
consensus.
\end{invariant}

Layer~6 Software Artifact} whose institutional care is entrusted to a dedicated \textbf{Layer~5
Working Group} ($V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$ scoped to $N_s$ active working-group members), exactly as
the OpenGL specification is cared for by the Khronos Group. Inside Oasis's simulated physical
world, software exhibits only a \textbf{mild Layer~1 and Layer~2 footprint} (workstation and server
rack electrical draw in \texttt{mW}/\texttt{uJ}, waste-heat rejection in \texttt{mK} with
$\ge 8{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ hydronic recapture, and fan/SSD wear), while executing its full
five-phase organizational lifecycle across Layers~4 through~6:
\begin{enumerate}

        or $\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$ spikes prompt affected nodes to publish a Layer~6
        \texttt{SoftwareRequirementRfcIntent} ($\le 512\,\mathrm{MiB}$ target footprint).

        charter a Scenario~Zero Stewardship Node ($N_s$ members, $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$,
        $\tau_{1/2} = 180\,\mathrm{d}$).
\item \textbf{Phase 3 --- Inception and Build ($\mathrm{L4} + \mathrm{L2} \to \mathrm{L3/L5}$):}
        \texttt{col-execd} runs scoping, code review, hermetic 3-arch build, and $10{,}000$-tick
        canary conformance workflows while Layer~2 meters rack watt-hours and Layer~5 credits
        $\Delta R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}$ upon verified milestones.
\item \textbf{Phase 4 --- Spec Ratification ($\mathrm{L4} \to \mathrm{L5} \to \mathrm{L6}$):}
        Once deterministic gates pass, the Layer~5 Working Group ratifies an immutable,

        zero, $R_i(t) \to 0$, and the working group rests in \textbf{Dormant Equilibrium} protected

        \texttt{col-kmsd} escalates into a \textbf{Layer~6 Semantic Diff Card}, waking the working

\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Clean-Slate Boot, Onboarding Progression, and Disability Parity}

As validated in \texttt{SharedStackRuntime::boot\_clean\_slate()}, a new node boots with
\textbf{zero} unsolicited background workflows, defaulting food and power sourcing to
\texttt{L7\_LEGACY\_CHANNEL} until residents explicitly ratify \texttt{R0\_LOCAL\_WORKFLOWS} and
\texttt{R1\_MESH\_PEERS}. Node~0's root identity is forged via the \textbf{3-of-5 Faraday \texttt{FROST}
Ceremony} mixing avalanche-diode, \texttt{ATECC608A}, and LoRa atmospheric entropy (Chapter~\ref{ch:l5-hardware-custody}).

Newcomers arriving from the wider world ($R_6$) receive unconditional $B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$ access
from their very first hour ($\mathtt{has\_bt\_survival\_floor} = \mathsf{true}$). Progression to
probationary watershed standing ($R_3$) requires $\ge 7\,\mathrm{days}$ of peaceful shelter tenure
paired with \textit{either} $\ge 36\,\mathrm{GJ}$ ($36 \times 10^{15}\,\mu\mathrm{J}$ or ratified
household equivalent) of Proof-of-Thermodynamic-Work \textit{or} a self-/peer-attested
\texttt{disability\_flare\_shield} parity exemption ($\mathtt{workfare\_coercion\_flag} = 0$).
Advancement from $R_3$ to full $R_1$ Settlement Steward requires $\ge 30\,\mathrm{days}$ tenure,
zero unresolved Peace Circle mediations, and a physical \textbf{2-of-3 $R_1$ \texttt{FROST} NFC Vouching
Ceremony} ($\mathtt{min\_nfc\_rssi\_mdbm} > -55{,}000$). Conversely, \texttt{SafeHarborExit}
guarantees any departing member $100\%$ of $R_0$ personal belongings, portable \texttt{BBS+} skill
credentials, zero exit slashing, and staggered liquidity return protecting cooperative reserves
against sudden bank-runs.

\begin{invariant}[Anti-Workfare Onboarding Parity and Costless Exit]
\label{inv:wavea-tau-parity}
No person's $B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$ nutrition, warmth, shelter, or clinical care may ever be gated on
onboarding stage, physical labor output, or disability status; and \texttt{disability\_flare\_shield}
holders achieve $10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ civic parity across $R_6 \to R_3 \to R_1$ without invasive
medical interrogation.
\end{invariant}

\section{Scenario Sigma (\texttt{SCN-SIGMA-BURNOUT}): Anti-Burnout Homeostasis}
\label{sec:wavea-sigma}

In cooperative movements, the most generous stewards frequently work themselves to exhaustion while
unpleasant maintenance tasks pile up unclaimed \parencite{han2015,cannon1932}. Scenario~Sigma closes
a cross-scenario homeostatic loop inside \texttt{col-kmsd} using \textbf{zero} biometric wearables
($\mathtt{biometric\_wearables\_used} = 0$).

\subsection{Dual-Freeze Rest Shields and Cross-Scenario Automation Bounties}

Whenever a steward logs $> 144{,}000\,\mathrm{s}$ ($40\,\mathrm{h/wk}$), completes $> 6$ high-hazard
shifts, responds to a \texttt{Chi}/\texttt{Upsilon} emergency, or voluntarily requests rest,
\texttt{col-kmsd} activates the \textbf{Dual-Freeze Care Leave Shield}: simultaneously freezing Layer~5
$\mathcal{R}_d$ decay ($\mathtt{decay\_rate\_bp} = 0$), pausing Scenario~Kappa skill-fade timers,
and blinding non-urgent task notifications while preserving $100\%$ of $B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$.

Conversely, when tedious sanitation, grease-trap, or weeding tasks remain voluntarily unclaimed in
Layer~4, \texttt{col-kmsd} progressively escalates their standing recognition multiplier
\texttt{tediousness\_multiplier\_bp} up to $30{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($3.0\times$). If a drudgery task
still remains chronically unpicked past \texttt{escalation\_threshold\_ticks}, Layer~5 refuses to
coerce anyone---instead, it \textbf{automatically spawns a cross-scenario R\&D automation bounty} into
Scenario~Alpha and Scenario~Zero to engineer a mechanical washer or workflow redesign that eliminates
the drudgery at its root!

\begin{invariant}[Zero-Biometric Fatigue Shield and Drudgery-to-Automation Escalation]
\label{inv:wavea-sigma-bounty}
\texttt{col-kmsd} caps \texttt{tediousness\_multiplier\_bp} at $30{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$, enforces
$\mathtt{biometric\_wearables\_used} \equiv 0$, and converts chronic unclaimed drudgery into an
\texttt{Alpha}/\texttt{Zero} engineering docket rather than compulsory labor assignment.
\end{invariant}
