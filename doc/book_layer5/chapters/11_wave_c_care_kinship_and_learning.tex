\section{Scenario Delta (\texttt{SCN-DELTA-CHILD}): Perinatal Sanctuary and Care Pods}
\label{sec:wavec-delta}

Scenario~Delta surrounds pregnancy, birth, postpartum recovery, and early childhood with calm
material abundance and radical privacy ($\mathtt{care\_tokens\_minted} \equiv 0$,
$\mathtt{nursery\_cameras\_or\_ble} \equiv 0$).

\subsection{Perinatal Shields, Pairwise Pod DIDs, and Neurodivergent-Weighted Ratio Gates}

Upon registering a birth or adoption, \texttt{col-kmsd} grants a \textbf{$180\text{--}365$-day Dual-Parent
(or $N$-Ary) Perinatal Care Leave Shield} ($\mathtt{decay\_rate\_bp} = 0$) and automatically spawns
expanded postpartum $B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$ workflows into Scenario~Gamma ($6/\mathrm{day}$ high-iron
lactation/recovery meals), Scenario~Zeta (uninterrupted nursery hydronic warmth), and sterilization
bays ($\ge 358{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ [$85.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$] cloth-diaper autoclave cycles).

Early-childhood care pods use ephemeral pairwise \texttt{did:peer} identifiers with zero cloud or
Bluetooth tracking tags on children, surrounded by a Scenario~Beta \texttt{No-Transit} geofence.
Adult stewards present zero-knowledge \texttt{BBS+} proofs (\texttt{Midwife\_Steward\_L2},
\texttt{Pediatric\_CPR\_Steward\_L1}), while \texttt{col-kmsd} enforces a \textbf{Dynamic Age and
Neurodivergent Weighted Ratio Gate} before admitting additional check-ins:
\begin{equation}
    \sum_{i=1}^{M} w_i \le 40{,}000\,\mathrm{bp} \cdot N_{\mathrm{adults}},
\end{equation}
where infants ($<18\,\mathrm{mo}$) carry $w_i = 20{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($2{:}1$ adult ratio),
toddlers carry $13{,}333\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($3{:}1$), preschoolers carry $10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$
($4{:}1$), and children needing high-sensory neurodivergent support carry $25{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$,
triggering \texttt{RATIO\_OVERFLOW\_HOLD} and summoning a backup $R_1$ caregiver rather than
crowding the room.

\label{inv:wavec-delta-ratio}
