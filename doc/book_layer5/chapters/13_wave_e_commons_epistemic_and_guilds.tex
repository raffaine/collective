\section{Scenario Epsilon (\texttt{SCN-EPSILON-COWORK}): Docked Carapace-as-a-Service}
\label{sec:wavee-epsilon}

During Stage~1 and Stage~2 transitions, fledgling neighborhood nodes rarely want to pay lawyers
thousands of dollars each to incorporate redundant cooperatives. Scenario~Epsilon enables an anchor
hub to offer \textbf{Docked Carapace-as-a-Service} via a Ricardian \texttt{SharedCarapaceDockingIntent}
(\texttt{Treaty\_CID}) under the Tripartite Legal Chassis (PPT/CLT + Subchapter~T \texttt{26 U.S.C.\ \S\S\,1381--1388}
+ \texttt{UUNAA} Sec.~6), while exercising \textbf{zero VSM command authority}
($\mathtt{vsm\_command\_over\_docked} \equiv 0$) over any docked node's internal governance.

\subsection{Cluster $\Phi_{\mathrm{cluster}}$ Shutoff, Restorative Acoustics, and VLAN Isolation}

\texttt{col-kmsd} aggregates docked nodes' external tax/utility obligations into $\Phi_{\mathrm{cluster}} = \sum_i \Phi_{t,i}$
and automatically closes external Layer~7 coworking day-passes and consultancy intake the instant
$\Phi_{\mathrm{cluster}}$ reaches $100\%$ offset. Inside the coworking hall, sustained noise
$> 550\,\mathrm{cB}$ for $> 180\,\mathrm{s}$ triggers a calm amber desk LED and offers a soundproof
\texttt{STC-50} call booth with zero reputational slashing; guest devices isolate onto per-session
\texttt{802.1Q} VLANs, external clients interact only with pseudonymous guild handles, and
$\ge 8{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($80\%$) of server waste heat routes into Scenario~Zeta's hydronic loop.

\begin{invariant}[Docked Node Autonomy and Cluster Fiat-Ceiling Auto-Shutoff]
\label{inv:wavee-epsilon-dock}
Docking under a shared legal carapace grants the host node \textit{zero} vote or veto inside the
docked node (\texttt{vsm\_command\_over\_docked} $= 0$), and external L7 intake halts whenever
\texttt{cluster\_fiat\_earned\_cents} $\ge$ \texttt{cluster\_fiat\_target\_cents}.
\end{invariant}

\section{Scenario Iota (\texttt{SCN-IOTA-EPISTEMIC}): Open Epistemic Science}
\label{sec:wavee-iota}

Scenario~Iota replaces paywalled journals, tuition debt, and publication bias with decommodified
inquiry ($\mathtt{tuition\_charged\_cents} \equiv 0$, zero student surveillance, zero bell-curve
grading) and a cryptographic \textbf{Topological Anti-Sybil Replication Gate} governing updates to Oasis
(\texttt{Layer 1b}) simulation coefficients \parencite{douceur2002sybil,grieves2014digital}.

\subsection{Three-Node Anti-Sybil Replication, Null-Result Parity, and Indigenous Refusal}

No single node---nor a colluding local cluster---can inject falsified crop yields, battery curves,
or pharmacological constants into the federation's Oasis \texttt{L1b} physics engine. Updating a
canonical \texttt{L1b} empirical coefficient requires signed experimental bundles from \textbf{$\ge 3$
topologically distinct Autonomous Nodes} across $R_3\text{--}R_5$ with independent \texttt{ATECC608A}
hardware roots, instrument calibration drift $\le 50\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($0.50\%$), and combined
statistical significance $p < 100\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($1.00\%$). Well-controlled null and negative
replications earn \textbf{$10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($100\%$) standing parity} with positive discoveries,
published findings enter the \texttt{DPL v1} defensive prior-art commons, and indigenous ecological
knowledge remains protected under the \texttt{Protocol of Refusal} \parencite{kimmerer2013}.

\begin{theorem}[Anti-Sybil Epistemic Integrity of Oasis \texttt{L1b} Physics Updates]
\label{thm:wavee-iota-replication}
Under Scenario~Iota's replication gate requiring $m \ge 3$ vertex-disjoint $R_3\text{--}R_5$
hardware-attested nodes with calibration drift $\le 50\,\mathrm{bp}$, no adversary controlling fewer
than three independent physical node enclaves across distinct watershed vouch cuts can alter any
federated Oasis \texttt{L1b} physical parameter.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each replication bundle carries an \texttt{ATECC608A}-bound \texttt{FROST} council signature whose
lineage must traverse vertex-disjoint BPVP vouch paths in $R_3\text{--}R_5$ (Chapter~\ref{ch:l5-trust-rings}).
By Menger's theorem \parencite{ford1956maximal}, forging $m \ge 3$ vertex-disjoint attestations
requires compromising at least three physically separate node enclaves across independent trust cuts;
with $\le 2$ compromised nodes, \texttt{independent\_r3\_r5\_nodes < 3} rejects the patch deterministically.
\end{proof}

\section{Scenario Lambda (\texttt{SCN-LAMBDA-CULTURE}): Amphitheater and Sacred Refusal}
\label{sec:wavee-lambda}

Scenario~Lambda protects the soul of the commons---music, theater, luthiery, seasonal feasts, and
grief vigils---through bicameral cultural sovereignty with \textbf{zero art NFTs or speculative tokens}
($\mathtt{art\_nft\_tokens\_minted} \equiv 0$).

\subsection{Bicameral Cultural Fellowships, Acoustic Caps, and Physical Vigil Cutoffs}

The egalitarian Commons Chamber (1p1v) ratifies festival calendars, \textbf{Cultural Fellowships} (freeing
artists and musicians from routine shop shifts without means-testing), and perimeter acoustic bounds
($\le 550\,\mathrm{cB}$ day, $\le 450\,\mathrm{cB}$ night, and $\le 650\,\mathrm{cB}$ low-frequency
\texttt{dBC} protecting \texttt{Eta} wildlife), while the Stewardship Chamber ($\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{culture}} \le 800\,\mathrm{bp}$
single-person share cap) governs stage rigging and pyrotechnic safety. During grief vigils or sacred
ceremonies, invoking the \texttt{Protocol of Refusal} physically opens hardware relays cutting power
to all stage microphones and cameras while perimeter monitors log only scalar centibel integers
($\mathtt{raw\_audio\_bytes\_retained} \equiv 0$).

\begin{invariant}[Zero Art Financialization and Zero Raw Audio Retention]
\label{inv:wavee-lambda-culture}
\texttt{col-kmsd} enforces $\mathtt{art\_nft\_tokens\_minted} \equiv 0$, $\mathtt{raw\_audio\_bytes\_retained} \equiv 0$,
and $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{culture}} \le 800\,\mathrm{bp}$.
\end{invariant}

\section{Scenario Pi (\texttt{SCN-PI-GUILD}): Polycentric Guilds and ZK Growth Beacons}
\label{sec:wavee-pi}

Scenario~Pi operationalizes the Ostromian Three-Axis Jurisdiction Matrix and Zero-Knowledge
Homomorphic Growth Beacons established in Chapter~\ref{ch:l5-bpvp-federation}, ensuring work-bee
swarms (\texttt{TaskSwarm\_CID}) dissolve deterministically to \texttt{ZERO\_AUTHORITY} at
\texttt{swarm\_ttl\_hlc} while enforcing Anti-Siphon and Anti-Hoarding reciprocity ($\ge 7{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$).

\begin{theorem}[Deterministic Temporal Dissolution of Task-Swarm Authority]
\label{thm:wavee-pi-swarm}
For every chartered \texttt{TaskSwarm\_CID} with expiration $\mathtt{swarm\_ttl\_hlc}$, at all
Hybrid Logical Clock ticks $\mathtt{current\_hlc} \ge \mathtt{swarm\_ttl\_hlc}$ without explicit
renewal, \texttt{col-kmsd} evaluates $\mathtt{swarm\_authority\_state} = 0$ (\texttt{ZERO\_AUTHORITY}),
preventing temporary emergency or construction coordinators from ossifying into permanent bosses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every \texttt{0x0503} claim and \texttt{0x0504} FIB check evaluates the pure-integer predicate
$\mathtt{current\_hlc} < \mathtt{swarm\_ttl\_hlc}$ prior to issuing capabilities. Because HLC
timestamps advance monotonically \parencite{kulkarni2014logical}, once $\mathtt{current\_hlc} \ge \mathtt{swarm\_ttl\_hlc}$,
all subsequent capability evaluations return \texttt{false} identically with zero manual revocation delay.
\end{proof}
