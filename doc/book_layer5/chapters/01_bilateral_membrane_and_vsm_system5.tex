In conventional distributed software, ``policy'' is rarely an isolated architectural layer.
Authorization checks are scattered across web-framework middleware, stored procedures inside
shared relational databases, hard-coded microservice JSON tokens, and third-party cloud
identity providers \parencite{saltzer1975protection,rose2020zero}. When an organization
attempts to bolt democratic governance onto such a tangle, two pathologies immediately emerge.
Either user interfaces acquire direct mutating backdoors into operational machinery---rendering
formal community votes ceremonial---or the policy engine itself becomes a central surveillance
database holding every citizen's identity alongside timestamped logs of every tool they
touched, room they entered, and task they declined.

Within the seven-layer Sovereign Stack, \textbf{Layer~5: Polycentric Governance and Trust}
(\texttt{col-kmsd}) eliminates both failure modes through topological and cryptographic
isolation. Sitting directly between the keyless, deterministic orchestrator (\texttt{col-execd}
at Layer~4) and the human-facing semantic Agora (\texttt{col-commonsd} and
\texttt{col-gatewayd} at Layer~6), \texttt{col-kmsd} acts as the sole custodian of sovereign
identity, trust-ring topology, capability issuance, standing policy envelopes, and inter-node
Ricardian treaties. Yet despite holding the community's normative authority, \texttt{col-kmsd}
cannot open a TCP socket to the internet, cannot touch a physical GPIO pin, cannot read or
write raw rows in Layer~3's thermodynamic ledger, and cannot accept unmediated HTTP mutations
from a browser.

This opening chapter establishes the four structural invariants of the Layer~5 daemon:
(1)~strict bilateral \texttt{AF\_UNIX} \texttt{SOCK\_SEQPACKET} adjacency across
\texttt{l4\_l5.sock} and \texttt{l5\_l6.sock}; (2)~process hardening, SQLCipher vault
partitioning, and deterministic ARM64 memory bounds; (3)~Clean-Slate Day-1 Genesis, the
read-only diagnostic hood, and the three-hop Constitutional Intent U-Turn; and (4)~three levels
of cybernetic meta-recursion grounded in Stafford Beer's Viable System Model (VSM) System~5 and
System~$3^*$ \parencite{beer1972,beer1979}.

\section{Process Hardening, Vault Partitioning, and Frugal Footprint}
\label{sec:ch1-vault-isolation}

Because \texttt{col-kmsd} evaluates Web-of-Trust edges, blinding nonces, and threshold signature
commitments, its volatile and persistent memory must withstand both physical theft of an edge
single-board computer and compromise of neighboring user-space daemons.

\subsection{RAM Pinning, Zero-Dump Discipline, and Ephemeral Scrubbing}

During startup, prior to communicating with its hardware secure element or opening
\texttt{/var/lib/collective/l5/vault.enc}, \texttt{col-kmsd} executes three irreversible OS
hardening primitives:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Swap Prohibition (\texttt{mlockall}):} Invoking
        \texttt{mlockall(MCL\_CURRENT | MCL\_FUTURE)} pins all current and future virtual pages
        into physical DRAM, guaranteeing the Linux kernel never writes decrypted SQLCipher
        pages, PRF blinding subkeys, or \texttt{BBS+} witness scalars to swap partitions on
        flash media.
\item \textbf{Ptrace and Core-Dump Immunity:} Invoking \texttt{prctl(PR\_SET\_DUMPABLE, 0)}
        blocks core-file generation upon abnormal termination and forbids same-UID debugging
        attachment, complemented by \texttt{seccomp-bpf} syscall filtering.
\item \textbf{Immediate Post-Ceremony Zeroization:} Any ephemeral scalar, Shamir recovery
        share, or session blinding factor constructed in RAM is scrubbed immediately upon scope
        exit via \texttt{explicit\_bzero()} (preventing dead-store elimination by optimizing
        C++20 compilers).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Zero Shared Storage Between Layer 3 and Layer 5}

A foundational privacy rule of the Sovereign Stack is the \textbf{Strict Partition Between
Thermodynamic Truth and Social Identity}. Layer~3 (\texttt{col-storaged}) stores physical mass
balances, microjoule exergy flows, resource leases, and pairwise node-to-node Bilateral Clearing
Channels inside RocksDB and Automerge CRDTs \parencite{shapiro2011crdt}. Layer~5
(\texttt{col-kmsd}) stores pairwise \texttt{did:peer} records, local Web-of-Trust edges,
domain-scoped Reputational Wealth ($\mathcal{R}_d$), and its 256-bit Sparse Merkle Tree (SMT)
credential revocation accumulator inside an independent, AES-256-GCM encrypted SQLCipher vault
(\texttt{/var/lib/collective/l5/vault.enc}) keyed via hardware-assisted Argon2id derivation.

Neither database holds a single foreign key into the other. Layer~3 knows that a pasteurization
batch consumed $4.2\,\mathrm{MJ}$ of solar electricity and satisfied Bigelow thermal lethality
\parencite{bigelow1921thermal}, and that anonymous \texttt{ephemeral\_token\_id}
\texttt{0x9fa3...} completed the stir task; it never knows which human held that handle.
Conversely, Layer~5 knows which citizens hold valid \texttt{Sovereign\_Kitchen\_Steward\_VC}
credentials, yet holds zero write privileges over physical inventory balances.

\subsection{The Read-Only Cybernetic Hood (\texttt{HTTP 405}) and Offline Sequence Replay Protection}

To prevent user interfaces or compromised browser sessions from mutating policies behind
citizens' backs, \texttt{col-gatewayd} exposes Layer~5 state solely as an immutable diagnostic

(\texttt{L5\_GOVERNANCE\_IS\_READ\_ONLY}).

All lawful human governance actions enter through a single constitutional aperture:
\texttt{POST /api/v1/l6/intent}, following the mandatory three-step ceremony:
\[
{\small \text{Optional L6 LLM Draft} \rightarrow
\text{Deterministic Template Read-Back} \rightarrow
\text{Hardware Enclave Signature}.}
\]
Because stewards on rural parcels or storm-islanded meshes frequently sign intents while

\begin{definition}[Three-Hop L4--L7 Separation Across the Constitutional Intent U-Turn]
\label{def:ch1-three-hop-uturn}
No direct socket (\texttt{l4\_l7.sock}) exists between Layer~4 (\texttt{col-execd}) and Layer~7

$\mathrm{L7} \to \mathrm{L6} \to \mathrm{L5} \to \mathrm{L4}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Impossibility of Unratified Operational or External Fiat Mutation]
\label{thm:ch1-unratified-mutation}
Suppose kernel \texttt{SO\_PEERCRED} authentication holds on all \texttt{/run/collective/ipc/*}
sockets, \texttt{col-gatewayd} enforces read-only rejection on \texttt{/api/v1/l1..l5/*} and
\texttt{/api/v1/l7/*}, and \texttt{ATECC608A} enclave keys remain uncompromised. Then neither a
hallucinating Layer~6 language model nor an external Layer~7 network adversary can (i)~deploy or
mutate a BPMN workflow in Layer~4, or (ii)~trigger an unbudgeted Layer~7 GPO expenditure,
without passing deterministic policy verification inside \texttt{col-kmsd}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Consider the directed IPC graph $G_{\mathrm{IPC}} = (V, E)$ over $V = \{\mathrm{L1}, \dots,
\mathrm{L7}\}$, where edges exist solely between adjacent layers $k$ and $k+1$. Removing vertex
$\mathrm{L5}$ partitions $G_{\mathrm{IPC}}$ into two disconnected components
$V_{\mathrm{low}} = \{\mathrm{L1}, \mathrm{L2}, \mathrm{L3}, \mathrm{L4}\}$ and
$V_{\mathrm{high}} = \{\mathrm{L6}, \mathrm{L7}\}$; hence $\mathrm{L5}$ is a strict vertex cut
between $\{\mathrm{L6}, \mathrm{L7}\}$ and $\mathrm{L4}$. Any downward mutation reaching
$\mathrm{L4}$ must traverse \texttt{l4\_l5.sock} from UID \texttt{uid\_col\_kmsd}. Inside
\texttt{col-kmsd}, frames arriving on \texttt{l5\_l6.sock} either match a pre-ratified standing
policy envelope (whose auto-settlement ceiling is bounded in integer \texttt{cents}) or require
a valid hardware-enclave signature over the deterministic template digest with monotonic
sequence $s > s_{\mathrm{last}}$. Because neither Layer~6 LLM weights nor external Layer~7
callers possess access to the \texttt{ATECC608A} signing key, unratified payloads fail signature
or envelope verification in \texttt{col-kmsd} and never emit opcodes \texttt{0x0501..0x0504} or
co-signed \texttt{l6\_l7.sock} procurement authorizations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Immunity to Dormant-Node Single-Maintainer Hijack Under $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$]

\[
    V_i(t) = B + R_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t) \quad

$N_s + \Delta R_{\mathrm{attacker}}$ and share $S_{\mathrm{attacker}} =
\frac{1 + \Delta R_{\mathrm{attacker}}}{N_s + \Delta R_{\mathrm{attacker}}}$. Algebraic
rearrangement yields $S_{\mathrm{attacker}} < \frac{1}{2} \iff 2 + 2\Delta R_{\mathrm{attacker}} <

\end{proof}
