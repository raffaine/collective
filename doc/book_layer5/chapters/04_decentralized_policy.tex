\chapter{Decentralized Policy and Multi-sig Governance}

\section{The Myth of the Central Judiciary}
Legacy governance relies on central judiciaries and the monopoly on state violence to enforce contracts. When a contract is breached, the resolution requires years of legal arbitration, relying entirely on the subjective interpretation of human judges and the physical enforcement of police. In the Sovereign Stack, code is not law—physics is law. Policy must be enforced autonomously via Layer 2 hardware, completely bypassing human arbitration where possible.

\section{N-of-M Threshold Governance}
Policy changes and community resource allocation are governed by $n$-of-$m$ multi-signature thresholds. For example, upgrading the community solar inverter firmware might require cryptographic signatures from 3 out of 5 verified Master Electricians (DIDs holding the required SBT). 

This logic is hardcoded into Layer 3 smart contracts, but the execution is physically bound to the edge. When the threshold is met, the contract emits a \texttt{PolicyUpdatedIntent} that Layer 4 ingests to update the local routing matrices. 

\section{Actuator-Driven Enforcement}
Crucially, Layer 5 policies are enforced by physical actuators. If a citizen attempts to consume more than their allocated thermodynamic exergy limit during a grid blackout (Scenario Upsilon), the system does not send them a fine in the mail. The local edge node, responding to the decentralized policy matrix, simply commands the Layer 2 physical relay to open, immediately cutting off power to their localized sub-grid. 

By binding social and legal policy directly to cryptographic hardware relays, Layer 5 eliminates the need for state violence. Policy becomes a deterministic physical constraint, as immutable as gravity, bounded only by the collective cryptographic consensus of the Trust Rings.

\section{Iterative Refinement: Emergency Overrides \& Fatigue Lockouts}
Scenario stress-testing (Upsilon, Sigma) mandated that rigid physics must yield to life-support. Layer 4 introduces \textit{Emergency State Overrides}—if a Level 1 disaster is declared, Trust Ring barriers (e.g., Acoustic Budgets or resource locks) mathematically decay, allowing unvouched actors temporary access to life-saving infrastructure. Conversely, to prevent Altruism Fatigue, the system enforces \textit{Fatigue Lockouts}, algorithmically blocking overtired citizens from accepting high-risk bounties and utilizing Dutch Auctions to incentivize rested peers.

\section{Iterative Refinement: Oracles, Zoning, and Simulation Consensus}
Physical environments are messy. Layer 5 implements \textit{Physical-to-Policy Oracle Bridging} and \textit{Spatial Policy Zoning}, mapping specific cryptographic thresholds directly to 3D voxel chunks in the mesh (Scenario Lambda). Furthermore, before a complex architectural policy is approved (Scenario Xi), the nodes achieve \textit{Simulation-based Policy Evaluation}—running deterministic thermal and structural simulations via CRDTs, ensuring consensus on the physics \textit{before} the multi-sig is executed.
