\chapter{Thermodynamic Baselines, Non-Commensurable Care, and Ecological Preemption}
\label{ch:l5-constitutional-floors}

\epigraph{A society that prices the cup of water given to a feverish child or the hand held beside a dying elder has not achieved efficiency; it has merely forgotten why an economy exists at all.}{---Adapted from \textcite{illich1973} and \textcite{raworth2017}}

Every socio-technical system encodes an implicit anthropology in what it chooses to meter, price,
and withhold. State-socialist bureaucracies rationed survival through political loyalty cards;
neoliberal platforms ration survival through wage precarity and algorithmic surge pricing; and
first-generation ``Web3'' experiments proposed putting toddlers' nursery care, household dishwashing,
and neighborhood first aid onto tokenized Dutch auctions. All three paradigms violate the first
principle of a convivial commons \parencite{kropotkin1902,illich1973,raworth2017}: \textit{neither
living ecosystems nor human dignity are commensurable commodities}.

Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}) enforces this constitutional boundary in deterministic fixed-point
code. Before any Layer~4 workflow may allocate a single microjoule of solar electricity, a
milliliter of spring water, a gram of harvest grain, or a minute of commercial export capacity,
\texttt{col-kmsd} interposes three unassailable structural laws: (1)~\textbf{Constitutional
Non-Commensurability of Care}, banning every form of domestic or clinical care tokenization;
(2)~the \textbf{Canonical Five-Tier Lexicographic Allocation Hierarchy} placing planetary
regeneration ($F_{\mathrm{eco}}$) and unconditional human survival ($B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$) strictly
above federated trade, discretionary guilds, and external Layer~7 fiat export ($\Phi_t$); and
(3)~\textbf{Lifecycle Care Leave Shields, Disability Parity, and Emeritus Wisdom Standing}.

\section{Constitutional Non-Commensurability and the Ban on Care Financialization}
\label{sec:ch6-non-commensurability}

Why does the Sovereign Stack strictly forbid minting ``care tokens,'' ``value tokens,'' or
peer-to-peer ledger credits for rocking an infant (Scenario~Delta), sitting beside an elder in
cognitive decline (Scenario~Phi), mentoring a novice at a lathe (Scenario~Kappa), or stabilizing a
trauma patient (Scenario~Chi)?

Behavioral economics and commons sociology converge on a devastating empirical law: introducing
piece-rate monetary or token rewards into intimate solidarity domains actively crowds out intrinsic
mutuality, turning unconditional kinship into transactional score-keeping while structurally
penalizing whoever cares for high-needs children, disabled companions, or dying elders whose support
cannot be ``optimized'' for throughput \parencite{mansbridge1980,kimmerer2013,han2015}.

\begin{invariant}[Zero Care Tokenization and Anti-Workfare Law]
\label{inv:ch6-zero-care-tokens}
Across all twenty-five Sovereign Scenarios, \texttt{col-kmsd} rejects at schema-validation time
any workflow or policy bundle attempting to price, auction, or issue transferable credits for
intimate care, perinatal support, elder companionship, acute clinical triage, or basic survival
provisioning ($\mathtt{domestic\_care\_token\_minting} \equiv 0$,
$\mathtt{workfare\_coercion\_permitted} \equiv \mathsf{false}$). Instead of tokenizing care, Layer~5
guarantees caregivers unconditional material security ($B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$), freezes reputational
and skill decay during care seasons, and bounds any individual steward's rotational care burden to
$\le 2{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($20\%$) of weekly waking hours.
\end{invariant}

\subsection{Export Surplus Bound, Prohibited Registers, and the ERC Saturation Clamp}

To guarantee mathematically that external market demand can never starve a kitchen or strip-mine a
forest---repeating the historical tragedy of cash-crop famines---Layer~5 enforces the
\textbf{Constitutional Export Surplus Bound}:
\begin{equation}
    Q_{\mathrm{avail}}(t) = \max\Bigl(0,\; Q_{\mathrm{inv}}(t) - F_{\mathrm{eco}}(t) - B_{\mathrm{baseline}}(t)\Bigr)
    \cdot \mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{export\_permitted}} \cdot \mathbf{1}_{\{\Phi_{\mathrm{rem}}(t) > 0\}}.
\end{equation}
Three structural locks reinforce this equation:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Prohibited Export Registers (Scenario~Mu \& Scenario~Eta):} Living topsoil,
    \textit{Terra Preta}, biochar, vermicompost, recovered struvite phosphorus, old-growth Mother
    Trees, and indigenous heirloom germplasm carry $\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{export\_permitted}} \equiv 0$
    permanently. Soil fertility is watershed patrimony, never an exportable cash crop.
    \item \textbf{Tier-3 Virgin Extraction Last-Resort Gate (Scenario~Theta):} Virgin geospheric
    mining or quarrying is cryptographically blocked whenever technospheric urban scrap
    ($Q_{\mathrm{scrap\_mg}} > 0$) is available across $R_1\text{--}R_5$; if genuinely zero scrap
    exists, virgin extraction requires an $R_4$ Watershed Assembly supermajority ($6{,}600\,\mathrm{bp}$),
    a $10\times$ ($100{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$) Ecological Replacement Cost (ERC) surcharge, and a
    $10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($100\%$) pre-funded site-reclamation escrow.
    \item \textbf{Pre-Division Saturation Clamp on ERC Multipliers:} When computing integer
    depletion multipliers $M_{\mathrm{erc\_bp}}(r) = \lfloor \frac{10{,}000 \cdot 10{,}000}{10{,}000 - r} \rfloor$
    as stock utilization $r \in [0, 10{,}000]\,\mathrm{bp}$ approaches exhaustion, naive division at
    $r \ge 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ would trigger divide-by-zero or signed 64-bit wrap-around. Verified
    in \texttt{verification/test\_companion\_architecture\_challenge.py}, \texttt{col-kmsd} applies
    a mandatory pre-division saturation clamp $r_{\mathrm{eff}} = \min(r, 9{,}900\,\mathrm{bp})$
    (capping $M_{\mathrm{erc\_bp}}$ cleanly at $1{,}000{,}000\,\mathrm{bp} = 100\times$) and halts
    non-survival draws entirely once $r \ge 9{,}900\,\mathrm{bp}$.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{theorem}[Survival Baseline Invariance and Overflow-Free ERC Preemption]
\label{thm:ch6-survival-invariance}
Let $Q_{\mathrm{inv}}(t) \ge F_{\mathrm{eco}}(t) + B_{\mathrm{baseline}}(t)$ at tick $t$, and let
arbitrary finite or adversarial demand vectors $(D_3, D_4, D_5) \in \mathbb{N}_0^3$ arrive
simultaneously from Federated Treaties (Tier~3), Local Guilds (Tier~4), and Layer~7 Commercial
Export (Tier~5). Under \texttt{col-kmsd}'s Five-Tier Preemption Gate and pre-division clamp
$r_{\mathrm{eff}} = \min(r, 9{,}900\,\mathrm{bp})$: (1)~post-dispatch inventory strictly satisfies
$Q_{\mathrm{post}}(t) \ge F_{\mathrm{eco}}(t) + B_{\mathrm{baseline}}(t)$; and (2)~no 64-bit integer
evaluation of $M_{\mathrm{erc\_bp}}(r)$ can divide by zero or overflow \texttt{uint64\_t} for any
input $r \in [0, 65{,}535]$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Because Tier~1 and Tier~2 reserves $R_{12}(t) = F_{\mathrm{eco}}(t) + B_{\mathrm{baseline}}(t)$ are
subtracted prior to computing the residual pool $S(t) = \max(0, Q_{\mathrm{inv}}(t) - R_{12}(t))$,
Tiers~3, 4, and~5 allocate sequentially from $S(t)$ via $A_3 = \min(D_3, S)$,
$A_4 = \min(D_4, S - A_3)$, and $A_5 = \min(D_5, S - A_3 - A_4) \cdot \mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{export}}
\cdot \mathbf{1}_{\{\Phi_{\mathrm{rem}}>0\}}$. Summing allocations yields
$A_3 + A_4 + A_5 \le S(t) = Q_{\mathrm{inv}}(t) - R_{12}(t)$, whence
$Q_{\mathrm{post}}(t) = Q_{\mathrm{inv}}(t) - (A_3 + A_4 + A_5) \ge R_{12}(t)$, proving~(1)
independently of the magnitude of $(D_3, D_4, D_5)$. For~(2), for every \texttt{uint16\_t} $r$,
$r_{\mathrm{eff}} = \min(r, 9900)$ satisfies $10{,}000 - r_{\mathrm{eff}} \ge 100 > 0$, eliminating
division by zero; the numerator $10{,}000 \times 10{,}000 = 10^8 \ll 2^{64}-1$ never overflows
\texttt{uint64\_t}, and $M_{\mathrm{erc\_bp}} \le 10^6\,\mathrm{bp}$ remains monotonically bounded.
\end{proof}

Crucially, as local $R_0/R_1$ workflows collapse monthly external fiat obligations $\Phi_t$ toward

\section{Lifecycle Care Shields, Disability Parity, and Emeritus Standing}
\label{sec:ch6-care-shields}

Because Layer~5 uses domain-scoped Reputational Wealth $\mathcal{R}_d$ with seasonal half-life
decay (Chapter~\ref{ch:l5-intranode-envelopes}) and Scenario~Kappa recency timers to keep technical
chambers dynamic, an unshielded decay equation would inadvertently penalize parents welcoming a
newborn, stewards recovering from surgery, disabled residents experiencing chronic flares, or elders
aging out of heavy physical labor. Layer~5 eliminates this structural bias through four
constitutional \textbf{Lifecycle Care Shields} submitted via opcode \texttt{0x0603 L6\_SUBMIT\_CARE\_SHIELD}
over \texttt{l5\_l6.sock}:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Dual-Freeze Somatic \& Care Leave Shield (Scenario~Sigma):} Triggered voluntarily
    during illness, grief, family caregiving, or automatically after $> 144{,}000\,\mathrm{s}$
    ($40\,\mathrm{h/wk}$) of contributed effort or acute emergency response (\texttt{Chi}/\texttt{Upsilon}),
    simultaneously setting Layer~5 reputational decay to zero ($\mathtt{decay\_rate\_bp} = 0$) and
    pausing Scenario~Kappa skill-fade clocks with \textit{zero} biometric wearables.
    \item \textbf{Dual and $N$-Ary Perinatal Shields (Scenarios~Delta \& Omicron):} Grants a
    $180\text{--}365\,\mathrm{day}$ full Dual-Freeze Shield plus expanded postpartum $B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$
    ($6/\mathrm{day}$ high-iron \texttt{Gamma} meals and $358{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ [$85^\circ\mathrm{C}$]
    cloth-diaper autoclave service) equally to \textit{all} biological, adoptive, queer, and chosen
    co-parents registered in an $N$-ary \texttt{KinshipCovenant\_CID}.
    \item \textbf{Disability and Chronic-Illness Parity (Scenario~Tau):} Self-declared or
    peer-attested \texttt{disability\_flare\_shield} exemptions fulfill onboarding and full civic
    standing without invasive medical tribunals, preserving $10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ voting parity
    and full access to non-physical design, governance, cultural, and research domains.
    \item \textbf{Non-Decaying \texttt{T4\_Emeritus\_Sage} Standing (Scenario~Phi):} When veteran
    stewards retire from physically taxing shop or forestry shifts, they transition into
    non-decaying \texttt{T4\_Emeritus\_Sage} standing: holding \textit{zero} coercive bossing rank
    over youth, yet exercising mandatory Long-Horizon Watershed Memory consultation on land-use
    amendments and receiving a $+3{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($+35\%$) pedagogical recognition multiplier
    when mentoring apprentices.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{definition}[Lifecycle Decay-Invariant Standing Transformation]
\label{def:ch6-care-freeze}
For any steward $i$ holding domain standing $\mathcal{R}_{i,d}(t_0)$ and mastery state $P(L_{i,d})$
upon entering an active Care, Perinatal, or Disability Shield interval $[t_0, t_1]$, \texttt{col-kmsd}
enforces $\mathcal{R}_{i,d}(t) = \mathcal{R}_{i,d}(t_0)$ and $\Delta t_{\mathrm{skill\_fade}}(t) = 0$
for all $t \in [t_0, t_1]$, ensuring zero loss of civic or craft standing across human vulnerability.
\end{definition}
