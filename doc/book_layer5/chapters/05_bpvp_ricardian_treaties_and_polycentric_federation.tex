\chapter{Inter-Node Federation: Bioregional Path-Vector Routing, Ricardian Treaties, and Ostromian Polycentricity}
\label{ch:l5-bpvp-federation}

\epigraph{Polycentric systems are characterized by multiple governing authorities at differing scales rather than a monocentric unit. Each unit within a polycentric system exercises considerable independence to make norms and rules within a specific domain.}{---\textcite{ostrom2010}}

When governance moves beyond the perimeter of a single Sovereign Node, two opposite failure modes
historically appear. Either autonomous settlements retreat into autarkic ``lifeboat'' compounds
that hoard surplus while neighboring communities suffer ecological or economic shocks, or they
surrender local self-determination to a centralized regional bureaucracy---a watershed politburo
whose distant planners rapidly reproduce the legibility pathologies diagnosed by
\textcite{scott1998}. Neither failure mode can coordinate hundreds of inter-node workflows across
solar microgrids, tool-making guilds, seed sanctuaries, and regional clinics without sacrificing
human freedom.

Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}) resolves this tension by translating \textcite{ostrom1990,ostrom2010}'s
empirical theory of polycentric commons and \textcite{bookchin1982}'s confederal municipalism into
three deterministic cryptographic protocols: (1)~an \textbf{Ostromian Three-Axis Jurisdiction
Matrix} that orthogonalizes land sovereignty, craft safety, and ephemeral mutual-aid swarms;
(2)~the \textbf{Bioregional Path-Vector Protocol (BPVP)}---also referred to at the inter-domain
socket boundary as the \textit{Border Policy Vector Protocol}---adapting path-vector loop-freedom
\parencite{rekhter2006} and delay-tolerant bundle routing \parencite{fall2003} to concentric trust
rings and thermodynamic transport budgets; and (3)~\textbf{Tripartite Ricardian Treaties}
(\texttt{Treaty\_CID}) \parencite{grigg2004} paired with zero-knowledge stigmergic growth beacons.

\section{Ostrom's Three-Axis Polycentric Jurisdiction Matrix}
\label{sec:ch5-three-axis}

In a mature bioregional federation, a single physical workshop or watershed parcel simultaneously
participates in spatial ecology, specialized technical crafts, and time-bounded emergency or
seasonal campaigns. Monocentric states collapse all three dimensions into a single territorial
hierarchy. Layer~5 instead partitions inter-node authority across three orthogonal, non-dominating
axes (Table~\ref{tab:ch5-jurisdiction-axes}):

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Axis I: Contiguous Spatial Polities ($R_1\text{--}R_4$).} Households ($R_0$),
    neighborhood settlements ($R_1$), adjacent commons ($R_2$), and river-basin assemblies
    ($R_3\text{--}R_4$) exercise exclusive veto over physical land tenure, ecological regeneration
    floors ($F_{\mathrm{eco}}$), survival baselines ($B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$), acoustic curfews,
    and autonomous \texttt{No-Transit} geofence polygons. No external guild or distant federation
    may override a local spatial polity's ecological or sanctuary floor.
    \item \textbf{Axis II: Non-Spatial Trans-Local Craft Guilds ($R_2\text{--}R_6$).} Distributed
    stewards practicing foundry metallurgy, clinical pharmacology, timber engineering, or
    precision fermentation associate across non-contiguous nodes via Scenario~Pi guilds. Craft
    guilds hold bicameral Stewardship Chamber veto over \textit{technical safety rubrics}
    (Scenario~Kappa mastery gates) and \textit{metrological calibration protocols} (Scenario~Iota
    peer replication), yet hold \textit{zero} territorial command over any node's land, housing,
    or surplus allocation.
    \item \textbf{Axis III: Ephemeral Task Swarms (\texttt{TaskSwarm\_CID}).} When a storm severs
    a culvert (Scenario~Upsilon), a barn-raising assembles (Scenario~Xi), or an autumn harvest
    peaks (Scenario~Gamma), participating nodes charter a time-bounded \texttt{TaskSwarm\_CID}.
    Delegated operational capabilities bind strictly to the ratified BPMN saga and expire
    deterministically to \texttt{ZERO\_AUTHORITY} the instant the swarm's Hybrid Logical Clock
    \parencite{kulkarni2014logical} time-to-live (\texttt{ttl\_hlc}) elapses or its terminal
    milestone settles.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{definition}[Orthogonal Jurisdiction Non-Domination]
\label{def:ch5-orthogonal-jurisdiction}
Let $\mathcal{J} = \mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{spatial}} \times \mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{guild}} \times
\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{swarm}}$ denote the product space of active Layer~5 jurisdictions on node
$u$. An operational workflow instance $W$ touching physical land $L \in \mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{spatial}}$
and hazardous craft domain $D \in \mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{guild}}$ executes if and only if
$\mathrm{Permit}_{\mathrm{spatial}}(u, L, W) \land \mathrm{Permit}_{\mathrm{guild}}(D, W) = \mathsf{true}$.
Neither axis can unilaterally compel actuation within the other's constitutional veto domain.
\end{definition}

\section{The Bioregional Path-Vector Protocol (BPVP)}
\label{sec:ch5-bpvp}

When an intra-node workflow in Layer~4 (\texttt{col-execd}) encounters a local stock miss or
specialized tooling gap that cannot be satisfied within $R_0$ or $R_1$, how does the node discover
and negotiate fulfillment across $R_2\text{--}R_6$ without querying a centralized cloud directory
or flooding the radio mesh with link-state topology broadcasts?

Layer~5 solves inter-domain capability discovery and policy-compliant routing through the
\textbf{Bioregional Path-Vector Protocol (BPVP)}. Just as BGP-4 \parencite{rekhter2006} routes
packets across autonomous systems without a global routing czar---and Delay-Tolerant Networking
\parencite{fall2003} carries bundles across intermittent rural links---BPVP exchanges signed
capability advertisements $\mathcal{A}$ across bilateral trust edges. Crucially, as established in
Chapter~\ref{ch:l5-bilateral-membrane}, \texttt{col-kmsd} never opens raw TCP/IP sockets: every
BPVP update travels downward over \texttt{l4\_l5.sock} into Layer~4, Layer~3, and Layer~2's
\texttt{col-meshd} Reticulum/LoRa/fiber transport, and ascends symmetrically at the peer node.

\subsection{Capability Advertisements, Loop-Freedom, and Thermodynamic Pruning}

Each BPVP advertisement $\mathcal{A} = (\mathtt{cap\_cid}, Q_{\mathrm{surplus}}, \mathcal{P},
\eta_{\mathrm{route\_bp}}, \tau_{\mathrm{att\_bp}}, \mathtt{treaty\_cid}, \sigma_{\mathrm{FROST}})$
announces an export-cleared capability or surplus lot ($Q_{\mathrm{surplus}}$ strictly bounded by
Law~3's survival firewall $Q_{\mathrm{inv}} - F_{\mathrm{eco}} - B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$), accompanied
by an explicit \textbf{Ring-Path Vector} $\mathcal{P} = \langle u_0, u_1, \dots, u_k \rangle$ of
autonomous node \texttt{did:mesh} digests traversed so far.

Whenever node $w$ receives $\mathcal{A}$ from neighbor $u_k$, \texttt{col-kmsd} executes two
immediate constant-space admission filters before inserting the route into \texttt{Adj-RIB-In}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{$O(|\mathcal{P}|)$ Path-Vector Loop-Freedom:} If $w \in \mathcal{P}$, the
    advertisement has cycled back to an ancestor node and is dropped immediately in $O(|\mathcal{P}|)$
    integer comparisons ($\max |\mathcal{P}| \le 8$). Verified empirically across $100$-node
    Erd\H{o}s--R\'enyi random graphs and chordal mesh topologies in
    \texttt{verification/test\_layer5\_federated\_routing\_challenge.py}, explicit path-vector
    membership eliminates count-to-infinity routing loops under arbitrary asynchronous link churn.
    \item \textbf{Thermodynamic Transport Attenuation Gate:} Every transit hop $e = (u_i, u_{i+1})$
    attenuates the cumulative thermodynamic delivery efficiency in integer basis points according
    to the ratio of embodied capability exergy $E_{\mathrm{embodied\_uj}}$ to cumulative transport
    exergy $\sum E_{\mathrm{transit\_uj}}$ \parencite{odum1971,georgescu1971}:
    \begin{equation}
        \eta_{\mathrm{route\_bp}}(\mathcal{P}) =
        \left\lfloor \frac{10{,}000 \cdot E_{\mathrm{embodied\_uj}}}
        {E_{\mathrm{embodied\_uj}} + \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} E_{\mathrm{transit\_uj}}(u_i, u_{i+1})} \right\rfloor.
    \end{equation}
    Whenever $\eta_{\mathrm{route\_bp}}(\mathcal{P}) < 6{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($60\%$),
    \texttt{col-kmsd} prunes the advertisement automatically, preventing absurdly wasteful
    long-distance haulage of heavy, low-exergy bulk goods (such as shipping wet compost $400\,\mathrm{km}$
    by truck when local $R_1$ biorefineries exist).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{theorem}[BPVP Loop-Freedom and Finite Convergence Under Link Churn]
\label{thm:ch5-bpvp-convergence}
Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite bioregional federation graph ($|V| = N$) exchanging BPVP advertisements
with explicit node-digest path vectors $\mathcal{P}$, strict ring-monotonic or positive exergy
cost $E_{\mathrm{transit\_uj}}(e) > 0$ per edge, and efficiency floor $\eta_{\min} = 6{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$.
Then: (1)~no routing cycle can ever enter \texttt{Adj-RIB-In}; (2)~every accepted path satisfies
$|\mathcal{P}| \le \min\bigl(N, \lfloor \frac{2 \cdot E_{\mathrm{embodied\_uj}}}{3 \cdot e_{\min}} \rfloor + 1\bigr)$
where $e_{\min} = \min_{e \in E} E_{\mathrm{transit\_uj}}(e)$; and (3)~following cessation of
topological changes, all nodes' \texttt{Loc-RIB} tables converge in finite message rounds to a
unique loop-free fixed point.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Suppose toward contradiction that a cyclic path $\mathcal{P} = \langle u_0, \dots, u_j, \dots, u_k \rangle$
with $u_j = w$ is accepted at node $w$. Ingress filter~(1) scans $\mathcal{P}$ prior to
\texttt{Adj-RIB-In} insertion and drops $\mathcal{A}$ whenever $w \in \mathcal{P}$, contradicting
acceptance; hence~(1) holds unconditionally even while links flap. For~(2), $\eta_{\mathrm{route\_bp}} \ge 6{,}000$
implies $10{,}000 E_{\mathrm{embodied}} \ge 6{,}000 (E_{\mathrm{embodied}} + \sum E_{\mathrm{transit}})$,
so $\sum_{i=0}^{k-1} E_{\mathrm{transit}}(u_i, u_{i+1}) \le \frac{2}{3} E_{\mathrm{embodied}}$. Since
each edge expends at least $e_{\min} \ge 1\,\mu\mathrm{J}$, the hop count $k = |\mathcal{P}| - 1$
cannot exceed $\lfloor \frac{2 E_{\mathrm{embodied}}}{3 e_{\min}} \rfloor$, and loop-freedom
simultaneously bounds $|\mathcal{P}| \le N$. Consequently, the set of admissible simple paths is
finite. Because the 5-tier lexicographic ranking over $(\text{Ring}, -\mathtt{Local\_Pref},
-\eta_{\mathrm{route}}, \text{BCC\_OK}, |\mathcal{P}|, \mathtt{did\_hash})$ forms a strict well-ordering
and extending a path monotonically increases ring distance and cumulative transit exergy, induction
on path rank establishes finite quiescence~(3) \parencite{rekhter2006,dijkstra1959note}.
\end{proof}

#include <cstdint>
#include <array>

namespace collective::l5::bpvp {

constexpr uint16_t kMinRouteEfficiencyBp = 6000U; // 60.00% thermodynamic floor
constexpr uint8_t  kMaxRingPathHops      = 8U;

struct alignas(8) BpvpRouteEntryV31 {
    std::array<uint8_t, 32> capability_cid;       // Content-addressed capability/blueprint
    std::array<uint8_t, 32> treaty_cid;           // Governing Ricardian Treaty_CID
    std::array<uint64_t, kMaxRingPathHops> path_node_hashes; // Truncated did:mesh IDs
    uint64_t surplus_avail_si;                    // Cleared above F_eco + B_baseline
    uint64_t embodied_exergy_uj;                  // Useful capability exergy (uJ)
    uint64_t cumulative_transit_uj;               // Accumulated transport exergy (uJ)
    uint16_t local_pref_bp;                       // Includes Alpha MTBF drift bonus
    uint16_t route_efficiency_bp;                 // 0..10000 bp integer efficiency
    uint8_t  trust_ring;                          // R0=0 .. R6=6
    uint8_t  path_len;                            // Active entries in path_node_hashes
    uint8_t  bcc_headroom_ok;                     // 1 if within [-C1, +C2] credit bound
    uint8_t  export_firewall_flags;               // Bit 0: Refusal, Bit 1: NoTransit
};
static_assert(sizeof(BpvpRouteEntryV31) == 160, "BpvpRouteEntryV31 binary ABI lock");

[[nodiscard]] constexpr bool admit_inbound_bpvp(
    const BpvpRouteEntryV31& adv, uint64_t self_node_hash) noexcept {
    if (adv.path_len == 0U || adv.path_len > kMaxRingPathHops) return false;
    if ((adv.export_firewall_flags & 0x03U) != 0U) return false;
    for (uint8_t i = 0U; i < adv.path_len; ++i) {
        if (adv.path_node_hashes[i] == self_node_hash) return false; // Loop drop
    }
    const uint64_t denom_uj = adv.embodied_exergy_uj + adv.cumulative_transit_uj;
    if (denom_uj == 0ULL) return false;
    const uint64_t eff_bp = (10000ULL * adv.embodied_exergy_uj) / denom_uj;
    return eff_bp >= kMinRouteEfficiencyBp;
}

} // namespace collective::l5::bpvp
\end{lstlisting}

\section{Tripartite Ricardian Treaties, Bilateral Clearing, and Commons Jubilees}
\label{sec:ch5-ricardian-treaties}

BPVP discovers \textit{where} a capability lives; actual inter-node execution requires a binding
covenant governing quality tolerances, reciprocity ceilings, dispute Peace Circles, and failure
compensation. Layer~5 abolishes opaque ``smart contracts''---whose unamendable code bugs become
predatory traps---in favor of \textbf{Tripartite Ricardian Compacts} \parencite{grigg2004}.

\subsection{Canonical \texttt{Treaty\_CID} Binding and FROST Counter-Signatures}

Every inter-node compact binds three human- and machine-verifiable representations into a single
32-byte digest:
\begin{equation}
    \mathtt{Treaty\_CID} = \mathrm{BLAKE3}\bigl(\mathtt{Prose\_UTF8} \parallel
    \mathtt{Canonical\_JSONLD} \parallel \mathtt{Invariants\_BPMN}\bigr).
\end{equation}
Stewards in both nodes read the plain-language prose covenant (for example, Lot~402 exchanging
pasteurized soy-free mirepoix broth with Neighbor Node~B's solar timber kiln), verify the deterministic
JSON-LD parameter bounds \parencite{w3c2020jsonld}, and co-sign \texttt{Treaty\_CID} via each node's
hardware-rooted \texttt{FROST} threshold council \parencite{komlo2020frost}. Only after both
threshold signatures verify does \texttt{col-kmsd} push \texttt{Treaty\_CID} downward into Layer~4's
FIB and Layer~3's Bilateral Clearing Channel (BCC) state machine.

\begin{invariant}[Pairwise Zero-Sum Bilateral Clearing and Anti-Debt-Trap Ceiling]
\label{inv:ch5-bcc-zerosum}
For every active Ricardian treaty between nodes $u$ and $v$, Layer~5 binds asymmetric mutual-credit
bounds $[-C_{v\to u}, +C_{u\to v}]$ denominated in thermodynamic microjoule-equivalents or labor-seconds
with \textbf{zero interest} ($r_{\mathrm{interest\_bp}} \equiv 0$). At every settled epoch,
$B_{u\to v} + B_{v\to u} = 0$ and $-C_{v\to u} \le B_{u\to v} \le +C_{u\to v}$. Physical handoffs across
$R_2\text{--}R_5$ settle atomically via chained Hash-Time-Locked Contracts (HTLCs) paired with
dual-NFC steward custody receipts, while \textbf{Shock-Verified Commons Jubilees} forgive emergency
deficits if and only if Layer~2 physical shock telemetry (wildfire, flood, grid collapse) and an
$R_4$ Watershed Assembly supermajority ($6{,}600\,\mathrm{bp}$) concur.
\end{invariant}

\begin{invariant}[Anti-Siphon and Anti-Hoarding Reciprocity Bounds]
\label{inv:ch5-reciprocity-headroom}

(\textit{Anti-Siphon Gate}: immediate quarantine of external export capabilities upon unratified
resale proof) nor perpetually draw $R_2\text{--}R_4$ mutual aid while holding local shared guild
bays below $7{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($70\%$) utilization without an active shock or disability shield
(\textit{Anti-Isolationism Reciprocity Gate}).
\end{invariant}
