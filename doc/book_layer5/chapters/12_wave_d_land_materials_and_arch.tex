\section{Scenario Eta (\texttt{SCN-ETA-COPPICE}): Syntropic Agroforestry and Coppice}
\label{sec:waved-eta}

Scenario~Eta governs multi-strata food forests, rotational coppice timber stands, solar drying kilns,
and wild apothecary foraging under Robin Wall Kimmerer's \textit{Honorable Harvest} \parencite{kimmerer2013}.

\subsection{Mother-Tree Refusal, Dormancy Gates, and Biodiversity Floors}

Designated old-growth \textbf{Mother Trees} and sacred groves are enrolled in Layer~5's
\texttt{Protocol of Refusal}, physically locking out forestry harvesters and excising their timber
from Layer~3 valuation. Coppice felling is permitted solely during winter sap dormancy
($\mathtt{soil\_temp\_mk} < 278{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ [$5.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$]), provided bio-acoustic
bird/amphibian Shannon diversity holds at $H' \ge 28{,}000$ ($2.80$ scaled $\times 10^4$), fungal
ergosterol exceeds $150\,\mu\mathrm{g/g}$, and $\ge 3{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($30\%$) of coarse woody
debris remains on the forest floor ($F_{\mathrm{eco}}$). Structural poles must cure to $\le 1{,}200\,\mathrm{bp}$
($12\%$) moisture in solar kilns before Scenario~Xi release, wild foraging caps harvest at $\le 2{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$
($1$-in-$4$ plants) with mandatory \texttt{Iota}/\texttt{Chi} HPLC amatoxin clearance, and Layer~7
maintains \texttt{RCW 84.33} designated-forest-land tax protection.

\begin{invariant}[Mother-Tree Inviolability and Winter Dormancy Felling Lock]
\label{inv:waved-eta-forest}
\texttt{col-kmsd} denies coppice harvest whenever $\mathtt{mother\_tree\_refusal\_hit} \ne 0$,
$\mathtt{soil\_temp\_mk} \ge 278{,}150$, or $\mathtt{coarse\_woody\_debris\_bp} < 3{,}000$.
\end{invariant}

\section{Scenario Mu (\texttt{SCN-MU-EXTRACTION}): Biorefinery and Nutrient Sovereignty}
\label{sec:waved-mu}

Scenario~Mu closes Karl Marx and Justus von Liebig's ``metabolic rift'' through aerated static-pile
thermophilic composting, biochar pyrolysis, struvite phosphorus precipitation, and hyperaccumulator
phytomining.

\subsection{Prohibited Soil Export, Phytomining Airgap, and EPA 503 Resetting Hold}

Layer~5 places $100\%$ of living topsoil, \textit{Terra Preta}, biochar, vermicompost, and struvite
onto the \textbf{Inviolable Prohibited Export Register} ($\mathtt{topsoil\_biochar\_export\_req} \equiv 0$).
Critically, \texttt{col-kmsd} enforces a hard cryptographic and physical \textbf{Phytomining Quarantine
Airgap}: biomass harvested from heavy-metal hyperaccumulator plots (\textit{Alyssum}, \textit{ Pteris})
is tagged as toxic ore ash and routed \textit{exclusively} to Scenario~Theta's electrowinning cells
($\mathtt{food\_chain\_heavy\_metal\_mg} \equiv 0$). Humanure/biosolids batches must sustain EPA
Part~503 Class~A temperatures $\ge 328{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($55.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$) continuously for
$72\,\mathrm{hours}$ ($259{,}200{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}$, resetting to $0$ on any thermal dip), while
pyrolysis kilns lock out whenever wind speed reaches $\ge 6{,}667\,\mathrm{mm/s}$ ($24\,\mathrm{km/h}$).

\begin{invariant}[Zero Soil Export and Heavy-Metal Phytomining Quarantine]
\label{inv:waved-mu-quarantine}
Any workflow attempting external export of topsoil/biochar or routing phytomined biomass toward
\texttt{Gamma}/\texttt{Eta} agricultural soils is aborted immediately in \texttt{col-kmsd}.
\end{invariant}

\section{Scenario Theta (\texttt{SCN-THETA-FOUNDRY}): Induction Foundry and Urban Mining}
\label{sec:waved-theta}

Scenario~Theta governs induction metal-casting, scrap shredding, glass cullet remelting, and battery
hydrometallurgy under a strict \textbf{Three-Tier Constitutional Sourcing Hierarchy}: Tier~1 Direct
Component Reuse ($< 500\,\mathrm{bp}$ remelt exergy) $\succ$ Tier~2 Technospheric Urban Scrap $\succ$
Tier~3 Geospheric Virgin Mining.

\subsection{Virgin Extraction Last-Resort Block, $10\times$ ERC, and Scrubber Interlocks}

\texttt{col-kmsd} cryptographically blocks Tier~3 virgin extraction whenever recoverable urban scrap
exists anywhere across $R_1\text{--}R_5$ ($\mathtt{technospheric\_scrap\_avail\_mg} > 0$). Even when
regional scrap is genuinely zero, virgin extraction requires an $R_4$ Watershed Assembly supermajority
($\ge 6{,}600\,\mathrm{bp}$), a $10\times$ ($100{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$) ERC surcharge, and a $10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$
($100\%$) reclamation escrow, while induction melts require $\ge 8{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ crucible
packing, $\ge 7{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ cascade heat recovery, and active VOC/PM2.5 baghouse scrubbers.

\begin{invariant}[Technospheric Scrap Preemption Over Virgin Mining]
\label{inv:waved-theta-scrap}
Whenever \texttt{technospheric\_scrap\_avail\_mg} $> 0$, any Tier~3 virgin-mining workflow request
is unconditionally rejected by \texttt{col-kmsd}.
\end{invariant}

        return (t.watershed_yes_bp >= 6600U) &&
               (t.erc_surcharge_bp >= 100000U) &&
               (t.reclamation_escrow_bp >= 10000U);
    }
    return t.requested_tier == 1U || t.requested_tier == 2U;
}

\section{Scenario Xi (\texttt{SCN-XI-ARCH}): Reversible Timber Architecture and Wabi-Sabi Hearth}

\subsection{Anti-Dogma Material Variance vs.\ Structural Support and $R_0$ LiDAR Ban}

urban Stage~1/2 retrofit node may pragmatically use code-required gypsum or steel connectors (honestly
logged in Layer~3 ERC), while Support elements strictly require Scenario~Kappa civil co-signature,

mandate and $\mathtt{r0\_lidar\_scans\_count} \equiv 0$, anchored on CLT land ($\mathtt{real\_estate\_tokens\_minted} = 0$).

while $R_0$ Hearth Infill enforces $\mathtt{r0\_lidar\_scans\_count} \equiv 0$.
