\chapter{Cybernetics of Living Matter: Actuation Envelopes, Phenology, and Salvage Sagas}
\label{ch:cybernetics-salvage}

\epigraph{``Matter-energy enters the economic process in a state of low entropy and comes out of it in a state of high entropy; no mechanical equation can run a living or thermal transformation backward.''}{\textsc{Nicholas Georgescu-Roegen}, \textit{The Entropy Law and the Economic Process} (1971)}

A cloud workflow engine orchestrates symbols that obey reversible algebra. If an airline
reservation microservice crashes mid-transaction, rolling back a PostgreSQL row restores the
universe of discourse to a state indistinguishable from before the transaction began
\parencite{garciamolina1987sagas}. A sovereign orchestrator embedded in soil, broth, timber,
molten bronze, and microbial cultures enjoys no such luxury. Physical and biological matter
evolves continuously between discrete orchestrator ticks, integrates thermal and radiative
history non-linearly, and obeys the Second Law of Thermodynamics \parencite{georgescu1971,odum1971}.
One cannot un-boil split-pea potage, un-pour a cold-shut bronze casting, or un-lyse frost-damaged
pea meristems by executing a database \texttt{ROLLBACK}.

At the same time, attempting to turn Layer 4 (\texttt{col-execd}) into a hard-real-time
continuous differential-equation solver or raw GPIO bit-banger would destroy its deterministic
simplicity and violate functional safety engineering \parencite{iec61508,astrom2008feedback}. A
POSIX userspace process running Wasmtime bytecode at $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$ ($100\,\mathrm{ms}$ ticks)
is a \emph{supervisory cybernetic coordinator}---System 2 and System 3 in Beer's Viable System
Model \parencite{beer1972,cannon1932}---never a $1\,\mathrm{kHz}$ proportional-integral-derivative
(\texttt{PID}) loop or MOSFET gate driver. This chapter establishes the three foundational
bridges by which discrete $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$ integer choreography safely governs continuous
living and thermodynamic matter: (1) hardware-leased \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1} contracts
enforced autonomously on Layer 2 microcontrollers, (2) photo-thermal biological phenology and
piecewise-quadratic integer Bigelow lethality ($F_0$), and (3) the Two-Regime Saga Model
pivoted at physical actuation ($t_{\mathrm{actuate}}$) to conserve $10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ of
milligram mass across forward physical salvage sagas.

\section{Hardware-Leased \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1} and Edge Reflexes}
\label{sec:actuation-envelope-reflexes}

In naive domestic automation stacks, a high-level script opens a water valve or energizes a
$2.4\,\mathrm{kW}$ heating element by writing an unguarded boolean register (\texttt{valve\_open
= 1} or \texttt{heater\_on = true}) over a serial bus, intending to write \texttt{0} once a
subsequent poll observes the target moisture or temperature. During our audit of early Oasis
workflow definitions (Council Ceiling \#3), we noted how dangerous raw boolean actuation becomes
under fault injection: if \texttt{col-execd} stalls, if a UNIX socket buffer saturates, or if a
cable is unplugged while \texttt{valve\_open == 1}, a cistern can drain into a waterlogged root
zone (\texttt{moisture\_vwc\_bp >= 9200}) or a dry kettle shell can climb toward element burnout
(\texttt{kMaxDryVesselSafetyTempMk = 523150 mK}).

Furthermore, high-frequency physical stabilization---such as $1\,\mathrm{kHz}$ pulse-width
modulation (\texttt{PWM}) of an induction coil, zero-crossing triac firing, or over-current
crowbar tripping---requires microsecond interrupt determinism that non-real-time Linux userspace
cannot guarantee \parencite{liu1973scheduling,zephyr2024}. Layer 4 resolves both hazards by
strictly prohibiting raw GPIO or open-ended boolean actuator writes. Instead, \texttt{col-execd}
issues bounded, time-leased \textbf{Supervisory Actuation Envelopes} routed via Layer 3 down to
Layer 2 Zephyr RTOS field microcontrollers \parencite{zephyr2024}.

\subsection{Exact 64-Byte \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1} Memory Contract}

Every physical actuation command crossing from \texttt{col-execd} through \texttt{l3\_l4.sock}
to Layer 2 conforms to the cache-line-sized $64$-byte \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1} binary frame
shown in Listing~\ref{lst:actuation-envelope-v1}.

\begin{lstlisting}[language=C++, label={lst:actuation-envelope-v1}, caption={Exact 64-byte hardware-leased \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1} contract enforced by Layer 2 RTOS.}]

    uint64_t issued_hlc{0ULL};           // [8..15]  Hybrid Logical Clock issue stamp
    uint32_t target_pin_id{0U};          // [16..19] Canonical UHAI L2 actuator ID
    uint32_t lease_ttl_ms{5000U};        // [20..23] Autonomous L2 expiry horizon (ms)

static_assert(alignof(ActuationEnvelopeV1) == 64, "Envelope must be cache-line aligned.");
\end{lstlisting}

Each field in \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1} plays a distinct cybernetic and safety-critical role:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Monotonic Fencing (\texttt{fencing\_token}):} Derived from the Layer 3
    \texttt{L3ResourceLeaseLock} sequence counter \parencite{kleppmann2017designing}. The Layer 2
    Zephyr driver stores \texttt{max\_seen\_fencing\_token} per actuator pin and immediately drops
    any delayed or replayed frame with $\texttt{fencing\_token} < \texttt{max\_seen\_fencing\_token}$,
    making zombie-process actuation physically impossible.
    \item \textbf{Schmitt Hysteresis \& Duty Capping (\texttt{setpoint\_low}, \texttt{setpoint\_high},
    \texttt{deadband}, \texttt{max\_duty\_bp}):} Rather than toggling a relay across IPC ten
    times per second, Layer 2 runs a local $100\,\mathrm{Hz}\text{--}1\,\mathrm{kHz}$ closed
    reflex loop inside $[\texttt{setpoint\_low} - \texttt{deadband},\; \texttt{setpoint\_high} +
    \texttt{deadband}]$ capped at $\texttt{max\_duty\_bp} \in [0, 10{,}000]$ (enabling non-interrupting
    solar curtailment to cap heater power at $4{,}300\,\mathrm{bp}$ without changing thermal
    setpoints).
    \item \textbf{Hard Autonomous Lease Expiry (\texttt{lease\_ttl\_ms}, \texttt{safe\_fallback\_state}):}
    Upon accepting an envelope, the Layer 2 microcontroller arms a hardware watchdog countdown for
    \texttt{lease\_ttl\_ms} (typically $2{,}000\text{--}10{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}$). Active Layer 4
    workflows renew the lease periodically prior to half-TTL; if \texttt{col-execd} crashes, is
    killed by \texttt{SIGKILL}, or loses bus power, the Layer 2 timer expires and drives the
    actuator directly to \texttt{safe\_fallback\_state} (closing irrigation solenoids, de-energizing
    induction coils, and opening fail-safe vent dampers) within $< 1\,\mathrm{ms}$.
\end{itemize}

\begin{invariant}[Autonomous Layer 2 Safe-Parking Under Orchestrator Fault]
\label{inv:l2-safe-parking}
For any physical actuator controlled via \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1}, total uncommanded energy
or fluid delivery following an arbitrary crash, hang, or IPC severance of \texttt{col-execd} at
time $t_{\mathrm{fault}}$ is strictly upper-bounded by $\min\bigl(\texttt{dose\_cap\_units},\;
P_{\max} \cdot (\texttt{max\_duty\_bp}/10{,}000) \cdot \texttt{lease\_ttl\_ms}\bigr)$, after
which the hardware deterministically enters \texttt{safe\_fallback\_state} with zero Layer 4
intervention.
\end{invariant}

\section{Biological Time vs.\ Wall-Clock Time}
\label{sec:biological-vs-wallclock}

During the Specialist Council review (Ceiling \#3), two subtle biological modeling defects were
uncovered in early prototype code: first, \texttt{L4WorkflowInstance} ran a shadow soil
estimator inside Layer 4 gated by fixed wall-clock \texttt{maturity\_sim\_seconds}, diverging
from Layer 1b's physical \texttt{SoilCellRegisters}; second, \texttt{thermal\_vessel.hpp}
accumulated pasteurization seconds monotonically without resetting when a pot cooled back into
the bacterial danger zone. Living cells and bacterial spores do not read wall clocks---they
respond to continuous thermodynamic integrals.

\subsection{Photo-Thermal Growing Degree-Days (\texttt{thermal\_time\_mks}) and Rhizosphere Moisture}

In the ratified architecture, Layer 4 contains \emph{zero} biological solvers; it subscribes
strictly to physical or simulated rhizosphere state in \texttt{soil\_physics.hpp}. Rather than
declaring a pea crop mature after a fixed $43{,}200\,\mathrm{s}$ wall-clock timer regardless of
spring chill or overcast skies, phenological progression integrates photo-thermal biological
time (\texttt{thermal\_time\_mks}, measured in integer milli-Kelvin-seconds $\mathrm{mK\cdot s}$
above cultivar base temperature $T_{\mathrm{base}} = \texttt{min\_growth\_temp\_mk}$, e.g.,
$277{,}150\,\mathrm{mK} = 4.00^\circ\mathrm{C}$ for \emph{Pisum sativum}):
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:photo-thermal-mks}
\Delta \Theta_{\mathrm{mks}} = \left\lfloor
\frac{\Delta T_{\mathrm{eff,mk}}(T_{\mathrm{soil}}) \cdot \Phi_{\mathrm{PAR,bp}} \cdot \Phi_{\mathrm{VWC,bp}}(\theta) \cdot \Phi_{\mathrm{NPK,bp}}}{10^{12}}
\cdot \Delta t_{\mathrm{s}}
\right\rfloor,
\end{equation}
where $\Delta T_{\mathrm{eff,mk}}$ ramps linearly up to optimal plateau $[288{,}150,\,
301{,}150]\,\mathrm{mK}$ and tapers to zero at heat-stress ceiling $313{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$
($40.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$), $\Phi_{\mathrm{PAR,bp}} \in [0, 10{,}000]$ captures photosynthetically
active radiation, and Euclidean remainder accumulators (\texttt{growth\_sub\_accum},
\texttt{moisture\_sub\_accum}, \texttt{nitrogen\_sub\_accum}) preserve sub-unit remainders so
$\Delta t_{\mathrm{s}} = 1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $\Delta t_{\mathrm{s}} = 60\,\mathrm{s}$ produce
identical trajectories.

Root-zone Volumetric Water Content (\texttt{moisture\_vwc\_bp} $\in [0, 10{,}000]\,\mathrm{bp}$)
couples directly to soil carbon regeneration. Incorporating pyrolysis biochar and thermophilic
compost (\texttt{AmendSoilWithBiochar}, \texttt{AmendSoilWithCompost}) raises
\texttt{organic\_carbon\_bp}, expanding Terra Preta field water-holding capacity
(\texttt{ComputeSoilFieldCapacityBp}) linearly from $2{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($25.0\%$ VWC in
unamended mineral soil) up to $4{,}800\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($48.0\%$ VWC), cutting evaporative water
loss by up to $55\%$ via micropore capillarity, lengthening thermal relaxation time constant
$\tau_{\mathrm{soil}} \in [3{,}600,\, 18{,}000]\,\mathrm{s}$ against cold snaps, and supporting
symbiotic \emph{Rhizobium leguminosarum} nitrogen fixation (\texttt{+1 ppm} mineral $\mathrm{N}$
per $600\,\mathrm{s}$ at optimal warmth when \texttt{is\_legume\_fixing\_n == 1}).

\subsection{Continuous Cellular Stress Integral (\texttt{necrosis\_damage\_bp})}

When soil freezes or parches, growth does not merely pause harmlessly until next month: living
tissues accumulate irreversible cellular necrosis. Layer 1b/Layer 2 therefore integrates a
monotonic stress register $\texttt{necrosis\_damage\_bp} \in [0, 10{,}000]\,\mathrm{bp}$ driven
by four physiological hazard regimes:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Root-Zone Anoxia (\texttt{moisture\_vwc\_bp >= 9200 bp}):} Waterlogged pore
    spaces starve roots of dissolved $\mathrm{O_2}$ and invite \emph{Pythium} root rot.
    \item \textbf{Permanent Wilting Desiccation (\texttt{moisture\_vwc\_bp <= 600 bp}):} Xylem
    cavitation collapses leaf stipule turgor beyond rehydration recovery.
    \item \textbf{Intracellular Frost Lysis (\texttt{soil\_temp\_mk < 273150 mK}):} Sub-zero ice
    crystal nucleation ruptures unhardened meristem cell membranes.
    \item \textbf{Protein Heat Scald (\texttt{soil\_temp\_mk > 313150 mK}):} Supra-optimal soil
    and canopy temperatures denature Rubisco and abort legume blossom set.
\end{enumerate}

Whenever $\texttt{necrosis\_damage\_bp}$ crosses an interrupting boundary threshold (canonically
$6{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$), \texttt{col-storaged} fires an interrupting conditional event to
\texttt{col-execd}, aborting the doomed cultivation workflow immediately rather than wasting
weeks of scarce cistern water on dead vines, and transitioning into phytosanitary crop salvage.

\subsection{Piecewise-Quadratic Integer Bigelow Thermal Lethality ($F_0$) and the Danger-Zone Reset Rule}

Thermal food safety and bioprocess sterilization obey logarithmic first-order microbial death
kinetics established by \textcite{bigelow1921thermal}. The instantaneous lethality rate relative
to a reference temperature $T_{\mathrm{ref}}$ with thermal resistance constant $z$ (the
temperature increment required for a tenfold reduction in decimal reduction time $D$) is:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:bigelow-exact}
L(T) = 10^{(T - T_{\mathrm{ref}}) / z}.
\end{equation}
Evaluating $\texttt{pow}(10.0, \dots)$ via IEEE-754 \texttt{libm} inside a workflow predicate
is forbidden in \texttt{col-execd} because transcendental floating-point approximations vary in
their least-significant bits across C standard libraries and CPU microarchitectures. Instead,
the Sovereign Stack evaluates Bigelow lethality across two canonical biological regimes using
\textbf{exact integer decade-splitting and piecewise-quadratic interpolation}:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Open-Kettle Vegetative Pasteurization (\texttt{thermal\_vessel.hpp}):} Targets
    \emph{Salmonella}, \emph{Listeria}, and vegetative pathogens in atmospheric simmered potage
    using $T_{\mathrm{pasteur}} = 347{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($74.00^\circ\mathrm{C}$) and
    $z_{\mathrm{veg}} = 5{,}000\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($5.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$), requiring cumulative
    $\ge 120\,\mathrm{s}$ equivalent hold ($120{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}_{\mathrm{eq}}$).
    \item \textbf{Pressure-Retort Spore Sterilization ($F_0$):} Targets proteolytic
    \emph{Clostridium botulinum} spores in low-acid ($\mathrm{mpH} \ge 4{,}600$) shelf-stable
    commissary jars and medical autoclaves using canonical reference temperature
    $T_{\mathrm{ref}} = 394{,}260\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($121.11^\circ\mathrm{C}$ / $250^\circ\mathrm{F}$)
    and $z = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($10.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$).
\end{itemize}

For $F_0$ integration starting at lethal onset $T_{\mathrm{onset}} = T_{\mathrm{ref}} - 2z =
374{,}260\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($101.11^\circ\mathrm{C}$, where $L = 10^{-2}$), let $u = T -
T_{\mathrm{onset}}$, decade index $d = \min(4, \lfloor u / z \rfloor)$, and intra-decade
remainder $r = u \bmod z \in [0, 10{,}000)$. Over $r \in [0, z]$, the integer polynomial
$Q(r) = 1{,}000 + \lfloor 800 r / z \rfloor + \lfloor 8{,}200 r^2 / z^2 \rfloor$ maps $[0, z]
\to [1{,}000, 10{,}000]$ smoothly, monotonically, and with zero floating-point instructions!

Crucially, the Council also ratified the \textbf{Danger-Zone Non-Monotonic Reset Rule}: if an
unsealed culinary vessel cools below $327{,}000\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($53.85^\circ\mathrm{C}$),
entering the thermophilic and mesophilic bacterial incubation zone, any incomplete vegetative
pasteurization hold (\texttt{pasteurization\_hold\_s}, \texttt{lethality\_equivalent\_ms})
\textbf{must reset to zero}. Worse, if cumulative dwell below $327{,}000\,\mathrm{mK}$ (and
above refrigeration ceiling $277{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$) exceeds $7{,}200\,\mathrm{s}$ ($2\,\mathrm{h}$),
re-boiling is strictly prohibited: heat-stable enterotoxins synthesized by \emph{Staphylococcus
aureus} or \emph{Bacillus cereus} survive $373{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ boiling, forcing an immediate
interrupting condemnation into non-food salvage!

\begin{lstlisting}[language=C++, caption={Float-free piecewise-quadratic Bigelow $F_0$ rate and danger-zone non-monotonic reset.}]
inline constexpr uint32_t kDangerZoneUpperMk = 327000U; // 53.85 C incubation ceiling
inline constexpr uint32_t kF0ReferenceTempMk = 394260U; // 121.11 C botulinum reference
inline constexpr uint32_t kF0ZValueMk        = 10000U;  // 10.00 C decade z-slope
inline constexpr uint32_t kF0OnsetTempMk     = kF0ReferenceTempMk - (2U * kF0ZValueMk);

// Returns F_0 equivalent microseconds accumulated per 1 real second of hold:
// At 374,260 mK: 10,000 us/s (0.01x); at 394,260 mK: 1,000,000 us/s (1.00x).
[[nodiscard]] constexpr uint64_t ComputeBigelowF0RateUsPerSec(uint32_t temp_mk) noexcept {
    if (temp_mk < kF0OnsetTempMk) {
        return 0ULL;
    }
    const uint32_t over_mk = temp_mk - kF0OnsetTempMk;
    const uint32_t decades = (over_mk / kF0ZValueMk > 4U) ? 4U : (over_mk / kF0ZValueMk);
    const uint64_t r       = (decades >= 4U) ? 0ULL : static_cast<uint64_t>(over_mk % kF0ZValueMk);

    uint64_t mult = 10ULL; // 10 * 1000 = 10,000 us/s at -2 decades (374.26 K)
    for (uint32_t d = 0U; d < decades; ++d) {
        mult *= 10ULL;
    }
    const uint64_t quad = 1000ULL + ((800ULL * r) / 10000ULL)
                                  + ((8200ULL * r * r) / 100000000ULL);
    return mult * quad;
}

\section{Irreversible Entropy and the Two-Regime Saga Model}
\label{sec:two-regime-salvage-sagas}

What happens when a workflow must abort mid-flight---because solar curtailment starves an
induction furnace, a blizzard freezes a pea bed, or a power dip stalls a soup kettle in the
danger zone for three hours? In distributed database theory, \textcite{garciamolina1987sagas}
defined a \emph{Saga} as a sequence of local transactions $T_1, T_2, \dots, T_n$ paired with
backward compensating transactions $C_1, C_2, \dots, C_{n-1}$ such that executing
$T_1, \dots, T_k, C_k, \dots, C_1$ semantically erases the partial execution.

In cyber-physical orchestration, backward compensation hits a hard thermodynamic wall at the
exact Hybrid Logical Clock instant $t_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ when the first physical actuation
envelope opens or human hand transforms matter. Layer 4 formalizes this asymmetry as the
\textbf{Two-Regime Saga Model}:

\begin{definition}[The Thermodynamic Actuation Horizon ($t_{\mathrm{actuate}}$)]
\label{def:two-regime-saga}
Every BPMN workflow instance $W$ partitions its execution timeline at the irreversible physical
horizon $t_{\mathrm{actuate}}$:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Regime 1 ($t < t_{\mathrm{actuate}}$, Pre-Actuation Digital Escrow):} The
    workflow holds only informational reservations---Layer 3 inventory locks
    (\texttt{L3\_QUERY\_AND\_LOCK\_INVENTORY}), ordered station leases
    (\texttt{L3ResourceLeaseLock}), and unrevealed mesh \texttt{HTLC}s. Zero physical atoms have
    mixed, heated, cut, or moved. Aborting at $t < t_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ executes classical
    \emph{Backward Digital Rollback}, releasing all escrows in exact reverse acquisition order
    with zero mass or exergy loss ($\Delta S_{\mathrm{matter}} = 0$).
    \item \textbf{Regime 2 ($t \ge t_{\mathrm{actuate}}$, Post-Actuation Physical Entropy):}
    Physical matter has undergone irreversible chemical, thermal, mechanical, or biological
    change ($\Delta S_{\mathrm{univ}} > 0$). Backward rollback is forbidden by the Second Law of
    Thermodynamics. Any interrupting fault, timeout, or contamination boundary at
    $t \ge t_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ transitions the \texttt{BpmnTokenFrame} into state
    \texttt{SALVAGE\_COMPENSATING}, invoking a \textbf{Forward Physical Salvage Saga} that parks
    L2 hardware safely and routes every milligram of degraded matter down the ecological exergy
    cascade (\texttt{Scenario Mu} Biorefinery, Foundry Remelt, or Phytoremediation) while
    strictly conserving $10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($100.00\%$) of mass!
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Exact Milligram Mass Conservation Across Forward Salvage Sagas]
\label{thm:mass-conserving-salvage}
Let an interrupted Regime 2 workflow hold total escrowed physical input mass $M_{\mathrm{in}} \in
\mathbb{N}$ milligrams (adjusted for metered evaporative gas exchange $M_{\mathrm{evap}}$ logged
in \texttt{ThermalVesselRegisters}), and let a ratified salvage routing vector specify $k \ge 1$
downstream sink pools with integer basis-point weights $(w_1, \dots, w_k) \in \mathbb{N}^k$
satisfying $\sum_{j=1}^k w_j = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$. Under deterministic quotient-and-remainder
allocation
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:salvage-mass-conservation}
m_j = \left\lfloor \frac{M_{\mathrm{rem}} \cdot w_j}{10{,}000} \right\rfloor + \mathbb{I}(j = j^*)\cdot R_{\mathrm{rem}}, \qquad
R_{\mathrm{rem}} = M_{\mathrm{rem}} - \sum_{i=1}^k \left\lfloor \frac{M_{\mathrm{rem}} \cdot w_i}{10{,}000} \right\rfloor,
\end{equation}
where $M_{\mathrm{rem}} = M_{\mathrm{in}} - M_{\mathrm{evap}}$ and $j^* = \arg\max_j w_j$
(breaking ties by lowest index), total credited salvage mass satisfies
$\sum_{j=1}^k m_j = M_{\mathrm{rem}}$ with zero phantom ledger loss or creation, while every sink
lot $\ell_j$ receives a strictly lower thermodynamic exergy grade $\mathcal{G}(\ell_j) <
\mathcal{G}(\text{primary})$, preventing infinite salvage-to-primary oscillation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Summing Equation~\ref{eq:salvage-mass-conservation} across $j = 1, \dots, k$ yields:
\[
\sum_{j=1}^k m_j = \sum_{j=1}^k \left\lfloor \frac{M_{\mathrm{rem}} \cdot w_j}{10{,}000} \right\rfloor
+ R_{\mathrm{rem}} \cdot \sum_{j=1}^k \mathbb{I}(j = j^*)
= \sum_{j=1}^k \left\lfloor \frac{M_{\mathrm{rem}} \cdot w_j}{10{,}000} \right\rfloor + R_{\mathrm{rem}} = M_{\mathrm{rem}},
\]
since $j^*$ is uniquely selected and $0 \le R_{\mathrm{rem}} < k$ is non-negative because
$\sum_i \lfloor M_{\mathrm{rem}} w_i / 10{,}000 \rfloor \le M_{\mathrm{rem}} \sum_i w_i / 10{,}000
= M_{\mathrm{rem}}$. Furthermore, because each salvage sink class $\mathcal{G}(\ell_j)$ sits
strictly below culinary/structural primary grade in the finite lattice of material grades
($\text{Potable/Edible} \succ \text{Feed/Vinegar} \succ \text{BSFL/Digester} \succ
\text{Thermophilic/Pyrolysis} \succ \text{Inert Mineral}$), any chained secondary fault in a
salvage workflow can descend at most $4$ steps before terminating in inert mineral ash or
containment, guaranteeing finite termination.
\end{proof}

[[nodiscard]] constexpr bool ComputeMassBalancedSalvage(CompostRoutingIntent& intent) noexcept {
    const uint32_t w_sum = static_cast<uint32_t>(intent.sink_weights_bp[0]) +
                           static_cast<uint32_t>(intent.sink_weights_bp[1]) +
                           static_cast<uint32_t>(intent.sink_weights_bp[2]);

        return false;

    size_t   max_idx      = 0U;

            (intent.total_salvage_mass_mg * static_cast<uint64_t>(intent.sink_weights_bp[i])) / 10000ULL;

\section{Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$), Scavenged Layer 7 Hardware Friction, and the 3-Tier Hardware Fleet Progression (\texttt{CCI-6}, \texttt{D5}, \texttt{D8})}
\label{sec:erc-scavenged-l7-fleet-progression}

The Second Law of Thermodynamics governs not only the biological and culinary transformations inside a vessel, but also the physical tools, containers, fasteners, and electronic controllers that execute those transformations \parencite{georgescu1971,odum1971}. In both early smart-home prototypes and video-game resource managers, physical inventory is routinely flattened into a single homogeneous table where a fresh sourdough loaf and a cast-iron milling machine differ only by an item ID string. Worse, when games or simulations attempt to model technological progress, they almost universally invent fake RPG ``Tech Trees'' (``Spend 500 Science Points to unlock $+20\%$ Oven Efficiency'').

The Sovereign Stack v5.0.0-HORIZON rejects both abstractions (\texttt{CCI-3}, \texttt{CCI-6}, \texttt{D5}, \texttt{D8}). First, it establishes a strict thermodynamic demarcation between \textbf{Continuous Arrhenius Perishable Entropy} (the domain of \emph{The Pantry} lens) and \textbf{Discrete Mechanical Cycle Wear} (the domain of \emph{The Workshop} lens). Second, it prices every material component's cradle-to-cradle \textbf{Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$)} in canonical microjoules ($\mu\mathrm{J}$), driving voluntary convergence onto open modular geometry. Third, it models real-world Stage 1A bootstrap friction on \textbf{Scavenged Layer 7 Hardware} and replaces fictional RPG stat buffs with the physical \textbf{3-Tier Hardware Fleet Progression}.

\subsection{Continuous Arrhenius Perishable Entropy (\emph{The Pantry}) vs.\ Discrete Mechanical Cycle Wear (\emph{The Workshop})}
\label{subsec:arrhenius-pantry-vs-workshop-wear}

Why must Layer 6 separate \emph{The Pantry} from \emph{The Workshop} as distinct application lenses over \texttt{col-commonsd}, and why must \texttt{col-execd} schedule them under distinct mathematical clocks? Because perishable organic stock and durable mechanical tooling obey orthogonal entropy regimes at Layers 1, 2, and 3:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Continuous Arrhenius Perishable Entropy (\emph{The Pantry} Lens):}
    Biological foodstuffs, lacto-ferments, seed stocks, and clinical biologics (\texttt{pantry:StockLot}) degrade continuously with wall-clock time regardless of whether a human touches them. Their shelf-life Time-to-Live (\texttt{pantry:expiryHlcMs}) is governed by temperature-activated enzymatic and microbial respiration integrated continuously over millikelvin-milliseconds ($\mathrm{mK \cdot ms}$). When cold-chain storage holds $T(t) \in [275{,}150,\, 277{,}150]\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($2.0\text{--}4.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$), the Arrhenius hazard rate $\dot{h}_{\mathrm{spoil}}(T)$ remains minimal; if a door seal leaks or ambient temperature climbs to $293{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($20.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$), Layer 2 integrates the accelerated thermal-time depletion curve in integer basis points (\texttt{pantry:spoilageHazardBp}), triggering pre-spoilage neighbor surplus offers (\texttt{L3SurplusOffer}) or preventive retort canning workflows in Layer 4 before the lot crosses into Regime 2 waste.
    \item \textbf{Discrete Mechanical Cycle Wear (\emph{The Workshop} Lens):}
    Conversely, a box of $\mathrm{M6}$ socket-head cap screws, a 304-stainless Gastronorm pan, a hand plane, or a CNC spindle (\texttt{workshop:ToolAsset}) does \emph{not} rot on the shelf on Friday afternoon. Its degradation is calendar-agnostic and strictly proportional to discrete physical actuation: cumulative energized spindle milliseconds (\texttt{workshop:cumulativeUseMs}), thermal expansion-contraction cycles, and mechanical torque-impulse integrals (\texttt{workshop:wearIndexBp}). Consequently, \texttt{col-execd} never schedules tool calibration or bearing replacement off a naive 30-day calendar cron job; it triggers preventive maintenance workflows strictly when cumulative Layer 2 usage registers cross the tool's verified mechanical wear horizon (\texttt{workshop:calibrationDueUse}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Layer 3 Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$) and Emergent Modular Convergence}
\label{subsec:lambda-erc-modular-convergence}

In legacy Layer 7 markets, corporations deliberately proliferate fifteen incompatible battery sleds, bespoke plastic latch clips, proprietary fastener heads, and non-standard tray dimensions. Artificial fragmentation is lucrative under capitalism because the vendor externalizes cradle-to-grave extraction and landfill entropy onto the biosphere while capturing monopoly rents on replacement parts. How does the Sovereign Stack converge on universal, interchangeable hardware standards without establishing an authoritarian central standardization ministry \parencite{scott1998}?

The answer lies in Layer 3's (\texttt{col-storaged}) exact lifecycle accounting of \textbf{Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$)}. For every physical component or container class $c$ in the Ontological Graph, Layer 3 computes its per-cycle amortized replacement exergy in canonical 64-bit integer microjoules ($\mu\mathrm{J}$, where $1\,\mathrm{MJ} = 10^{12}\,\mu\mathrm{J}$):
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lambda-erc-definition}
\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}(c) = E_{\mathrm{embodied}}(c) + E_{\mathrm{transport}}(c) + \frac{E_{\mathrm{retooling\_amortized}}(c)}{N_{\mathrm{reuse\_cycles}}(c)} - E_{\mathrm{recoverable\_stock}}(c),
\end{equation}
where $E_{\mathrm{embodied}}(c)$ is the primary smelting, synthesis, or machining exergy required to produce $c$; $E_{\mathrm{transport}}(c)$ is the hauling exergy required to source $c$; $E_{\mathrm{retooling\_amortized}}(c)$ is the die, mold, or fixture setup exergy divided by the empirical lifetime reuse cycle count $N_{\mathrm{reuse\_cycles}}(c)$; and $E_{\mathrm{recoverable\_stock}}(c)$ is the closed-loop remelt or reuse credit recovered at end-of-life.

\begin{proposition}[Thermodynamic Gradient Descent Toward Open Modular Standards]
\label{prop:erc-modular-convergence}
Let $\mathcal{P}$ be the set of feasible workflow execution paths evaluated by \texttt{col-execd}'s Petri-net solver for a material transformation, where each path $p \in \mathcal{P}$ consumes direct operational exergy $E_{\mathrm{direct}}(p)$ and utilizes hardware/container set $C(p)$. When \texttt{col-execd} minimizes total lifecycle path exergy
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:erc-solver-objective}
J(p) = \sum_{s \in p} \Bigl( E_{\mathrm{direct}}(s) + \lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}\bigl(c(s)\bigr) \Bigr),
\end{equation}
and Layer 6's Orchestration Registry ranks recipe forks by empirical \texttt{collective:thermodynamicProof} efficiency, independent sovereign nodes voluntarily converge on open modular standards---specifically \textbf{Gastronorm GN 1/1 stainless pans ($530 \times 325\,\mathrm{mm}$)}, \textbf{ISO $600 \times 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ Euro-totes}, and \textbf{ISO metric $\mathrm{M6}$ / $\mathrm{M8}$ fasteners}---with zero bureaucratic coercion.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Compare a bespoke proprietary container or fastener $c_{\mathrm{prop}}$ against an open modular standard $c_{\mathrm{open}}$ (such as a 304-stainless Gastronorm GN 1/1 pan of $530 \times 325\,\mathrm{mm}$). For $c_{\mathrm{prop}}$, non-interchangeability across stations restricts $N_{\mathrm{reuse\_cycles}}(c_{\mathrm{prop}})$ to a small local count ($\sim 10^1\text{--}10^2$), leaving $E_{\mathrm{retooling\_amortized}} / N_{\mathrm{reuse\_cycles}}$ large, while potted or glued composite construction forces $E_{\mathrm{recoverable\_stock}}(c_{\mathrm{prop}}) \approx 0$ and requires long-distance Layer 7 courier replacement ($E_{\mathrm{transport}} \gg 0$). Conversely, because $c_{\mathrm{open}}$ fits steam ovens, cold-chain racks, cargo e-trike panniers, lacto-fermentation stations, and robotic grippers across all twenty-five Sovereign Scenarios, its reuse horizon approaches $N_{\mathrm{reuse\_cycles}}(c_{\mathrm{open}}) \ge 10^4$, driving $\lfloor E_{\mathrm{retooling\_amortized}} / N_{\mathrm{reuse\_cycles}} \rfloor \to 0$, while $100\%$ local alloy recyclability maximizes $E_{\mathrm{recoverable\_stock}}(c_{\mathrm{open}})$. Thus $\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}(c_{\mathrm{open}}) \ll \lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}(c_{\mathrm{prop}})$, so minimizing Equation~\ref{eq:erc-solver-objective} strictly selects $c_{\mathrm{open}}$.
\end{proof}
