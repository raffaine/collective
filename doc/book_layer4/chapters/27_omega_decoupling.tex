\chapter{Scenario Omega: The Sovereign Singularity (Terminal Decoupling)}

\section{The Legacy Paradigm \& Its Failures}
Layer 7 (The Legacy Proxy) is a necessary compromise during the Genesis phase, but it remains a critical vulnerability. Relying on a state-sanctioned Social Purpose Corporation (SPC) to hold land, pay fiat taxes, or shield members means the Node is ultimately subservient to extractive state mechanisms. On an individual level, legacy systems treat death as a final extraction event via probate courts, estate taxes, and the privatization of generational knowledge. True sovereignty is achieved only when a Node produces its own energy, food, and culture, stripping fiat of its coercive power. The legacy system fails because it demands infinite centralized growth, acting as a parasite on localized thermodynamic systems.

\section{The Incremental Automation Pathway}
The terminal decoupling from the fiat matrix is an irreversible event, approached in three phases:

\subsection{Phase 1: Human-in-the-Loop (Manual Orchestration)}
Citizens manually track the Node's energy and asset reserves. When a consensus believes the "Sovereignty Threshold" is met, human legal officers execute the dissolution of the SPC and manually sever banking APIs.

\subsection{Phase 2: The Centaur (AI-Assisted)}
The Orchestrator monitors physical asset reserves (e.g., biochar, solar capacity) and advises the Node when the Sovereignty Threshold is statistically breached. It drafts the final legal dissolution forms and queues the liquidation of fiat reserves, awaiting a final human multi-sig execution.

\subsection{Phase 3: Full Autonomy}
The AI autonomously detects the threshold and seamlessly triggers the `SovereignFederationIntent`. It liquidates remaining fiat, drops API webhooks to legacy banking, and switches all network routing to P2P decentralized infrastructure without requiring human prompting.

\section{The Human Boundary (Limits of Automation)}
The Orchestrator can seamlessly garbage-collect legacy software modules, tear down webhooks, and enforce a pure thermodynamic ledger peg. However, the AI cannot declare sovereignty on its own. The threshold is meaningless unless the human citizens can physically sustain their biological and communal lives off-grid. The final dissolution is a physical, social, and legal leap of faith that only the human collective can take. Furthermore, at an individual's end-of-life, the AI can re-route their tools, but humans must preserve their cultural and emotional legacy.

\section{Orchestration Mechanics (The ``How'')}
Scenario Omega is a systemic architecture shift. The BPMN engine orchestrates the `SovereignFederationIntent` by systematically deprecating external dependencies. The Orchestrator terminates all long-polling and webhook connections to centralized endpoints (e.g., Stripe, Plaid). 

Networking strictly falls back to localized routing protocols. The engine mandates `Gossipsub` for state synchronization across the decentralized ledger, relying on physical media (LoRaWAN, dark fiber) rather than standard ISP protocols. In the C++ Oasis implementation, legacy proxy modules (`LegacyUplinkManager`, `FiatReserveCounter`) are entirely de-allocated from memory, optimizing CPU cycles. The economic simulation transitions to a closed-loop system where Value Tokens are strictly pegged to localized Joules (exergy) and Biomass yield, rendering fiat calculations entirely obsolete within the Orchestrator's mathematical boundaries.
