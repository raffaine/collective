Every civilization encodes its theory of power into the machinery that coordinates daily
work. In the industrial and platform economies of the twentieth and early twenty-first
centuries, software orchestration evolved along two equally corrosive trajectories.
Enterprise workflow engines froze corporate chains of command into brittle approval
hierarchies, requiring maintenance tickets, managerial sign-offs, and credentialed rank
before a leaking pipe could be mended or a hungry neighbor fed \parencite{scott1998}. Later,
platform capitalism replaced the human middle manager with algorithmic dispatchers that
priced desperation in real time, tracked every bodily pause through pervasive telemetry, and
maintained workers in a permanent state of sympathetic fight-or-flight
\parencite{zuboff2019,han2015}. Most recently, an industry enchanted by probabilistic
foundation models has proposed installing stochastic language generators directly atop
physical infrastructure---inviting systems that hallucinate plausibly, drift across prompt
revisions, and cannot reproduce their own state transitions to act as opaque executives over
microgrids, water pumps, and human schedules.

The Sovereign Stack rejects both the bureaucratic gatekeeper and the stochastic boss. In
Volume~1 of this series, we introduced the seven-layer constitutional architecture of The
Collective, establishing how physical truth enters from hardware reflexes (Layer~2) into an
append-only, conflict-free thermodynamic ledger (Layer~3), how polycentric human assemblies
and cryptographic webs of trust ratify collective policy (Layer~5), how natural-language
synthesis assists deliberation without holding execution authority (Layer~6), and how legal
and economic carapaces insulate the commons from extractive markets (Layer~7). Positioned at
the exact waist of that hourglass sits \textbf{Layer~4: The Deterministic Orchestrator},
realized in POSIX silicon as the hardened C++20 daemon \texttt{col-execd}.

This companion monograph provides the complete engineering specification, formal concurrency
theory, cybernetic control mathematics, and 25-scenario operational synthesis for Layer~4 at
Version~3.1. Its central thesis is uncompromising: \textit{the executive engine of a living
community must be mathematically deterministic, cryptographically blind to personal
identity, stateless in volatile memory, bounded by physical conservation laws, and calm in
its relationship to human attention} \parencite{weiser1996calm,illich1973}.

\section*{Why Determinism Is a Constitutional Requirement}

When software crosses the threshold from rendering pixels to opening irrigation solenoids,
energizing induction vessels, routing cargo e-trikes, and tripping high-voltage
direct-current (HVDC) contactors, nondeterminism ceases to be a minor UX annoyance and
becomes a physical hazard \parencite{iec61508}. A stochastic language model asked whether to
energize a greenhouse heater on a freezing, cloud-bound night may produce two opposite
answers across identical sensor states depending on sampling temperature, floating-point
accumulation order, or subtle phrasing artifacts. Worse, when a failure occurs---when a
battery bank is drawn below its safe depth of discharge or an aquifer is pumped past its
recharge floor---a probabilistic black box offers post-hoc confabulation rather than an
auditable proof trace.

In the Sovereign Stack, artificial intelligence and semantic language translation live
strictly in Layer~6, above the governance membrane. Layer~6 may help Stewards draft a
seasonal crop rotation, summarize hydrological forecasts, or translate a plain-spoken
household need into a candidate process graph. Yet no output of Layer~6 ever touches an
actuator or mutates an active schedule directly. Every candidate process must pass downward
through Layer~5 governance ratification, be compiled into a content-addressed Business
Process Model and Notation (BPMN~2.0) state machine \parencite{omg2011bpmn}, and pass static
verification before entering \texttt{col-execd}.

Inside Layer~4, execution proceeds on a strict $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$ ($100\,\mathrm{ms}$)
deterministic tick loop inside capability-sandboxed WebAssembly (\texttt{Wasmtime}) modules
\parencite{haas2017wasm,miller2006robust}. Floating-point instructions are banned outright
at module load time; all thermodynamic, agronomic, and stoichiometric calculations execute
in canonical 64-bit integer International System (SI) units with carry-save Euclidean
remainder accumulators. Given an identical Layer~3 ledger snapshot and an identical Hybrid
Logical Clock (HLC) tick \parencite{kulkarni2014logical}, an \texttt{x86\_64} salvaged
laptop in a workshop, an \texttt{aarch64} single-board computer in a solar shed, and a
\texttt{riscv64} controller in a root cellar are guaranteed to compute bit-for-bit identical
Merkle state transitions, reserve identical microjoule exergy leases, and emit identical
actuation envelopes. Determinism is not rigidity for its own sake; it is the precondition
for democratic auditability. Citizens can govern an autonomous infrastructure engine only if
its behavior is an exact, inspectable consequence of the covenants they ratified.

\section*{Lessons from the Oasis Proving Ground (\texttt{Layer 1b})}

Theory untempered by simulation easily degenerates into vapourware, while infrastructure
deployed directly onto living land without a proving ground risks catastrophic harm.
Throughout the development of the Sovereign Stack, \textbf{Oasis (\texttt{Layer 1b})}---our
pure-integer Software-in-the-Loop (SITL) digital twin and interactive simulation proving
ground \parencite{grieves2014digital}---has served as the crucible in which Layer~4's
abstractions were tested against coupled physical constraints.

On the canonical proving parcel of \textbf{Lot~402}, live C++20 execution across
\texttt{SharedStackRuntime}, \texttt{col-execd}, and \texttt{col-swarmd} validated six
foundational architectural commitments that anchor Part~I of this volume:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Pure-Integer SI Physics and Zero-Sum Ledger Coupling:} Core physical and
        economic registers---spanning root-zone soil cells (\texttt{SoilCellRegisters}),
        culinary pasteurization vessels (\texttt{ThermalVesselRegisters}), legume cultivars
        (\texttt{CultivarWorkflowSpec}), and pairwise Bilateral Clearing Channels
        (\texttt{L3PeerBccRecord})---execute with zero IEEE-754 floating-point arithmetic,
        preserving exact microjoule ($\mu\mathrm{J}$) conservation and piecewise-quadratic
        Bigelow thermal lethality \parencite{bigelow1921thermal}.
\item \textbf{Clean-Slate Genesis and the Read-Only L4 Membrane:} Lot~402 boots on Day~1
        with zero unsolicited workflows (\texttt{workflows\_.empty()}). No process runs
        until a Steward expresses an intent and ratifies a governance transition, while
        \texttt{col-gatewayd} enforces HTTP \texttt{405 Method Not Allowed}
        (\texttt{L4\_IS\_STRICTLY\_READ\_ONLY\_NO\_MUTATIONS}) against any attempt by a user
        interface to bypass Layer~5 governance and mutate Layer~4 directly.
\item \textbf{Multi-Scale Homestead and Neighborhood Topology:} Even when operating in
        solo-household mode prior to joining a neighborhood federation, Lot~402 coordinates
        a four-node personal cluster (\texttt{node-01:office}, \texttt{node-02:garage},
        \texttt{node-03:garden}, and \texttt{node-04:hearth}), mediating cross-SBC
        requisitions and material handoffs before seamlessly toggling to external Trust Ring
        $R_1$ peer clusters via deterministic \texttt{SplitMix64} affinity and zero-sum
        \texttt{BCC} clearing.
\item \textbf{Hardware-Gated Epistemic Privacy and Modality Switching:} Rather than
        hallucinating room or soil conditions when sensors are absent, the hardware bus
        returns \texttt{DENIED\_NO\_L2\_SENSOR} until physical transducers
        (\texttt{soil\_probe\_v1}, \texttt{irrigation\_valve\_v1},
        \texttt{culinary\_vessel\_v1}) are explicitly commissioned, dynamically shifting
        BPMN tasks between \texttt{MANUAL\_HUMAN\_OBSERVATION} and
        \texttt{AUTOMATED\_L2\_EXECUTION}.
\item \textbf{Declarative Cultivar Parameterization and Matter-Phase Hauling:} A single
        ratified BPMN lifecycle (\texttt{pea\_garden\_and\_soup\_lifecycles.bpmn.json}) is
        parameterized via JSON-LD catalogs (\texttt{fabaceae\_cultivars.jsonld}) across
        diverse nitrogen-fixing and soil-consuming cultivars, while physical transport
        enforces container invariants across liquids, granular particulates, and heavy
        discrete solids.
\item \textbf{Somatic Agency Without Gamification:} By separating task visibility into
        voluntary two-stage pull queues (\texttt{Available} versus \texttt{Committed}),
        rendering human states through qualitative 1-to-5 somatic feel tiers rather than
        productivity leaderboards, and engaging burnout hysteresis loops, Lot~402
        demonstrated that complex physical logistics can run without algorithmic coercion.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Convening the Five-Specialist Architectural Council}

Precisely because Oasis made Layer~4 concrete, it also exposed the exact structural ceilings
where a single-lot prototype would fracture under full-scale community operation. Moving
from six seed workflows on a suburban parcel to a mature polycentric node revealed subtle
prototype shortcuts that could not survive production deployment: hardcoded imperative
\texttt{if/else} ladders masquerading as BPMN evaluation; single-instance workflow locks
that prevented planting four staggered legume beds simultaneously; shadow soil-moisture
arithmetic leaking into Layer~4 structs; culinary lethality timers that failed to reset upon
danger-zone cooling dips; missing Hybrid Logical Clock lease expirations on peer task
claims; a $1000\times$ unit-scaling discrepancy between kilojoules and megajoules in an
upward yield packet field; and a two-card UI pagination model that would collapse under
hundreds of concurrent processes.

To resolve these ceilings systematically rather than through ad-hoc patches, a
\textbf{Five-Specialist Layer~4 Architectural Council} was convened:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The \textbf{Oasis \& Runtime Systems Architect} audited the live POSIX daemon topology, C++20 memory layouts, ahead-of-time BPMN-to-WebAssembly compilation pipeline, fuel-metered \texttt{Wasmtime} host ABI, and epistemic provenance gates.
    \item The \textbf{BPMN Choreography \& Concurrency Theorist} formalized multi-instance token semantics, cache-aligned 64-byte token frames, mediated intra-node signal and correlation-key choreography, and lexicographic resource lease ordering to eliminate Coffman deadlocks \parencite{coffman1971deadlocks}.
    \item The \textbf{Distributed Mesh \& Protocol Architect} established strict Viable System Model (VSM) Recursion Level~$N$ closure \parencite{beer1972,beer1979}, multi-hop Bilateral Clearing Channel (\texttt{BCC}) and Hashed Timelock Contract (HTLC) settlement, Ricardian \texttt{Treaty\_CID} guardrails \parencite{grigg2004}, and partition-tolerant choreography across Trust Rings $R_0$ through $R_4$.
    \item The \textbf{Cybernetics \& Bioprocess Control Engineer} purged shadow physics from Layer~4, replacing boolean actuator writes with hardware-leased 64-byte actuation envelopes (\texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1}), photo-thermal growing degree-day phenology, and Second-Law forward physical salvage sagas \parencite{georgescu1971,garciamolina1987sagas}.
    \item The \textbf{Sovereign Stack \& Human Factors Auditor} purged legacy conceptual drift, formalized DID-blinded 24-byte \texttt{ClaimHandle} credentials, and engineered Autonomic/Somatic plane separation, spatial round coalescing, and Little's Law ($\rho_{\max} = 0.70$) admission control \parencite{little1961proof} so human serenity deepens rather than erodes at high workflow density.
\end{itemize}

\section*{The Reality of High-Density Node Choreography}

Why must an edge orchestrator running on frugal, twenty-year-lifespan hardware be engineered
for hundreds or thousands of simultaneous state machines? A recurring illusion in software
design is to imagine community life as one workflow at a time: sow a garden bed, harvest
peas, simmer soup. In living reality---as systematically mapped across the \textbf{25
Sovereign Scenarios} (\texttt{Scenario Zero} through \texttt{Scenario Omega})---even a
modest household or neighborhood commons is a densely interwoven metabolic fabric.

While four staggered raised beds accumulate photo-thermal degree-days at different
phenological stages, a solar MPPT array continuously balances a $48\,\mathrm{V}$
$\mathrm{LiFePO_4}$ battery bank; a thermophilic compost bay progresses through
pathogen-sanitizing heat curves; a bone-broth vessel holds pasteurization lethality on the
hearth; greywater reed beds cycle hydraulic dosing valves \parencite{kadlec1996}; e-trike
milk-runs clear neighborhood tiffin and tool transfers; workshop jigs await glue cure
windows; perinatal and elder care pods maintain uninterrupted baseline warmth and clinical
power; and reproducible software builds (\texttt{Scenario Zero}) donate $80\%$ of their
compute waste heat into hydronic floor loops.

\section*{Structure of This Companion Volume}

This book is organized into two complementary parts so that systems engineers, control
theorists, and community stewards can read it both as a rigorous daemon specification and as
a living encyclopedic reference across every domain of civic life.

\textbf{Part~I (Chapters~1--8)} establishes the core architecture of the Deterministic Orchestrator:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Chapter~1} specifies the bilateral Unix-socket membrane (\texttt{l3\_l4.sock}
        and \texttt{l4\_l5.sock}), the Constitutional Intent U-Turn, keyless cryptographic
        blindness via 24-byte \texttt{ClaimHandle} tokens, and Stafford Beer's VSM Recursion
        Level~$N$ closure.
\item \textbf{Chapter~2} details the ahead-of-time BPMN~2.0 bytecode compiler, the
        Zero-Float Law across heterogeneous CPU architectures, canonical fixed-point SI unit
        scaling, and the cache-aligned 64-byte \texttt{BpmnTokenFrame} 12-state machine.
\item \textbf{Chapter~3} formalizes stateless execution over Layer~3 atomic
        \texttt{BatchStepCommit} frames, deadlock-free lexicographic
        \texttt{L3ResourceLeaseLock} ordering, the Constitutional 5-Tier Priority Queue, SoC
        hysteresis fuel shedding, and lookahead horizon admission control.
\item \textbf{Chapter~4} develops high-density intra-node choreography across hundreds of
        concurrent workflows using Layer~3 broadcast signals, 256-bit correlation keys, and
        acyclic temporal Bill-of-Materials (BOM) directed acyclic graphs.
\item \textbf{Chapter~5} scales choreography across nodes---from the 4-node solo homestead
        cluster to neighborhood \texttt{BCC}/HTLC clearing, BPVP kinetic freight dispatch,
        Ricardian \texttt{Treaty\_CID} guardrails, and mesh-partition compensation sagas.
\item \textbf{Chapter~6} grounds supervisory cybernetics in physical reality:
        hardware-leased 64-byte \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1} contracts, photo-thermal
        biological time, Bigelow pasteurization safety, and Second-Law forward physical
        salvage sagas.
\item \textbf{Chapter~7} protects human autonomy at scale through Qualitative Friction,
        somatic feel-tier telemetry, spatial round coalescing, causal curtailment grouping,
        and Little's Law capacity bounds.
\item \textbf{Chapter~8} examines the Oasis (\texttt{Layer 1b}) proving ground in full
        code-level detail, documenting its UHAI ring-buffer demarcation, the Council's seven
        structural audits and fixes, and six-tier epistemic provenance gates.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Part~II (Chapters~9--14)} synthesizes concrete multi-workflow choreographies across all \textbf{25 Sovereign Scenarios}, grouped into six horizontal metabolic waves (\textbf{Waves~A through~F}): from autopoietic software self-hosting (\texttt{Zero}), pluralistic genesis (\texttt{Tau}), and somatic homeostasis (\texttt{Sigma}) in Chapter~9; through nourishment (\texttt{Gamma}), solar fabrication (\texttt{Alpha}), and five-artery transit (\texttt{Beta}) in Chapter~10; perinatal care (\texttt{Delta}), kinship (\texttt{Omicron}), Bayesian apprenticeship (\texttt{Kappa}), and hospitality (\texttt{Zeta}) in Chapter~11; silviculture (\texttt{Eta}), biorefineries (\texttt{Mu}), metallurgy (\texttt{Theta}), textiles (\texttt{Nu}), and open-building architecture (\texttt{Xi}) in Chapter~12; coworking (\texttt{Epsilon}), epistemic science (\texttt{Iota}), cultural commons (\texttt{Lambda}), and craft guilds (\texttt{Pi}) in Chapter~13; to acute paramedics (\texttt{Chi}), ecological shock (\texttt{Upsilon}), elder sanctuary (\texttt{Phi}), restorative justice (\texttt{Psi}), adversarial immunity (\texttt{Rho}), and terminal legacy decoupling (\texttt{Omega}) in Chapter~14.
