\chapter{Multi-Scale Inter-Node Collaborations Across Sovereign Meshes}
\label{ch:internode-mesh}

\epigraph{``When resource users design their own operational rules within nested enterprises, polycentric systems achieve adaptive coherence that neither central Leviathans nor atomized markets can sustain.''}{\textsc{Elinor Ostrom}, \textit{Governing the Commons} (1990)}

Chapter~\ref{ch:intranode-choreography} demonstrated how hundreds of concurrent workflows
execute safely inside a single governed node without shared mutable memory. Yet no household,
workshop, or cooperative campus is a closed thermodynamic island \parencite{odum1971}. Even on
a solo homestead lot, embedded single-board computers (\texttt{SBC}s) are physically
distributed across distinct thermal, moisture, and dust zones---an indoor desk coordinator, a
sawdust-laden joinery garage, an outdoor weatherproof agroecology enclosure, and an induction
kitchen hearth. Beyond the lot boundary, neighboring households form Ring 1 (\texttt{R1})
mutual-aid clusters, Stage 2 cooperatives pool commissary and CNC fabrication capacity, and
Stage 3 watershed federations span intermittent LoRa and Wi-Fi HaLow links across rugged
terrain.

A conventional cloud orchestrator handles multi-node distribution by electing a central Raft
or Paxos cluster leader that treats every remote room, workshop, and household as a
subordinate worker thread. In the Sovereign Stack, such centralization is constitutionally
prohibited. Under Stafford Beer's Viable System Model (\texttt{VSM}) Recursion Theorem
\parencite{beer1972,beer1979}, every viable system contains viable systems and is embedded
within wider viable systems, each retaining full Recursion Level-$N$ operational autonomy over
its own metabolic floor. If a neighborhood fiber cut or wildfire power shutoff severs
communication between two streets, neither street's kitchen, cistern, or perinatal pod may
stall waiting for a remote master scheduler.

BPMN 2.0 provides the exact structural distinction required to honor \texttt{VSM} recursion
\parencite{omg2011bpmn,chinosi2012bpmn}: whereas an intra-node \emph{Process} moves tokens
across sequence flows inside a single engine, an inter-node \emph{Collaboration} treats each
sovereign node as an opaque black-box \emph{Participant Pool}. Sequence flows can never cross
a pool boundary; pools coordinate exclusively via asynchronous, schema-validated \emph{Message
Flows}. This chapter traces how Layer 4 realizes pool-isolated BPMN collaborations across
three spatial and institutional scales---the intra-lot four-node cluster, the neighborhood
bilateral clearing mesh, and the delay-tolerant bioregional federation---while preserving
zero-coin thermodynamic accounting and clean right-of-exit fission \parencite{hirschman1970}.

\section{Micro-Scale Choreography: The Intra-Lot Multi-Node Cluster (\texttt{SOLO\_4\_NODE\_CLUSTER})}
\label{sec:micro-scale-four-node}

Inter-node choreography begins before a steward ever federates with external neighbors. On a
real physical homestead---modeled canonically by Lot 402 in the Oasis \texttt{Layer 1b}
proving ground and \texttt{SharedStackRuntime}---running long analog thermistor and solenoid
wires from backyard keyline beds through masonry walls into a living-room desktop invites
ground loops, lightning surge coupling, and single-point hardware failure. Instead, even a
solo steward operates a personal four-node sovereign cluster (\texttt{SOLO\_4\_NODE\_CLUSTER},
serialized in snapshot state as \texttt{SOLO\_STEWARD\_4\_NODE\_CLUSTER} when external
\texttt{R1} swarm federation is paused), partitioning daemons and \texttt{L2} transducers
across four specialized zone nodes connected over local Layer 3 mesh transport
(\texttt{col-meshd}).

\subsection{Matter-Phase Hauling Physics Across Pool Boundaries}

Crucially, completing a digital message flow between \texttt{node-02:garage} and
\texttt{node-03:garden} does not teleport physical matter across fourteen meters of yard!
Every cross-pool material transfer must instantiate an explicit BPMN hauling task governed by
the pure-integer mechanics of \texttt{hauling\_physics.hpp} and typed by \texttt{HaulModality}.
Layer 4 enforces three physical laws of matter-phase transport across four canonical
cross-room hauling modalities, summarized in Table~\ref{tab:haul-modality-laws}:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Law 1 --- Liquid Containment (\texttt{BUCKET\_LIQUID} and \texttt{TIFFIN\_CARRIER}):}
    Uncontained bare-handed liquid capacity is strictly zero milliliters
    (\texttt{kBarehandLiquidMaxMl == 0u}). Any workflow step moving cistern water to raised
    beds ($10{,}000\,\mathrm{mL}$ in \texttt{wf.garden.irrigate\_and\_weed\_manual}) without an
    escrowed \texttt{watering\_can} or \texttt{bucket} evaluates to
    \texttt{IMPOSSIBLE\_NEEDS\_LIQUID\_VESSEL}. Similarly, transferring $2{,}000\,\mathrm{mL}$
    ($2{,}160\,\mathrm{g}$) of pasteurized split-pea soup from \texttt{node-04:hearth} to the
    porch handoff station requires \texttt{TIFFIN\_CARRIER} (\texttt{TIFFIN\_CARRIER\_HAUL}),
    verifying hot-fill containment $\ge 338{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($65.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$) inside
    insulated 304-stainless vessels.
    \item \textbf{Law 2 --- Granular Particulate Divisibility (\texttt{HAND\_GRANULAR}):}
    Divisible bulk solids such as topsoil, thermophilic compost, and pyrolysis biochar
    ($49{,}000\,\mathrm{g}$ total in \texttt{wf.garden.plow\_and\_amend\_soil}) can technically
    be scooped bare-handed (\texttt{GRANULAR\_HAUL}), but \texttt{HaulingPhysics} caps bare-hand
    scoops at \texttt{kBarehandGranularHandfulMassG = 500u} and penalizes movement speed to
    \texttt{kBarehandGranularSpeedBp = 3500u} ($35\%$), requiring $\lceil 49{,}000 / 500 \rceil
    = 98$ tedious round trips (\texttt{VERY\_SLOW\_BAREHAND\_GRANULAR}) versus $3$ brisk trips
    at $9{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ with a $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ bucket or $1$ trip at
    $10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ with a $120\,\mathrm{kg}$ wheelbarrow.
    \item \textbf{Law 3 --- Discrete Solid Lift Ceilings (\texttt{WHEELBARROW\_HEAVY}):}
    Indivisible fabricated assemblies obey ergonomic human biomechanics: unpenalized walking
    speed holds up to \texttt{kBarehandComfortSolidMassG = 20000u} ($20\,\mathrm{kg}$,
    permitting bare-handed carry of the $18.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ trellis kit), degrades linearly up
    to the hard human lift ceiling \texttt{kBarehandMaxSolidLiftMassG = 35000u} ($35\,\mathrm{kg}$,
    barely permitting the $32\,\mathrm{kg}$ cedar bed frame at reduced speed), and strictly
    rejects heavier indivisible units (\texttt{IMPOSSIBLE\_TOO\_HEAVY\_BAREHAND}). Thus hauling
    the $68\,\mathrm{kg}$ \texttt{TLUD} pyrolysis kiln, $74\,\mathrm{kg}$ rotating compost drum,
    or $115\,\mathrm{kg}$ predator-proof poultry coop from \texttt{node-02:garage} to
    \texttt{node-03:garden} (\texttt{WHEELBARROW\_DOLLY\_HEAVY\_HAUL}) mandates acquiring an L3
    resource lease on a \texttt{wheelbarrow} ($120\,\mathrm{kg}$ ceiling) or \texttt{hand\_dolly}
    ($200\,\mathrm{kg}$ ceiling, requiring \texttt{path\_is\_paved == true}).
\end{enumerate}

For a one-way voxel distance $d_{\mathrm{vox}}$ ($1\,\mathrm{voxel} = 1{,}000\,\mathrm{mm}$) at
base walking speed $v_0 = 1{,}400\,\mathrm{mm/s}$, \texttt{EstimateTotalHaulDurationSimMs}
computes deterministic round-trip transport time in integer milliseconds ($4{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}$
load/unload handling overhead per trip):
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:haul-duration-ms}
T_{\mathrm{haul,ms}} = N_{\mathrm{trips}} \cdot \left( \left\lfloor \frac{2{,}000 \cdot \max(1, d_{\mathrm{vox}}) \cdot 1{,}000}{\max\bigl(100,\, \lfloor v_0 \cdot \eta_{\mathrm{speed,bp}} / 10{,}000 \rfloor\bigr)} \right\rfloor + 4{,}000 \right),
\end{equation}
ensuring BPMN message-flow correlation timers across Lot 402 reflect real physical footsteps
and equipment constraints.

\section{Meso-Scale Collaborations: Neighborhood Swarm (\texttt{col-swarmd}), BPVP, and Bilateral Clearing}
\label{sec:meso-scale-bpvp-bcc}

When a steward enables Ring 1 (\texttt{R1}) federation, the four-node homestead cluster joins
a neighborhood and cooperative mesh (Stages 1B--2). In the live stack, \texttt{col-swarmd}
(\texttt{swarm\_engine.hpp}) models three peer neighborhood clusters around Lot 402:
\texttt{cluster:north\_oak\_commons} (5 households specializing in agroecology and soil),
\texttt{cluster:creekside\_guild} (4 garages specializing in joinery and heavy haul), and
\texttt{cluster:sunnyside\_care\_ring} (6 families specializing in hearth tiffins and
postpartum/elder care).

\subsection{\texttt{SplitMix64} Simulation Determinism vs.\ Live BPVP \texttt{FIB} Selection}

In reproducible simulation harnesses (\texttt{col-swarmd}), peer task claims and
semi-stochastic surplus offers must execute with bit-for-bit determinism across \texttt{x86\_64}
and \texttt{ARM64} architectures without touching IEEE-754 floats or OS entropy pools.
\texttt{SwarmState} therefore embeds an $8$-byte aligned \texttt{SplitMix64} integer generator
stepped with golden-ratio increment \texttt{0x9E3779B97F4A7C15ULL} and Stafford mixing
constants \texttt{0xBF58476D1CE4E5B9ULL} and \texttt{0x94D049BB133111EBULL}. When a local task
is released to \texttt{R1} peers (\texttt{RELEASED\_TO\_R1\_PEERS}),
\texttt{SelectClusterIndexForTask} routes $75\%$ of claims by physical specialty affinity
(joinery and heavy hauls to Creekside Guild; hearth tiffins to Sunnyside Care Ring; soil tasks
to North-Oak Commons) and $25\%$ via \texttt{SplitMix64} neighborhood load-sharing, firing mesh
cadence events against an integer basis-point threshold (\texttt{event\_probability\_bp = 7000U}).

In live multi-node deployments, synthetic PRNG selection is replaced by Layer 5's
\textbf{Border Policy Vector Protocol (\texttt{BPVP})}---a sovereignty-preserving path-vector
routing protocol inspired by BGP-4 \parencite{rekhter2006} but advertising ratified treaty
capabilities, surplus exergy capacity, and bilateral credit headroom rather than IP prefixes.
Layer 5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}) compiles verified \texttt{BPVP} announcements into a local
\textbf{Forwarding Information Base (\texttt{FIB})}. When \texttt{col-execd} yields a stock
miss over \texttt{l4\_l5.sock} under \texttt{R1\_MESH\_PEERS} policy, \texttt{col-kmsd} queries
its \texttt{BPVP FIB} for the lowest-friction treaty-compliant peer route and injects a signed
\texttt{Dispatch\_Intent} back down \texttt{l4\_l5.sock} containing blinded peer endpoint
handles---keeping \texttt{col-execd} completely stateless with respect to mesh topology.

\subsection{Pairwise Bilateral Clearing Channels (\texttt{BCC}) with Zero Global Coin}

How do autonomous neighborhood clusters reciprocate thermodynamic labor, cedar timber, biochar,
and hot tiffins without reintroducing fiat money, speculative utility tokens, or a global
blockchain ledger? Layer 3 (\texttt{col-storaged}) implements \textbf{Pairwise Bilateral
Clearing Channels (\texttt{BCC})}, represented in \texttt{shared\_stack\_runtime.hpp} by
\texttt{L3PeerBccRecord}. Every federated neighbor pair $(u, v)$ maintains an isolated,
mutually co-signed accounting register denominated strictly in integer microjoules ($\mu\mathrm{J}$)
of embodied exergy \parencite{odum1971,ostrom1990}:

\begin{invariant}[Pairwise Zero-Sum Exergy Clearing and Credit Ceilings]
\label{inv:bcc-zero-sum}
For every active bilateral treaty edge $\{u, v\}$ with mutually ratified credit window
$L_{u,v} \ge 0$ (defaulting to $200\,\mathrm{MJ} = 200{,}000{,}000{,}000\,\mu\mathrm{J}$ in
\texttt{L3PeerBccRecord::credit\_limit\_uj}), the signed channel balances
$B_{u \to v}, B_{v \to u} \in \mathbb{Z}$ satisfy at every committed tick:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:bcc-invariants}
B_{u \to v} + B_{v \to u} = 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad |B_{u \to v}| \le L_{u,v}.
\end{equation}
Any proposed settlement delta $\Delta_{u \to v} \in \mathbb{Z}$ that would violate
$|B_{u \to v} + \Delta_{u \to v}| \le L_{u,v}$ is deterministically rejected prior to actuation,
preventing unbounded free-riding while requiring zero global currency supply, zero
interest-bearing debt, and zero third-party consensus nodes.
\end{invariant}

\begin{lstlisting}[language=C++, caption={Exact \texttt{SplitMix64} topology PRNG and zero-sum \texttt{L3PeerBccRecord} settlement.}]
struct alignas(8) SplitMix64 {
    uint64_t state{0x9E3779B97F4A7C15ULL};

    [[nodiscard]] constexpr uint64_t Next() noexcept {
        uint64_t z = (state += 0x9E3779B97F4A7C15ULL);
        z = (z ^ (z >> 30U)) * 0xBF58476D1CE4E5B9ULL;
        z = (z ^ (z >> 27U)) * 0x94D049BB133111EBULL;
        return z ^ (z >> 31U);
    }
};
static_assert(sizeof(SplitMix64) == 8, "SplitMix64 must be 8 bytes.");

struct alignas(8) L3PeerBccCore {
    int64_t  balance_local_to_peer_uj{0};
    int64_t  balance_peer_to_local_uj{0};
    int64_t  credit_limit_uj{200000000000LL}; // 200 MJ ceiling in uJ
    uint64_t total_cleared_volume_uj{0};

    [[nodiscard]] constexpr bool IsZeroSumValid() const noexcept {
        return (balance_local_to_peer_uj + balance_peer_to_local_uj) == 0;
    }

    [[nodiscard]] constexpr bool SettleSignedDelta(int64_t delta_uj) noexcept {
        if (!IsZeroSumValid() || credit_limit_uj < 0) {
            return false;
        }
        const int64_t next_local = balance_local_to_peer_uj + delta_uj;
        const int64_t next_peer  = balance_peer_to_local_uj - delta_uj;
        if (next_local > credit_limit_uj || next_local < -credit_limit_uj ||
            next_peer  > credit_limit_uj || next_peer  < -credit_limit_uj) {
            return false;
        }
        balance_local_to_peer_uj = next_local;
        balance_peer_to_local_uj = next_peer;
        total_cleared_volume_uj += static_cast<uint64_t>(delta_uj >= 0 ? delta_uj : -delta_uj);
        return IsZeroSumValid();
    }
};
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Chained Multi-Hop HTLCs, Thermodynamic Delivery Receipts, and Ricardian \texttt{Treaty\_CID} Guardrails}

Direct pairwise channels handle immediate neighbors, but in a larger Stage 1B or Stage 2
cooperative mesh, Sunnyside Care Ring ($u$) may requisition kiln-dried joinery stock or hot
commissary tiffins from Creekside Guild ($w$) when $u$ and $w$ have not established a direct
\texttt{BCC} treaty, yet both maintain active \texttt{BCC} channels with North-Oak Commons ($v$).
Furthermore, unlike pure digital payment networks where revealing a cryptographic secret
completes the transfer, physical mesh logistics must bridge \emph{atoms and bits}: what
prevents an intermediary or carrier from claiming exergy credit while delivering spilled or
lukewarm soup?

Layer 4 and Layer 5 compose three cryptographic mechanisms to guarantee atomic, physically
verified multi-hop collaboration:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Ricardian \texttt{Treaty\_CID} WASM Guardrails:} Every inter-node
    collaboration pool is governed by a Ricardian contract \parencite{grigg2004} uniting
    human-readable legal/commons prose, machine-readable JSON-LD schemas
    \parencite{w3c2020jsonld}, and executable invariants into a single content-addressed
    identifier:
    \begin{equation}
    \label{eq:treaty-cid}
    \texttt{Treaty\_CID} = \mathrm{SHA256}\bigl(\texttt{Text\_prose} \parallel \texttt{Schema\_JSON-LD} \parallel \texttt{Invariants\_BPMN}\bigr).
    \end{equation}
    At treaty ratification, \texttt{Invariants\_BPMN} compiles into a deterministic float-free
    WASM guardrail module pinned in Layer 3. Before \texttt{col-execd} accepts any inbound
    cross-pool message or settles any outbound handoff, it executes the \texttt{Treaty\_CID}
    guardrail over the proposed payload (verifying acoustic windows, phytosanitary moisture
    bounds $< 1{,}400\,\mathrm{bp}$, and credit ceilings $L_{u,v}$).
    \item \textbf{Chained Multi-Hop Hashed Timelock Contracts (\texttt{HTLC}s):} Across path
    $u \to v \to w$, consumer $u$'s receiving hardware enclave generates a fresh $256$-bit
    preimage $R \in \{0,1\}^{256}$ and publishes hashlock $h = \mathrm{H}(R)$ in the
    requisition header. Hop $u \to v$ escrows $\Delta E\,\mu\mathrm{J}$ of \texttt{BCC}
    capacity conditioned on $h = \mathrm{H}(R)$ with outer Hybrid Logical Clock expiry
    $T_1 = T_0 + 2\Delta_{\mathrm{hop}}$, while hop $v \to w$ escrows $\Delta E\,\mu\mathrm{J}$
    conditioned on the identical $h = \mathrm{H}(R)$ with strictly tighter inner expiry
    $T_2 = T_0 + \Delta_{\mathrm{hop}}$ ($\Delta_{\mathrm{hop}} > 0$). Staggering
    $T_1 - T_2 = \Delta_{\mathrm{hop}}$ guarantees that once producer $w$ claims credit from
    $v$ by presenting $(R, \sigma_{\mathrm{delivery}})$ prior to $T_2$, intermediary $v$
    possesses deterministic HLC margin $\Delta_{\mathrm{hop}}$ to forward $R$ and settle
    against $u$ before $T_1$ expires.
    \item \textbf{Two-Phase Thermodynamic Delivery Receipts ($\sigma_{\mathrm{dispatch}},
        \sigma_{\mathrm{delivery}}$):}
    Preimage $R$ is bound inside hardware-attested Layer 5/Layer 2 credentials
    \parencite{camenisch2004signature,w3c2022vc,microchip_atecc608a} and is released only when
    two physical receipts co-verify:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \emph{Phase 1 ($\sigma_{\mathrm{dispatch}}$):} At departure from producer $w$,
        calibrated scale and probe registers sign $(\texttt{lot\_cid}, m_{\mathrm{disp,g}},
        T_{\mathrm{disp,mk}}, \tau_{\mathrm{disp}})$.
        \item \emph{Phase 2 ($\sigma_{\mathrm{delivery}}$):} At destination $u$, receiving
        scale and thermistor registers measure $(m_{\mathrm{recv,g}}, T_{\mathrm{recv,mk}},
        \tau_{\mathrm{recv}})$ and co-sign $\sigma_{\mathrm{delivery}}$---releasing $R$ to
        settle the chained \texttt{HTLC}---if and only if mass conservation
        $|m_{\mathrm{recv,g}} - m_{\mathrm{disp,g}}| \le \epsilon_{m,\mathrm{g}}$ and thermal
        safety ($T_{\mathrm{recv,mk}} \ge 338{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ for hot tiffins or
        $\le 277{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ for cold-chain biologics) hold within treaty bounds.
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\section{Macro-Scale Federation, Reticulum DTN Partitions, and Graceful Fission}
\label{sec:macro-scale-dtn-fission}

At Stage 3 bioregional scale, sovereign campuses interconnect across kilometers of forest,
coastal ridge, or urban mesh using sub-gigahertz LoRa, IEEE 802.11ah Wi-Fi HaLow, and Reticulum
Delay-Tolerant Networking (\texttt{DTN}) store-and-forward bundles \parencite{fall2003}. Across
such links, multi-hour or multi-day network partitions are not exceptional edge cases---they
are routine seasonal weather.

\subsection{Pre-Partition Custody-Bounded Shards ($\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{local}}(u)$)}

A classic hazard of naive Conflict-Free Replicated Data Types (\texttt{CRDT}s)
\parencite{shapiro2011crdt} in material logistics is \emph{physical over-allocation under
partition}: if a single shared Automerge map tracks $12$ cedar planks stored in Node $u$'s barn
while LoRa connectivity drops, and both Node $u$ and partitioned neighbor Node $v$
optimistically lock $8$ planks for local joinery workflows, merging the CRDT logs after
reconnection yields $-4$ physical planks---an impossible negative-matter state!

Layer 3 and Layer 4 eliminate split-brain physical allocation without requiring synchronous
global consensus (which would halt local work during partitions \parencite{kleppmann2017designing})
by enforcing \textbf{Custody-Bounded Sharding}:

\begin{theorem}[Partition-Safe Local Custody Non-Double-Spend]
\label{thm:custody-shard-safety}
Let $\mathcal{L}$ be the global universe of discrete and bulk physical inventory lots across a
federated mesh $V$. Partition $\mathcal{L}$ at every instant into pairwise disjoint custody
shards $\bigl\{\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{local}}(u)\bigr\}_{u \in V}$
(\,$\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{local}}(u) \cap \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{local}}(v) = \emptyset$ for
$u \neq v$\,), where $\ell \in \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{local}}(u)$ holds if and only if the latest
cryptographically committed custody state of $\ell$ designates sovereign pool $u$ as physical
custodian (or exclusive \texttt{IN\_TRANSIT} carrier). Under the \textbf{Custodian-Exclusive
Escrow Rule}---which restricts \texttt{L3\_QUERY\_AND\_LOCK\_INVENTORY} write authority on
$\ell \in \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{local}}(u)$ strictly to pool $u$'s local \texttt{col-storaged}
engine---arbitrary network partitions $V = V_1 \sqcup \dots \sqcup V_k$ permit every node
$u \in V$ to execute local BPMN workflows over $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{local}}(u)$ at full
$10\,\mathrm{Hz}$ throughput while guaranteeing zero double-allocation upon CRDT anti-entropy
reconvergence.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Consider any physical lot $\ell \in \mathcal{L}$ with non-negative scalar quantity $Q(\ell) \ge 0$
at pre-partition epoch $\tau_0$. By disjointness of the custody partition, there exists a
unique custodian $u^* \in V_m$ such that $\ell \in \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{local}}(u^*)$. During
the network partition, any node $v \neq u^*$ (whether inside $V_m$ or in a disconnected
component $V_{m'}$) lacks write capability over $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{local}}(u^*)$; any
cross-node requisition initiated by $v$ must block at an asynchronous BPMN message-catch
boundary awaiting an explicit custody-transfer receipt signed by $u^*$. Meanwhile, inside
$u^*$, local \texttt{col-storaged} serializes all intra-node workflow locks
$\sum_i q_i \le Q(\ell)$ through single-node RocksDB transactions. Because only $u^*$ appends
allocation mutations to $\ell$'s single-writer oplog during the partition, the set of mutations
on $\ell$ across all components $V_1, \dots, V_k$ is totally ordered by $u^*$'s monotonic
sequence number. When Reticulum DTN links heal and CRDT logs merge, replay of $u^*$'s
single-writer log preserves $\sum_i q_i \le Q(\ell)$ identically across all replicas.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Deterministic Lease-Expiry Sagas Under Link Severance}

During our audit of early Oasis swarm prototypes (Council Ceiling \#5), we found that if a task
entered \texttt{CLAIMED\_BY\_PEER\_CLUSTER} just before a peer cluster disconnected, the local
workflow could hang indefinitely awaiting completion. In the ratified protocol, every cross-pool
task claim or \texttt{IN\_TRANSIT} reservation carries an explicit Hybrid Logical Clock lease
\texttt{claim\_lease\_expiry\_hlc}. If Reticulum DTN bundles fail to deliver a heartbeat
renewal or \texttt{ThermodynamicDeliveryReceiptV1} prior to \texttt{claim\_lease\_expiry\_hlc},
\texttt{col-execd}'s timer wheel deterministically fires an interrupting escalation boundary
event:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item If the task had not yet transferred physical custody out of $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{local}}(u)$
    (Regime 1), \texttt{col-execd} revokes \texttt{CLAIMED\_BY\_PEER\_CLUSTER}, increments the
    epoch fencing token so any late-arriving stale peer packet is cleanly ignored, and returns
    the task to local \texttt{UNASSIGNED} or yields upward to Layer 5 for alternate
    \texttt{BPVP} re-routing.
    \item If physical goods are already \texttt{IN\_TRANSIT} across a severed link, the local
    workflow spawns a non-destructive \texttt{WAIT\_DTN\_RECONCILIATION} monitor while
    authorizing Layer 5 to draw from local buffer stock so baseline household meals never stall.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Safe-Harbor Exit and Clean Treaty Fission (\texttt{Scenario Omicron} \& \texttt{Scenario Psi})}

The ultimate test of a non-coercive orchestrator is not how smoothly nodes join a mesh, but how
cleanly they can \emph{leave} it. Platform monopolies and authoritarian collectives trap

(\textit{Pluralistic Kinship \& Household Reconfiguration}) and \texttt{Psi}

When Layer 5 ratifies a \texttt{Fission\_Intent} over \texttt{l4\_l5.sock}, \texttt{col-execd}

\begin{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}
