\chapter{Stateless Execution, Resource Leases, and Five-Tier Preemption}
\label{ch:state-leases}

\epigraph{An orchestrator that hoards authoritative memory in volatile silicon is one loose wire away from amnesia or double actuation. Durability belongs to the ledger; discipline belongs to the lease; restraint belongs to the priority floor.}{---Systems Reliability Notes, Lot~402 Solar Shed}

In Chapter~\ref{ch:bilateral-membrane}, we sealed \texttt{col-execd} behind two POSIX
sockets and stripped it of citizen identities; in Chapter~\ref{ch:wasm-engine}, we compiled
declarative BPMN~2.0 graphs into deterministic, float-free WebAssembly modules stepping
64-byte token frames at $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$. A third foundational question now confronts us:
\textit{where does truth live when the power flickers, when two workflows reach for the same
physical workbench at the same millisecond, or when three days of November coastal rain
drain the settlement's $48\,\mathrm{V}$ lithium-iron-phosphate ($\mathrm{LiFePO_4}$) battery
bank?}

On twenty-year salvaged edge hardware powered by intermittent renewable microgrids,
brownouts, kernel panics, watchdog resets, and severed LoRa links are ordinary operational
weather rather than rare catastrophes. If \texttt{col-execd} held authoritative workflow
progress or resource locks solely inside its own volatile RAM, an unexpected
\texttt{SIGKILL} or DC bus brownout mid-tick would inflict one of two physical hazards upon
reboot: either forgetting an active irrigation envelope (leaving a solenoid stuck open until
its hardware timeout), or re-executing an already-completed step (dosing a raised bed twice
or double-settling a Bilateral Clearing Channel transfer). Equally dangerous, as our
Council's audit of the Oasis prototype demonstrated (Ceiling~\#5), running dozens of
concurrent workflows without formal lock ordering invites classic circular-wait deadlocks
across shared hearth hobs, joinery benches, and wheelbarrows
\parencite{coffman1971deadlocks}.

This chapter specifies the five mechanisms by which Layer~4 achieves crash-safe durability,
mathematical deadlock freedom, spatial polycentric coexistence, and thermodynamic survival
prioritization:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Stateless Execution Over Layer~3 (\texttt{col-storaged}):} Treating
        \texttt{col-execd}'s L1-cache arena strictly as an ephemeral, reconstructible
        projection over atomic \texttt{BatchStepCommit} frames persisted in RocksDB
        write-ahead logs, Automerge/Yrs Conflict-Free Replicated Data Types (CRDTs), and
        content-addressed InterPlanetary Linked Data (IPLD) Merkle-DAGs
        \parencite{shapiro2011crdt,kleppmann2017designing}.
\item \textbf{Lexicographic \texttt{L3ResourceLeaseLock} Ordering:} Eliminating Coffman
        deadlocks by construction across four canonical physical resource classes, fortified
        by Hybrid Logical Clock (HLC) lease Time-To-Live (TTL) expirations and monotonic
        fencing tokens \parencite{kulkarni2014logical}.
\item \textbf{Enveloping Node Hypervisor Boundary Leases and Two-Speed Jurisprudence (\texttt{CCI-4}, \texttt{D5}, \texttt{D6}):}
        Wrapping multiple co-located polycentric Layer~5 nodes ($N=1$ Solo Studio, $N=1$ Maker
        Bench, $N=4$ Household Hearth) inside a fractal, domain-blind \textbf{Enveloping Node
        Hypervisor} (\textit{Room/Building $\to$ Street/Microgrid $\to$ Bioregional
        Watershed}) that replaces $O(K^2)$ bilateral political treaties with $O(K)$
        domain-blind physical boundary leases (\texttt{EnvelopingBoundaryLeaseV50}) governed
        by Fast-Path Layer~2 hazard preemption and Slow-Path Semantic Diff precedent
        compilation.
\item \textbf{The Constitutional Five-Tier Priority Queue and SoC Shedding:} Enforcing
        strict ecological and human survival precedence ($F_{\mathrm{eco}} \succ B_{\mathrm{baseline}} \succ \mathrm{MutualAid} \succ \mathrm{Discretionary} \succ \mathrm{Export}$)
        via Schmitt-trigger fuel-budget hysteresis curves and single-tick Tier~5 export
        curtailment.
\item \textbf{Lookahead Horizon Admission Control:} Verifying multi-hour thermal and
        biological batch profiles against conservative P90 solar and storage trajectories
        \textit{before} crossing irreversible physical actuation boundaries.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Stateless Execution Over Layer 3 (\texttt{col-storaged})}
\label{sec:ch3-stateless-execution}

The cardinal durability invariant of Layer~4 is deceptively simple:
\textbf{\texttt{col-execd} holds zero authoritative durable state in volatile RAM}. Although
\texttt{col-execd} maintains hot, cache-aligned \texttt{BpmnTokenFrame} arrays and runnable
bitsets in static memory to achieve sub-millisecond $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$ tick sweeps, every
byte of that arena is a disposable cache of the committed head stored inside Layer~3
(\texttt{col-storaged}).

Formally, each $100\,\mathrm{ms}$ tick $t$ of \texttt{col-execd} is a pure deterministic
state-transition reducer:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{L4}}\!\left( S_{t-1}^{\mathrm{L3}}, \, \mathcal{I}_t^{\mathrm{L5}}, \, t_{\mathrm{HLC}} \right) \longrightarrow \mathrm{BatchStepCommit}_t,
\label{eq:ch3-stateless-reducer}
\end{equation}
where $S_{t-1}^{\mathrm{L3}}$ is the verified Layer~3 Merkle snapshot at tick $t-1$,
$\mathcal{I}_t^{\mathrm{L5}}$ is the ordered sequence of ratified Layer~5 command frames
drained from \texttt{l4\_l5.sock} at the start of tick $t$, and $\mathrm{BatchStepCommit}_t$
is a single self-contained binary transaction frame transmitted downward over
\texttt{l3\_l4.sock}.

\subsection{Three Storage Strata Behind \texttt{l3\_l4.sock}}

When \texttt{col-storaged} receives a \texttt{BatchStepCommit} frame across
\texttt{/run/collective/ipc/l3\_l4.sock}, it commits the frame across three complementary
storage strata engineered for different concurrency and replication properties:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{RocksDB Write-Ahead Log (WAL) for Local Atomic Serialization:} All token state
        transitions (\texttt{BpmnTokenFrame} deltas), exclusive resource lease
        acquisitions/releases (\texttt{L3ResourceLeaseLock}), pairwise \texttt{BCC}
        microjoule transfers (\texttt{PairwiseBccChannel} in \texttt{storaged\_engine.hpp}),
        and outbound hardware actuation envelopes (\texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1}) are bundled
        into a single RocksDB \texttt{WriteBatch} flushed via \texttt{fdatasync()} group
        commit. Either the entire $100\,\mathrm{ms}$ tick batch becomes durable on
        non-volatile flash, or---if power fails mid-write---CRC-32C frame validation
        truncates the partial tail cleanly on reboot, leaving $S_{t-1}^{\mathrm{L3}}$
        intact.
\item \textbf{Automerge / Yrs CRDT Registers for Partition-Tolerant State Merging:} Shared
        state structures that admit concurrent multi-writer updates across sub-nodes or
        neighborhood peers---such as environmental telemetry projections, broadcast signal
        logs (\texttt{sig:*}), surplus availability offers (\texttt{L3SurplusOffer}), and
        mesh weather feeds (\texttt{L3MeshWeatherEvent})---are encoded as state- and
        operation-based CRDTs (Last-Writer-Wins Element Sets bounded by HLC timestamps,
        Positive-Negative Counters where lawful, and Observe-Remove Maps)
        \parencite{shapiro2011crdt}. When a severed garden-shed link heals, CRDT registers
        converge deterministically without coordination round-trips.
\item \textbf{Content-Addressed IPLD Merkle-DAG Checkpoints:} Every $N_{\mathrm{chk}} = 10$
        ticks ($1.0\,\mathrm{s}$)---and immediately upon any Tier~1 floor trip, Ricardian
        treaty milestone, or software artifact ratification (\texttt{MerkleArtifactRecord}
        in \texttt{storaged\_engine.hpp})---\texttt{col-storaged} seals the state tree into
        a content-addressed SHA-256 IPLD Merkle root $\mathrm{CID}_t$. This immutable DAG
        anchors cryptographic audit trails and cross-node Proof-of-Thermodynamic-Work (PoTW)
        verification.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Split-Brain-Free Crash Recovery After \texttt{SIGKILL} or Brownout}

Suppose an electrician accidentally trips the breaker feeding
\texttt{node-01:office-coordinator}, or the Linux out-of-memory killer delivers
\texttt{SIGKILL} to \texttt{col-execd} mid-tick while \texttt{col-storaged} remains alive
(or after the entire SBC reboots from cold power). How does the Sovereign Stack prevent
split-brain double actuation?

Upon startup, \texttt{col-execd} executes a deterministic four-step \textbf{Cold Rehydration
Handshake} over \texttt{l3\_l4.sock} before enabling its $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$ timer loop:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Epoch Bump and Stale Writer Fencing:} \texttt{col-execd} sends
        \texttt{L3\_HELLO\_REHYDRATE} to \texttt{col-storaged}. Layer~3 atomically
        increments the persistent \texttt{orchestrator\_epoch} counter in RocksDB and
        returns the new epoch $E_{\mathrm{new}}$ alongside the latest committed
        \texttt{prev\_merkle\_root} and \texttt{tick\_id}. Any delayed IPC packet carrying
        an older \texttt{orchestrator\_epoch} $< E_{\mathrm{new}}$ is unconditionally
        dropped by \texttt{col-storaged}.
\item \textbf{Zero-Allocation Arena Streaming:} \texttt{col-storaged} streams all active
        64-byte \texttt{BpmnTokenFrame} records ($N \le 32{,}768$, at most $2\,\mathrm{MiB}$
        even on a Stage~3 campus) directly into \texttt{col-execd}'s aligned arena,
        reconstructing the five priority-tier runnable bitsets in $< 5\,\mathrm{ms}$.
\item \textbf{Pending Upward Yield Reconciliation:} \texttt{col-execd} queries
        \texttt{l4\_l5.sock} with the deterministic \texttt{yield\_id}s of all tokens in
        state \texttt{YIELDED\_UPWARD\_L5}. Because \texttt{yield\_id} is derived
        deterministically from \texttt{(instance\_id, current\_node\_idx,
        step\_epoch\_seq)}, Layer~5 deduplicates any retransmitted
        \texttt{UpwardYieldPacket} idempotently.
\item \textbf{Idempotent Envelope Continuity on Layer~2:} Outbound
        \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1} structs carry \texttt{(target\_register\_id,
        instance\_id, step\_epoch\_seq)} as an idempotency key. Even if power failed
        immediately \textit{after} \texttt{col-storaged} committed tick $t$,
        \texttt{col-execd} resumes cleanly at tick $t+1$; if power failed \textit{before}
        commit, re-computing tick $t$ against $S_{t-1}^{\mathrm{L3}}$ produces the exact
        byte-identical \texttt{BatchStepCommit} frame.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{invariant}[Crash-Recovery Observational Equivalence]
\label{inv:ch3-crash-equivalence}
Let $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{clean}}$ denote the sequence of committed Layer~3 states $\{S_0, S_1, \dots, S_K\}$
produced by an uninterrupted execution of \texttt{col-execd}, and let
$\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{crash}}$ denote an execution subjected to arbitrary fail-stop crashes
(\texttt{SIGKILL} or power loss) between or during any ticks, followed by Cold Rehydration
against \texttt{col-storaged}. For identical Layer~3 sensor ingresses and Layer~5 governance
frames indexed by HLC tick, $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{crash}}$ commits the exact same sequence
of Merkle roots $\{\mathrm{CID}_0, \dots, \mathrm{CID}_K\}$ with zero duplicate resource
debits and zero duplicate actuation envelopes.
\end{invariant}

\section{Eliminating Coffman Deadlocks via Lexicographic \texttt{L3ResourceLeaseLock}}
\label{sec:ch3-resource-leases}

In a high-density Sovereign Node running $200+$ concurrent workflows, contention for scarce
physical apparatus is the norm rather than the exception. Consider two workflows awakening
on the same morning tick on Lot~402:
\begin{itemize}
\item Workflow~$W_A$ (\texttt{wf.hearth.simmer\_bone\_broth}) needs $8.6\,\mathrm{MJ}$ of
        battery exergy, \textbf{Induction Hob~\#1}, and \textbf{Insulated Tiffin
        Carrier~\#2}.
\item Workflow~$W_B$ (\texttt{wf.hearth.pasteurize\_pea\_soup}) already holds
        \textbf{Insulated Tiffin Carrier~\#2} and now requests \textbf{Induction Hob~\#1}
        plus $4.2\,\mathrm{MJ}$ of battery exergy.
\end{itemize}
If $W_A$ locks Induction Hob~\#1 first and blocks waiting for Tiffin Carrier~\#2 while $W_B$
holds Tiffin Carrier~\#2 and blocks waiting for Induction Hob~\#1, both workflows freeze in
a classic circular-wait deadlock until soup spoils in the pot.

Recall the four necessary and sufficient conditions for system deadlock established by
\textcite{coffman1971deadlocks}: (1) \textit{Mutual Exclusion}, (2) \textit{Hold and Wait},
(3) \textit{No Preemption}, and (4) \textit{Circular Wait}. In cyber-physical orchestration,
we cannot abolish Mutual Exclusion (two pots cannot occupy one induction coil
simultaneously), nor can we casually preempt a midway thermal pasteurization hold without
ruining physical biomass. Layer~4 therefore defeats deadlock mathematically by
\textbf{simultaneously breaking Hold-and-Wait (per step) and Circular Wait (across steps)}
via canonical lexicographic resource lease ordering
\parencite{dennis1966programming,coffman1971deadlocks}.

\subsection{The Four Canonical Resource Classes and Total Order $\prec_{\mathrm{res}}$}

Every reservable physical or thermodynamic entity in Layer~3 belongs to one of four strictly
ranked \textbf{\texttt{ResourceClass}} strata:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Class 0 --- \texttt{EXERGY\_BUDGET}:} Microjoule (\texttt{\_uj}) electrical or
        thermal reservoir budgets (e.g., $48\,\mathrm{V}$ $\mathrm{LiFePO_4}$ bank capacity,
        hydronic buffer tank megajoules). Always acquired \textit{first} so a workflow never
        locks a physical station if the node lacks the energy to run it.
\item \textbf{Class 1 --- \texttt{PHYSICAL\_STATION}:} Stationary physical workcells, beds,
        vessels, and machine tools (e.g., \texttt{station:hearth\_hob\_01},
        \texttt{station:garage\_cnc\_01}, \texttt{station:garden\_bed\_03},
        \texttt{station:autoclave\_01}).
\item \textbf{Class 2 --- \texttt{HAUL\_TOOL}:} Mobile kinetic containers and transport
        implements (e.g., \texttt{tool:wheelbarrow\_01}, \texttt{tool:hand\_dolly\_01},
        \texttt{tool:etrike\_cargo\_02}, \texttt{tool:tiffin\_carrier\_02}).
\item \textbf{Class 3 --- \texttt{MATERIAL\_LOT}:} Fungible or lot-tracked physical
        inventory inputs in milligrams (\texttt{\_mg}) or milliliters (\texttt{\_ml}) (e.g.,
        $20{,}000{,}000\,\mathrm{mg}$ of thermophilic compost, $200{,}000\,\mathrm{mg}$ of
        heirloom pea seed, $4{,}500\,\mathrm{mL}$ of dechlorinated cistern water).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{definition}[Canonical Resource Identifier Order $\prec_{\mathrm{res}}$]
\label{def:ch3-lex-order}
Every lockable resource $r \in \mathcal{R}$ is uniquely identified by the pair $(c(r), \mathrm{id}(r)) \in \{0,1,2,3\} \times \{0, \dots, 2^{64}-1\}$,
where $c(r)$ is its \texttt{ResourceClass} and $\mathrm{id}(r)$ is its canonical 64-bit
entity CID hash. For any two distinct resources $r_1 \neq r_2$, we define the strict total
lexicographic order:
\begin{equation}
r_1 \prec_{\mathrm{res}} r_2 \iff \bigl(c(r_1) < c(r_2)\bigr) \;\lor\; \bigl(c(r_1) = c(r_2) \;\land\; \mathrm{id}(r_1) < \mathrm{id}(r_2)\bigr).
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

Ahead of compilation, \texttt{col-bpmnc} statically sorts every task's required resource
descriptor list in strictly ascending $\prec_{\mathrm{res}}$ order. Furthermore, if a
workflow segment holds an earlier lease $r_{\mathrm{held}}$ across multiple BPMN steps and
subsequently requests an additional lease $r_{\mathrm{new}}$ with $r_{\mathrm{new}} \preceq_{\mathrm{res}} r_{\mathrm{held}}$,
\textbf{\texttt{col-bpmnc} rejects the workflow at compile time} unless $r_{\mathrm{new}}$
is hoisted into the initial atomic acquisition set!

\begin{theorem}[Cycle-Free Wait-For Graph and Deadlock Freedom]
\label{thm:ch3-deadlock-freedom}
Under atomic per-node sorted batch acquisition in \texttt{col-storaged} and the compile-time
monotonic acquisition rule ($\forall r_{\mathrm{held}} \in H(W), \forall r_{\mathrm{req}} \in Q(W): r_{\mathrm{held}} \prec_{\mathrm{res}} r_{\mathrm{req}}$),
the runtime wait-for graph $G_{\mathrm{WFG}} = (\mathcal{W}, E_{\mathrm{wait}})$ across all
concurrent workflow instances $\mathcal{W}$ is a strict directed acyclic graph (DAG) at
every tick $t$. Consequently, Coffman circular-wait deadlocks are impossible.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Suppose toward a contradiction that at tick $t$ there exists a directed cycle of waiting
workflow instances $W_1 \to W_2 \to \dots \to W_m \to W_1$ ($m \ge 2$) in
$G_{\mathrm{WFG}}$. Notice first that during a single atomic sorted \texttt{WriteBatch}
request $Q(W_i)$, \texttt{col-storaged} either grants \textit{all} leases in $Q(W_i)$ or
grants \textit{none} and parks $W_i$ in \texttt{WAITING\_LEASE} without holding partial
elements of $Q(W_i)$. Therefore, if $W_i \to W_{i+1}$, $W_i$ must already hold some
earlier-step lease $h_i \in H(W_i)$ (if $H(W_1) = \emptyset$, $W_1$ holds nothing and cannot
have an incoming edge $W_m \to W_1$), while waiting for a resource $q_i \in Q(W_i)$
currently held by $W_{i+1}$, meaning $q_i \in H(W_{i+1})$.

For each $i \in \{1, \dots, m\}$ (indices modulo $m$), define $r_i^{*} = \max_{\prec_{\mathrm{res}}} H(W_i)$,
the highest-ordered resource currently held by $W_i$. Because $W_i$ requests $q_i \in Q(W_i)$
while already holding $r_i^{*} \in H(W_i)$, the monotonic acquisition rule guarantees
$r_i^{*} \prec_{\mathrm{res}} q_i$. Because $q_i \in H(W_{i+1})$ and $r_{i+1}^{*}$ is the
maximum of $H(W_{i+1})$ under $\prec_{\mathrm{res}}$, we have $q_i \preceq_{\mathrm{res}} r_{i+1}^{*}$.
By transitivity of the strict total order $\prec_{\mathrm{res}}$, we obtain $r_i^{*} \prec_{\mathrm{res}} r_{i+1}^{*}$
for all $i$, yielding the impossible chain $r_1^{*} \prec_{\mathrm{res}} r_2^{*} \prec_{\mathrm{res}} \dots \prec_{\mathrm{res}} r_m^{*} \prec_{\mathrm{res}} r_1^{*}$.
Hence $G_{\mathrm{WFG}}$ contains no cycles.
\end{proof}

\subsection{HLC Lease TTLs and Monotonic Fencing Tokens}

Deadlock freedom prevents circular stalemates among live workflows, but what if a remote
neighborhood peer cluster claims a shared mobile cider press
(\texttt{CLAIMED\_BY\_PEER\_CLUSTER}) right before a windstorm severs its wireless mesh
link? Without lease expiration, that lock would remain orphaned indefinitely.

Every \texttt{L3ResourceLeaseLock} (Listing~\ref{lst:ch3-lease-struct}) enforces two
temporal safety fields \parencite{kulkarni2014logical,kleppmann2017designing}:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Bounded HLC Time-To-Live (\texttt{granted\_hlc + ttl\_ms}):} No lease in
        Layer~3 is perpetual; long-running workflows must actively renew heartbeats prior to
        \texttt{granted\_hlc + ttl\_ms}. If a partitioned peer or stalled task misses its
        deadline, \texttt{col-storaged} automatically reclaims the lease on the very tick
        $t_{\mathrm{HLC}} \ge \mathrm{granted\_hlc} + \mathrm{ttl\_ms}$ and wakes the
        highest-priority waiter.
\item \textbf{Strict Monotonic \texttt{fencing\_token}:} Each time resource $r$ is leased or
        renewed to a new holder, Layer~3 increments its per-resource 64-bit
        \texttt{fencing\_token} ($\phi_1 < \phi_2 < \dots$) and embeds $\phi_k$ inside every
        resulting \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1} and settlement commit. Layer~2
        microcontrollers and Layer~3 registers store $\phi_{\max}(r)$ and unconditionally
        reject any delayed packet bearing $\phi < \phi_{\max}(r)$, guaranteeing zero stale
        zombie writes after partition healing.
\end{itemize}

\section{Enveloping Node Hypervisor Boundary Leases and Two-Speed Jurisprudence (\texttt{CCI-4}, \texttt{D5}, \texttt{D6})}
\label{sec:ch3-enveloping-hypervisor}

In modernist municipal zoning and legacy enterprise software, institutional boundaries are
conflated with physical walls: a building is assumed to be \textit{either} a domestic home,
\textit{or} a recording studio, \textit{or} a machine shop. In living convivial communities
(\texttt{D5}, \texttt{D6}), however, Layer~5 institutional topology is decoupled from
Layer~2 spatial coordinates \parencite{ostrom2010,habraken1972supports}. A single physical
house or cooperative wing frequently hosts multiple overlapping polycentric Layer~5 nodes
sharing one local Layer~6 Semantic Daemon (\texttt{col-commonsd} + \texttt{col-gatewayd}):
for instance, a \textbf{Household Hearth ($N_s = 4$)}, a \textbf{Solo Studio ($N_s = 1$)}
stewarding \texttt{Scenario Zero} software or acoustic mastering, and a \textbf{Maker Bench
($N_s = 1$)} running precision CNC joinery.

Even an $N_s = 1$ solo node---where one Steward holds $V_1(t) = 1 + R_1(t) = 100\%$ of
internal process voice---does not float in a physical vacuum. To actuate shared tools or emit
noise, heat, and electrical load inside a shared structure, an $N_s = 1$ node imports
external Layer~5 governance templates (such as a Tool Guild's Pillar~II safety and custody
covenant or Scenario~Zero reproducible build gates) and operates inside the physical
partition boundaries of the shared building.

\subsection{Eliminating $O(K^2)$ Horizontal Politics via Fractal Domain-Blind Envelopes}

If $K$ specialized child nodes co-located in the same physical footprint had to negotiate
bilateral treaties with one another over spindle decibels, dust extraction, thermal exhaust,
and breaker amperage, coordination overhead would scale quadratically as $\binom{K}{2} = O(K^2)$
horizontal political negotiations.

Dialogue~6 (\texttt{D6}) and Cross-Cutting Invariant~\texttt{CCI-4} eliminate $O(K^2)$
treaty friction by wrapping shared physical footprints in a fractal hierarchy of
\textbf{Domain-Blind Enveloping Node Hypervisors}, analogous to an operating-system container
hypervisor:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Room / Building Envelope Hypervisor:} Wraps co-located child nodes ($N=1$ Solo
        Studio, $N=1$ Maker Bench, $N=4$ Household Hearth) inside a shared structure. Every
        human operating a child node inside that footprint holds universal civic baseline
        membership $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$ in the Enveloping Node. The Room/Building Envelope
        is \textbf{completely domain-blind} to child-node internals---it never votes on the
        Maker Bench's CNC feed rate, the Studio's software backlog, or the Hearth's soup
        spices. It ratifies and enforces \textit{only} shared physical boundary invariants
        measured by domain-blind Layer~2 partition sensors: acoustic flux in centibels
        (\texttt{cB}), continuous electrical power in milliwatts (\texttt{mW}), window exergy
        in microjoules (\texttt{uJ}), exhaust thermal delta in millikelvins (\texttt{mK}),
        spatial bounding boxes in millimeters (\texttt{mm}), and finite lease durations in
        milliseconds (\texttt{ms}).
\item \textbf{Street / Microgrid Envelope Hypervisor:} Wraps twenty House Envelopes and a
        Neighborhood Bakery around distribution transformer kVA limits (\texttt{mW}), shared
        cargo e-trike logistics lanes (\texttt{mm}), and nocturnal street acoustic ceilings
        (\texttt{cB}).
\item \textbf{Bioregional Watershed Envelope Hypervisor:} Wraps multiple Street Envelopes
        around hydrological aquifer drawdown ceilings ($\mathrm{mL/s}$), watershed emissions
        ($\mu\mathrm{g}$), and Layer~3 Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$)
        asymptotes.
\end{enumerate}

At each fractal level, $K$ child nodes negotiate $O(K)$ vertical physical boundary leases
with their immediate Enveloping Node Hypervisor rather than $O(K^2)$ peer treaties.
Listing~\ref{lst:ch3-enveloping-lease} presents the exact production C++20

    SheddingRegime current, int32_t soc_bp, int64_t export_residual_uj) noexcept {
    SheddingRegime next = current;
    if (soc_bp < 2000) {
        next = SheddingRegime::LIFELINE_SANCTUARY;
    } else if (soc_bp < 4000) {
        if (current != SheddingRegime::LIFELINE_SANCTUARY || soc_bp >= 2300) {
            next = SheddingRegime::AUSTERITY_SHIELD;
        }
    } else if (soc_bp < 8000 || export_residual_uj <= 0) {

            return current;
        }
        next = SheddingRegime::CONSERVATION;
    } else if (soc_bp >= 8300 && export_residual_uj > 0) {
        next = SheddingRegime::NOMINAL_SURPLUS;
    }
    return next;
}

        case SheddingRegime::LIFELINE_SANCTUARY:

    }

\section{Lookahead Horizon Admission Control}
\label{sec:ch3-lookahead-admission}

Instantaneous State-of-Charge gating protects the microgrid against sudden drops, yet by
itself it remains myopic with respect to \textbf{non-preemptible multi-hour physical
commitments}. Suppose at 16:30 on an autumn afternoon $\mathrm{SoC}_{\mathrm{bp}} = 5{,}400\,\mathrm{bp}$
($54\%$), comfortably inside the Conservation band where Tier~4 kitchen tasks are permitted.
A workflow proposes starting a $12\text{-hour}$ collagen bone-broth extraction followed by a
Bigelow pasteurization hold requiring $14.4\,\mathrm{MJ}$ ($14.4 \times 10^{12}\,\mu\mathrm{J}$)
through the night. If \texttt{col-execd} admits that batch based solely on 16:30's SoC,
nocturnal dwelling heating ($B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$) will pull the battery below
$4{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ at 01:00---forcing an emergency mid-simmer abort right inside the
$[277{,}150, 333{,}150]\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($4\text{--}60\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$) bacterial danger
zone!

To prevent starting irreversible physical journeys that the node cannot safely finish,
Layer~4 enforces \textbf{Lookahead Horizon Admission Control} ahead of every workflow's
irreversible commitment boundary $t_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ (such as wetting seed inoculant,
pouring foundry melt, heating culinary stock, or loading an autoclave).

\begin{definition}[Discrete-Time Lookahead Admission Gate]
\label{def:ch3-lookahead-gate}
Let a candidate workflow instance $W$ of priority tier $\tau \in \{2,3,4,5\}$ declare a
non-preemptible horizon of $H$ discrete $15\text{-minute}$ ($900{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}$)
buckets with piecewise-constant microjoule demand profile
$\{e_{\mathrm{cand}}(k)\}_{k=1}^{H}$. Let $E_{\mathrm{SoC}}(t_0)$ be the current verified
usable battery exergy in $\mu\mathrm{J}$, $\widehat{E}_{\mathrm{gen}}^{\mathrm{P90}}(k)$ be
Layer~3's conservative 90th-percentile lower-confidence renewable generation forecast for
bucket $k$ (zeroed at night), and $E_{\mathrm{leased}}^{(\le \tau)}(k)$ be the sum of all
already-admitted lookahead exergy profiles of priority tier $\le \tau$ (plus mandatory
Tier~1 and Tier~2 nocturnal thermal baselines). \texttt{col-execd} admits $W$ across
$t_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ if and only if for every horizon step $k \in \{1, \dots, H\}$:
\begin{equation}
E_{\mathrm{SoC}}(t_0) + \sum_{j=1}^{k} \left( \widehat{E}_{\mathrm{gen}}^{\mathrm{P90}}(j) - E_{\mathrm{leased}}^{(\le \tau)}(j) - e_{\mathrm{cand}}(j) \right) \ge E_{\mathrm{soc\_min}}(\tau),
\label{eq:ch3-lookahead}
\end{equation}
where $E_{\mathrm{soc\_min}}(\tau)$ maps the tier's minimum permissible SoC floor
($\mathrm{SoC}_{\mathrm{min}}(4) = 4{,}300\,\mathrm{bp}$, $\mathrm{SoC}_{\mathrm{min}}(3) = 2{,}300\,\mathrm{bp}$,
$\mathrm{SoC}_{\mathrm{min}}(2) = 1{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ hardware cell protection floor) into
microjoules.
\end{definition}

When Inequality~\eqref{eq:ch3-lookahead} fails at some future bucket $k^*$ (for instance,
02:15 AM), \texttt{col-execd} does not discard the Steward's request or risk spoiling
ingredients. Instead, while $t < t_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ (ingredients still dry and
shelf-stable in pantry storage), it computes the earliest horizon shift $\Delta k_{\mathrm{start}}$---typically
the following morning's 09:30 solar ramp---at which Inequality~\eqref{eq:ch3-lookahead}
holds across all $H$ buckets, parks the token cleanly in \texttt{WAITING\_TIMER} (or emits a
calm schedule-shift notice via Layer~5), and leaves every physical ingredient unharmed.

Together, stateless \texttt{BatchStepCommit} persistence over Layer~3, deadlock-free
lexicographic \texttt{L3ResourceLeaseLock} ordering, fractal Enveloping Node Hypervisor
boundary leases (\texttt{EnvelopingBoundaryLeaseV50}) with Two-Speed Jurisprudence, 5-tier
Schmitt-trigger exergy shedding, and lookahead horizon admission control give
\texttt{col-execd} the ironclad operational reliability of an industrial safety
PLC---operating entirely on community-governed, twenty-year sovereign silicon.
