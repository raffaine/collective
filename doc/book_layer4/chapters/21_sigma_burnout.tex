\chapter{Scenario Sigma: The Maintenance Commons}
\label{ch:scenario_sigma}

\section{The Legacy Paradigm \& Its Failures}
In legacy capitalist structures, maintenance—such as cleaning, repairing, organizing, and emotional labor—is treated as low-status, low-pay work. In informal communes or co-operatives, this invisible labor inevitably falls onto a small fraction of hyper-conscientious individuals. Eventually, these individuals suffer complete psychological entropy (burnout). Resentment fractures the social graph, and the physical infrastructure degrades into chaos.

This centralized, fiat-based approach fails because it divorces thermodynamic reality from economic reward. Human beings are thermodynamic engines that require downtime, rest, and psychological safety to recharge. A system that extracts labor without mathematically measuring and mitigating human fatigue will structurally collapse under its own entropy. The legacy world relies on exploiting this invisible labor, a method incompatible with the strict thermodynamic limits of a Sovereign Node.

\section{The Incremental Automation Pathway}

\subsection{Phase 1: Human-in-the-Loop (Manual Orchestration)}
At inception, maintenance tasks are managed manually. Citizens visually inspect the commons and manually generate \texttt{MaintenanceBounties} on the L3 ledger. The BPMN gateways for claiming these bounties are entirely human-driven. When an individual feels fatigued, they must manually communicate their need for rest to the Trust Ring, relying on social consensus to enforce downtime and redistribute chores.

\subsection{Phase 2: The Centaur (AI-Assisted)}
In the transitional phase, localized LLMs assist by parsing IoT telemetry (e.g., weight sensors under trash bins or runtime counters on 3D printers) and drafting the required bounties. The AI monitors the distribution of labor and flags individuals who are taking on a disproportionate share of the work. However, the final issuance of bounties and the enforcement of "Rest Locks" strictly require manual cryptographic signing by a human Steward before becoming active on the network.

\subsection{Phase 3: Full Autonomy}
In the autonomous state, the Orchestrator autonomously generates and routes chores based on physical node homeostasis. The system dynamically prices chores using an algorithmic Dutch Auction, increasing the token reward until a citizen accepts the task. The Orchestrator autonomously enforces L5 "Rest Locks," mathematically preventing a citizen from claiming bounties if they are at risk of burnout, completely automating the equitable distribution of invisible labor.

\section{The Human Boundary (Limits of Automation)}
The AI is strictly bounded in its jurisdiction over human biology and psychology. The system can measure the velocity of completed bounties and enforce a digital "Fatigue Lockout" to prevent further labor, but it cannot algorithmically determine a citizen's true emotional state or forcibly mandate specific psychological therapies. The AI can route a \texttt{MaintenanceBounty} or suggest a \texttt{RestIntent}, but it cannot physically force a human to rest, sleep, or socialize. The human body remains an impenetrable cryptographic boundary; the Orchestrator manages the incentive structures and queue access, leaving the actual physical and emotional healing to the human being and the Trust Ring.

\section{Orchestration Mechanics (The "How")}
The Orchestrator processes maintenance routing through a dynamically weighted DAG. When an IoT sensor crosses an entropy threshold, it asynchronously injects a \texttt{MaintenanceBounty} node into the task graph. The topological sorting of the DAG constantly re-evaluates the priority of these maintenance nodes against ongoing fabrication or social intents, ensuring systemic health does not fall behind productive output.

The queueing mechanism employs an M/M/1 holding queue with a dynamic priority escalation model. Bounties sit in the queue where their associated Value Token reward is continuously escalated by the \texttt{BountyManager} engine. This continuous escalation acts as a localized economic gradient, mathematically pulling citizens toward low-status tasks as the reward matches their thermodynamic willingness to perform them.

To interact with L3 state changes without violating the Strict Boundary Theorem, the Orchestrator employs a non-blocking, asynchronous Fatigue Check mechanism. When a citizen attempts to claim a bounty, the discrete BPMN node queries the L3 ledger for the citizen's recent task velocity. If the velocity exceeds the mathematically defined burnout threshold, the BPMN gateway resolves to a "Rest Lock" state, cryptographically dropping the claim and emitting a suggestion for somatic play. The AI never controls the citizen's physical body, but strictly limits their interaction with the automated ledger.
