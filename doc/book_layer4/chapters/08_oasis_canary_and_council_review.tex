\section{Constitutional Demarcation of \texttt{Layer 1b} and the SITL Canary Gate}
\label{sec:oasis-constitutional-demarcation}

In conventional ``smart home'' and industrial Internet-of-Things platforms, a digital twin ingests continuous live telemetry from cameras, motion detectors, smart meters, and wearables inside an occupied dwelling to render a real-time mirror of its residents \parencite{zuboff2019}. The Sovereign Stack categorically forbids that architecture. Under the ratified stack constitution, Oasis (\texttt{Layer 1b}) is \emph{never} a live mirror-world dashboard of a physical household, \emph{never} ingests telemetry from inhabited homes, and is \emph{never} a transitional user interface wrapped around physical surveillance.

Instead, Oasis serves three strictly bounded, non-surveillant roles:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Deterministic Software-in-the-Loop (SITL) Canary Harness:} Exercising exact production Layer~3--Layer~6 daemons against synthetic soil, hydrology, thermal, structural, and hauling physics prior to hardware deployment \parencite{grieves2014digital}.
  \item \textbf{Ecological and Stoichiometric Foresight Laboratory:} Allowing stewards and curriculum learners to explore multi-season crop rotations, battery sizing, and watershed keyline earthworks under accelerated time-warp (\texttt{2x}, \texttt{5x}, \texttt{10x}) with Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (\texttt{BKT}) pedagogical scaffolding \parencite{corbett1995bkt,forrester1961}.
  \item \textbf{Unidirectional Synthetic Telemetry Producer:} Emitting synthetic sensor frames strictly outward across the Unified Hardware Abstraction Interface (\texttt{UHAI}) ring buffer tagged with provenance byte \texttt{QUALITY\_SIMULATED = 0x01}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{\texttt{QUALITY\_SIMULATED} (\texttt{0x01}) vs.\ \texttt{QUALITY\_HARDWARE\_ROOT} (\texttt{0x02})}

The binary demarcation between simulated silicon and physical reality is enforced at two independent choke points: the \texttt{UHAI} shared-memory ring (\texttt{core/1\_simulation/src/uhai/uhai\_ring\_buffer.hpp}) and the Layer~2 Hardware Airgap Filter (\texttt{src/daemons/col-telemetryd/airgap\_filter.hpp}). Every \texttt{TelemetrySample} frame carries an explicit \texttt{quality\_flags} byte:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{QUALITY\_SIMULATED = 0x01}: Assigned unconditionally to all frames synthesized by Oasis (\texttt{Layer 1b}) or \texttt{col-sim}.
  \item \texttt{QUALITY\_HARDWARE\_ROOT = 0x02}: Assigned exclusively to physical telemetry frames whose payload MAC or Ed25519/ECDSA signature chains to a hardware-rooted Layer~2 secure element (\texttt{ATECC608A}) on an air-gapped field bus \parencite{microchip_atecc608a,rose2020zero}.
\end{itemize}

Inside \texttt{col-telemetryd}, \texttt{oasis::airgap::HardwareAirgapFilter::Evaluate()} inspects every incoming sample before any physical Hardware Abstraction Layer (\texttt{HAL}) relay, MOSFET driver, or motorized ball valve can be addressed. If \texttt{quality\_flags == QUALITY\_SIMULATED}, the filter increments \texttt{stats\_.dropped\_simulated} and returns \texttt{AirgapDecision::DROPPED\_SIMULATED\_SECURITY\_VIOLATION}; any unrecognized flag yields \texttt{DROPPED\_INVALID\_FLAG}. Consequently, a synthetic Lot~402 drought frame can never open a physical garden solenoid, even if a developer accidentally bridges test sockets on the same host.

\begin{invariant}[Unidirectional SITL Demarcation and Hardware Airgap Isolation]
\label{inv:sitl-airgap}
Let $\mathcal{F}_{1b}$ denote the stream of frames emitted by Oasis (\texttt{Layer 1b}) and $\mathcal{A}_{1a}$ denote the set of physical GPIO/fieldbus actuation registers in \texttt{Layer 1a}. (i)~Data flow from \texttt{Layer 1a} live domestic sensors into \texttt{Layer 1b} is identically empty ($\mathcal{F}_{1a \to 1b} = \varnothing$); (ii)~every frame $f \in \mathcal{F}_{1b}$ carries \texttt{quality\_flags = 0x01}; and (iii)~\texttt{HardwareAirgapFilter} authorizes physical actuation on $a \in \mathcal{A}_{1a}$ if and only if \texttt{quality\_flags == 0x02} verified against a live \texttt{ATECC608A} hardware root.
\end{invariant}

\subsection{The \emph{Scenario Zero} $10{,}000$-Tick Canary Gate}

While \texttt{0x01} frames are barred from physical relays, they are indispensable for gating software upgrades to Layer~4 itself. When a commune or developer compiles an upgraded \texttt{col-execd} binary or ratifies a new BPMN workflow bundle under \emph{Scenario Zero} (\texttt{src/daemons/col-execd/execd\_self\_host.hpp}), \texttt{ExecdSelfHostEngine::VerifyCanaryGate()} enforces three non-negotiable promotion predicates before atomic symlink activation:
\begin{enumerate}

  \item \texttt{layer\_skipping\_imports\_detected == 0}: Zero direct socket or library imports bypassing adjacent stack layers \parencite{saltzer1984end}.

\end{enumerate}

\section{Six Working Architectural Highlights Live on Lot~402}
\label{sec:six-oasis-highlights}

Before examining the limitations uncovered by the Specialist Council, it is essential to document what the Lot~402 reference stack already achieves in running code. Across \texttt{src/stack/shared\_stack\_runtime.hpp} ($4{,}452$ lines), \texttt{core/1\_simulation/src/engine/}, and \texttt{core/4\_orchestrator/workflows/}, six nontrivial architectural patterns operate deterministically today.

\subsection{Highlight 1: Clean-Slate Genesis and Garden-to-Soup Upward-Yield Coupling}

At \texttt{tick = 0}, \texttt{InitializeGenesisDecisionsAndWorkflows()} (\texttt{shared\_stack\_runtime.hpp:3663}) boots with zero pre-spawned workflows, awaiting Layer~6 ratification of governance proposals (\texttt{dec:pea\_cultivation}, \texttt{dec:pea\_soup\_hearth}). Once ratified, \texttt{core/4\_orchestrator/workflows/pea\_garden\_and\_soup\_lifecycles.bpmn.json} orchestrates coupled agricultural (\texttt{wf.garden.*}) and culinary (\texttt{wf.soup.*}) lifecycles without violating layer isolation. When the kitchen workflow attempts to lock harvested legumes via \texttt{L3\_QUERY\_AND\_LOCK\_INVENTORY} over \texttt{l3\_l4.sock} and discovers zero stock in the pantry, \texttt{wf.soup.*} \emph{cannot} directly spawn or mutate \texttt{wf.garden.*}. Instead, it emits an upward \texttt{YIELD\_UPWARD\_L5} stock-miss packet over \texttt{l4\_l5.sock}, allowing Layer~5 governance policy to correlate the deficit with ratified cultivation policy and instantiate or bind the upstream garden workflow cleanly.

\subsection{Highlight 2: Four-Node Homestead Cluster vs.\ Ring-1 Federated Mutual Aid}

Lot~402 models both intra-homestead topological specialization and inter-neighborhood federation without conflating the two:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Intra-Homestead (\texttt{SOLO\_STEWARD\_4\_NODE\_CLUSTER}):} \texttt{InitializeFourNodeTopology()} (\texttt{shared\_stack\_runtime.hpp:3351--3395}) partitions local execution across four physical station nodes---\texttt{node-01:office-coordinator}, \texttt{node-02:garage-fab}, \texttt{node-03:garden-agro}, and \texttt{node-04:hearth-galley}---routing cross-station material dependencies (such as timber trellis stakes fabricated in \texttt{node-02} required by tall peas in \texttt{node-03}) via \texttt{L4\_CROSS\_NODE\_REQUISITION}.
  \item \textbf{Inter-Neighborhood (\texttt{R1\_FEDERATED\_SWARM}):} In \texttt{src/daemons/col-swarmd/swarm\_engine.hpp} (lines~22--143 and 250--377), \texttt{SwarmEngine} federates across three Ring-1 neighborhood clusters (\texttt{cluster:north\_oak\_commons}, \texttt{cluster:creekside\_guild}, and \texttt{cluster:sunnyside\_care\_ring}) using deterministic $75\%$ domain-affinity routing paired with $25\%$ \texttt{SplitMix64} load sharing. Cross-cluster assistance settles through zero-sum Bilateral Mutual Credit (\texttt{PairwiseBccChannel} and \texttt{L3PeerBccRecord}) \parencite{grigg2004,ostrom1990}, enforcing strict conservation $\text{local\_balance} + \text{remote\_balance} = 0$ without speculative tokens.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Highlight 3: \texttt{VirtualPinBus} Epistemic Privacy and Dynamic Modality Switching}

In \texttt{core/1\_simulation/src/engine/l2\_bridge/virtual\_pin\_bus.hpp} (lines~60--289), \texttt{VirtualPinBus} refuses to leak omniscient simulation state to Layer~4. Calling \texttt{ReadSoilProbe()} when \texttt{has\_l2\_sensor == false} returns \texttt{PinReadStatus::DENIED\_NO\_L2\_SENSOR} with zeroed registers, forcing Layer~4 to yield upward for human qualitative observation. The moment the steward fabricates and installs physical Layer~2 probes or actuators on Lot~402 (\texttt{soil\_probe\_v1}, \texttt{irrigation\_valve\_v1}, or \texttt{culinary\_vessel\_v1}), \texttt{RefreshL4AutomationModalities()} hot-swaps the corresponding BPMN task bindings from \texttt{MANUAL\_HUMAN\_OBSERVATION} to \texttt{AUTOMATED\_L2\_EXECUTION} without restarting active workflow tokens.

\subsection{Highlight 4: Euclidean Sub-LSB Remainder Accumulators and Bigelow Lethality}

Standard discrete-time simulations lose mass or thermal energy whenever per-tick increments fall below integer truncation thresholds. Both \texttt{SoilCellRegisters} ($40$ bytes, \texttt{core/1\_simulation/src/engine/domains/soil\_physics.hpp:26--41}) and \texttt{ThermalVesselRegisters} ($32$ bytes, \texttt{core/1\_simulation/src/engine/domains/thermal\_vessel.hpp:23--56}) eliminate truncation drift without a single floating-point variable via \textbf{Euclidean carry-save remainder accumulators} (\texttt{growth\_sub\_accum}, \texttt{nitrogen\_sub\_accum}, \texttt{moisture\_sub\_accum}, \texttt{evap\_sub\_ml\_accum}, and \texttt{energy\_sub\_mj\_accum}). Given numerator impulse $\Delta N$ and divisor $D$:
\[
  A_{t+1} = A_t + \Delta N, \qquad \Delta Q = \lfloor A_{t+1} / D \rfloor, \qquad R_{t+1} = A_{t+1} \bmod D,
\]
conserving every sub-milligram and sub-millijoule across arbitrary tick rates. Simultaneously, \texttt{ComputeBigelowLethalityRateMsPerSec()} (\texttt{thermal\_vessel.hpp:64--92}) integrates food-safety pathogen lethality \parencite{bigelow1921thermal} around reference temperature $T_{\text{ref}} = 358{,}150\,\text{mK}$ ($85.0\,^\circ\text{C}$) with $z = 5{,}000\,\text{mK}$ ($5.0\,\text{K}$) using pure integer piecewise-quadratic interpolation.

\subsection{Highlight 5: JSON-LD Cultivar Parameterization and Three-Law Hauling Physics}

Rather than hardcoding one generic ``crop,'' \texttt{CultivarWorkflowSpec} (\texttt{shared\_stack\_runtime.hpp:432--462, 4163--4354}) ingests W3C JSON-LD agronomic ontologies (\texttt{fabaceae\_cultivars.jsonld}) \parencite{w3c2020jsonld} spanning four distinct phenotypes: rapid SITL canary \texttt{chrono\_virtual\_pea}, bush \texttt{little\_marvel\_pea}, climbing \texttt{alderman\_telephone\_pea} ($180\,\text{cm}$ vine height triggering a structural trellis gate), and warm-season \texttt{kentucky\_wonder\_bean} (\emph{Phaseolus vulgaris}, requiring soil temperature $\ge 288{,}650\,\text{mK}$ and disabling rhizobial $\text{N}_2$-fixation credits). Material movement between stations obeys \texttt{HaulingPhysics} (\texttt{core/1\_simulation/src/engine/logistics/hauling\_physics.hpp:112--243}), enforcing three matter-phase laws:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Law~1 (\texttt{LIQUID}):} \texttt{0 ml} barehand transport---water and soup strictly require sealed vessels or plumbed pipework.
  \item \textbf{Law~2 (\texttt{GRANULAR\_PARTICULATE}):} \texttt{500 g} barehand pocket/palm ceiling with a \texttt{3500 bp} ($35\%$) walking speed penalty unless carried in a sack, bucket, or barrow.
  \item \textbf{Law~3 (\texttt{DISCRETE\_SOLID}):} \texttt{35,000 g} ($35\,\text{kg}$) human ergonomic lift cap, plus a voxel surface check requiring contiguous paved flagstones for small-caster \texttt{hand\_dolly} transport while permitting pneumatic \texttt{wheelbarrow} traversal over unpaved loam.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Highlight 6: Three-Mode \texttt{UnmodeledStepAdapter} Ontology Bridge}

A BPMN library describing $300+$ real-world crafts inevitably references specialized URIs (\texttt{col://process/ferment\_tempeh}, \texttt{col://process/dress\_millstone}) before Oasis possesses bespoke 3D C++ fluid or fracture solvers for every step. In \texttt{core/1\_simulation/src/engine/l2\_bridge/unmodeled\_step\_adapter.hpp} (lines~16--20 and 154--198), \texttt{UnmodeledStepAdapter} classifies every workflow step into one of three execution modes---\texttt{MODELED\_PHYSICAL\_DOMAIN}, \texttt{PLAYER\_OBSERVATION\_PROMPT}, or \texttt{DEFAULT\_VALUES\_AND\_TIMED\_STATION\_WORK}---allowing arbitrary \texttt{col://} BPMN graphs to execute timed station work and stoichiometric mass transfers on Lot~402 without crashing on unmodeled micro-physics.

\section{The Five-Specialist Council Audit: Seven Runtime Ceilings and Fixes}
\label{sec:council-audit-seven-ceilings}

To prepare Layer~4 for promotion from the Lot~402 prototype to self-hosted $300$-workflow production, a \textbf{Five-Specialist Architectural Council}---comprising specialists in (1)~Deterministic Virtual Machines, (2)~Distributed Concurrency \& Storage, (3)~Soil/Thermal/Control Physics, (4)~Zero-Trust Cryptography, and (5)~Human-Factors Calm UX---conducted a line-by-line audit of the repository. The Council identified \textbf{seven structural runtime ceilings} in the current prototype and ratified exact binary and algorithmic remedies for each.

\subsection{Ceiling 1: Imperative C++ \texttt{if/else} Ladders and the \texttt{col-execd} Daemon Stub}

\paragraph{Audit Diagnosis.}
In \texttt{src/stack/shared\_stack\_runtime.hpp}, workflow instantiation inside \texttt{EnsureSpecificWorkflowSpawned()} (line~3833) and token advancement inside \texttt{EvaluateBpmnTokensAndDependencies()} (line~3158) are encoded as monolithic C++ \texttt{if/else} string-matching ladders over specific IDs (\texttt{"wf.garden.pea\_lifecycle"}, \texttt{"wf.soup.pea\_hearth"}). Meanwhile, the standalone daemon binary \texttt{src/daemons/col-execd/main.cpp} (line~157) currently executes a $10\,\text{Hz}$ \texttt{UHAI} ring-buffer heartbeat stub rather than hosting the real state machine. Adding $300$ workflows as C++ branches would require recompiling the orchestrator binary for every governance amendment.

\paragraph{Ratified v3.1 Fix.}
Compile ratified BPMN 2.0 JSON-LD definitions offline into canonical, content-addressed flat bytecode bundles identified by a 32-byte Blake3 \texttt{workflow\_def\_cid}, executed inside \texttt{col-execd} by an embedded, float-free \textbf{Wasmtime} sandbox \parencite{haas2017wasm}. Module validation rejects Wasm floating-point opcodes (\texttt{0x43} \texttt{f32.const}, \texttt{0x44} \texttt{f64.const}, and all IEEE-754 arithmetic instructions \texttt{0x8B..0xA6}) at load time, exposes a minimal 4-import deterministic host ABI, and enforces 5-tier battery-SoC-aware deterministic instruction fuel metering (Listing~\ref{lst:wasm-host-abi}).

\begin{lstlisting}[language=C++,label={lst:wasm-host-abi},caption={Float-free Wasmtime opcode gate and 4-import deterministic Layer~4 host ABI (Ceiling 1).}]
#include <cstdint>
#include <span>

// Rejects any Wasm module containing IEEE-754 f32/f64 constants, loads, or arithmetic
constexpr bool HasForbiddenWasmFloatOpcode(std::span<const uint8_t> wasm_code_sec) noexcept {
    for (const uint8_t op : wasm_code_sec) {
        if (op == 0x43U || op == 0x44U) return true; // f32.const, f64.const
        if (op >= 0x2AU && op <= 0x2BU) return true; // f32.load, f64.load
        if (op >= 0x38U && op <= 0x39U) return true; // f32.store, f64.store
        if (op >= 0x5BU && op <= 0x66U) return true; // f32/f64 comparisons
        if (op >= 0x8BU && op <= 0xA6U) return true; // f32/f64 arithmetic & casts
    }
    return false;
}

// Complete 4-import host ABI exposed to sandboxed Layer 4 BPMN Wasm modules
struct ColExecdDeterministicHostAbi {
    int64_t (*l3_read_slot)(uint64_t scope_entity_id, uint64_t prop_hash) noexcept;
    int32_t (*l3_request_lease)(uint8_t res_class, uint64_t res_id, uint64_t ttl_hlc) noexcept;
    int32_t (*l4_emit_actuation_envelope)(uint64_t env_ptr_offset) noexcept;
    int32_t (*l4_yield_upward)(uint8_t yield_reason, uint64_t payload_hash) noexcept;
};
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Ceiling 2: Single-Instance \texttt{HasWorkflow} Lock, Linear Scan, and State Pollution}

\paragraph{Audit Diagnosis.}
Line~3834 of \texttt{shared\_stack\_runtime.hpp} guards workflow creation with \texttt{if (HasWorkflow(wf\_id)) return;}, restricting the homestead to at most \emph{one} instance per workflow definition---making it impossible to cultivate four staggered pea beds two weeks apart or simmer two soup pots concurrently. Furthermore, \texttt{EvaluateBpmnTokensAndDependencies()} (lines~3170--3205) performs an $O(N)$ linear scan assuming a single active step cursor per workflow (blocking true parallel \texttt{AND}-split and inclusive \texttt{OR}-join Petri-net token semantics \parencite{petri1962kommunikation,vanderaalst1998application}), while \texttt{L4WorkflowInstance} (lines~757--788) embeds pea-specific agronomic fields (\texttt{cultivar\_id}, \texttt{vine\_height\_cm}, \texttt{inoculated\_rhizobia}) directly inside the generic orchestrator struct.

\paragraph{Ratified v3.1 Fix.}
Key workflow instances by unique \texttt{(workflow\_def\_cid, scope\_entity\_id, instance\_nonce)} tuples so arbitrary concurrent beds and vessels instantiate freely. Replace \texttt{L4WorkflowInstance} domain fields with Layer~3 RocksDB property slots keyed by \texttt{(scope\_entity\_id, prop\_hash)}. Represent execution state using cache-aligned 64-byte \texttt{BpmnTokenFrame} arenas indexed by a 64-bit \texttt{active\_token\_bitset} (\texttt{std::countr\_zero} $O(1)$ runnable dispatch) across a formal 12-state token lifecycle supporting parallel \texttt{AND}/\texttt{OR} join barriers.

\subsection{Ceiling 3: Split-Brain L4 Physics, Non-Resetting Lethality, and Boolean Valves}

\paragraph{Audit Diagnosis.}
The Physics Specialist uncovered four coupled control anomalies:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{Duplicate Split-Brain Physics:} \texttt{StepGardenWorkflowBiologyAndTimers()} (\texttt{shared\_stack\_runtime.hpp:2980--3156}) recomputes simplified soil moisture decay and crop growth inside Layer~4, diverging over multi-week runs from the authoritative \texttt{SoilCellRegisters} model in \texttt{soil\_physics.hpp}.
  \item \emph{Wall-Clock Crop Maturity:} Crop progression uses elapsed simulated seconds (\texttt{maturity\_sim\_seconds}) rather than photo-thermal Growing Degree-Days ($\text{GDD}$), and omits irreversible cellular \texttt{necrosis\_damage\_bp} under hard frost ($<271{,}150\,\text{mK}$) or wilting-point drought.
  \item \emph{Pasteurization Danger-Zone Hold Bug:} \texttt{thermal\_vessel.hpp:18} hardcodes \texttt{kTargetPasteurizationHoldSec = 15u} whereas \texttt{shared\_stack\_runtime.hpp:1679} checks for \texttt{120} seconds; worse, \texttt{thermal\_vessel.hpp:154--164} accumulates \texttt{pasteurization\_hold\_s} and \texttt{lethality\_equivalent\_ms} monotonically \emph{without resetting to zero} if power curtailment lets \texttt{core\_temp\_mk} cool back into the $[277{,}150, 333{,}150]\,\text{mK}$ ($4\,^\circ\text{C}\text{--}60\,^\circ\text{C}$) bacterial spore germination danger zone.
  \item \emph{Open-Loop Boolean Actuation:} Layer~4 toggles raw open/closed valve booleans; if \texttt{col-execd} stalls mid-irrigation, an unleased valve stays open indefinitely.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Ratified v3.1 Fix.}
Purge all biological and thermal integration from Layer~4 so \texttt{col-execd} reads state solely via Layer~3 projections. Upgrade \texttt{soil\_physics.hpp} to integrate photo-thermal \texttt{thermal\_time\_mks} (kelvin-seconds above cultivar base temperature) and cumulative \texttt{necrosis\_damage\_bp}. Unify pasteurization targets in JSON-LD cultivar/recipe parameters and strictly zero \texttt{lethality\_equivalent\_ms} whenever unsealed \texttt{core\_temp\_mk < 333150U} (Listing~\ref{lst:bigelow-reset}). Replace boolean pin writes with a 64-byte hardware-leased \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1} carrying Schmitt-trigger deadband bounds \parencite{astrom2008feedback} and a hard HLC lease \texttt{expiry\_hlc} enforced autonomously by the Layer~2 MCU to park actuators in \texttt{safe\_park\_state} upon timeout.

\begin{lstlisting}[language=C++,label={lst:bigelow-reset},caption={Safe Bigelow pasteurization accumulator resetting hold lethality on $<60\,^\circ\text{C}$ danger-zone dips (Ceiling 3).}]
constexpr void StepPasteurizationHoldSafe(
    uint32_t core_temp_mk,
    uint32_t dt_sec,
    uint32_t lethality_rate_ms_per_sec,
    uint32_t target_lethality_ms,
    bool     hermetically_sealed,
    uint32_t& io_lethality_equiv_ms,
    bool&     io_pasteurized_valid) noexcept
{

\subsection{Synthesis: The Deterministic Orchestrator in Full Perspective}

Across the eight chapters of this companion volume, a single architectural thesis has governed every design decision: \emph{freedom in a material commons requires determinism in its physical substrate and restraint in its human interface} \parencite{beer1974,illich1973,ostrom1990}. When a workflow engine relies on nondeterministic cloud services, IEEE-754 floating-point drift, untyped string prompts, or coercive push notifications, inhabitants can trust neither its safety envelopes nor its social neutrality. Conversely, when Layer~4 is reduced to a content-addressed, float-free state machine---reading conserved SI integers from Layer~3, delegating closed-loop Schmitt-deadband actuation to hardware-rooted Layer~2 microcontrollers, routing irreversible entropy through mass-conserving physical salvage sagas, testing every upgrade across $10{,}000$ air-gapped Lot~402 canary ticks, and presenting human stewards with calm, DID-blinded voluntary rounds bounded by $\rho_{\text{human}} \le 0.70$---orchestration disappears into the quiet background of daily life. What remains in the foreground is what mattered all along: fertile soil, shared hearths, resilient neighborhoods, and unhurried human agency.
