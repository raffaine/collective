\begin{invariant}[Bigelow Danger-Zone Reset, Acidification, and ZKP Allergen Hard-Stop]
\label{inv:gamma-haccp}

sampled every $\Delta t = 1000\,\mathrm{ms}$. Culinary release and smart-larder latch
energization require:
{\footnotesize
\begin{align}
    &\text{(Danger-Zone Reset)} && T_{\mathrm{core\_mk}}(t) \in [277{,}150,\, 333{,}150] \;\land\; F_{0,\mathrm{ms}}(t) < F_{0,\mathrm{req}} \implies F_{0,\mathrm{ms}}(t^+) = 0, \label{eq:gamma-reset}\\

\subsection{\emph{The Pantry} vs.\ \emph{The Bakery / Hearth}, the Dual-Loop Bakery, and the 85/15 \emph{Montage} Split (\texttt{D4}, \texttt{D5})}
\label{sec:gamma-dual-loop-bakery}

A central architectural contribution of the v3.2 Horizon Council (\texttt{D4}, \texttt{D5}) is the
thermodynamic and constitutional separation between household perishable consumption---\emph{\textbf{The
Pantry}}---and communal culinary production---\emph{\textbf{The Bakery / Hearth Craft Lens}}---served over
the local Layer~6 Semantic Daemon (\texttt{col-commonsd} + \texttt{col-gatewayd}):

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Thermodynamic Split Between \emph{The Pantry} and \emph{The Bakery / Hearth}:}
    Grouping a household refrigerator larder and a neighborhood commissary bakery into one generic
    ``inventory UI'' obscures two distinct physical dynamics. \emph{\textbf{The Pantry}} is a
    \textbf{Perishable Biological Entropy Lens}: it integrates continuous Arrhenius temperature-time
    spoilage curves ($T(t)$ in $\mathrm{mK} \cdot \mathrm{ms}$ across cold-chain windows
    $275{,}150\text{--}277{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$), tracks local stock drawdown rates
    (\texttt{pantry:drawdownRateMgPerDay}), and solves the \textbf{Spontaneous Weekend Detour Problem}
    with zero surveillance. Suppose forty neighbors express an ambient preference for Saturday sourdough,
    and a sunny morning inspires half of them to hike spontaneously into the mountains. Under Layer~7
    apps, the system either bakes twenty wasted loaves or badgers citizens with Friday push-notification
    confirmations. In The Collective, near-term production is driven by short-lived, auto-expiring Layer~3

    \textbf{zero calendar inspection, zero GPS tracking, and zero nagging alerts}---while automatically
    posting pre-spoilage neighbor surplus offers (\texttt{L3SurplusOffer}) if perishable greens or milk

    \item \textbf{The Dual-Loop Bakery Architecture ($N_s$ Craft Governance vs.\ $N_p$ Zero-Surveillance Demand):}
    A neighborhood bakery fails if $N_p = 500$ external bread eaters vote on starter hydration, oven

    tomorrow's bake in the dark \parencite{ostrom1990}. Scenario Gamma resolves this tension through two
    strictly decoupled cybernetic loops:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \emph{Loop 1 (Node Membership Governance, $N_s$):} Constitutional voting weight
        $V_i(t) = B + R_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$ ($V_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t) = 10{,}000 + R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t)$)
        inside the Bakery Guild's \texttt{col-kmsd} kernel is scoped strictly to the $N_s$ active

    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{The 85\% Verified Staples / 15\% Compositional \emph{Montage} Schedule Split:}

    Pillar~I (\texttt{collective:SurfaceAllocationPolicy}) of the Scenario Gamma Hearth charter partitions

    \begin{equation}
    \begin{aligned}
        \mathtt{hearth:stapleAllocationBp} &= 8{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}\;(85\%), \\
        \mathtt{hearth:montageAllocationBp} &= 1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}\;(15\%).

        \label{eq:gamma-85-15-montage}
    \end{equation}
    Eighty-five percent of Layer~4 batch slots answer verified neighborhood staples (country sourdough,
    seeded rye, split-pea baseline tiffins), while fifteen percent is constitutionally reserved for
