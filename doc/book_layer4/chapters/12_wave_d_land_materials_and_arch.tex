\section{Scenario \texttt{Eta}: Multi-Tier Silviculture, Sterile Mycology, and Botanical Apothecary}
\label{sec:scn-eta}

Industrial forestry treats living watersheds as standing lumber inventories to be clear-cut on financialized discount cycles, compacting
ectomycorrhizal soil networks and burning forty percent of canopy biomass in open slash piles \parencite{scott1998,holling1973}. Scenario
\texttt{Eta} (\texttt{SCN-ETA-COPPICE}) replaces clear-cut monocultures with multi-story \emph{Coppice-with-Standards} silviculture, sterile
laminar-flow culinary and medicinal mycology, and lot-tracked botanical pharmacognosy.

\subsection{Workflow Topology and Multi-Horizon Couplings}

(\texttt{sig:*}) and 256-bit material correlation keys ($\kappa$). First, a long-horizon somatic-autonomic \emph{Silvicultural Rotation Calendar}
manages $5\text{--}12\text{-year}$ deciduous understory stools (\emph{Alnus rubra}, \emph{Robinia pseudoacacia}, \emph{Corylus avellana},
\emph{Salix}) beneath $60\text{--}120\text{-year}$ overstory canopy standards (\emph{Pseudotsuga menziesii}, \emph{Thuja plicata}, \emph{Quercus
alba}). Felling tasks remain parked in the \texttt{DORMANT} token state within the 64-byte \texttt{BpmnTokenFrame} until seasonal Layer 2
telemetry attested with \texttt{QUALITY\_HARDWARE\_ROOT} (\texttt{0x02}) confirms winter sap dormancy

($H'_{\mathrm{scaled}} \ge 28000$), and subterranean fungal vitality via rhizosphere ergosterol assays ($\ge 150\,\mu\mathrm{g/g}$ dry soil).
Once felled by low-impact electric capstan winches and milled on $48\,\mathrm{V}/380\,\mathrm{V}$ bandsaws, green timber enters an autonomic
\emph{Solar-Thermal Kiln Dehumidification Loop} coupled to Stage 3 heat from Scenario \texttt{Theta} and syngas exchangers from Scenario

Second, a high-frequency autonomic \emph{Sterile Mycology Substrate \& Fruiting Pipeline} transforms hardwood sawdust and winter-cut alder bolts
