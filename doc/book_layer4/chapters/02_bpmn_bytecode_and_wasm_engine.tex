combinations of imperative branches

As noted in the Council's audit of the Oasis proving ground (Ceiling~\#1), even our early
\texttt{SharedStackRuntime} prototype exhibited this tension: while
\texttt{pea\_garden\_and\_soup\_lifecycles.bpmn.json} declared clean Business Process Model
and Notation (BPMN~2.0) graphs \parencite{omg2011bpmn,chinosi2012bpmn}, the C++ prototype
evaluated several transitions via domain-specific methods such as
\texttt{EnsureSpecificWorkflowSpawned()} and \texttt{StepGardenWorkflowBiologyAndTimers()},
while \texttt{col-execd/main.cpp} operated as a $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$ UHAI telemetry ring-buffer
harness awaiting live bytecode execution.

Version~3.1 of \texttt{col-execd} eliminates all domain-specific C++ branching inside the
orchestrator. Layer~4 is strictly a \textbf{generic, deterministic state-machine and

Zero}, every process enters \texttt{col-execd} as a content-addressed BPMN~2.0 bytecode
bundle paired with a float-free WebAssembly (\texttt{Wasmtime}) predicate module
\parencite{haas2017wasm}.

In this chapter, we specify the ahead-of-time compilation pipeline, formalize Open-Source

        \texttt{pea\_garden\_and\_soup\_lifecycles.bpmn.json} specifies exact socket
        bindings (\texttt{ipc\_socket}), inventory lock lists

        \texttt{target\_min\_vwc\_bp}, \texttt{irrigation\_dose\_ml},

\subsection{Static Soundness Verification and the Four-Import Host ABI}

As shown in Figure~\ref{fig:ch2-compiler-pipeline}, \texttt{col-bpmnc} enforces four
compile-time soundness gates derived from workflow-net verification theory
\parencite{vanderaalst1998application} before emitting a single byte of machine code:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{No Unbounded Waiting States:} Every \texttt{IntermediateCatchEvent},
        \texttt{UserTask}, resource lease wait, or peer-claim state must carry a finite
        Hybrid Logical Clock deadline $\Delta_{\mathrm{timeout\_ms}} > 0$ wired to an
        explicit timeout or compensation edge. Zombie workflows that lock community tools
        indefinitely cannot compile.
\item \textbf{Mandatory Ecological Floor Wrapping:} Every \texttt{ServiceTask} that emits an
        \texttt{ActuationEnvelopeV1} has the node's Tier~1 ecological floor predicates
        ($F_{\mathrm{eco}}$) injected ahead of its actuation call so no author can omit an
        aquifer or thermal ceiling check.
\item \textbf{Instantaneous Loop Prohibition:} Every directed cycle in the BPMN
        sequence-flow graph must traverse at least one tick-yielding boundary
        (\texttt{WAITING\_TIMER}, \texttt{WAITING\_SIGNAL\_OR\_MSG},
        \texttt{WAITING\_HUMAN\_CLAIM}, or \texttt{YIELDED\_UPWARD\_L5}), guaranteeing
        termination within a single $100\,\mathrm{ms}$ tick even before WebAssembly
        instruction fuel metering engages.
\item \textbf{Hermetic Four-Import WebAssembly Host ABI:} Guard expressions and
        stoichiometric calculations compile to a freestanding WebAssembly module
        (\texttt{nostd}, zero WASI filesystem/clock/socket imports) restricted to
        \textbf{four host functions} in the \texttt{col\_l4\_abi} namespace:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \texttt{l3\_read\_slot\_i64(entity\_id: i64, prop\_hash: i64) -> i64}
\item \texttt{l3\_propose\_lease(res\_class: i32, res\_id: i64, qty: i64, ttl\_ms: i64) ->
            i32}
\item \texttt{l3\_emit\_envelope(target\_id: i64, env\_ptr: i32, env\_len: i32) -> i32}
\item \texttt{l5\_emit\_yield(reason: i32, gate\_hash: i64, deficit\_uj: i64) -> i32}
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

In \texttt{VerifySoftwareReleasePipeline()}
(\texttt{src/daemons/col-execd/execd\_self\_host.hpp}), any constitutional module reporting
\texttt{external\_network\_calls\_during\_build > 0},
\texttt{layer\_skipping\_imports\_detected > 0}, or mismatching digests across three
independent build nodes (\texttt{builder\_1\_sha256}, \texttt{builder\_2\_sha256},
\texttt{builder\_3\_sha256}) is unconditionally rejected.

\section{The Zero-Float Law (\texttt{floating\_point\_opcodes == 0})}
\label{sec:ch2-zero-float-law}

Why does the Sovereign Stack go beyond ``canonicalizing'' floating-point NaNs and instead
\textbf{ban all IEEE-754 floating-point instructions outright} across Layer~3 and Layer~4?

A Sovereign Node is engineered for twenty-year hardware longevity on heterogeneous,
salvaged, and community-repaired silicon: a refurbished ThinkPad (\texttt{x86\_64}) in the
coworking hall, a low-power ARM Cortex-A55 board (\texttt{aarch64}) in the garden shed, and
an open-hardware RISC-V board (\texttt{riscv64}) supervising the micro-hydro weir. Whenever
peer nodes verify a Proof-of-Thermodynamic-Work (PoTW) transition or replay a workflow log
from Layer~3's IPLD Merkle-DAG, they must derive the \textbf{exact same 256-bit state root}.

IEEE-754 floating-point arithmetic (\texttt{float} / \texttt{double}, WebAssembly
\texttt{f32} / \texttt{f64}) subverts cross-architecture determinism through four distinct
hardware mechanisms:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Algebraic Non-Associativity:} In IEEE-754 arithmetic, $(a \oplus b) \oplus c \neq a \oplus (b \oplus c)$
        due to intermediate mantissa rounding at 24 or 53 bits. Summing microgrid solar
        inflows or bilateral clearing deltas in a slightly different collection order shifts
        least-significant bits, fracturing Merkle-tree hashes.
\item \textbf{Cross-ISA \texttt{NaN} Payload and Sign Divergence:} Although the WebAssembly
        1.0 specification defines ``arithmetic'' and ``canonical'' \texttt{NaN} sets
        \parencite{haas2017wasm}, \texttt{x86\_64} SSE/AVX propagates the first operand's
        quiet-\texttt{NaN} payload with negated sign bits on certain instructions,
        \texttt{aarch64} requires explicit \texttt{FPCR.DN} (Default NaN) mode
        configuration, and \texttt{riscv64} mandates returning the single canonical
        \texttt{0x7fc00000} / \texttt{0x7ff8000000000000} quiet \texttt{NaN} regardless of
        input payloads. Emulating canonical \texttt{NaN} checks in software around every
        float instruction adds prohibitive JIT overhead on low-power edge cores.
\item \textbf{Fused Multiply-Add (\texttt{FMA}) and Subnormal Flushing:} Hardware with
        native \texttt{FMA} computes $\mathrm{rn}(a \cdot b + c)$ with a single rounding
        step, whereas CPUs lowering the same expression to $\mathrm{rn}(\mathrm{rn}(a \cdot b) + c)$
        round twice. Similarly, ambient subnormal flushing flags (\texttt{FTZ}/\texttt{DAZ})
        silently zero small decay rates on certain embedded runtimes.
\item \textbf{Catastrophic Cancellation in Cumulative Accounting:} Adding a
        $100\,\mathrm{ms}$ trickle-charge increment ($10\,\mathrm{mW} \times 100\,\mathrm{ms} = 1{,}000\,\mu\mathrm{J}$)
        to a multi-gigajoule seasonal energy total in 32-bit \texttt{f32} silently drops the
        increment to zero once the accumulator exceeds $2^{24}\,\mu\mathrm{J} \approx 16.7\,\mathrm{J}$,
        violating the First Law of Thermodynamics by silent rounding loss!
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Load-Time WebAssembly Opcode Rejection}

To make float-induced state divergence impossible by construction, \texttt{col-execd} embeds
a linear-time binary bytecode scanner ahead of \texttt{Wasmtime} compilation (corresponding
to \texttt{r.floating\_point\_opcodes\_detected == 0} in \texttt{execd\_self\_host.hpp}).
Any WASM binary containing any of the following byte sequences is rejected prior to
instantiation:
\begin{itemize}
\item Value-type descriptors \texttt{0x7D} (\texttt{f32}) or \texttt{0x7C} (\texttt{f64}) in
        function signatures, locals, or globals;
\item Constant immediate opcodes \texttt{0x43} (\texttt{f32.const}) or \texttt{0x44}
        (\texttt{f64.const});
\item Float linear-memory loads and stores (\texttt{0x2A}--\texttt{0x2B},
        \texttt{0x38}--\texttt{0x39});
\item Float comparison, unary/binary arithmetic, and int-float conversion opcodes
        (\texttt{0x5B}--\texttt{0x66}, \texttt{0x8B}--\texttt{0xA6},
        \texttt{0xB2}--\texttt{0xBF}), as well as \texttt{0xFC} saturating float-to-int
        truncations and \texttt{0xFD} SIMD float lane operations.
\end{itemize}

\begin{theorem}[Cross-ISA Bitwise Execution Equivalence]
\label{thm:ch2-cross-isa}
Let $M$ be a WebAssembly workflow module accepted by the \texttt{col-execd} load-time
validator (zero floating-point opcodes, single-threaded linear memory bounded by static
arena cap $B_{\max}$, deterministic 4-import ABI, and positive per-opcode fuel table $F$).
For any Layer~3 state snapshot $S_t$, HLC tick $t$, and initial fuel budget $\Phi_0 \in \mathbb{N}$,
executing $M$ on \texttt{x86\_64}, \texttt{aarch64}, and \texttt{riscv64} yields bit-for-bit
identical terminal linear memory $M'$, remaining fuel $\Phi'$, and emitted
\texttt{BatchStepCommit} byte sequence.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
With all \texttt{f32}/\texttt{f64}, SIMD float, thread/atomic (\texttt{0xFE}), and ambient
host imports excluded, $M$'s instruction set is restricted to two's-complement 32-bit and
64-bit integer arithmetic, bitwise shifts with modulo-width masks, deterministic trapping
integer division (\texttt{i64.div\_s}/\texttt{i64.rem\_s}), little-endian linear memory
accesses, and structured control flow. Under the WebAssembly formal small-step operational
semantics \parencite{haas2017wasm}, the transition relation $(S_t, \sigma_k, \Phi_k) \to (S_t, \sigma_{k+1}, \Phi_k - F(\mathrm{op}_k))$
over this integer subset is a total deterministic function independent of host endianness
(WASM linear memory is strictly little-endian) and host FPU control registers. Induction
over the bounded step count $k \le \Phi_0 / \min F$ establishes bitwise identity of $(M', \Phi')$
and the ordered sequence of host ABI calls across all three ISAs.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Carry-Save Euclidean Division with Persistent Remainder Accumulators}

While power-time integration avoids division altogether, physical rate laws---such as
evapotranspiration moisture draw, heat-exchanger recovery ratios, or photo-thermal maturity
progression over $43{,}200\,\mathrm{s}$---unavoidably divide a product by a scaling
denominator $D > 0$. If a workflow naively computes $\Delta x_t = \lfloor N_t / D \rfloor$
on each $100\,\mathrm{ms}$ tick and discards the remainder, any rate producing $N_t < D$ per
tick yields $\Delta x_t = 0$ forever, freezing slow biological processes completely!

Layer~1b and Layer~4 eliminate integer truncation drift via \textbf{persistent Euclidean
remainder accumulators} (exemplified by \texttt{growth\_sub\_accum} and
\texttt{moisture\_et\_sub\_accum} in \texttt{SharedStackRuntime}):

\begin{lstlisting}[language=C++,caption={Deterministic carry-save Euclidean integer division with zero cumulative truncation drift.},label={lst:ch2-euclidean-accum}]
struct alignas(8) EuclideanRateAccumulator {
    int64_t rem{0}; // Persistent remainder strictly in [0, den - 1]
};

[[nodiscard]] constexpr int64_t StepCarrySaveDivide(
    int64_t num,
    int64_t den,
    EuclideanRateAccumulator& state) noexcept {
    if (den <= 0) {
        return 0;
    }
    const int64_t accum = num + state.rem;
    int64_t delta = accum / den;
    int64_t next_rem = accum % den;
    // Enforce true Euclidean division: 0 <= next_rem < den even when accum < 0
    if (next_rem < 0) {
        next_rem += den;
        delta -= 1;
    }
    state.rem = next_rem;
    return delta;
}
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{invariant}[Sub-LSB Conservation of Carry-Save Integration]
\label{inv:ch2-carry-save}
For any sequence of tick numerators $\{N_k\}_{k=1}^{T}$ in $\mathbb{Z}$ and constant
denominator $D \in \mathbb{N}^+$ initialized with $r_0 = 0$, the cumulative integer output
$\sum_{k=1}^{T} \Delta_k$ produced by \texttt{StepCarrySaveDivide} satisfies the exact
identity:
\begin{equation}
D \sum_{k=1}^{T} \Delta_k + r_T = \sum_{k=1}^{T} N_k, \qquad 0 \le r_T < D.
\end{equation}
Consequently, cumulative quantization discrepancy over an arbitrary multi-year horizon $T \to \infty$
never grows with $T$; it remains strictly bounded within $[0, 1)$ least-significant output
unit.
\end{invariant}

\section{Cache-Aligned 64-Byte \texttt{BpmnTokenFrame} and the 12-State Lifecycle}
\label{sec:ch2-token-frame}

In the Oasis prototype (Ceiling~\#2), \texttt{L4WorkflowInstance} suffered from three
structural bottlenecks when scaled beyond Lot~402's six seed workflows: (1)
\texttt{HasWorkflow(wf\_id)} enforced a single-instance lock per workflow definition, making
it impossible to run four staggered pea beds or twelve parallel fermentation crocks under
the same BPMN graph; (2) domain-specific fields (\texttt{crop\_growth\_bp},
\texttt{estimated\_nitrogen\_ppm}) were embedded directly inside the orchestrator struct
alongside heap-allocated \texttt{std::string}s that scattered state across CPU cache lines;
and (3) \texttt{BpmnTokenState} tracked only five coarse states via a linear vector scan.

In Version~3.1, all domain-specific variables are evicted out of Layer~4 into
content-addressed Layer~3 entity registers keyed by \texttt{(scope\_entity\_id,
prop\_hash)}. Multiple concurrent instances of the same \texttt{workflow\_def\_cid} are
uniquely addressed by the tuple \texttt{(workflow\_def\_cid, scope\_entity\_id,
instance\_nonce)}---allowing \texttt{bed\_01} through \texttt{bed\_04} to run independent,
staggered lifecycles over identical bytecode.

\section{Immutable \texttt{workflow\_def\_cid} Version Pinning and Mid-Flight Upgrades}
\label{sec:ch2-version-pinning}

Agricultural, silvicultural, and architectural workflows span weeks, seasons, or years.
During a six-month crop cycle, the community will inevitably refine its irrigation deadbands
or amend a recipe in June based on lessons learned in May. In naive dynamic-scripting
engines, overwriting a workflow file on disk mutates the graph underneath currently
executing instances---stranding tokens on deleted node indices or skipping resource-release
edges.

In \texttt{col-execd}, bytecode is \textbf{content-addressed and immutable}. Every
\texttt{BpmnTokenFrame} pins its exact \texttt{workflow\_def\_cid} at creation time, and
Layer~3 retains every historical bytecode blob referenced by at least one non-terminated
instance. When Layer~5 ratifies a successor bundle $\mathrm{CID}_{\mathrm{new}}$ for a
logical workflow family, Governance must explicitly choose one of three deterministic
transition policies:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{\texttt{DRAIN\_NATURAL} (Default Conservative Policy):} All in-flight
        instances continue stepping against their pinned $\mathrm{CID}_{\mathrm{old}}$ until
        they reach \texttt{TERMINATED\_SETTLED}; all newly spawned instances bind to
        $\mathrm{CID}_{\mathrm{new}}$. Zero mid-flight state translation occurs.
\item \textbf{\texttt{SAFEPOINT\_MIGRATE} (Compiler-Verified State Injection):} Governance
        supplies an explicit node-and-lease mapping descriptor $M: V_{\mathrm{old}} \rightharpoonup V_{\mathrm{new}}$.
        Ahead of ratification, \texttt{col-bpmnc} statically verifies that every quiescent
        safepoint in $\mathrm{dom}(M) \subseteq \{\texttt{WAITING\_TIMER}, \texttt{WAITING\_HUMAN\_CLAIM}, \texttt{BLOCKED\_PRECONDITION}\}$
        preserves identical held \texttt{L3ResourceLeaseLock} sets and salvage handlers in
        $V_{\mathrm{new}}$. At runtime, \texttt{col-execd} atomically rewrites
        \texttt{workflow\_def\_cid\_prefix} and \texttt{current\_node\_idx} only when an
        instance parks at a verified safepoint.
\item \textbf{\texttt{COMPENSATE\_AND\_RESTART} (Emergency Safety Drain):} Reserved for
        critical safety flaws, pathogen quarantines, or breached treaties. Upon receipt
        across \texttt{l4\_l5.sock}, \texttt{col-execd} immediately transitions all
        in-flight tokens of $\mathrm{CID}_{\mathrm{old}}$ into
        \texttt{SALVAGE\_COMPENSATING} (\texttt{salvage\_handler\_idx}), safely parking
        actuators, releasing digital escrows, or routing partially processed biomass to
        salvage workflows before optionally spawning a clean instance under
        $\mathrm{CID}_{\mathrm{new}}$.
\end{enumerate}

Through ahead-of-time BPMN soundness compilation, the load-time Zero-Float Law,
microjoule-exact fixed-point SI arithmetic, carry-save Euclidean accumulators, 64-byte
cache-line token frames, and content-addressed version pinning, \texttt{col-execd}
transforms community process diagrams into deterministic machine execution worthy of
