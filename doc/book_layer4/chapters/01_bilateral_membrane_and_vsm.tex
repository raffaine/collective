\chapter{The Bilateral Membrane, Keyless Blindness, and VSM Closure}
\label{ch:bilateral-membrane}

\epigraph{Control in viable systems is never a matter of shouting commands down a pyramid. It is the art of designing membranes across which requisite variety is attenuated and amplified without destroying the autonomy of the living parts.}{---Adapted from \textcite{beer1979}}

An orchestrator is defined less by the expressiveness of the programs it can run than by the
boundaries it cannot cross. In conventional cloud and industrial control architectures, the
workflow engine gradually becomes an omniscient integration hub: it opens TCP connections to
external REST APIs, queries user identity databases directly, polls Modbus sensors over
local Ethernet, writes raw actuation bits to programmable logic controllers, and exposes
mutable administrative endpoints to web dashboards. Convenience in early prototyping soon
hardens into a catastrophic blast radius. A single compromised credential, misparsed HTTP
payload, or errant script gains simultaneous visibility into personal human behavior and
unmediated authority over physical machinery \parencite{rose2020zero,saltzer1984end}.

Layer~4 of the Sovereign Stack (\texttt{col-execd}) inverts this tradition through
\textbf{structural containment}. Because \texttt{col-execd} sits at the exact locus where
collective digital intent becomes kinetic consequence, its power is made safe by stripping
away every channel of perception and action save two strictly typed, kernel-authenticated
POSIX sockets. It holds no network interfaces, possesses no cryptographic identity keys,
knows no citizen's name, touches no hardware pins, and rejects every direct mutation from
user-facing interfaces.

In this opening chapter of Part~I, we establish the four constitutional pillars that bound
\texttt{col-execd} within a Sovereign Node: (1) strict seven-layer adjacency across the
bilateral Unix-domain socket membrane; (2) clean-slate genesis and the Constitutional Intent
U-Turn; (3) keyless cryptographic blindness via 24-byte capability handles; and (4)
cybernetic closure at Recursion Level~$N$ of Stafford Beer's Viable System Model
\parencite{beer1972,beer1979}.

\subsection{Why \texttt{SOCK\_SEQPACKET} and Kernel Credential Pinning}

The choice of \texttt{AF\_UNIX} with \texttt{SOCK\_SEQPACKET}---rather than TCP loopback
sockets, \texttt{SOCK\_STREAM} byte pipes, or \texttt{SOCK\_DGRAM} datagrams---resolves four
classic classes of systems vulnerability simultaneously:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Elimination of Frame-Slicing Desynchronization:} Byte-stream sockets
        (\texttt{SOCK\_STREAM}) do not preserve message boundaries; a partial read or
        malformed length prefix can shift the parser window across subsequent frames,
        inducing confused-deputy state corruption. \texttt{SOCK\_SEQPACKET} guarantees
        atomic record delivery: each \texttt{recvmsg()} returns either one complete,
        length-verified binary IPC frame or an explicit error (\texttt{EMSGSIZE} /
        \texttt{EAGAIN}).
\item \textbf{Connection-Oriented FIFO Determinism with Backpressure:} Unlike connectionless
        \texttt{SOCK\_DGRAM} sockets, \texttt{SOCK\_SEQPACKET} maintains a dedicated virtual
        circuit with strict in-order delivery and kernel-enforced receive buffer
        backpressure, ensuring that bursts of upward yields or downward step commits can
        never reorder causal transitions.
\item \textbf{Unforgeable Kernel Peer Authentication:} Immediately upon \texttt{accept()} or
        \texttt{connect()}, \texttt{col-execd} queries the kernel socket descriptor via
        \texttt{getsockopt(..., SOL\_SOCKET, SO\_PEERCRED, ...)} on Linux (or
        \texttt{LOCAL\_PEERCRED} on BSD/macOS) to inspect the unforgeable process UID, GID,
        and PID of the peer daemon. Any connection on \texttt{l4\_l5.sock} whose effective
        UID is not \texttt{col-kmsd}, or on \texttt{l3\_l4.sock} whose effective UID is not
        \texttt{col-storaged}, is closed prior to reading a single payload byte.
\item \textbf{Irreversible Syscall Sandboxing:} After opening its two Unix-domain sockets
        and mapping its read-only telemetry projection page at startup, \texttt{col-execd}
        installs a strict \texttt{seccomp-bpf} filter and drops all Linux capabilities
        (\texttt{CAP\_NET\_ADMIN}, \texttt{CAP\_NET\_RAW}, \texttt{CAP\_SYS\_ADMIN}).
        Subsequent attempts to invoke \texttt{socket()}, \texttt{connect()},
        \texttt{openat()} on writable filesystem paths, or \texttt{execve()} terminate the
        offending thread immediately via \texttt{SIGSYS}.
\end{itemize}

\begin{invariant}[Strict Bilateral Adjacency]
\label{inv:ch1-bilateral-adjacency}
At every Hybrid Logical Clock instant $t$ after bootstrap initialization completes, the file
descriptor table $\mathcal{F}_t$ of \texttt{col-execd} satisfies:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{F}_t \subseteq \{ \mathrm{fd}_{\mathrm{l3\_l4}}, \, \mathrm{fd}_{\mathrm{l4\_l5}}, \, \mathrm{fd}_{\mathrm{telemetry\_ro}}, \, \mathrm{fd}_{\mathrm{log\_append}} \}
\end{equation}
with $\mathcal{F}_t \cap \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{AF\_INET}} = \emptyset$ and $\mathcal{F}_t \cap \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{dev\_io}} = \emptyset$.
Consequently, no state transition inside Layer~4 can observe physical reality except through
verified Layer~3 commits, nor alter human or external institutional state except through
ratified Layer~5 frames.
\end{invariant}

\section{Clean-Slate Genesis, the Read-Only Projection, and the Intent U-Turn}
\label{sec:ch1-intent-uturn}

A subtle authoritarian temptation lurks inside automated community infrastructure: shipping
the system pre-loaded with ``helpful'' default schedules. If a household or cooperative
boots a Sovereign Node on Day~1 and immediately finds eighteen pre-spawned gardening,
composting, and maintenance tasks blinking on its walls, the software has already assumed
the posture of an uninvited landlord.

In \texttt{SharedStackRuntime::Bootstrap()}, the live Sovereign Stack establishes
\textbf{Clean-Slate Day-1 Genesis}: the workflow table initializes strictly empty
(\texttt{workflows\_.clear()}). On Day~1 at 08:00, household needs such as warm soup or
electrical baseload remain transparently routed to their explicit Layer~7 fallback channels
(\texttt{SourcingMode::L7\_LEGACY\_CHANNEL}) while the local node waits silently. Not a
single BPMN state machine is instantiated in Layer~4 until a human Steward articulates an
intent in Layer~6 and explicitly ratifies a Layer~5 governance transition
(\texttt{DecisionStatus::APPROVED} or \texttt{ADAPTED\_AND\_APPROVED}) shifting that asset's
sourcing policy to \texttt{R0\_LOCAL\_WORKFLOWS} or \texttt{R1\_MESH\_PEERS}.

\subsection{Why \texttt{/api/v1/l4/*} Returns HTTP \texttt{405 Method Not Allowed}}

When Stewards inspect running workflows through the Layer~6 Agora interface or the Oasis
diegetic tablet, \texttt{col-gatewayd} serves read-only JSON-LD state projections derived
from Layer~4's telemetry ring and Layer~3's state snapshots. Inevitably, web developers
accustomed to Model-View-Controller CRUD apps ask: \textit{why not let clicking ``Start
Pump'' or ``Spawn Pea Workflow'' in the browser send a \texttt{POST /api/v1/l4/workflows}
directly to \texttt{col-execd}?}

Allowing Layer~6 to mutate Layer~4 directly would sever Layer~5 from the loop. A compromised
browser session, a buggy JavaScript widget, or an overly eager Layer~6 language-model
assistant could instantiate workflows, alter setpoints, or claim tasks without Web-of-Trust
verification, without Qualitative Friction capacity checks, and without Ricardian policy
audit. Therefore, \texttt{col-gatewayd} enforces an unyielding structural rule across every
mutating HTTP verb targeting the Layer~4 namespace:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\forall m \in \{\texttt{POST}, \texttt{PUT}, \texttt{PATCH}, \texttt{DELETE}\}: \quad
\mathrm{Gateway}(m, \texttt{/api/v1/l4/*}) \\
\longrightarrow \texttt{HTTP 405} \; (\texttt{L4\_IS\_STRICTLY\_READ\_ONLY\_NO\_MUTATIONS})
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

\subsection{The Constitutional Intent U-Turn}

Every lawful human interaction with Layer~4 instead traverses a complete, auditable
cybernetic arc known as the \textbf{Constitutional Intent U-Turn}, illustrated in

\label{def:ch1-intent-uturn}
The Constitutional Intent U-Turn is the bidirectional mediated path governing all
cross-boundary causation between human deliberation, physical execution, and external legal

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Downward Ratification Leg ($\mathrm{Human} \to \mathrm{L6} \to \mathrm{L5} \to \mathrm{L4} \to \mathrm{L3}$):}

        signed JSON-LD intent to Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}), which validates Web-of-Trust

        ratification, and transmits a binary command frame across \texttt{l4\_l5.sock} to
        \texttt{col-execd}. Layer~4 steps the corresponding BPMN token and commits resource

        \texttt{col-storaged}.
\item \textbf{Upward Yield Leg ($\mathrm{L4} \xrightarrow{\text{yield}} \mathrm{L5} \to \mathrm{L6} \to \mathrm{L7}$):}

\end{definition}

\section{Keyless Cryptographic Blindness and the 24-Byte \texttt{ClaimHandle}}
\label{sec:ch1-keyless-blindness}

Why do digital task systems in conventional cooperatives so frequently decay into
resentment, informal hierarchy, or quiet surveillance? Even when an organization abolishes
managers on paper, if its scheduling database logs ``Alice completed 14 tasks (19.4 hours)
this week; Bob completed 2 tasks (1.8 hours),'' the database itself becomes an engine of
panoptic discipline \parencite{zuboff2019,freeman1972}. Citizens who are ill, grieving,
caregiving, or simply resting feel an unspoken algorithmic gaze recording their absence;
citizens who enjoy visible mechanical tasks accumulate informal dominance over those
performing unmodeled emotional care.

Layer~4 prevents the emergence of a workplace panopticon not by promising ``strict privacy
settings'' on an administrative table, but through \textbf{Keyless Cryptographic Blindness}:
\texttt{col-execd} is architecturally incapable of representing who any person is.

\subsection{Zero Keys, Zero DIDs, Zero Verifiable Credential Graphs}

Within a Sovereign Node, all citizen Decentralized Identifiers (\texttt{did:peer:...}),
Ed25519 signing keys, BLS12-381 threshold keys, W3C Verifiable Credentials
(\texttt{Hydro\_Earthworks\_VC}, \texttt{HVDC\_HotWork\_VC},
\texttt{Perinatal\_Steward\_VC}) \parencite{w3c2022vc}, and pairwise Reputational Wealth
records reside exclusively inside Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}).

During the Council's audit of the Oasis prototype headers (Ceiling~\#6 in the Blueprint), the Sovereign Stack \& Human Factors Auditor flagged a critical prototype leak: \texttt{UpwardYieldPacket} in \texttt{uhai\_ring\_buffer.hpp} contained a legacy 64-byte \texttt{char claimant\_did[64]} field, and \texttt{execd\_self\_host.hpp} wrote the placeholder string \texttt{"did:peer:steward:l4-execd-bom-resolver"} into it. Had real citizen DIDs ever crossed \texttt{l4\_l5.sock} into that field, any read-only inspection of Layer~4 execution logs would have exposed a chronological dossier of every citizen's physical movements across the settlement.

In the ratified v3.1 wire specification, plaintext DIDs are unconditionally prohibited
across \texttt{l4\_l5.sock}. Whenever a Steward commits to a voluntary human task in
Layer~6, \texttt{col-kmsd} verifies that the Steward holds the required safety credentials
in Layer~5 and mints a one-time, unlinkable 24-byte \textbf{\texttt{ClaimHandle}}
\parencite{camenisch2004signature,miller2006robust}:

\begin{lstlisting}[language=C++,caption={The ratified 24-byte DID-blinded \texttt{ClaimHandle} crossing \texttt{l4\_l5.sock}.},label={lst:ch1-claim-handle}]
struct alignas(8) ClaimHandle {
    uint64_t ephemeral_token_id{0}; // Unlinkable per-claim PRF output from L5 (8B)
    uint64_t capability_mask{0};    // Bitmask of verified safety VCs (no DID!) (8B)
    uint64_t expiry_hlc{0};         // HLC lease deadline for automatic release (8B)
};
static_assert(sizeof(ClaimHandle) == 24, "ClaimHandle must be exactly 24 bytes.");
static_assert(alignof(ClaimHandle) == 8, "ClaimHandle must be 8-byte aligned.");
\end{lstlisting}

Let us examine how each 64-bit word of \texttt{ClaimHandle} preserves safety and
coordination while guaranteeing total anonymity inside Layer~4:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{\texttt{ephemeral\_token\_id}:} Computed inside \texttt{col-kmsd} as the
        truncated keyed pseudorandom function
        $\mathrm{PRF}_{K_{\mathrm{L5}}}(\mathrm{Steward\_DID} \parallel \mathrm{task\_instance\_id} \parallel \mathrm{nonce})$,
        where $K_{\mathrm{L5}}$ never leaves Layer~5's hardware-backed key enclave
        (\texttt{ATECC608A}) \parencite{microchip_atecc608a}. If the same Steward claims ten
        garden tasks in a single morning, \texttt{col-execd} observes ten statistically
        independent uniform 64-bit integers.
    \item \textbf{\texttt{capability\_mask}:} Encodes up to 64 domain safety clearances verified by Layer~5 at claim time (for example, bit~0 = \texttt{SOIL\_EARTHWORKS}, bit~4 = \texttt{FOOD\_PASTEURIZATION}, bit~11 = \texttt{CNC\_SPINDLE}, bit~19 = \texttt{HVDC\_380V\_LOCKOUT}). Before \texttt{col-execd} transitions a high-hazard BPMN \texttt{UserTask} into \texttt{ACTIVE\_TOKEN}, it checks bitwise inclusion $(\mathrm{handle.capability\_mask} \mathbin{\&} \mathrm{node.required\_cap\_mask}) == \mathrm{node.required\_cap\_mask}$ in a single CPU cycle---enforcing hard functional safety \parencite{iec61508} without knowing \textit{who} holds the credential.
\item \textbf{\texttt{expiry\_hlc}:} Encodes the Hybrid Logical Clock timestamp at which the
        claim lease automatically expires if unfulfilled. If a Steward commits to turning a
        compost bay, realizes halfway through the morning that they need a nap or wish to
        sit with a child, and simply walks away, \texttt{expiry\_hlc} elapses silently.
        \texttt{col-execd} returns the task to \texttt{UNASSIGNED} (or invokes its safe
        fallback path) without recording a ``demerit,'' because it has no persistent
        identity against which a demerit could even be indexed.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{theorem}[Unlinkability Under Orchestrator Memory Compromise]
\label{thm:ch1-claim-unlinkability}
Suppose an adversary obtains complete read access to the volatile address space of
\texttt{col-execd} and the entire historical \texttt{BatchStepCommit} log stored in Layer~3
over $M$ completed human tasks, while Layer~5's blinding key $K_{\mathrm{L5}}$ remains
uncompromised inside \texttt{col-kmsd}. Let $d_a, d_b$ be any two citizen DIDs holding
identical safety clearances for a task class $\mathcal{C}$. For any polynomial-time
distinguisher $\mathcal{A}$ attempting to determine whether two task completions $\tau_1, \tau_2 \in \mathcal{C}$
were performed by the same citizen or by two distinct citizens, the distinguishing advantage
satisfies:
\begin{equation}
\left| \Pr[\mathcal{A}(\mathrm{View}_{\mathrm{L3,L4}}) = 1 \mid \mathrm{same}] - \Pr[\mathcal{A}(\mathrm{View}_{\mathrm{L3,L4}}) = 1 \mid \mathrm{distinct}] \right| \le \epsilon_{\mathrm{PRF}}(\lambda) + \frac{M^2}{2^{65}}
\end{equation}
where $\epsilon_{\mathrm{PRF}}(\lambda)$ is the negligible PRF advantage of
$\mathrm{HMAC\text{-}SHA256}$ and $M^2 / 2^{65}$ bounds birthday collisions on 64-bit
ephemeral token identifiers.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Every record visible in $\mathrm{View}_{\mathrm{L3,L4}}$ for a task $\tau_i \in \mathcal{C}$
depends on the citizen's identity solely through the tuple $\langle \mathrm{ephemeral\_token\_id}_i, \mathrm{capability\_mask}_i, \mathrm{expiry\_hlc}_i \rangle$.
By assumption, $d_a$ and $d_b$ share the restricted capability projection
$\mathrm{capability\_mask}_i = \mathrm{mask}(\mathcal{C})$, and $\mathrm{expiry\_hlc}_i = t_{\mathrm{claim},i} + \Delta_{\mathrm{TTL}}(\mathcal{C})$
is a deterministic function of the public task class TTL and claim tick. Replacing
$\mathrm{PRF}_{K_{\mathrm{L5}}}$ with a truly random function $R$ incurs distinguishing loss
at most $\epsilon_{\mathrm{PRF}}(\lambda)$. Under $R$, distinct nonces $(i, \mathrm{nonce}_i)$
map to independent uniform draws in $\{0,1\}^{64}$ regardless of whether the prefix is $d_a$
or $d_b$, which are identical in distribution up to collision events bounded by
$\binom{M}{2} 2^{-64} < M^2 / 2^{65}$.
\end{proof}

When a task completes and Layer~2 hardware transducers (or a verified witness quorum)
confirm the physical outcome on Layer~3, \texttt{col-execd} emits an
\texttt{UpwardYieldPacket} referencing only \texttt{ephemeral\_token\_id}. Layer~5 alone
looks up the blinding record, retires the ephemeral handle, and credits non-transferable
local Reputational Wealth if applicable. Work is coordinated with precision; the worker
remains sovereign and unmonitored.

\section{Viable System Model (VSM) Recursion Level $N$ Closure}
\label{sec:ch1-vsm-closure}

Having bounded \texttt{col-execd} vertically between Layer~3 and Layer~5, we must bound it
horizontally across geographical scale. When engineers first encounter BPMN choreography
across federated settlements, a seductive mistake frequently arises: proposing a
``Bioregional Orchestrator'' that ingests sensor streams from fifty neighborhoods and
schedules crops, trucks, and batteries across an entire watershed as one giant distributed
state machine.

Both cybernetic theory and twentieth-century history warn that centralized
meta-orchestration is fatal \parencite{scott1998,beer1972}. W. Ross Ashby's \textbf{Law of
Requisite Variety} proves that a regulator can stabilize a system only if the variety of
states available to the regulator matches or exceeds the variety of disturbances confronting
the regulated medium \parencite{ashby1956}. Across a heterogeneous bioregion spanning
coastal fog belts, alpine micro-hydro valleys, and urban cooperative blocks, ecological
micro-disturbances explode combinatorially. Any central orchestrator attempting to step
token-level workflows across the whole watershed either chokes on communication latency,
collapses during storm-induced radio partitions \parencite{fall2003}, or flattens living
local nuance into brittle administrative quotas.

Stafford Beer resolved this dilemma in the \textbf{Viable System Model (VSM)} through the
principle of \textbf{recursive cybernetic isomorphism} \parencite{beer1972,beer1979}: every
viable system contains autonomous viable systems at Recursion Level~$N-1$ and is embedded
within a polycentric viable system at Recursion Level~$N+1$, with \textit{structural closure
of operational control at each recursion level}.

\subsection{Mapping the Sovereign Node at Recursion Level $N$}

At \textbf{Recursion Level~$N$} (a single Sovereign Node---whether a Stage~1A 4-SBC
homestead cluster on Lot~402 or a Stage~3 Community Land Trust campus), the five subsystems
of Beer's VSM map cleanly onto the seven layers of the Sovereign Stack:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{System 1 (Primary Metabolic Operations):} The physical stations, raised beds,
        pasteurization vessels, solar arrays, workshops, and care pods whose localized
        reflex loops run on Layer~2 microcontrollers (\texttt{col-peripheral}) over physical
        reality (Layer~1). Each station possesses autonomous local safety reflexes
        (over-current trip, thermal cutoff, Schmitt deadband regulation) that survive even
        if Layers~3--7 power down completely.
\item \textbf{System 2 (Anti-Oscillation and Sympathetic Damping):} Realized inside
        \textbf{Layer~4 (\texttt{col-execd})} via its lexicographic resource lease
        coordinator, 5-tier priority scheduler, and staggered load-leveling windows.
        System~2 prevents two autonomous System~1 processes---say, a timber kiln and an
        induction soup kettle---from firing simultaneously and collapsing the
        $48\,\mathrm{V}$ DC bus, damping inter-workflow oscillations without dictating
        community goals.
\item \textbf{System 3 and System $3^*$ (Internal Homeostasis and Sporadic Physical Audit):}
        Jointly realized by \textbf{Layer~4 (\texttt{col-execd})} and \textbf{Layer~3
        (\texttt{col-storaged})}. System~3 allocates shared node exergy budgets across
        running workflows according to ratified priority tiers, while System~$3^*$
        corresponds to Layer~3's independent Proof-of-Thermodynamic-Work (PoTW) hardware
        verification and Meshed Spatial Reasoning (MSR) sensor cross-checks, ensuring
        workflows cannot hallucinate inventory or mask a stuck valve.
\item \textbf{System 4 (Environmental Intelligence and Outside-and-Then Simulation):}
        Realized by \textbf{Layer~6 (\texttt{col-intentd})} paired with \textbf{Oasis
        (\texttt{Layer 1b})}. System~4 looks outward to weather forecasts, watershed
        hydrology, and prospective treaties, simulating candidate BPMN revisions across
        $10{,}000+$ accelerated shadow soak ticks before proposing adaptations to the
        community.
\item \textbf{System 5 (Constitutional Identity and Normative Closure):} Realized by
        \textbf{Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd})}---the living assembly of Stewards who ratify
        ecological floors ($F_{\mathrm{eco}}$), survival baselines
        ($B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$), and inter-node covenants, arbitrating any value conflict
        between System~3's present operational stability and System~4's future adaptation
        \parencite{ostrom1990}.
\end{itemize}

\begin{invariant}[VSM Recursion Level $N$ Operational Closure]
\label{inv:ch1-vsm-closure}
Every instance of \texttt{col-execd} executes System~2 and System~3 supervisory control
strictly and exclusively over the physical resources, stations, and ledger shard of its
local Sovereign Node (Recursion Level~$N$). No global or bioregional Layer~4 daemon exists
at Recursion Level~$N+1$; inter-node coordination occurs solely via ratified Layer~5
BPVP/Treaty covenants and Layer~3 Bilateral Clearing Channels compiled into local boundary
invariants.
\end{invariant}

\subsection{How Recursion Level $N+1$ Emerges Without a Central Boss}

Consider how this architectural boundary operates across two concrete multi-node topologies
already proven in the codebase and scaled in Chapter~5:

First, within a \textbf{Solo Homestead Personal Cluster} (Recursion Level~$N$, sub-nodes
$N-1$), Lot~402 distributes its local metabolism across four low-power SBCs connected over a
local wired/VLAN fabric: \texttt{node-01:office-coordinator},
\texttt{node-02:garage-fablab}, \texttt{node-03:garden-shed}, and
\texttt{node-04:kitchen-hearth}. Because all four SBCs belong to the same single household
governance domain ($R_0$), their local BPMN tokens coordinate via deterministic
intra-cluster handoffs (\texttt{L4\_CROSS\_NODE\_REQUISITION} and
\texttt{L3\_INTERNODE\_HANDOFF}) pinned near the physical actuators they supervise---so that
a severed garden Wi-Fi link never interrupts the garden shed's local root-zone irrigation
envelope.

Second, when Lot~402 enables external neighborhood federation
(\texttt{SetExternalR1SwarmEnabled(true)}) across \textbf{Recursion Level~$N+1$} (Trust
Rings $R_1$ through $R_4$), Lot~402's \texttt{col-execd} \textit{never} reaches into a
neighbor's \texttt{col-execd} to mutate their token table or lock their joinery bench.
Instead, coordination ascends to Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}) and Layer~3
(\texttt{col-storaged}):
\begin{enumerate}
\item Layer~5 nodes exchange sovereign capability advertisements and route mutual-aid
        surpluses via the Bioregional Path-Vector Protocol (BPVP) \parencite{rekhter2006},
        pushing compiled next-hop \texttt{FIB} entries and ratified \texttt{Treaty\_CID}
        invariants (\texttt{Invariants\_BPMN}) downward into each participating node's local
        \texttt{col-execd}.
\item Layer~3 nodes lock pairwise zero-sum microjoule credit windows
        (\texttt{PairwiseBccChannel} / HTLCs) and append two-phase Thermodynamic Delivery
        Receipts.
\item Each node's \texttt{col-execd} then executes its half of the covenant locally and
        autonomously, retaining full capability to shed Tier~5 exports or enter safe
        partition sagas if radio links fail.
\end{enumerate}

By sealing \texttt{col-execd} behind a two-socket POSIX membrane, blinding it to citizen
identities via 24-byte \texttt{ClaimHandle}s, routing all human and external interactions
around the Constitutional Intent U-Turn, and closing its cybernetic authority strictly at
VSM Recursion Level~$N$, Layer~4 achieves a rare synthesis in systems engineering: an
executive engine powerful enough to coordinate thousands of physical processes, yet
structurally incapable of becoming a ruler.
