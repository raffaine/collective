\chapter{Scenario Psi: Restorative Justice \& Conflict Resolution}

\section{The Legacy Paradigm \& Its Failures}
The legacy justice system is fundamentally punitive, adversarial, and violently extractive. It relies on a state monopoly on violence (police) and ruinously expensive legacy gatekeepers (lawyers, courts). When a dispute occurs, legacy courts focus on assigning blame and extracting fiat fines, destroying human relationships in the process. In a highly interdependent, decentralized mesh, unresolved interpersonal conflict acts as systemic friction (heat). If two Stewards operating the Fabrication Commons refuse to coordinate, the entire Node's supply chain fractures. The Node requires a method to resolve disputes that repairs the social graph rather than severing it.

\section{The Incremental Automation Pathway}
Transitioning to algorithmic restorative justice requires careful staging to maintain trust:

\subsection{Phase 1: Human-in-the-Loop (Manual Orchestration)}
Citizens manually file mediation intents and human committees attempt to find an unbiased third party to arbitrate. Escrows, if any, are managed via multi-sig wallets negotiated manually out-of-band.

\subsection{Phase 2: The Centaur (AI-Assisted)}
The AI maps the Trust Ring and calculates potential neutral mediators using graph theory, presenting a curated list to the conflicting parties. The Orchestrator drafts the binding arbitration agreements, but human consensus is strictly required to initialize the mediation and freeze escrowed tokens.

\subsection{Phase 3: Full Autonomy}
The Orchestrator autonomously parses the `MediationIntent`, instantly locks the disputed Value Tokens in L3 CRDT wallets, calculates absolute mathematical neutrality to assign a mediator, and schedules a secure physical space for the session.

\section{The Human Boundary (Limits of Automation)}
The AI excels at analyzing graph topography. It can mathematically guarantee the selection of an unbiased mediator by calculating edge weights and degrees of separation. It can also manage the cryptographic escrow and route restitution bounties. However, it cannot perform the emotional labor of conflict de-escalation, active listening, or psychological healing. The AI sets the physical stage and ensures systemic neutrality, but the actual resolution and human restitution must be negotiated and executed by conscious human actors in the physical realm.

\section{Orchestration Mechanics (The ``How'')}
The core of Scenario Psi is Graph Theory routing. To find a neutral mediator, the Orchestrator executes a bidirectional Breadth-First Search (BFS) algorithm across the Layer 5 Trust Graph, evaluating edge weights (reputation and vouches). It searches for a DID whose path distance and trust weighting to both conflicting parties are mathematically symmetrical (within a strict margin of error).

Upon filing the intent, the Orchestrator triggers an atomic state lock on the disputed L3 assets to prevent capital flight. The BPMN engine then spawns a concurrent process to book an acoustically secure space (Layer 1 interaction). When the physical mediation concludes, the state is resolved via a multi-sig NFC payload (3-of-3 signature). Only this physical L2 interaction can asynchronously signal the BPMN gateway to release the escrow or route the offender to a thermodynamic `MaintenanceBounty` queue to pay off their kinetic debt.
