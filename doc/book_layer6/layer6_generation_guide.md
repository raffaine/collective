# Layer 6 Semantic Commonwealth: Authoring Charter & Developer Guide (v3.1)

## 1. Constitutional Role: The Most Visible Layer of the Sovereign Stack
This charter governs `doc/book_layer6/` (*The Sovereign Stack — Companion Volume: Layer 6: The Semantic Commonwealth*). Layer 6 is the **most visible layer of the Sovereign Stack**—the human-facing interface that residents, visitors, neighbours, and application developers interact with directly, often without needing to know the internal mechanics of the lower cybernetic layers. Accordingly, this companion monograph serves two primary goals:

1. **The Wide-Purpose Semantic & Ontological Surface (`col-commonsd` + `col-gatewayd`):** Layer 6 exposes a self-describing, domain-spanning semantic and ontological surface (`https://w3id.org/collective/v1/`, W3C JSON-LD 1.1, RDF Named Graphs, SHACL shapes, RFC 8785 JCS, W3C `RDFC-1.0` canonicalization, and 64-bit fixed-point QUDT SI units) over the cybernetic reality underneath:
   - Translating raw physical transducer readings (Layer 2), conflict-free thermodynamic and mutual-credit ledgers (Layer 3), deterministic BPMN 2.0 state machines (Layer 4), and zero-knowledge polycentric governance records (Layer 5) upward into human-meaningful concepts (Needs, Recipes, Cultivars, Facility Blueprints, Stewardship Rounds, Rest Shields, Proposals, and Treaties).
   - Enforcing strict bilateral `AF_UNIX` `SOCK_SEQPACKET` adjacency (`SO_PEERCRED`): inward across `/run/collective/ipc/l5_l6.sock` to `col-kmsd` (Layer 5) and outward across `/run/collective/ipc/l6_l7.sock` to `col-adversaryd` (Layer 7, VLAN 40 DMZ) behind the **Mundane Semantic Camouflage Filter**, while blocking direct mutations to Layers 1–4 (`HTTP 405 L4_IS_STRICTLY_READ_ONLY_NO_MUTATIONS`).
2. **Developing an Open-Ended Constellation of Application Lenses:** Layer 6 is **not a monolithic super-app**, nor is it coupled to any spatial simulation engine. Instead, it provides the foundational Developer Contract & Lens SDK for an **open-ended Constellation of Convivial Applications and Screenless Modalities** that act as specialized **Semantic Lenses** ($\mathcal{L}_a : \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{surface}} \to \mathcal{V}_a$) to understand and steward the cybernetic reality underneath:
   - Hearth & Pantry Slates, Agroecology Cultivar Fieldbooks, Shop-Floor BOM E-Ink Terminals, Perinatal & Care Sanctuary Slates, Cargo-Trike Offline Courier Decks, Agora Deliberation Readers, Ricardian Treaty Studios, Paramedic Zero-LLM Pagers, Printable QR Paper Travelers, Helios Verifiable Paper Ballots, Offline Voice Loops, and CLI Scripts.
   - Every Application Lens holds zero authoritative business logic, pairs via VLAN 30 TLS 1.3 `#pair_token` to obtain a least-privilege `did:peer` session scoped to its `AppCapabilityManifestV1`, frames domain subgraphs via JSON-LD Framing and `/ws/v1` topic deltas, validates inputs via declarative SHACL shapes, and commits state strictly through `Draft -> Deterministic Read-Back -> Personal Hardware Sign` (`Web Crypto` / `ATECC608A`) backed by local-first Domain B Automerge CRDT outboxes.
3. **Human Authorship, Variety Attenuation, and Non-Commensurable Care:**
   - *The machine assists, people author, and only people sign.* Every mutation carries explicit `TextProvenance` (`human_authored`, `machine_drafted_human_approved`, `machine_generated`, `system_authenticated`).
   - A single governed node executes $38$ to $2{,}600+$ concurrent intra-node and inter-node workflows across the 25 Sovereign Scenarios. Layer 6 attenuates operational variety ($500:1$ to $3{,}200:1$) via **Spatial Homestead Round Coalescing**, qualitative 1-to-5 somatic feel envelopes, pull-only Commons Pools, and the zero-penalty **Protocol of Refusal**.
   - Domestic care, nourishment ($B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$), childcare, eldercare, mentorship, and emergency triage are never tokenized or auctioned (`domestic_care_token_minting == 0`); zero banned adversarial rhetoric ("Trojan", "leeching", "parasitism") or internal fiat pricing appears in Layer 6.
4. **Canonical Fixed-Point SI Units:** `uJ` ($3.6\,\mathrm{MJ} = 3.6 \times 10^{12}\,\mu\mathrm{J} = 1\,\mathrm{kWh}$), `mW`, `mV`, `mK`, `mks`, `bp` ($10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp} = 100\%$), `mg`, `mL`, `mm`, `mpH`, `ppm`, `cB`, and `ms`.
