\section{Appendix 23: Scenario Psi - Dispute Mediation}

\subsection{The Legacy Paradigm \\\& Its Failures}
Legacy dispute resolution happens on polarized, algorithmic social media or in slow, expensive, centralized courts. Digital platforms optimize for engagement via outrage, utilizing dark patterns that obscure consensus and amplify division. There is no shared, verifiable reality, only competing echo chambers.

\subsection{The Incremental Automation Pathway}
Phase 1: Disputing parties use shared, tamper-proof local ledgers to manually submit evidence and claims.
Phase 2: The system uses ambient interfaces to present a neutral, visually balanced map of the dispute, highlighting areas of agreement and contention without prioritizing inflammatory content.
Phase 3: The Centaur interface actively suggests equitable compromises based on community precedent, generating zero-click consensus proposals for the parties to review.

\subsection{The Human Boundary (Limits of Automation)}
The final resolution of a dispute involving community resources or personal rights cannot be auto-executed by an algorithm. The interface requires all parties to physically convene or provide multi-signature cryptographic confirmation. The system forces a "cool-down" period, introducing intentional friction to prevent hasty, emotionally driven consensus.

\subsection{Interface Mechanics (The "How")}
This relies heavily on Cognitive Load \\\& Algorithmic Triage. The UI acts as a neutral arbiter, filtering out emotive language and presenting facts using conflict-free replicated data types to ensure all parties are looking at the exact same state of the dispute. The interface may use ambient, low-power displays in communal areas to transparently show the status of public disputes.
