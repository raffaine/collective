\chapter{Appendix 23: Psi Mediation}

\section{Legacy}
Conflict resolution systems previously relied on dual-key manual entries that exacerbated tension between parties.

\section{Incremental}
Automated mediation scripts helped, but they couldn't capture the subtle intent of compromise, leading to rejected proposals.

\section{Human Boundaries}
The interface must act as a neutral ground, absorbing emotional input without projecting bias or prematurely locking in decisions.

\section{Interface Mechanics}
Symmetric visibility and provisional staging allow both parties to see how their intent is being interpreted before final commitment.

\section{Semantic NLP Translation}
The local LLM parses the human's messy intent into a strict Layer 5 cryptographic payload. It extracts the core compromises from emotionally charged negotiations and formalizes them into multi-signature cryptographic contracts.
