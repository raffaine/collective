\chapter{Wave A: Sovereign Forge, Multi-Pathway Genesis, and Somatic Homeostasis (\texttt{Zero}, \texttt{Tau}, \texttt{Sigma})}
\label{ch:wave-a}

\epigraph{A living system is organized in such a way that its processes produce the very components which are necessary for the continuation of those processes.}{---\textcite{maturana1980}, \textit{Autopoiesis and Cognition}}

Part~II of this monograph moves from general constitutional theory into the lived metabolism of the \textbf{twenty-five Sovereign Scenarios} (\texttt{03\_SCENARIOS.md}), organized across six horizontal waves (\textbf{Waves~A through~F}). Throughout Part~II, we examine two interlocking realities that define the Version~3.1 architecture:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Constellation Plurality Over One Foundation:} How the wide-purpose semantic surface projects naturally into an open-ended Constellation of Application Lenses and screenless modalities---each governed by a least-privilege \texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1} yet speaking the identical canonical W3C JSON-LD~1.1 and fixed-point SI substrate; and
    \item \textbf{High-Density Multi-Workflow Serenity:} How a single governed settlement sustains dozens to thousands of concurrent intra-node and inter-node Layer~4 workflows while Layer~6 attenuates $89\text{--}92\%$ of autonomic variety so human stewards remain unhurried, sovereign, and free from burnout.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Horizontal Wave~A} establishes the \textbf{autopoietic preconditions} of the Semantic Commonwealth itself across three foundational scenarios: \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Zero}} (\textit{The Sovereign Software, Game \& Digital Commons}), \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Tau}} (\textit{Genesis, Multi-Pathway Onboarding \& Accessible Inclusion}), and \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Sigma}} (\textit{Burnout Prevention, Somatic Homeostasis \& Rotational Care}). Before a community can bake bread, pour bronze, or sign watershed treaties, it must compile and verify its own Constellation applications without external cloud dependency (\texttt{Zero}), welcome every human body and neurotype without coercive gatekeeping (\texttt{Tau}), and protect stewards from martyrdom and exhaustion (\texttt{Sigma}).

\section{Scenario \texttt{Zero} (\texttt{SCN-ZERO-SOFTWARE}): The Sovereign Forge Studio and Capability Nutrition Registry}
\label{sec:wave-a-zero}

\subsection{Constellation Surfaces and Semantic Intents}

In conventional platforms, users are passive tenants of proprietary app stores whose binaries phone home telemetry, embed dark patterns \parencite{gray2018darkpatterns}, and demand biennial hardware upgrades \parencite{kucklich2005}. Scenario~\texttt{Zero} turns software and game creation into a democratic guild craft served by two specialized Constellation applications:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{The Sovereign Forge Studio (\texttt{forge:author}, \texttt{bpmn:review}):} A local-first development and mod-authoring workbench running comfortably within the \textbf{20-Year Salvaged Hardware Mandate} ($\le 256\,\mathrm{MiB}$ base OS RAM, $\le 512\,\mathrm{MiB}$ application arena on salvaged ThinkPads or RISC-V/ARM64 thin clients). Stewards author new Layer~6 Application Lenses, SHACL form bundles, and declarative domain ontologies while inspecting bit-identical multi-architecture Nix/Guix reproducibility status across \texttt{x86\_64}, \texttt{aarch64}, and \texttt{riscv64}.
    \item \textbf{The Capability Nutrition Label Registry (\texttt{crdt:read}, \texttt{gov:ratify}):} Before any Application Lens or community package can be installed onto a node, Layer~6 renders its \texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1} as a plain-language ``Nutrition Label''---displaying exact memory footprint, zero-float WASM verification (\texttt{0} IEEE-754 opcodes in deterministic modules \parencite{haas2017wasm}), requested socket scopes, and \texttt{>= 10,000}-tick deterministic sandbox canary results (\texttt{0x01}).
\end{itemize}

Three canonical JSON-LD intent classes drive Scenario~\texttt{Zero}: \texttt{SoftwareOrGameProductionIntent} (commissioning hermetic builds whose compiler waste heat routes via hydronic heat exchangers into domestic water pre-heating under $\le 380\,\mathrm{cB}$ bedroom acoustic limits), \texttt{CommunityModPublicationIntent} (publishing zero-royalty creative mods and pedagogical sandboxes \parencite{girard2013}), and \texttt{SoftwareAgoraPackageManifest} (registering multi-party threshold-signed release CIDs).

\subsection{Intentional Friction, Detox Quarantine Diode, and Camouflage}

While routine local compilation and automated test runs execute silently in the autonomic plane ($91\%$ suppression), Scenario~\texttt{Zero} enforces strict \texttt{Draft -> Deterministic Read-Back -> Personal Hardware Sign} ceremonies at two constitutional gates:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Creative Studio Veto (\texttt{Protocol of Refusal}):} Any developer or playtester can invoke a zero-penalty architectural veto halting a release candidate if it introduces variable-ratio compulsion loops, public productivity leaderboards, or hidden network sockets.
    \item \textbf{Dual-Guild Threshold Release Co-Sign:} Promoting a new Constellation app into the node's live \texttt{/var/lib/collective/apps/} store requires multi-party hardware signatures from independent maintainers after reading back the exact capability diff.
\end{enumerate}
Upstream open-source tarballs arriving from the public internet enter strictly across \texttt{l6\_l7.sock} through a \textbf{Detox Quarantine Diode} that strips telemetry SDKs, tracking beacons, and proprietary binary blobs prior to offline vendoring. Outwardly across Layer~7, software guilds operate under mundane cooperative open-source studio and Subchapter~T engineering consultancy carapaces.

\section{Scenario \texttt{Tau} (\texttt{SCN-TAU-GENESIS}): Sensory-Calm Genesis Slate and Four-Pathway Inclusion}
\label{sec:wave-a-tau}

\subsection{Constellation Surfaces Across Four Onboarding Pathways}

How does a neighbour actually join The Collective without encountering bureaucratic interrogation, crypto-jargon seed phrases, or sensory overwhelm? Scenario~\texttt{Tau} replaces intimidating account-creation funnels with the \textbf{Sensory-Calm Genesis Pairing Slate}---paired with a \textbf{Zero-Screen Pathway~4 Walk-In Mode} \parencite{costanzachock2020design,w3c2023wcag}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Pathway 1 (Hybrid Suburban/Urban Neighbor):} Keeps their existing apartment and job while pairing a mobile Constellation client via VLAN~30 QR fragment (\texttt{\#pair\_token}) to share hearth soup and tool-library access, shielded from employer or landlord retaliation by \textit{NAACP v.\ Alabama} Data Non-Existence \parencite{naacp1958}.
    \item \textbf{Pathway 2 (Full Residential Co-Steward):} Moves into a Community Land Trust (\texttt{CLT}) suite (Scenario~\texttt{Zeta}) with full physical key and Shamir social-recovery setup.
    \item \textbf{Pathway 3 (Visiting Guild Artisan / Seasonal Scholar):} Pairs federated Ring~$R_2\text{--}R_3$ BBS+ skill credentials (\texttt{KappaEquivalenceClaim}) without re-proving identity.
    \item \textbf{Pathway 4 (Zero-Device / Crisis Sanctuary Guest, \texttt{R0\_GUEST}):} Requires \textbf{zero smartphone, zero DID, and zero literacy test}---issuing an unmonitored physical brass room latch and full unconditional $B_{\mathrm{baseline}} = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ access to meals, warmth, and bathhouse care on the spot.
\end{itemize}

From its very first frame, the Genesis Pairing Slate honors neurodivergent and disability sovereignty (\texttt{AccessibilityAndSensoryManifest}): zero flashing animations, optional \texttt{OpenDyslexic} typography, $\ge 7.0:1$ AAA contrast, refreshable Braille display bindings, sip-and-puff and eye-gaze switch scanning, and offline spoken TTS read-back.

\subsection{Physical NFC Ceremony, Shamir Recovery, and Zero Inherited Hierarchy}

When a newcomer chooses to mint a sovereign identity (\texttt{GenesisOnboardingIntent}), Layer~6 never asks them to write down fragile 24-word mnemonic phrases. Instead, an in-person NFC tap ($\le 40\,\mathrm{mm}$) with two vouching stewards initializes a non-exportable \texttt{ATECC608A} or Web Crypto Ed25519 keypair and distributes a \textbf{3-of-5 Shamir Secret Sharing} social-recovery split \parencite{shamir1979} across trusted peers. Every member starts at exact constitutional parity: $B_{\mathrm{baseline}} = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ unconditional baseline right to exist, \textbf{zero inherited reputational wealth} ($R_{\mathrm{init}} = 0\,\mathrm{bp}$, defeating plutocratic buy-ins), and an explicit covenant that personal $R_0$ assistive devices (wheelchairs, AAC tablets, hearing aids, sensory earmuffs) are permanently non-requisitionable personal property.

\section{Scenario \texttt{Sigma} (\texttt{SCN-SIGMA-BURNOUT}): The \texttt{RestShield} Homeostat and Sanctuary Latch}
\label{sec:wave-a-sigma}

\subsection{Somatic Privacy and Zero-Click Friction to Rest}

Even the noblest cooperative collapses if conscientious members quietly burn themselves out carrying invisible sanitation, maintenance, and emotional labor \parencite{freeman1972,han2015}. Scenario~\texttt{Sigma} pairs a minimal digital Constellation widget---the \textbf{\texttt{RestShield} Somatic Homeostat}---with a physical, screenless \textbf{Wooden \& Brass Sanctuary Door Latch}.

Crucially, Scenario~\texttt{Sigma} inverts the friction gradient of Layer~6:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Zero-Friction Entry Into Rest:} Sliding the physical brass sanctuary latch on one's bedroom door or tapping \texttt{Rest Now} emits a blinded 1-bit \texttt{RestIntent} across \texttt{l5\_l6.sock} with \textbf{zero justification prompt, zero supervisor approval, and zero guilt modal}. Local \texttt{BiologicalFeelTiers} never leave $R_0$ device memory; when cumulative exertion crosses the Schmitt-trigger upper threshold ($\ge 8{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$), the \texttt{ChillLoop} homeostat engages \texttt{RestShield} automatically, dropping $100\%$ of Class~2--4 Layer~6 notifications until recovery falls below $\le 4{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ and enforcing bedroom sanctuary quiet ($\le 300\,\mathrm{cB}$).
    \item \textbf{Dual-Freeze Care Leave \& Anti-Martyrdom Beacons:} Declaring \texttt{CareLeaveDeclarationIntent} (for illness, grief, caregiving, or postpartum recovery) freezes any time-decaying guild obligations while preserving $100\%$ of $B_{\mathrm{baseline}}$. Conversely, if an essential node function (such as greywater filter backwashing) repeatedly triggers exhaustion across multiple stewards, \texttt{col-commonsd} compiles a \texttt{DrudgeryAutomationQuestIntent} or \texttt{AntiMartyrdomReliefBeacon}---applying deliberate \texttt{Draft -> Read-Back -> Sign} review to summon Ring~$R_1\text{--}R_2$ relief pods or commission a Scenario~\texttt{Alpha} mechanical automation jig.
    \item \textbf{Rotational Work-Bee Sanitation (\texttt{0 uJ} Minted):} Necessary unglamorous chores are shared via randomized, capability-fair rotational Work-Bee circles (\texttt{domestic\_care\_token\_minting == 0}), outlawing Dutch auctions that would push unpleasant work onto newcomers.
\end{itemize}

\begin{invariant}[Asymmetric Rest-vs.-Commitment Friction Invariant]
\label{inv:wave-a-asymmetric-friction}
Across all Constellation surfaces in Wave~A, stepping back into rest (\texttt{RestIntent}, \texttt{UNCLAIM\_GUILT\_FREE}, or Sanctuary Latch closure) MUST complete in a single local gesture ($\le 1$ action, $0$ explanation text fields, $\Delta R = 0\,\mathrm{bp}$), whereas publishing executable code (\texttt{Zero}), binding social-recovery guardians (\texttt{Tau}), or broadcasting inter-node relief beacons (\texttt{Sigma}) MUST traverse full \texttt{Draft -> Deterministic Read-Back -> Personal Hardware Sign}.
\end{invariant}
