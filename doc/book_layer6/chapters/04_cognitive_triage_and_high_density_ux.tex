\chapter{Variety Attenuation, Spatial Round Coalescing, and Calm UX Across Hundreds of Workflows}
\label{ch:cognitive-triage}

\epigraph{The most profound technologies are those that disappear. They weave themselves into the fabric of everyday life until they are indistinguishable from it\ldots{} Calm technology engages both the center and the periphery of our attention, and in fact moves back and forth between the two.}{---\textcite{weiser1996calm}, ``Designing Calm Technology''}

In Chapter~\ref{ch:authorship-crdt}, we established how a single human commitment traverses the six-stage pipeline from expression through deterministic read-back to hardware signature and local-first CRDT synchronization. Yet securing the cryptographic integrity of an individual click solves only half of Layer~6's human-factors challenge. A far subtler pathology threatens cooperative self-governance as soon as a settlement moves past its opening week: \textbf{operational variety overload} \parencite{ashby1956,beer1979}.

Early prototypes of civic software almost always assume a toy world in which a community runs three or four linear to-do lists at a time. The twenty-five Sovereign Scenarios codified in \texttt{03\_SCENARIOS.md} dispel that illusion permanently. Even a single governed homestead node (\textbf{Stage~1A}) orchestrates $38$ to $68$ concurrent workflows across its room-scoped single-board computers (\texttt{office-coordinator}, \texttt{garage-fab}, \texttt{garden-agro}, and \texttt{hearth-galley}); by the time a neighborhood cooperative (\textbf{Stage~2}) or bioregional campus (\textbf{Stage~3}) reaches metabolic maturity, $850$ to $2{,}600+$ intra-node and inter-node workflows execute simultaneously. If Layer~6 projected those workflows onto human screens as an unfiltered enterprise dashboard, notification stream, or gamified ``quest log,'' residents would drown in alarm fatigue and cognitive exhaustion \parencite{miller1956magical,sweller1988cognitive,han2015}.

This chapter formalizes the cybernetic architecture by which Layer~6 absorbs thousands of concurrent workflow transitions while preserving unhurried human serenity: three-stage \textbf{Variety Attenuation}, \textbf{Spatial Homestead Round Coalescing} constrained by physical hauling phases, epistemic 1-to-5 \textbf{Biological Feel Strips}, pull-only \textbf{Blinded Claim Handles}, and the unconditional, zero-penalty \textbf{Protocol of Refusal}.

\section{The High-Density Node Problem: Information-Theoretic Bounds on Human Serenity}
\label{sec:high-density-problem}

\subsection{Quantifying Concurrent Workflow Density Across Stages 1A--3}

Why does a single governed node run hundreds of concurrent workflows? Because physical metabolism does not pause while humans sleep or eat. On a lived parcel, soil moisture sensors sample root zones across staggered \textit{Fabaceae} and mirepoix guilds; lacto-fermentation crocks evolve $\mathrm{CO}_2$ toward the $4{,}600\,\mathrm{mpH}$ safety threshold; thermophilic compost drums hold $\ge 328{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ across $72$-hour pathogen-kill windows; solar MPPT charge controllers arbitrate battery State-of-Charge (\texttt{SoC\_bp}) admission tiers for workshop CNC spindles and homelab compilation jobs; hydronic loops recover server heat into domestic hot water tanks; bilateral clearing channels (\texttt{BCC}) reconcile cargo-trike milk-runs; and asynchronous sortition panels deliberate watershed easements across multi-week windows.

Let $\mathcal{W}(t) = \{w_1, w_2, \dots, w_N\}$ denote the active Layer~4 BPMN workflow instances on a node or settlement cluster at epoch $t$, emitting aggregate state-transition events at arrival rate $\Lambda_{\mathrm{raw}}$ (measured in transitions per day). By W.\ Ross Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety \parencite{ashby1956}, a viable controller must match the variety of disturbances in the regulated system, yet human conscious working memory is physiologically bounded to $4 \pm 1$ to $7 \pm 2$ concurrent chunks \parencite{miller1956magical,sweller1988cognitive}, and human deliberative decision throughput $C_{\mathrm{human}}$ rarely exceeds $12\text{--}20$ non-trivial physical switches per day without inducing autonomic stress and task-switching fatigue \parencite{little1961proof,han2015}.

Whenever an interface forwards raw operational transitions $\Lambda_{\mathrm{raw}}$ directly to human attention without an intervening cybernetic attenuator $\mathcal{A}$ such that
\begin{equation}
    \Lambda_{\mathrm{somatic}} = \mathcal{A}(\Lambda_{\mathrm{raw}}) \le C_{\mathrm{human}},
    \label{eq:ashby-attenuation-bound}
\end{equation}
queue length $L = \Lambda_{\mathrm{somatic}} W$ explodes by Little's Law \parencite{little1961proof}, degrading civic stewardship into compulsive screen checking.

\section{Three-Stage Variety Attenuation and Spatial Homestead Round Coalescing}
\label{sec:spatial-round-coalescing}

Layer~6 achieves the $500:1$ to $3{,}200:1$ attenuation ratios of Table~\ref{tab:ch4-variety-attenuation} through three compositional filters executed inside \texttt{col-commonsd} and \texttt{col-gatewayd} before a single pixel updates on any Constellation surface.

\subsection{Filter 1: Autonomic Suppression and Topic-Scoped Delta Streams}

First, $89\text{--}92\%$ of all Layer~4 workflow transitions occur purely within the \textbf{Autonomic Plane}---automated drip-valve pulses (\texttt{wf.garden.irrigate\_l2\_automated}), MPPT load-tier adjustments, background Nix store builds, and multi-day \texttt{DORMANT} phenological timers (such as coppice stool rotations or $72$-hour compost holds). Unless an autonomic loop breaches a physical safety boundary or encounters a cache miss requiring an upward Intent U-Turn, \texttt{col-commonsd} suppresses it completely from somatic task feeds, retaining it solely in passive diagnostic inspect views.

Second, rather than polling an entire node snapshot every $1{,}500\,\mathrm{ms}$ across a monolithic four-tab DOM (a prototype ceiling diagnosed in Chapter~\ref{ch:oasis-council-l6}), \texttt{col-gatewayd} multiplexes \textbf{topic-scoped CBOR/JSON-Patch delta streams} over \texttt{/ws/v1/subscribe}. Each Constellation application subscribes solely to the semantic namespaces permitted by its \texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1}: the Hearth E-Ink Slate receives deltas matching \texttt{topic:hearth.*} and \texttt{need:pea\_soup} timers, never waking its electrophoretic controller when a woodworking router completes a jig in the garage.

\subsection{Filter 2: Spatial Homestead Round Coalescing with \texttt{HaulingPhysics} Constraints}

Even after filtering out silent autonomic transitions, a homestead or neighborhood campus still accumulates $14$ to $85$ human-actionable tokens (\texttt{WAITING\_HUMAN}) per day---checking soil moisture on Bed~B, collecting four pasture eggs from the coop, skimming bone broth in the galley, turning the compost drum, and carrying kitchen peelings to the Black Soldier Fly larvae bin. Presented as $18$ separate timestamped pings throughout the day, these tasks fragment attention into relentless micro-interruptions.

In lived agrarian and artisanal practice, however, stewards do not sprint back and forth across a lot eighteen times; they walk an unhurried \textbf{Morning Homestead Round} and \textbf{Evening Hearth Round} \parencite{hutchins1995cognition,suchman1987plans}. \texttt{col-commonsd} formalizes this situated rhythm via \textbf{Spatial Homestead Round Coalescing}.

Let $G_{\mathrm{space}} = (Z, E_Z)$ be the directed walking-path graph of spatial zones on a parcel (for example, $z_{\mathrm{hearth}} \leftrightarrow z_{\mathrm{patio}} \leftrightarrow z_{\mathrm{garden}} \leftrightarrow z_{\mathrm{coop}} \leftrightarrow z_{\mathrm{compost}} \leftrightarrow z_{\mathrm{garage}}$), with walking-time weights $d(z_u, z_v)$ in milliseconds. Each pending human-actionable workflow token $\tau_i$ carries:
\begin{itemize}
    \item an origin zone $z_{\mathrm{orig}}(\tau_i) \in Z$ and optional destination zone $z_{\mathrm{dest}}(\tau_i) \in Z$;
    \item an earliest-valid window $[e_i, l_i]$ derived from its BPMN boundary timer slack $\sigma(\tau_i) = l_i - t_{\mathrm{now}}$;
    \item a physical mass $m_i$ (in milligrams \texttt{mg}) and volume $v_i$ (in milliliters \texttt{mL}); and
    \item a thermodynamic matter phase $\phi_i \in \{\texttt{NONE}, \texttt{LIQUID}, \texttt{GRANULAR\_PARTICULATE}, \texttt{DISCRETE\_SOLID}\}$ paired with sanitation class $\chi_i \in \{\texttt{POTABLE\_CULINARY}, \texttt{CLEAN\_DRY}, \texttt{ORGANIC\_RESIDUE}\}$.
\end{itemize}

Crucially, Layer~6 never proposes physically absurd or unsanitary combinations---such as carrying unsealed compost residue in the same vessel as potable tiffin soup, or exceeding comfortable human single-trip ergonomics ($M_{\max} = 12{,}000{,}000\,\mathrm{mg} = 12\,\mathrm{kg}$ handheld tote, or $35{,}000{,}000\,\mathrm{mg}$ with a garden barrow). Two hauling tasks $\tau_i, \tau_j$ are \textbf{carrier-compatible} ($\mathrm{Compat}(\tau_i, \tau_j) = 1$) along a subpath if and only if their time windows overlap ($[e_i, l_i] \cap [e_j, l_j] \neq \emptyset$), their sanitation classes never mix \texttt{ORGANIC\_RESIDUE} with \texttt{POTABLE\_CULINARY} in an unpartitioned carrier, and cumulative mass along every edge satisfies $\sum m_k \le M_{\mathrm{carrier}}$.

\begin{lstlisting}[language=C++,caption={Deterministic Spatial Homestead Round Coalescer respecting \texttt{HaulingPhysics} matter phases and sanitation boundaries (C++20).}]
#include <cstdint>
#include <span>
#include <vector>

namespace col::commons::triage {

enum class MatterPhase : std::uint8_t {
    NONE                 = 0,
    LIQUID               = 1,
    GRANULAR_PARTICULATE = 2,
    DISCRETE_SOLID       = 3
};

enum class SanitationClass : std::uint8_t {
    CLEAN_DRY        = 0,
    POTABLE_CULINARY = 1,
    ORGANIC_RESIDUE  = 2
};

struct ActionableToken {
    std::uint64_t   blinded_claim_id;
    std::uint16_t   origin_zone_id;
    std::uint16_t   dest_zone_id;
    std::int64_t    window_earliest_ms;
    std::int64_t    window_latest_ms;
    std::int64_t    haul_mass_mg;
    MatterPhase     phase;
    SanitationClass sanitation;
};

struct CoalescedHomesteadRound {
    std::uint16_t              anchor_zone_id{};
    std::int64_t               latest_deadline_ms{};
    std::int64_t               peak_carried_mass_mg{};
    SanitationClass            dominant_sanitation{SanitationClass::CLEAN_DRY};
    std::vector<std::uint64_t> ordered_claim_ids{};
};

[[nodiscard]] constexpr bool AreSanitationClassesCompatible(
    SanitationClass a, SanitationClass b) noexcept {
    if ((a == SanitationClass::POTABLE_CULINARY && b == SanitationClass::ORGANIC_RESIDUE) ||
        (a == SanitationClass::ORGANIC_RESIDUE && b == SanitationClass::POTABLE_CULINARY)) {
        return false; // Never co-mingle culinary vessels and raw compost residue in one carrier.
    }
    return true;
}

[[nodiscard]] inline bool TryAppendToRound(
    CoalescedHomesteadRound& round,
    const ActionableToken& candidate,
    std::int64_t max_ergonomic_mass_mg,
    std::int64_t coalescing_horizon_ms) {
    if (candidate.window_earliest_ms > round.latest_deadline_ms + coalescing_horizon_ms) {
        return false;
    }
    if (!AreSanitationClassesCompatible(round.dominant_sanitation, candidate.sanitation)) {
        return false;
    }
    if (round.peak_carried_mass_mg + candidate.haul_mass_mg > max_ergonomic_mass_mg) {
        return false;
    }
    round.ordered_claim_ids.push_back(candidate.blinded_claim_id);
    round.peak_carried_mass_mg += candidate.haul_mass_mg;
    if (candidate.window_latest_ms < round.latest_deadline_ms) {
        round.latest_deadline_ms = candidate.window_latest_ms;
    }
    if (candidate.sanitation != SanitationClass::CLEAN_DRY) {
        round.dominant_sanitation = candidate.sanitation;
    }
    return true;
}

} // namespace col::commons::triage
\end{lstlisting}

Instead of six nagging alerts, the Hearth or Garden Slate displays one calm, human-legible card: \textit{``Morning Garden \& Coop Walk ($\approx 14\,\mathrm{min}$, $3.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ tote): carry mirepoix peelings from Hearth to Compost Bay, check Alderman Pea moisture in Bed~2, and gather 4 pasture eggs on return.''}

\section{Epistemic Somatic Feel, Voluntary Pull, and the Protocol of Refusal}
\label{sec:feel-pull-refusal}

\subsection{Qualitative 1-to-5 \texttt{BiologicalFeelTiers} Without Fake Biometric Precision}

Industrial wellness apps alienate people from their own bodies by fabricating pseudo-precise ``readiness scores'' (``Recovery: $73.4\%$'') or continuously streaming intimate heart-rate telemetry to cloud servers \parencite{zuboff2019}. Layer~6 enforces strict \textbf{epistemic somatic honesty}, already grounded in \texttt{core/1\_simulation/src/core/chill\_loop.hpp} and \texttt{hud\_feel.js}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Five qualitative ordinal axes---Energy, Hydration, Nourishment, Thermal Comfort, and Autonomic Calm---are rendered exclusively on a human-legible 1-to-5 word scale (for example, \textit{Exhausted / Tired / Steady / Rested / Energized}, or \textit{Strained / Tense / Grounded / Calm / Serene}).
    \item Raw quantitative physiological figures (\texttt{bpm}, \texttt{kcal}, core skin millikelvins) are \textbf{suppressed by default} unless local hardware-attested biometric sensors are explicitly installed and paired by the user (\texttt{l2\_biometric\_installed == true}), and even then remain strictly inside $R_0$ edge memory---never gossiped across \texttt{l5\_l6.sock} or logged to Layer~3.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Two-Stage Voluntary Pull via \texttt{BlindedClaimHandle} and Zero Leaderboards}

Layer~4 holds zero DIDs, zero Web-of-Trust graphs, and zero personal push queues. When a workflow step enters \texttt{WAITING\_HUMAN}, Layer~4 emits an anonymous capability requirement upward across \texttt{l4\_l5.sock} into Layer~5, which filters visibility to stewards holding the matching zero-knowledge BBS+ skill credential (for example, \texttt{Food\_Safety\_Steward\_L1} or \texttt{Woodshop\_Router\_Safe}) \parencite{camenisch2004signature}. Layer~6 then presents a strictly two-stage voluntary surface:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Commons Pool (\texttt{Available Tasks / Coalesced Rounds}):} Paginated, pull-only cards showing physical scope, time window slack, and required safety gear---with \textbf{zero gamified streaks, zero public completion counters, and zero peer-pressure leaderboards}.
    \item \textbf{Personal Stewardship (\texttt{My Committed Rounds}):} Claiming an offer binds a random 24-byte \texttt{BlindedClaimHandle} via \texttt{l5\_l6.sock}, transitioning the token from \texttt{OPEN\_POOL} to \texttt{CLAIMED\_BLINDED} without revealing the volunteer's identity to the Layer~4 execution engine.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The Unconditional Protocol of Refusal, \texttt{RestShield}, and Four Notification Tiers}

As Albert O.\ Hirschman observed in \textit{Exit, Voice, and Loyalty} \parencite{hirschman1970} and Byung-Chul Han diagnosed in \textit{The Burnout Society} \parencite{han2015}, voluntary cooperation degenerates into self-exploitation whenever stepping back carries subtle algorithmic shame or irreversible lock-in. In the early bootstrap task widget (\texttt{hud\_feel.js}, lines~771--809), once a steward clicked \texttt{Commit}, no \texttt{Step Back / Unclaim} button yet existed---a gap corrected constitutionally in v3.1.

\begin{invariant}[Unconditional Protocol of Refusal and Zero-Penalty Release]
\label{inv:protocol-of-refusal}
Every Constellation surface rendering a \texttt{CLAIMED\_BLINDED} task or governance request MUST expose an immediate, single-gesture \textbf{Step Back / Release to Pool} (\texttt{UNCLAIM\_GUILT\_FREE}) control alongside a hardware or software \textbf{RestShield} toggle. Invoking \texttt{UNCLAIM\_GUILT\_FREE} or entering \texttt{RestShield} (automatically suggested via Schmitt-trigger hysteresis when local somatic feel reaches Tier~1 \textit{Exhausted} or \textit{Strained}, $\ge 8{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ cumulative load, clearing only below $\le 4{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item cleanly releases the \texttt{BlindedClaimHandle} back to the Layer~5 Commons Pool (or triggers a safe L2 hold if an active machine tool requires LOTO park) with \textbf{zero reputational penalty} ($\Delta R = 0\,\mathrm{bp}$), \textbf{zero baseline nourishment reduction} ($B_{\mathrm{baseline}} = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ invariant), and \textbf{zero public refusal log}; and
    \item mutes $100\%$ of Class~2, Class~3, and Class~4 notifications until the steward voluntarily exits sanctuary.
\end{enumerate}
\end{invariant}

To enforce Invariant~\ref{inv:protocol-of-refusal} across every audio, visual, and tactile peripheral, \texttt{col-commonsd} classifies all outbound human communications into exactly four hardware-enforced attention classes:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Class 1 --- Life \& Infrastructure Emergency (Immediate Breakthrough):} Clinical paramedic flares (Scenario~\texttt{Chi}), anaphylactic allergen interlocks, fire/flood/thermal runaways. Bypasses \texttt{RestShield} using zero-LLM fixed deterministic templates ($\le 500\,\mathrm{ms}$ latency).
    \item \textbf{Class 2 --- Time-Critical Committed Steward Prompt (Gentle Local Cue):} Boundary-timer warnings on a task \textit{already voluntarily claimed} by the active steward (for example, stockpot approaching $120\,\mathrm{s}$ pasteurization completion). Suppressed immediately if released back to the Commons Pool.
    \item \textbf{Class 3 --- Batched Deliberation Digest (Scheduled Calm Pull, 1--2$\times$/day):} Agora proposals, Ricardian treaty read-backs, Twin Fracture reconciliation cards, and non-urgent maintenance reviews, delivered strictly at user-chosen digest hours (or passive E-Ink wall refresh).
    \item \textbf{Class 4 --- Ambient Commons Pool Offers (Zero-Interrupt Pull Only):} Coalesced Homestead Rounds and unmapped need candidates. Class~4 items \textbf{never} ring a bell, vibrate a pocket device, or badge an icon; they wait silently until a steward opens a Constellation surface looking to help.
\end{enumerate}
