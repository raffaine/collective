\chapter{Appendix 24: Omega Decoupling}

\section{Legacy}
System decoupling was a catastrophic event requiring hard reboots and manual severing of network ties.

\section{Incremental}
Graceful degradation protocols were introduced, but they still required precise, pre-defined commands to execute correctly.

\section{Human Boundaries}
The operator must feel in control of the decoupling process, receiving clear feedback that the separation is safe and complete.

\section{Interface Mechanics}
Progressive isolation layers and clear visual indicators guide the operator through the decoupling phases without sudden state changes.

\section{Semantic NLP Translation}
The local LLM parses the human's messy intent into a strict Layer 5 cryptographic payload. Even when the operator's commands are disorganized due to system failure, it structures the final decoupling sequence into a secure, verifiable cryptographic state.
