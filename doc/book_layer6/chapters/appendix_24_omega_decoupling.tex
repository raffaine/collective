\section{Appendix 24: Scenario Omega - Complete Decoupling}

\subsection{The Legacy Paradigm \\\& Its Failures}
Complete decoupling (the severing of a community from the global internet and power grid) breaks all legacy Web2 software. Without DNS, centralized auth, or cloud databases, modern devices become expensive paperweights. The UX assumes a persistent, high-bandwidth connection, and fails catastrophically when deprived of it.

\subsection{The Incremental Automation Pathway}
Phase 1: Manual transition to local mesh networks, updating node directories via sneakernet.
Phase 2: Devices automatically discover and peer with nearby nodes, seamlessly shifting to local-first operation and using AR to visualize the bounds of the local mesh.
Phase 3: Autonomous shifting of the entire community's digital infrastructure to a resilient, asynchronous mode, balancing microgrid power and routing data via opportunistic physical encounters.

\subsection{The Human Boundary (Limits of Automation)}
The decision to permanently sever or reconnect the community to the wider global network is a profound political act. The interface will not automate this bridge. Re-establishing the global link requires explicit, democratic human consensus, facilitated by the UI but fundamentally executed by human operators authorizing the connection.

\subsection{Interface Mechanics (The "How")}
Omega Decoupling pushes thermodynamic rendering and CRDTs to their absolute limits. Devices aggressively down-clock their displays to E-ink modes to survive on ambient energy harvesting.
