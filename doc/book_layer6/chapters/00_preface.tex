\epigraph{People need not only to obtain things, they need above all the freedom to make things among which they can live, to give shape to them according to their own tastes, and to put them to use in caring for and about others.}{---\textcite{illich1973}, \textit{Tools for Conviviality}}

To the vast majority of human beings who live, cook, garden, craft, raise children, deliberate,
or visit within a governed settlement, \textbf{Layer~6 is the Sovereign Stack}. Almost no one
stirring a stockpot in a communal hearth, checking soil moisture along a contour swale, milling
a timber mortise in a neighborhood workshop, or welcoming an elder into a health sanctuary will
ever inspect a Layer~2 microcontroller interrupt table, a Layer~3 Merkle-CRDT causal graph, a
Layer~4 Wasmtime BPMN~2.0 bytecode instruction, or a Layer~5 zero-knowledge polynomial
commitment. Nor should they have to. If a self-governing community required every resident to
think like a systems programmer in order to request soup ingredients, borrow a router jig, step
back for a day of rest, or vote on a watershed covenant, the architecture would fail the first
test of conviviality \parencite{illich1973,weiser1996calm}.

This companion volume is therefore both the **constitutional specification** and the **practical
developer manual** for **Layer~6: The Semantic Commonwealth**---realized at the system level by
the Semantic Commons Daemon (\texttt{col-commonsd}) and the local-network capability mediator
(\texttt{col-gatewayd}). It answers two foundational questions for architects, application
builders, and domain specialists:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item How do we construct a **wide-purpose semantic and ontological surface** that translates
          the intricate cybernetic reality underneath (Layers~1 through~5) and the legal/fiscal
          carapace outward (Layer~7) into self-describing, human-meaningful concepts without
          losing mathematical determinism or physical safety?
    \item How do we support an **open-ended constellation of applications** that serve as
          purpose-built **semantic lenses** onto that underlying reality---allowing any guild,
          accessibility collective, or developer to craft the right tool for a specific physical
          setting without ever creating a monolithic ``super-app'' or embedding private business
          logic inside user interfaces?
\end{enumerate}

\section*{The Wide-Purpose Semantic Surface Over Cybernetic Reality}

Beneath Layer~6 operates a continuous, high-density cybernetic substrate. Hardware transducers
in Layer~2 sample root-zone volumetric water content in basis points, stockpot core temperatures
in millikelvins, battery shunt power in milliwatts, and load-cell masses in milligrams. Layer~3
conserves these physical flows inside an append-only, conflict-free thermodynamic ledger and
pairwise zero-sum mutual-credit graph. Layer~4 advances hundreds to thousands of deterministic
BPMN~2.0 workflow instances across room-scoped and neighborhood-scoped nodes, while Layer~5
enforces polycentric governance quorums, ecological floor invariants, and zero-knowledge
Web-of-Trust credentials.

Left in its raw binary form, this underlying cybernetic reality is an intimidating stream of
64-bit fixed-point registers, socket opcodes, and state-machine tokens. Layer~6 transforms that
substrate into a **Unified Semantic and Ontological Surface** anchored in W3C Linked Data
standards \parencite{bernerslee2001semantic,gruber1993ontology,w3c2020jsonld}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Upward Ontological Lifting ($\Pi_{\mathrm{up}}$):} \texttt{col-commonsd} lifts
          raw lower-layer states into rich, self-describing W3C JSON-LD~1.1 graphs governed by
          the offline content-addressed namespace \texttt{https://w3id.org/collective/v1/}. A
          cluster of thermocouple readings, timer ticks, and larder balances becomes a human-legible
          \texttt{SovereignNeed} (\texttt{need:pea\_soup}), a declarative botanical phenotype
          (\texttt{LegumeCultivarSpec}), a multi-stage \texttt{NodeFacilityBlueprint}, or a
          spatially coalesced \texttt{HomesteadRound}.
    \item \textbf{Downward Intentional Compilation ($\Pi_{\mathrm{down}}$):} Conversely, when a
          person expresses a need, adjusts a cultivar parameter, claims a stewardship task, or
          co-signs a treaty, Layer~6 validates the graph against declarative Shapes Constraint
          Language (\texttt{SHACL}) bounds \parencite{w3c2017shacl}, canonicalizes it via
          RFC~8785 (\texttt{JCS}) and W3C \texttt{RDFC-1.0} \parencite{rfc8785,w3c2024rdfc} in
          pure 64-bit fixed-point QUDT SI units \parencite{qudt2024}, presents a mandatory
          **Deterministic Read-Back**, and forwards the hardware-signed commitment across
          \texttt{l5\_l6.sock} or \texttt{l6\_l7.sock}---governed everywhere by the constitutional
          maxim: \textit{the machine assists, people author, and only people sign}
          \parencite{winograd1986understanding}.
\end{itemize}

\section*{An Open-Ended Constellation of Applications as Semantic Lenses}

Because Layer~6 is the most visible layer of the stack, it faces a classic design temptation:
funneling every community interaction into one all-encompassing dashboard. During early node
bring-up, a single unified starter client (four tabs covering *Needs*, *Decisions*, *Workflows*,
and *Cluster/Ledger*) is a helpful pedagogical scaffold for a solo steward or small developer
team inspecting the full vertical arc of a node.

Yet in lived community practice, **no single application can properly serve every craft,
environment, and human body**. A cook skimming a boiling stockpot with wet hands needs a
wipeable counter E-Ink slate showing three bold pasteurization figures and a tactile timer---not
a woodshop CNC tolerance table or a bioregional treaty diff. An arborist walking an off-grid
chestnut coppice needs a sunlight-readable offline fieldbook synced via local-first Automerge
CRDTs \parencite{kleppmann2017automerge,kleppmann2019localfirst}. A parent checking a toddler
into a sanctuary pod needs an ultra-calm, zero-token check-in slate. An elder with low vision or
a neighbour at an in-person town hall may prefer spoken offline voice confirmation, a passive
NFC tool tag, or a hand-marked verifiable paper ballot with zero digital screens present
\parencite{w3c2023wcag,costanzachock2020design}.

Layer~6 solves this by separating the **Wide-Purpose Semantic Surface** from the **Open-Ended
Constellation of Application Lenses**:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Applications as Pure Semantic Lenses ($\mathcal{L}_a : \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{surface}} \to \mathcal{V}_a$):}
          Every application in the Constellation is a **lens** that focuses, filters, and shapes
          a specific slice of the underlying ontological graph $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{surface}}$
          using W3C JSON-LD Framing (\texttt{@frame}) and topic-scoped delta streams
          (\texttt{/ws/v1/subscribe}), while introspecting declarative \texttt{sh:NodeShape}
          schemas to render input forms. No lens holds private business logic or direct write
          paths to Layers~1--4 (\texttt{HTTP 405}).
    \item \textbf{Open-Ended Developer Extensibility via \texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1}:}
          Developing a new Layer~6 application lens requires zero modifications to
          \texttt{col-commonsd} or lower-layer C++ daemons. Any developer or guild ships a
          declarative \texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1} requesting only the least-privilege RBAC
          scopes its lens requires (for example, \texttt{telemetry:read} and
          \texttt{bpmn:review} for a shop-floor BOM display), pairs over VLAN~30 TLS~1.3 via an
          out-of-band \texttt{\#pair\_token} QR handshake to bind a pairwise \texttt{did:peer}
          key \parencite{miller2006robust,w3c2022did}, and immediately interoperates with every
          other lens in the settlement.
    \item \textbf{Cybernetic Variety Attenuation Across Hundreds of Workflows:}
          As shown in Table~\ref{tab:preface-l6-density}, a single governed node or settlement
          cluster runs $38$ to $2{,}600+$ concurrent intra-node and inter-node workflows across
          the 25 Sovereign Scenarios (\texttt{03\_SCENARIOS.md}). Layer~6's ontological surface
          absorbs $89\text{--}92\%$ of silent autonomic homeostasis, partitions domain
          projections across specialized lenses, and coalesces co-located human checks into
          $2\text{--}4$ unhurried **Spatial Homestead Rounds** per day \parencite{ashby1956,beer1979},
          protected at every step by the unconditional, zero-penalty **Protocol of Refusal**.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Structure of This Companion Monograph and Specialist Council Review}

Part~I (Chapters~1--8) develops the complete engineering specification for building Layer~6 and
its Application Lenses:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Chapter~1} establishes the bilateral socket membrane (\texttt{l5\_l6.sock} and
          \texttt{l6\_l7.sock}), the read-only lower-layer guard (\texttt{HTTP 405}), the
          mathematical projection between cybernetic reality and the semantic surface, and the
          five-part **Application Lens Developer Contract** (\texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1},
          VLAN~30 \texttt{\#pair\_token} pairing, and lock-free SPSC telemetry rings).
    \item \textbf{Chapter~2} specifies the **Ontological Surface** itself: unifying the
          \texttt{https://w3id.org/collective/v1/} namespace, pluralistic overlapping Named
          Graphs, length-prefixed W3C \texttt{RDFC-1.0} and \texttt{RFC 8785 JCS} hashing,
          64-bit fixed-point QUDT SI units, **JSON-LD Framing (\texttt{@frame})**, declarative
          **SHACL form generation**, and multi-layer need/cultivar/facility blueprints.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~3} formalizes the six-stage human authorship pipeline
          (\texttt{Express -> Draft -> Ambiguity Halt -> Deterministic Read-Back -> Personal
          Hardware Sign -> Dispatch}), non-exportable Web Crypto and \texttt{ATECC608A} keys,
          and local-first Domain~A/Domain~B Automerge CRDT synchronization with Twin Fracture
          reconciliation.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~4} solves high-density cognitive ergonomics across hundreds of
          concurrent workflows via three-stage variety attenuation, **Spatial Homestead Round
          Coalescing**, 1-to-5 somatic feel envelopes, blinded voluntary task pull, and the
          unconditional **Protocol of Refusal**.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~5} bounds optional local edge language models within a dual-sandbox
          architecture (grammar-constrained Intake slot extraction vs.\ air-gapped Drafting
          assistance), SHA-256 weight attestation, microjoule (\texttt{uJ}) load shedding, and
          $100\%$ LLM-free graceful degradation.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~6} details thermodynamic display physics (0-mW bistable E-Ink, passive
          NFC, 72-hour black-start shedding), screenless modalities (QR paper travelers, Helios
          paper ballots, offline voice, Red Brass manual overrides, hardware-severed Unmediated
          Spaces), and WCAG~2.2 Level~AAA universal accessibility.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~7} scales the semantic surface to bioregional federation: Agora
          deliberation across Trust Rings $R_0 \dots R_6$, pure-integer exergy clearing with
          \texttt{domestic\_care\_token\_minting == 0}, four-stage Ricardian \texttt{Treaty\_CID}
          compilation, and the Mundane Semantic Camouflage Filter.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~8} grounds these principles in the repository's live C++20 and
          JavaScript reference implementation (\texttt{core/6\_intent/}, \texttt{col-commonsd},
          \texttt{col-gatewayd}, and the starter web companion lens), presenting a line-by-line
          audit by a **Six-Specialist Layer~6 Architectural Council** (Semantic Web Architect,
          Local-First Protocol Engineer, Cryptographic Authorship Specialist, Adversarial ML
          Auditor, Calm HCI \& Accessibility Specialist, and Legal Carapace Counsel) alongside a
          practical Developer Checklist and four-milestone roadmap.
\end{itemize}

Part~II (Chapters~9--14) then traverses Horizontal Waves~A through~F across all twenty-five
Sovereign Scenarios (\texttt{Scenario Zero} through \texttt{Scenario Omega}), demonstrating how
purpose-built Application Lenses allow ordinary people to steward complex, interwoven cybernetic
workflows with clarity, dignity, and calm.
