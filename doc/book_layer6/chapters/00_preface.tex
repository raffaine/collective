\epigraph{People need not only to obtain things, they need above all the freedom to make things among which they can live, to give shape to them according to their own tastes, and to put them to use in caring for and about others.}{---\textcite{illich1973}, \textit{Tools for Conviviality}}

To the vast majority of human beings who live, cook, garden, craft, raise children, deliberate,
or visit within a governed settlement, \textbf{Layer~6 is the Sovereign Stack}. Almost no one
stirring a stockpot in a communal hearth, checking soil moisture along a contour swale, milling
a timber mortise in a neighborhood workshop, or welcoming an elder into a health sanctuary will
ever inspect a Layer~2 microcontroller interrupt table, a Layer~3 Merkle-CRDT causal graph, a
Layer~4 Wasmtime BPMN~2.0 bytecode instruction, or a Layer~5 zero-knowledge polynomial
commitment. Nor should they have to. If a self-governing community required every resident to
think like a systems programmer in order to request soup ingredients, borrow a router jig, step
back for a day of rest, or vote on a watershed covenant, the architecture would fail the first
test of conviviality \parencite{illich1973,weiser1996calm}.

This companion volume is therefore both the \textbf{constitutional specification} and the \textbf{practical
developer manual} for \textbf{Layer~6: The Semantic Commonwealth}---realized at the system level by
the local Semantic Commons Daemon (\texttt{col-commonsd}) and the local-network boundary mediator
(\texttt{col-gatewayd}). It answers two foundational questions for architects, application
builders, and domain specialists:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item How do we construct a \textbf{wide-purpose semantic protocol and ontological surface}
          that translates the intricate cybernetic reality underneath (Layers~1 through~5) and the
          legal/fiscal carapace outward (Layer~7) into self-describing, human-meaningful concepts
          without losing mathematical determinism or physical safety?
    \item How do we support an \textbf{open-ended constellation of applications} that serve as
          purpose-built \textbf{semantic lenses} onto that underlying reality---allowing any guild,
          accessibility collective, or developer to craft the right tool for a specific physical
          setting without ever creating a monolithic ``super-app'' or embedding private business
          logic inside user interfaces?
\end{enumerate}

\section*{The Five Layer~6 Architectural Laws of Version~3.2}

Across the architectural dialogues of \textit{The Architecture of Boundless Time}, five core laws
govern how Layer~6 bridges human meaning and cybernetic execution:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Law 1 --- Layer~6 as an HTTP-Like Semantic Protocol (\texttt{col-commonsd} + \texttt{col-gatewayd} vs.\ Plural Application Lenses):}
          Just as HTTP is not ``one giant website to rule them all,'' but a shared application-layer
          protocol over which millions of independent applications operate, Layer~6 is a
          \textbf{semantic protocol and compilation/decompilation boundary}. A single local
          \textbf{Layer~6 Semantic Daemon} (\texttt{col-commonsd} paired with \texttt{col-gatewayd}
          on VLAN~30 TLS~1.3 \texttt{:8443}) maintains the node's sovereign W3C JSON-LD~1.1
          Ontological Graph ($\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{surface}}$), enforces \texttt{HTTP 405 Method Not
          Allowed} on direct lower-layer telemetry endpoints (\texttt{/api/telemetry},
          \texttt{/api/v1/l1..l5/*}, \texttt{/api/v1/l7/*}), and communicates strictly downward to
          Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd} over \texttt{/run/collective/ipc/l5\_l6.sock}) and outward to
          Layer~7 (\texttt{col-adversaryd} over \texttt{/run/collective/ipc/l6\_l7.sock}). Even when
          a single physical building hosts multiple polycentric overlapping Layer~5 nodes---such as
          a Household Hearth ($N_s=4$), a Solo Studio ($N_s=1$), and a Maker Bench ($N_s=1$) wrapped
          by a domain-blind \textbf{Enveloping Node Hypervisor}---all lenses on a steward's devices
          speak to the single local Layer~6 Semantic Daemon, which routes each signed intent
          downward to the matching Layer~5 node namespace.
    \item \textbf{Law 2 --- Thermodynamic Separation of Core Application Lenses (\textit{The Pantry} vs.\ \textit{The Workshop} vs.\ \textit{The Bakery/Hearth} vs.\ \textit{Scenario Zero} vs.\ \textit{The Enveloping Node}):}
          Conflating perishable food with durable tools inside a generic ``inventory UI,'' or
          conflating household larder consumption with communal commissary production, obscures
          fundamentally distinct physical laws in Layers~1 and~2. Layer~6 separates five canonical
          lens archetypes over \texttt{col-commonsd}:
          \begin{itemize}
              \item \textbf{\textit{The Pantry Lens} (\texttt{/api/l6/v1/pantry}):} Tracks
                    \textbf{Perishable Biological Entropy} (Arrhenius temperature-integrated
                    shelf-life TTL curves, stock drawdowns, spontaneous weekend-detour demand
                    throttling, short-lived auto-expiring pickup intent leases, and pre-spoilage
                    Ring~$R_1$ neighbor surplus sharing).
              \item \textbf{\textit{The Workshop Lens} (\texttt{/api/l6/v1/workshop}):} Tracks
                    \textbf{Durable Mechanical Cycles} (calendar-agnostic discrete usage wear,
                    spindle hours, calibration intervals, Pillar~II \texttt{Scenario Kappa} BBS+
                    capability checkouts, custody leases, and the three-stage hardware fleet
                    progression from \textit{Stage~I Scavenged Layer~7 Shimmed} appliances through
                    \textit{Stage~II Right-to-Repair Hybrid Retrofits} to \textit{Stage~III Native
                    Open-Spec Collective Twins}).
              \item \textbf{\textit{The Bakery / Communal Hearth Lens} (\texttt{/api/l6/v1/bakery}):}
                    Orchestrates \textbf{Stoichiometric Thermal Transformation} under Pillar~I's
                    constitutional \textbf{$85\%$ Verified Staples (\texttt{8,500 bp}) / $15\%$
                    Compositional \textit{Montage} (\texttt{1,500 bp})} capacity split.
              \item \textbf{\textit{The Scenario Zero Forge Studio Lens} (\texttt{/api/l6/v1/scenario-zero}):}
                    Governs the \textbf{Khronos/OpenGL-Style Five-Phase Software Lifecycle},
                    monitoring \textbf{Dormant Equilibrium (``The \TeX{} Asymptote'')} as
                    operational stewardship decays ($R_i(t) \to 0$) while the non-decaying Universal
                    Civic Baseline ($B = 1 = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$) guards against
                    single-maintainer hijack, and surfacing \textbf{Substrate Drift} Semantic Diff
                    wake-up cards when lower-layer hardware evolves.
              \item \textbf{\textit{The Enveloping Node Hypervisor Lens} (\texttt{/api/l6/v1/envelope}):}
                    Arbitrates \textbf{Domain-Blind Spatial Boundary Leases}
                    (\texttt{EnvelopingBoundaryLeaseV50}: partition acoustics in \texttt{cB},
                    breaker power in \texttt{mW}, exergy in \texttt{uJ}, thermal delta in
                    \texttt{mK}, spatial polygon in \texttt{mm}, and mandatory finite
                    \texttt{lease\_ttl\_ms}) across fractal envelopes (\textit{Room/Building $\to$
                    Street/Microgrid $\to$ Bioregional Watershed}) via \textbf{Two-Speed
                    Jurisprudence} (\textit{Fast-Path Layer~2 Hazard Preemption} and
                    \textit{Slow-Path Semantic Diff Precedent Compilation}).
          \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Law 3 --- Zero-Surveillance Local Need Capture and the Dual-Loop Bakery ($N_s$ Membership vs.\ $N_p$ Anonymous Appetite):}
          Every Layer~5 node evaluates member voting weight as $V_i(t) = B + R_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$
          (in fixed-point basis points: $V_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t) = 10{,}000 + R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t)$)
          scoped strictly to active \textbf{Node Membership ($N_s$)} across Solo Genesis ($N_s=1$),
          Dyadic Collaboration ($N_s=2$), and Community Commons ($N_s \gg 2$, anti-capture
          crossover $N_s > R_{\max} + 2$). External settlement consumers ($N_p$) never vote on a
          producer guild's internal dough hydration or oven schedules, nor undergo identity-linked
          tracking at the pickup counter; instead, \textit{The Pantry} captures dietary rhythms
          locally, strips personal DIDs in local Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}) via BBS+
          ring-membership zero-knowledge proofs, and emits 64-byte anonymous cohort appetite vectors
          and short-lived pickup leases (\texttt{AnonymousAppetiteLeaseV50}) to Layer~3.
    \item \textbf{Law 4 --- Layer~6 as Societal Compiler ($\Pi_{\mathrm{down}} : \mathrm{L6} \to \mathrm{L5} \to \mathrm{L4}$) and Upward Semantic Diff Decompiler ($\Pi_{\mathrm{up}}^{\mathrm{diff}} : \mathrm{L4} \to \mathrm{L5} \to \mathrm{L6}$):}
          Layers~4 (\texttt{col-execd}) and~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}) are formal cybernetic execution
          engines operating in WASM bytecode, capability bitmasks, and fixed-point ledgers;
          \textbf{Layer~6 carries $100\%$ of the burden of human meaning}. Downward
          ($\Pi_{\mathrm{down}}$), Layer~6 enables \textbf{Open-Source Franchising (``The Speedee
          Service System Without the Rent'')} across the \textbf{Three Sovereign Content-Addressed
          JSON-LD Libraries} (\textit{Ontological Graph}, \textit{Orchestration Registry}, and
          \textit{Physics Baseline} ranked by \texttt{collective:thermodynamicProof} and Ecological
          Replacement Cost $\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$). Upward ($\Pi_{\mathrm{up}}^{\mathrm{diff}}$),
          whenever Layer~4's \textbf{3-Tier BPMN Hardware Adaptation Cascade} (\textit{Tier~1: SI
          Parameter Scaling $\to$ Tier~2: Macro-Activity Substitution $\to$ Tier~3: True-Constraint
          Hard-Block}) or Layer~5's policy engine hits an unresolvable boundary,
          \texttt{col-commonsd} decompiles the machine yield packet into a plain-language
          \textbf{Layer~6 Semantic Diff Card}.
    \item \textbf{Law 5 --- The Two-Zone Metrological Invariant and Zero-Stat Liberation:}
          Internally, Layers~2, 3, and~4 execute exclusively in a single \textbf{Canonical 64-Bit
          Fixed-Point SI Metrological Core} (\texttt{int64\_t} \texttt{uJ}, \texttt{mW},
          \texttt{mg}, \texttt{mm}, \texttt{mL}, \texttt{mK}, \texttt{cB}, \texttt{ms}, \texttt{bp};
          \texttt{floating\_point\_opcodes == 0}, with $1\,\mathrm{MJ} = 10^{12}\,\mu\mathrm{J}$).
          At the human edge, Layer~6 provides a deterministic \textbf{SI Localization Functor}
          ($\mathcal{U}_{\mathrm{loc}} \leftrightarrow \mathrm{SI}_{\mathrm{fixed}}$) accepting
          familiar cultural and craft idioms (baker's hydration~$\%$, cups, imperial inches,
          $^\circ\mathrm{F}/^\circ\mathrm{C}$, $\mathrm{dBA}$) and displaying both the human
          expression and the exact canonical SI integer in Stage~4 Deterministic Read-Back. Every
          Layer~6 lens is engineered for \textbf{Zero-Stat Liberation}: zero gamified XP bars, zero
          fake RPG stat buffs, and $<30$-second interactions that return stewards immediately to
          unmonitored physical life.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Structure of This Companion Monograph}

Part~I (Chapters~1--8) develops the complete engineering specification for building Layer~6 and
its plural Application Lenses:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Chapter~1} establishes the \textbf{HTTP/Browser Protocol Analogy}, the bilateral
          socket membrane (\texttt{l5\_l6.sock} and \texttt{l6\_l7.sock}), the read-only telemetry
          guard (\texttt{HTTP 405 Method Not Allowed}), the thermodynamic taxonomy separating
          \textit{The Pantry}, \textit{The Workshop}, \textit{The Bakery/Hearth}, \textit{Scenario
          Zero Forge Studio}, and \textit{The Enveloping Node Hypervisor Lens}, the five-part
          \textbf{Application Lens Developer Contract}, and \textbf{Zero-Stat Liberation}.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~2} specifies the \textbf{Ontological Surface}, the \textbf{Two-Zone
          Metrological Invariant} (the deterministic Human Edge SI Localization Functor
          $\mathcal{U}_{\mathrm{loc}} \leftrightarrow \mathrm{SI}_{\mathrm{fixed}}$ converting
          cups, hydration~$\%$, inches, $^\circ\mathrm{F}/^\circ\mathrm{C}$, and $\mathrm{dBA}$ to
          canonical 64-bit fixed-point SI integers \texttt{uJ}, \texttt{mW}, \texttt{mg},
          \texttt{mm}, \texttt{mL}, \texttt{mK}, \texttt{cB}, \texttt{ms}, \texttt{bp}), the
          \textbf{Downward Societal Compiler ($\Pi_{\mathrm{down}}$)}, and the \textbf{Upward
          Semantic Diff Decompiler ($\Pi_{\mathrm{up}}^{\mathrm{diff}}$)} across all four canonical
          Layer~4/Layer~5 hard-block classes.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~3} formalizes the six-stage human authorship pipeline, non-exportable Web
          Crypto and \texttt{ATECC608A} keys, local-first Domain~A/Domain~B Automerge CRDT
          synchronization, and \textbf{Zero-Surveillance Local Need Capture}---proving how
          \textit{The Pantry} absorbs \textbf{Spontaneous Weekend Detours} via short-lived
          auto-expiring pickup leases (\texttt{AnonymousAppetiteLeaseV50}) while the
          \textbf{Dual-Loop Bakery} separates guild membership governance ($N_s$, where
          $V_i(t)=1+R_i(t)$) from anonymous community appetite ($N_p$).
    \item \textbf{Chapter~4} solves high-density cognitive ergonomics across hundreds of concurrent
          workflows via three-stage variety attenuation and **Spatial Homestead Round Coalescing**,
          and provides the \textbf{Definitive Five-Lens Reference Catalog} (complete state machines,

          in decompiling Layer~4 \textbf{Tier~3 True-Constraint Hard-Blocks} into plain-language
          \textbf{Semantic Diff Cards} without cloud APIs or hallucinated thresholds.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~6} details thermodynamic display physics (0-mW bistable E-Ink, passive

          Spaces, and WCAG~2.2 Level~AAA workshop and hearth terminals engineered for
