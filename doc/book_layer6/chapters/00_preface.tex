\epigraph{People need not only to obtain things, they need above all the freedom to make things among which they can live, to give shape to them according to their own tastes, and to put them to use in caring for and about others.}{---\textcite{illich1973}, \textit{Tools for Conviviality}}

Every architecture of collective self-governance eventually confronts its moment of truth at
the boundary where living human beings actually touch the system. Below that threshold, a
community may engineer the most rigorous thermodynamic ledger, the cleanest deterministic
workflow virtual machine, and the soundest cryptographic web of trust ever committed to
silicon. Yet if the human-facing layer forces neighbours to squint at opaque engineering
dashboards, funnels every domestic act through a single bloated ``super-app,'' relies on
cloud-hosted thin clients that go blank during a winter storm, or interposes an ungrounded
language model that subtly rewrites civic intent before signature, then sovereignty has been
surrendered at the last millimeter \parencite{weiser1996calm,zuboff2019,bender2021parrots}.

In Volume~1 of this series, we introduced the seven-layer constitutional architecture of The
Collective, establishing how physical truth enters from hardware reflexes (Layer~2) into an
append-only, conflict-free thermodynamic ledger (Layer~3), how deterministic state machines
coordinate multi-workflow choreography without holding identity keys (Layer~4), how
polycentric assemblies and zero-knowledge webs of trust ratify communal law (Layer~5), and
how legal and fiscal carapaces insulate the commons from external predation (Layer~7).
Between the ratified governance records of Layer~5 below and the legacy institutional
perimeter of Layer~7 above sits \textbf{Layer~6: The Semantic Commonwealth}, realized at the
system level by the Semantic Commons Daemon (\texttt{col-commonsd}) and its local-network
capability mediator (\texttt{col-gatewayd}).

Earlier working drafts of this companion volume bore the provisional title \textit{The Oasis
Interface}---a phrasing left over from an early exploratory phase before the constitutional
demarcation between spatial simulation and production civic interfaces had fully matured.
This Version~3.1 overhaul retires that conflation permanently. Layer~6 is neither a video-game
heads-up display nor a totalizing digital screen meant to mediate human life. Soil, craft,
unmediated council circles, shared meals, and face-to-face companionship remain the primary
medium of the living world \parencite{illich1973,ishii1997tangible}. Layer~6 is the calm,
local-first semantic fabric through which people author structured intents, deliberate across
concentric Trust Rings, inspect and steward shared infrastructure without algorithmic
coercion, and direct the legal carapace of their settlement---governed everywhere by one
inviolable rule: \textit{the machine assists, people author, and only people sign}
\parencite{winograd1986understanding}.

\section*{Separating Concerns: Oasis (\texttt{Layer 1b}) vs.\ Real-Life Layer~6 Applications}

A foundational breakthrough in the recent evolution of the repository was the strict
\textbf{separation of concerns} between \textbf{Oasis (\texttt{Layer 1b})} and \textbf{Layer~6
civic interactions}. In conventional simulation games and industrial ``digital twins,''
governance menus, inventory requests, policy toggles, and diagnostic inspectors are welded
directly into the 3D rendering engine as bespoke, in-engine widgets that talk to internal
game-loop variables \parencite{grieves2014digital}. Had Oasis followed that path, every
workflow ratified inside the proving ground would have exercised fake UI code disconnected
from the real Sovereign Stack.

Instead, Oasis excised Layer~6 interactions from the 3D WebGPU engine altogether. Inside
\texttt{core/1\_simulation/}, the WebGPU voxel raymarcher and C++20 physics loop retain zero
cryptographic private keys, zero network sockets, and zero direct authority to ratify
governance proposals or settle mutual-credit balances. All Layer~6 interactions---searching
and defining semantic community needs, deliberating and ratifying Layer~5 governance
proposals, parameterizing agronomic cultivars, inspecting read-only Layer~4 BPMN token flows,
and auditing cluster balances---are conducted through an independent \textbf{Layer~6
Companion Application} (\texttt{core/1\_simulation/src/web/companion/index.html} and
\texttt{companion\_l6.js}).

Crucially, this Companion Application is \textbf{not a simulation mock}. Mounted identically
whether opened as a slide-over drawer beside the Lot~402 viewport or launched standalone in a
mobile or desktop browser tab, it communicates over live HTTP and broadcast channels with
\texttt{col-gatewayd}, exercising the real Layer~2 through Layer~7 C++20 implementations and
real W3C JSON-LD catalogs (\texttt{pea\_to\_soup\_needs.jsonld},
\texttt{fabaceae\_cultivars.jsonld}, and \texttt{NodeFacilityBlueprint} manifests). When a
citizen transitions from rehearsing agroecology on Lot~402 to stewarding a physical
homestead or neighborhood commons, the Layer~6 application does not need to be rewritten from
game UI into production software---it \textit{already is} the production software, crossing
from simulated provenance (\texttt{QUALITY\_SIMULATED = 0x01}) to hardware-attested reality
(\texttt{QUALITY\_HARDWARE\_ROOT = 0x02}) under unmistakable visual and cryptographic modal
segregation.

\section*{From One Starter App to a Constellation of Convivial Applications}

On Lot~402 today, while Oasis operates in its initial bootstrap phase, Layer~6 interactions
are consolidated into a single four-tab Companion Application (\textit{Needs \& Requests},
\textit{L5 Decisions}, \textit{L4 Workflows}, and \textit{Cluster, L3 \& Bills}). For a solo
steward bringing up four seed workflows across a single parcel, one unified starter client is
pedagogically clear and immediately legible.

Yet as a settlement matures, \textbf{Layer~6 does not need to be---and must never ossify into---a
single monolithic application}. A cook skimming a pasteurizing stockpot with wet hands in a
commissary kitchen does not want a screen cluttered with bioregional Ricardian treaty diffs or
compiler Software Bills of Materials. A parent checking an infant into a camera-free perinatal
sanctuary pod requires an ultra-calm, zero-token check-in slate, not an exergy orderbook. A
cargo-trike courier pedaling across a rain-soaked valley during a LoRa mesh partition needs a
glove-friendly offline manifest scanner, while an elder with low vision or a neighbour at an
in-person assembly may prefer spoken voice confirmation, a bistable E-Ink wall placard, or a
hand-marked paper ballot with zero digital screens present \parencite{w3c2023wcag,costanzachock2020design}.

Because Layer~6 places zero business logic inside any user interface, the starter Companion
App generalizes naturally into a \textbf{Constellation of Convivial Applications} standing
upon a \textbf{Uniform Semantic Foundation}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Shared W3C JSON-LD Semantic Substrate:} Every application in the constellation
        reads and emits canonical Linked Data graphs anchored in the governed namespace
        (\texttt{https://w3id.org/collective/v1/}), validated by Shapes Constraint Language
        (\texttt{SHACL}) schemas \parencite{w3c2020jsonld,w3c2017shacl} and hashed via RFC~8785
        JSON Canonicalization (\texttt{JCS}) and W3C RDF Dataset Canonicalization
        (\texttt{RDFC-1.0}) \parencite{rfc8785,w3c2024rdfc} using pure 64-bit fixed-point SI
        units.
\item \textbf{Least-Privilege Capability Manifests:} Rather than granting every interface
        omniscient node access, each constellation client pairs via an out-of-band QR fragment
        handshake to obtain a pairwise \texttt{did:peer} session scoped strictly to its
        ratified \texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1} roles (for example, \texttt{intent:author}
        and \texttt{bpmn:review} on a woodworking bench E-Ink slate, with zero access to
        governance ballots or Layer~7 statutory ledgers) \parencite{miller2006robust,w3c2022did}.
\item \textbf{Universal Authorship and Local-First CRDT Sync:} Every mutation across every
        application traverses the identical six-stage \texttt{Draft -> Deterministic Read-Back
        -> Personal Hardware Sign} ceremony backed by non-exportable Web Crypto or hardware
        secure-element Ed25519 keys \parencite{w3c2017webcrypto,microchip_atecc608a}, queuing
        offline inside Automerge Conflict-Free Replicated Data Type (\texttt{CRDT}) outboxes
        \parencite{kleppmann2017automerge,kleppmann2019localfirst}.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Attenuating High-Density Workflow Choreography Across 25 Scenarios}

The necessity of this constellation architecture becomes unmistakable once we examine the
dramatically enriched \textbf{25 Sovereign Scenarios} (\texttt{03\_SCENARIOS.md}, spanning
\texttt{Scenario Zero} through \texttt{Scenario Omega} across Horizontal Waves~A--F). A naive
prototype imagines a node running two or three linear checklists. In living reality, a single
governed node sustains \textbf{dozens to thousands of intricate, interlocking workflows} talking
to one another across room-scoped sub-nodes (\texttt{office}, \texttt{garage},
\texttt{garden}, \texttt{hearth}) and federating across concentric Trust Rings ($R_0$ through
$R_6$).

While staggered Fabaceae guilds accumulate photo-thermal degree-days and trigger timber
trellis requisitions in the workshop, induction stockpots integrate Bigelow pasteurization
lethality; thermophilic compost bays and biochar retorts close nutrient loops; solar MPPT
controllers donate compute waste heat from hermetic multi-architecture builds into bathhouse
hydronics; rotational childcare pods enforce adult-to-infant safety ratios; cargo e-trikes
clear pairwise Bilateral Clearing Channel (\texttt{BCC}) milk-runs; and sortition panels
deliberate watershed treaties.

\section*{Convening the Six-Specialist Layer~6 Architectural Council}

Just as Lot~402 validated six major Layer~6 capabilities in running code---shared dual-mount
execution, live JSON-LD need and cultivar parameterization, HTTP \texttt{405} lower-layer
read-only protection, qualitative 1-to-5 somatic feel strips, and four-node vs.\ Ring-1 swarm
visibility---it also exposed concrete prototype ceilings requiring specialist review before
community-wide deployment. A \textbf{Six-Specialist Layer~6 Architectural Council} was
therefore convened across:
\begin{itemize}
\item The \textbf{Semantic Web \& Ontology Architect} (unifying \texttt{@context} IRIs, SHACL shapes, RFC~8785 / \texttt{RDFC-1.0} canonicalization, and zero-float QUDT fixed-point schemas);
\item The \textbf{Local-First \& Gateway Protocol Engineer} (replacing $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$ full-snapshot polling and in-process runtime coupling with topic-scoped delta streams, \texttt{SOCK\_SEQPACKET} daemon isolation, and Domain~B Automerge CRDT outboxes);
\item The \textbf{Cryptographic Authorship \& Deliberation Specialist} (enforcing \texttt{extractable: false} Web Crypto Ed25519 signing, mandatory pre-sign Deterministic Read-Back, four-class text provenance tags, and pure-integer Agora/Ricardian treaty pipelines);
\item The \textbf{Edge NLU \& Adversarial ML Security Auditor} (architecting dual-sandbox Intake vs.\ Drafting model isolation with grammar-constrained decoding, SHA-256 weight governance, and microjoule exergy load shedding);
\item The \textbf{Calm HCI, Somatic Ergonomics \& Accessibility Specialist} (engineering Spatial Homestead Round Coalescing, the zero-penalty Protocol of Refusal, WCAG~2.2 AAA tokens, and screenless paper/voice/E-Ink modalities); and
\item The \textbf{Legal Carapace \& Semantic Camouflage Counsel} (purging legacy currency bleed from Layer~6 views and hardening the Mundane Semantic Camouflage Filter across \texttt{l6\_l7.sock}).
\end{itemize}

Part~I (Chapters~1--8) develops these architectural foundations in full mathematical and
systems detail, culminating in Chapter~8's line-by-line Specialist Council audit of the live
codebase. Part~II (Chapters~9--14) then traverses Horizontal Waves~A through~F, proving how
the Constellation of Convivial Applications supports every human interaction across all
twenty-five Sovereign Scenarios from a suburban seed lot to terminal bioregional sovereignty.
