\chapter{Wave B: Communal Hearth, Convivial Fabrication, and Offline Five-Artery Transit (\texttt{Gamma}, \texttt{Alpha}, \texttt{Beta})}
\label{ch:wave-b}

\epigraph{Modernized poverty combines the lack of power over circumstances with a loss of personal potency\ldots{} Convivial reconstruction demands tools that enable people to nourish, equip, and move themselves within local metabolic bounds.}{---\textcite{illich1973}, \textit{Tools for Conviviality}}

Once a settlement can compile its own Constellation software, onboard stewards inclusively, and enforce burnout-proof rest boundaries (Wave~A), its next daily imperative is replacing the three largest household survival dependencies---grocery bills, retail hardware replacements, and fossil-fuel delivery errands. \textbf{Horizontal Wave~B} accomplishes this physical liberation across \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Gamma}} (\textit{The Communal Hearth, Keyline Agroecology \& Unconditional Nourishment}), \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Alpha}} (\textit{The Convivial Fabrication Shop \& Tool Library}), and \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Beta}} (\textit{Five-Artery Inter-Node Transit, Cold-Chain Milk-Runs \& Delay-Tolerant Couriers}).

Nowhere in the Sovereign Stack is the transition from a single starter app to a \textbf{Constellation of Specialized Surfaces} more tangible than in Wave~B. Steam-filled commissary kitchens, sawdust-laden router benches, and rain-swept cargo-trike handlebars destroy generic office laptops and punish tiny touchscreen menus. Wave~B deploys washable E-Ink kitchen slates, passive NFC allergen tiffins, shop-floor Bill-of-Materials (\texttt{BOM}) terminals wired to physical Lockout/Tagout (\texttt{LOTO}) contactors, and glove-friendly offline courier decks---coalescing up to \textbf{$1{,}150+$ concurrent metabolic workflows} at Stage~3 into $3\text{--}5$ calm human rounds per shift.

\subsection{Thermodynamic Separation of \textit{The Pantry Lens} from \textit{The Bakery / Hearth Lens} and the Dual-Loop Bakery ($N_s = 5$ vs.\ $N_p = 500$)}

Scenario~\texttt{Gamma} is the direct real-world fulfillment of the seed ontological graphs (\texttt{pea\_to\_soup\_needs.jsonld}, \texttt{fabaceae\_cultivars.jsonld}, and \texttt{bp\_gamma\_stage1\_hearth\_and\_garden.jsonld}) examined in Chapters~\ref{ch:semantic-foundation} and~\ref{ch:oasis-council-l6}. At production scale, Version~3.2 strictly separates household larder consumption from communal commissary production across four specialized surfaces governed by a Ground-Floor Hearth Enveloping Node Lease (\texttt{6,800 cB} acoustic cap, \texttt{6,000,000 mW} power ceiling, \texttt{+4,500 mK} flue exhaust delta):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{\textit{The Pantry Lens} (\texttt{/api/l6/v1/pantry}, Perishable Arrhenius Entropy \& Detour Absorption):} Runs on each household's fridge slate, integrating continuous Arrhenius shelf-life TTL curves over ambient millikelvins (\texttt{mK}). When household bread or tiffin stock crosses its replenishment threshold, \textit{The Pantry} asks local \texttt{col-kmsd} to strip the household's \texttt{did:peer} via a BBS+ Ring-$R_1$ ZKP and emit a 64-byte short-lived auto-expiring pickup lease (\texttt{AnonymousAppetiteLeaseV50}, $12\,\mathrm{h}$ TTL). If the household heads spontaneously to the mountains for the weekend, Layer~2 shelf drawdowns pause and \textit{The Pantry} throttles bakery demand automatically with zero calendar surveillance or nagging notifications, while offering pre-spoilage items to Ring-$R_1$ neighbors.
    \item \textbf{\textit{The Bakery / Communal Hearth Craft Lens} (\texttt{/api/l6/v1/bakery}, Dual-Loop 85\% Staple / 15\% \textit{Montage} Split):} Mounted on an IP67 washable E-Ink slate beside the induction stockpot and deck ovens. Constitutional voting weight $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$ belongs strictly to the $N_s = 5$ active member-bakers (never to the $N_p = 500$ bread eaters). Merging Layer~3 anonymous cohort appetite weather-maps with Layer~2 pickup-shelf clearance load cells, the bakers dedicate \textbf{$8{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($85\%$) of Layer~4 schedule capacity} to verified staples (sourdough boules, split-pea baseline tiffins) and reserve \textbf{$1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($15\%$) for Dr\_Sofia's compositional \textit{montage}} (unrequested culinary experiments validated post-bake by physical shelf clearance). Hardware adaptation uses Layer~4 \textbf{Tier~1 SI Parameter Scaling} (splitting $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ dough batches into $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{kg}$ spiral-mixer sub-batches), converges on \textbf{Gastronorm GN~1/1 ($530 \times 325\,\mathrm{mm}$)} stainless pans via $\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$ minimization, and upgrades its **Stage~I Scavenged commercial oven** via a **Stage~II Right-to-Repair open PID thermocouple retrofit** ($\Delta R_i > 0$).
    \item \textbf{The Garden Keyline \& Micro-Flock Fieldbook (\texttt{agro:cultivar}, \texttt{crdt:read}):} Offline Domain~B Automerge tablet pairing soil-moisture probes, \textit{Fabaceae} trellis guilds, Black Soldier Fly Larvae (\texttt{BSFL}) bioconversion bins, and the $4\text{--}8$ hen pasture micro-flock (fed strictly on trimmings, BSFL, and calcined bone-ash calcium grit with zero human-edible grain competition).
    \item \textbf{The Zero-Knowledge BBS+ Allergen NFC Tiffin Locker (\texttt{tiffin:dispense}):} Returnable 304-stainless insulated tiffin carriers tagged with passive ISO~14443 NFC chips.
\end{itemize}

Four canonical JSON-LD intents govern the loop: \texttt{DietaryNeedIntent} (registering household portion counts and cryptographic allergen constraints), \texttt{MealOffering} (publishing batch availability with \textbf{exact zero internal pricing}, \texttt{domesticCareTokenMinting: 0}), \texttt{AgroPastoralBatchAttestation}, and \texttt{CompostRoutingIntent}.

\subsection{Coalescing 96 Culinary/Garden Workflows and Anaphylactic Hard-Stops}

In a mature neighborhood hearth running $96$ concurrent biological and thermal state machines (staggered pea/bean beds, $24$-hour collagen bone broth simmers, kraut pH drops, compost holds, and tiffin washes), Layer~6 suppresses every routine sensor tick until action is needed, coalescing checks into one morning garden/coop walk and one afternoon prep shift.

Safety boundaries, however, enforce uncompromising physical and cryptographic gates:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Bigelow Pasteurization \& Botulism Acidification Gates:} Layer~6 never allows a human click to override physical food safety. A stockpot batch cannot transition to \texttt{DISPENSE\_READY} until Layer~2 PT100 probes attest core temperature $\ge 347{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($74.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$) continuously for $\ge 120{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}$ (resetting the hold timer if temperature dips into the $277{,}150\text{--}333{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ danger zone), and shelf-stable ferments require verified acidity $\le 4{,}600\,\mathrm{mpH}$ ($\mathrm{pH} \le 4.6$).
    \item \textbf{Zero-Knowledge Anaphylactic Solenoid Interlock:} A member with a severe peanut, sesame, or celiac allergy never publishes medical diagnoses to the kitchen roster. Instead, tapping their NFC badge against a tiffin locker evaluates a local BBS+ zero-knowledge set-disjointness proof \parencite{camenisch2004signature} against the tiffin's signed recursive \texttt{recipe\_bom}. Any allergen intersection triggers an immediate \textbf{Anaphylactic Hard-Stop}---mechanically keeping the solenoid latched and illuminating a zero-LLM crimson warning on the E-Ink fascia.
\end{enumerate}
Across Layer~7, communal hearths operate lawfully under UUNAA Section~6 private unincorporated food clubs, Right-to-Garden ordinances, and State Cottage Food / IRC Subchapter~T commissary exemptions.

\subsection{Recursive Cost Vector Read-Back and LOTO Badge Interlocks}

Scenario~\texttt{Alpha} transforms the garage workbench (\texttt{node-02:garage-fab}) and neighborhood maker-commons into a shared fabrication guild building timber pea trellises, poultry coops, root-washer jigs, CNC joinery, and replacement appliance parts. Two Constellation surfaces anchor the shop floor:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{The Parametric Blueprint \& Total Cost Browser (\texttt{intent:author}):} Renders \texttt{BlueprintSpecializationIntent} and \texttt{ManufacturingOrderIntent} graphs alongside a mandatory pre-sign Deterministic Read-Back of the \textbf{Total Production Cost Vector}:
    \begin{equation}
        \mathbf{C}_{\mathrm{prod}} = \bigl\langle E_{\mathrm{spindle}\,\mu\mathrm{J}},\; M_{\mathrm{stock}\,\mathrm{mg}},\; \lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}\,\mu\mathrm{J}},\; \tau_{\mathrm{machine}\,\mathrm{ms}} \bigr\rangle
    \end{equation}
    summarizing spindle microjoules, raw stock milligrams drawn from local salvage vs.\ Layer~7 fallback, Asymptotic Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$), and acoustic window compliance ($\le 550\,\mathrm{cB}$ daytime boundary cap) before locking stock.
    \item \textbf{The Dust-Sealed Shop-Floor BOM E-Ink Terminal (\texttt{tool:unlock}, \texttt{bpmn:review}):} Wired adjacent to physical \textbf{Lockout/Tagout (\texttt{LOTO})} keyed switches and contactless NFC readers. Checking out or energizing a table saw, CNC mill, or welder (\texttt{ToolCheckoutIntent}) verifies an unlinkable Scenario~\texttt{Kappa} BBS+ safety credential in $< 50\,\mathrm{ms}$, while opening any physical guard drops contactor power in $< 10\,\mathrm{ms}$ in Layer~2 hardware regardless of Layer~6 state.
\end{itemize}

Crucially, Scenario~\texttt{Alpha} ($N_s = 1 \to 6$ machinists holding $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$ voice inside an Industrial Bay Envelope: \texttt{8,500 cB} daytime / \texttt{5,000 cB} nighttime acoustic ceiling, \texttt{4,500,000 mW} power lease) showcases \textbf{\textit{The Workshop Lens} (\texttt{/api/l6/v1/workshop})} across three Version~3.2 pillars:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Calendar-Agnostic Cycle Wear \& Convivial Creative Privacy:} Unlike \textit{The Pantry}'s biological TTL clocks, \textit{The Workshop Lens} tracks discrete spindle hours, thermal cycles, and custody leases (\texttt{workshop:cumulativeUseMs}, \texttt{workshop:wearIndexBp}). Booking a \texttt{PersonalSlotIntent} for personal woodworking, luthiery, or hobby invention reserves machine microjoules and dust extraction while logging \textbf{zero telemetry on what object the steward crafted} (\texttt{0} personal output surveillance).
    \item \textbf{Open-Source Franchising \& Tier~2 Macro-Activity Substitution:} Machinists browse the Three Sovereign Libraries (\textit{Ontological Graph}, \textit{Orchestration Registry}, \textit{Physics Baseline}) ranked by \texttt{collective:thermodynamicProof}.     \item \textbf{Stage~I Scavenged CNC $\to$ Stage~II Right-to-Repair Hybrid Retrofit ($\Delta R_i > 0$):} On Day~0, the shop boots on a Stage~I DRM-locked commercial CNC router wrapped in a parasitic Layer~2 current-clamp shim. \end{enumerate}
