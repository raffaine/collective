\chapter{Wave B: Communal Hearth, Convivial Fabrication, and Offline Five-Artery Transit (\texttt{Gamma}, \texttt{Alpha}, \texttt{Beta})}

\epigraph{Modernized poverty combines the lack of power over circumstances with a loss of personal potency\ldots{} Convivial reconstruction demands tools that enable people to nourish, equip, and move themselves within local metabolic bounds.}{---\textcite{illich1973}, \textit{Tools for Conviviality}}

Once a settlement can compile its own Constellation software, onboard stewards inclusively, and enforce burnout-proof rest boundaries (Wave~A), its next daily imperative is replacing the three largest household survival dependencies---grocery bills, retail hardware replacements, and fossil-fuel delivery errands. \textbf{Horizontal Wave~B} accomplishes this physical liberation across \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Gamma}} (\textit{The Communal Hearth, Keyline Agroecology \& Unconditional Nourishment}), \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Alpha}} (\textit{The Convivial Fabrication Shop \& Tool Library}), and \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Beta}} (\textit{Five-Artery Inter-Node Transit, Cold-Chain Milk-Runs \& Delay-Tolerant Couriers}).

Nowhere in the Sovereign Stack is the transition from a single starter app to a \textbf{Constellation of Specialized Surfaces} more tangible than in Wave~B. Steam-filled commissary kitchens, sawdust-laden router benches, and rain-swept cargo-trike handlebars destroy generic office laptops and punish tiny touchscreen menus. Wave~B deploys washable E-Ink kitchen slates, passive NFC allergen tiffins, shop-floor Bill-of-Materials (\texttt{BOM}) terminals wired to physical Lockout/Tagout (\texttt{LOTO}) contactors, and glove-friendly offline courier decks---coalescing up to \textbf{$1{,}150+$ concurrent metabolic workflows} at Stage~3 into $3\text{--}5$ calm human rounds per shift.

\subsection{Coalescing 96 Culinary/Garden Workflows and Anaphylactic Hard-Stops}

In a mature neighborhood hearth running $96$ concurrent biological and thermal state machines (staggered pea/bean beds, $24$-hour collagen bone broth simmers, kraut pH drops, compost holds, and tiffin washes), Layer~6 suppresses every routine sensor tick until action is needed, coalescing checks into one morning garden/coop walk and one afternoon prep shift.

Safety boundaries, however, enforce uncompromising physical and cryptographic gates:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Bigelow Pasteurization \& Botulism Acidification Gates:} Layer~6 never allows a human click to override physical food safety. A stockpot batch cannot transition to \texttt{DISPENSE\_READY} until Layer~2 PT100 probes attest core temperature $\ge 347{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($74.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$) continuously for $\ge 120{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}$ (resetting the hold timer if temperature dips into the $277{,}150\text{--}333{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ danger zone), and shelf-stable ferments require verified acidity $\le 4{,}600\,\mathrm{mpH}$ ($\mathrm{pH} \le 4.6$).
    \item \textbf{Zero-Knowledge Anaphylactic Solenoid Interlock:} A member with a severe peanut, sesame, or celiac allergy never publishes medical diagnoses to the kitchen roster. Instead, tapping their NFC badge against a tiffin locker evaluates a local BBS+ zero-knowledge set-disjointness proof \parencite{camenisch2004signature} against the tiffin's signed recursive \texttt{recipe\_bom}. Any allergen intersection triggers an immediate \textbf{Anaphylactic Hard-Stop}---mechanically keeping the solenoid latched and illuminating a zero-LLM crimson warning on the E-Ink fascia.
\end{enumerate}
Across Layer~7, communal hearths operate lawfully under UUNAA Section~6 private unincorporated food clubs, Right-to-Garden ordinances, and State Cottage Food / IRC Subchapter~T commissary exemptions.

    \begin{equation}
        \mathbf{C}_{\mathrm{prod}} = \bigl\langle E_{\mathrm{spindle}\,\mu\mathrm{J}},\; M_{\mathrm{stock}\,\mathrm{mg}},\; \lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}\,\mu\mathrm{J}},\; \tau_{\mathrm{machine}\,\mathrm{ms}} \bigr\rangle

    summarizing spindle microjoules, raw stock milligrams drawn from local salvage vs.\ Layer~7 fallback, Asymptotic Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$), and acoustic window compliance ($\le 550\,\mathrm{cB}$ daytime boundary cap) before locking stock.
