\chapter{The Bilateral Membrane, Semantic Surface Architecture, and the Constellation of Application Lenses}
\label{ch:constellation-membrane}

\epigraph{A convivial society should be designed to allow all its members the most autonomous action by means of tools least controlled by others. People feel joy, as opposed to mere pleasure, to the extent that their activities are creative; while the growth of tools beyond a certain point increases regimentation, dependence, exploitation, and impotence.}{---Adapted from \textcite{illich1973}}

Every human-facing software layer encodes an implicit constitution. In conventional enterprise,
civic, and smart-infrastructure platforms, that constitution is overwhelmingly centralizing: a
single monolithic web portal or mobile ``super-app'' accumulates presentation code, domain rules,
session credentials, behavioral analytics, direct database write paths, and actuator overrides
behind one administrative screen. What begins during early prototyping as developer convenience
hardens rapidly into both a security hazard and a cognitive bottleneck. A single compromised
client context or misconfigured API route acquires simultaneous visibility into intimate
household routines and unmediated authority over physical infrastructure
\parencite{saltzer1984end,rose2020zero,zuboff2019}. Worse still, when an entire community's
activities are funneled through one generalist dashboard, people must mentally translate their
situated physical craft---cooking, farming, machining, caregiving, or deliberating---into the
rigid schema of an engineering control panel \parencite{illich1973,costanzachock2020design}.

Because \textbf{Layer~6: The Semantic Commonwealth} is the most visible layer of the Sovereign
Stack---the surface that almost every resident, visitor, neighbour, and third-party application
developer interacts with directly---its architecture must solve a dual challenge. First, it
must serve as a \textbf{wide-purpose semantic and ontological surface} that translates the
complex cybernetic reality underneath (Layers~1 through~5) and the legal/fiscal carapace outward
(Layer~7) into self-describing, human-meaningful concepts. Second, it must support an
\textbf{open-ended constellation of applications} that act as specialized \textbf{semantic
lenses} onto that shared reality, governed by three foundational principles:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Strict Bilateral Socket Containment:} Layer~6 (realized in POSIX user space by the
      semantic engine \texttt{col-commonsd} and the local boundary mediator
      \texttt{col-gatewayd}) possesses zero direct mutating communication paths to physical
      hardware (Layers~1--2), thermodynamic ledgers (Layer~3), or the deterministic workflow
      virtual machine (Layer~4). Its entire capacity to affect or ingest authoritative state is
      bounded by two kernel-authenticated \texttt{AF\_UNIX} \texttt{SOCK\_SEQPACKET} membranes:
      inward across \texttt{l5\_l6.sock} to polycentric governance (\texttt{col-kmsd}) and
      outward across \texttt{l6\_l7.sock} to the legacy institutional carapace
      (\texttt{col-adversaryd}).
\item \textbf{A Wide-Purpose Ontological Surface Over Cybernetic Reality:} Rather than exposing
      raw lower-layer C++ structs, binary ring buffers, or BPMN~2.0 bytecode to user interfaces,
      \texttt{col-commonsd} projects the state of the node as a unified, self-describing W3C
      JSON-LD~1.1 and SHACL knowledge graph (\texttt{https://w3id.org/collective/v1/}). This
      ontological surface insulates both human beings and application developers from underlying
      cybernetic plumbing while preserving exact fixed-point SI physical fidelity.
\item \textbf{An Open-Ended Constellation of Application Lenses:} Layer~6 is architecturally
      not a single application, but a platform for an open-ended \textbf{Constellation of
      Specialized Convivial Applications and Screenless Modalities}. Each application acts as a
      stateless or local-first CRDT \textbf{lens} holding \textbf{zero authoritative business
      logic}: it focuses and filters a domain-specific slice of the semantic surface, assists a
      human steward in composing a valid JSON-LD intent, presents a mandatory Deterministic
      Read-Back, signs with a personal hardware key, and dispatches the commitment through
      \texttt{col-gatewayd}.
\end{enumerate}

In this opening chapter of Part~I, we formalize the bilateral socket topology, the mathematical
projection between cybernetic reality and semantic lenses, the five-part **Application Lens
Developer Contract**, capability-scoped \texttt{did:peer} pairing, and lock-free shared-memory
telemetry isolation \parencite{beer1972,beer1974,miller2006robust}.

\section{Strict Seven-Layer Adjacency for Layer 6 (\texttt{col-commonsd} and \texttt{col-gatewayd})}
\label{sec:ch1-l6-adjacency}

Within the seven-layer linear topology of a Sovereign Node, Layer~6 occupies level $k = 6$,
situated immediately above Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}, Polycentric Governance and Web of Trust)
and immediately below Layer~7 (\texttt{col-adversaryd}, Legal, Fiscal, and Adversarial Carapace
on isolated DMZ VLAN~40). Under the Sovereign Stack's \textbf{Law of Strict Adjacency}, a daemon
at Layer~$k$ may exchange mutating IPC frames solely with Layer~$k-1$ and Layer~$k+1$.

Layer~6 splits its runtime responsibilities across two privilege-separated POSIX processes:
\texttt{col-gatewayd}, which terminates TLS~1.3 application-lens sessions on local civic
VLAN~30 (\texttt{:8443}), and \texttt{col-commonsd}, which maintains the ontological surface,
validates W3C JSON-LD semantic intents against SHACL shapes, evaluates Ricardian treaty digests,
clears bioregional Agora double-auctions, and drives the two adjacent Unix-domain
\texttt{SOCK\_SEQPACKET} membranes:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Inward / Downward Adjacency (\texttt{/run/collective/ipc/l5\_l6.sock}):}
      Point-to-point record socket connecting \texttt{col-commonsd} exclusively to Layer~5
      (\texttt{col-kmsd}). Downward across this membrane travel human-signed JSON-LD intent
      envelopes, assembly ballots, revocable liquid delegations, capability-scoped Commons Pool
      task pulls, personal rest and care-leave shields (\texttt{RestIntent}), and co-signed
      Ricardian \texttt{Treaty\_CID} ratifications. Upward from \texttt{col-kmsd} arrive
      ratified governance state transitions, identity-blinded task offers, upward-yield
      escalation notices requiring human review, deliberative rationale records, and
      audience-scoped community alerts.
\item \textbf{Outward / Upward Adjacency (\texttt{/run/collective/ipc/l6\_l7.sock}):}
      Point-to-point record socket crossing the internal host DMZ boundary to Layer~7
      (\texttt{col-adversaryd}, pinned to VLAN~40). Outward across this membrane travel strictly
      data-minimized legacy execution directives---Group Purchasing Organization (\texttt{GPO})
      procurement orders, cottage-food surplus settlement notices, municipal utility intertie

\subsection{The Read-Only Diagnostic Hood and HTTP \texttt{405} Enforcement}

While Layer~6 is forbidden from mutating lower layers directly, human stewards and specialized
Application Lenses need transparent, calm observability into the underlying cybernetic state:
whether an automated drip-irrigation valve in Layer~2 is open, whether Layer~3 mutual-credit
channels are balanced, or where a BPMN~2.0 token in Layer~4 is currently waiting on a boundary
timer. To provide this observability without introducing mutating backdoors,
\texttt{col-gatewayd} maps lock-free Single-Producer Single-Consumer (\texttt{SPSC})
shared-memory telemetry rings in strictly read-only consumer mode and exposes diagnostic
\texttt{GET} projections under \texttt{/api/v1/l1/*} through \texttt{/api/v1/l7/*}.

In \texttt{GatewayServer::HandleRequest()} (\texttt{src/daemons/col-gatewayd/gateway\_server.hpp}),
any attempt by an application lens, script, or compromised browser tab to issue a mutating HTTP
verb (\texttt{POST}, \texttt{PUT}, \texttt{PATCH}, or \texttt{DELETE}) against a lower-layer
namespace is intercepted by \texttt{IsDiagnosticLayerPath()} and rejected with an explicit
\texttt{HTTP 405 Method Not Allowed} response and \texttt{Allow: GET, OPTIONS} header:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\forall m \in \{\texttt{POST}, \texttt{PUT}, \texttt{PATCH}, \texttt{DELETE}\}, \quad
\forall k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7\}: \\
\mathrm{Gateway}(m, \texttt{/api/v1/l}k\texttt{/*}) \longrightarrow \texttt{HTTP 405}
\; (\texttt{L4\_IS\_STRICTLY\_READ\_ONLY\_NO\_MUTATIONS})
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Crucially, even \texttt{/api/v1/l5/*} rejects direct HTTP mutations with
\texttt{L5\_GOVERNANCE\_IS\_READ\_ONLY}. A steward ratifying a pending task gate or participating
in a 2-of-3 FROST vouch ceremony (\texttt{SCN-TAU-GENESIS}) never pokes raw RPC state into
\texttt{col-kmsd}'s diagnostic hood. Every human decision enters exclusively as a canonical,
human-signed JSON-LD semantic intent at Layer~6, where \texttt{col-commonsd} verifies schema
conformance and deterministic read-back provenance before forwarding the signed frame downward
across \texttt{l5\_l6.sock}.

\subsection{Layer 6 as the Apex of the Constitutional Intent U-Turn}

This structural asymmetry positions Layer~6 at the deliberative apex of the
\textbf{Constitutional Intent U-Turn} \parencite{beer1979}:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Downward Authorship Trajectory ($\mathrm{Human} \to \mathrm{Lens} \to \mathrm{L6} \to \mathrm{L5} \to \mathrm{L4}$):}
      A steward articulates a physical or civic objective inside an Application Lens, inspects
      the deterministic plain-language read-back of its exergy, ecological, and labor
      consequences, and signs the canonical digest with a hardware-bound \texttt{Ed25519} key.
      Layer~6 transmits the signed intent across \texttt{l5\_l6.sock} to \texttt{col-kmsd}; only
      after Layer~5 verifies Web-of-Trust credentials, ecological floor headroom, and governance
      quorum does \texttt{col-kmsd} instruct \texttt{col-execd} across \texttt{l4\_l5.sock} to
      instantiate or advance the corresponding BPMN~2.0 workflow.
\item \textbf{Upward Escalation and Camouflaged Outward Trajectory ($\mathrm{L4} \to \mathrm{L5} \to \mathrm{L6} \to \mathrm{L7}$):}
      When a deterministic workflow in \texttt{col-execd} encounters a human verification gate,
      a local-and-mesh inventory cache miss, or an unresolvable resource contention, it emits a
      456-byte \texttt{UpwardYieldPacket} upward to Layer~5. \texttt{col-kmsd} filters the event
      against active policy and projects human-actionable holds across \texttt{l5\_l6.sock} into
      Layer~6's ontological surface. If and only if a steward co-signs an external procurement
      fallback or surplus bill-offset directive does \texttt{col-commonsd} compile a camouflaged
      leaf instruction across \texttt{l6\_l7.sock} to \texttt{col-adversaryd}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Layer 6 as the Wide-Purpose Semantic Surface Over Cybernetic Reality}
\label{sec:ch1-semantic-surface}

Why must Layer~6 exist as a dedicated ontological daemon (\texttt{col-commonsd}) rather than
letting user interfaces query Layer~3 storage or Layer~4 workflow tables directly? Because the
data structures required for deterministic real-time cybernetics are fundamentally different
from the conceptual structures required for human understanding, pluralistic deliberation, and
open-ended application development \parencite{gruber1993ontology,winograd1986understanding}.

\subsection{The Semantic Surface Lifting Operator ($\Pi_{\mathrm{up}}$) and Lens Projection ($\mathcal{L}_a$)}

Consider what exists underneath Layer~6 at any instant $t$:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{cyber}}(t) = \bigl\langle \mathbf{T}_{\mathrm{L2}}(t),\; \mathbf{L}_{\mathrm{L3}}(t),\; \mathbf{W}_{\mathrm{L4}}(t),\; \mathbf{G}_{\mathrm{L5}}(t) \bigr\rangle
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{T}_{\mathrm{L2}}(t)$ is the vector of fixed-point transducer samples in the SPSC
telemetry ring, $\mathbf{L}_{\mathrm{L3}}(t)$ is the Merkle-CRDT inventory and pairwise
mutual-credit ledger, $\mathbf{W}_{\mathrm{L4}}(t)$ is the active table of Wasmtime BPMN~2.0
token positions and boundary timers, and $\mathbf{G}_{\mathrm{L5}}(t)$ is the set of ratified
governance policies, quorums, and blinded task claim handles.

If an application developer had to parse $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{cyber}}(t)$ directly, every user
interface would be tightly coupled to C++ memory layouts, numeric sensor pin IDs, and internal
workflow step indices. Adding a new soil sensor or refactoring a BPMN subprocess would break
every client screen in the community.

Instead, \texttt{col-commonsd} maintains the **Wide-Purpose Semantic Surface**
$\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{surface}}(t)$ via a continuous, deterministic lifting morphism
$\Pi_{\mathrm{up}}$:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{surface}}(t) = \Pi_{\mathrm{up}}\!\bigl(\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{cyber}}(t),\; \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{catalogs}}\bigr)
    \label{eq:semantic-lifting}
\end{equation}
Joining live lower-layer states $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{cyber}}(t)$ against the node's ratified
JSON-LD ontology catalogs $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{catalogs}}$ (\texttt{core/6\_intent/needs/},
\texttt{cultivars/}, \texttt{blueprints/}, and \texttt{ontologies/}), $\Pi_{\mathrm{up}}$
exposes a unified W3C RDF/JSON-LD dataset where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item raw sensor pin \texttt{pin\_0x14 = 347200} becomes the semantic triple
          \texttt{(hearth:cauldron\_01, col:coreTempMillikelvin, 347200)} linked to its active
          \texttt{need:pea\_soup} pasteurization threshold ($\ge 347{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$);
    \item raw workflow token \texttt{step\_idx = 3} becomes a self-describing
          \texttt{col:ActionableStep} linked to required safety equipment, stoichiometric
          \texttt{recipe\_bom} inputs, and spatial zone coordinates; and
    \item every assertion carries an unforgeable epistemic provenance attribute distinguishing
          hardware-attested physical reality (\texttt{QUALITY\_HARDWARE\_ROOT = 0x02}) from
          sandbox rehearsal or simulation (\texttt{QUALITY\_SIMULATED = 0x01}), ensuring
          rehearsal frames can never contaminate physical mutual-credit clearing or statutory
          audit logs.
\end{itemize}

Atop this wide ontological surface $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{surface}}(t)$, each application $a$ in
the Constellation operates as a pure **Semantic Lens** $\mathcal{L}_a$:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{V}_a(t) = \mathcal{L}_a\!\bigl(\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{surface}}(t)\bigr) = \mathrm{Frame}_{F_a}\!\Bigl(\sigma_{P_a}\bigl(\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{surface}}(t)\bigr)\Bigr)
    \label{eq:lens-projection}
\end{equation}
where $\sigma_{P_a}$ restricts the RDF dataset to the predicates and Trust Ring scopes authorized
by the lens's \texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1}, and $\mathrm{Frame}_{F_a}$ applies a declarative
W3C JSON-LD Frame $F_a$ \parencite{w3c2020jsonld} that shapes the flat triple graph into the
exact hierarchical view tree $\mathcal{V}_a(t)$ needed by that application's user interface.

developers from the trap of building one giant application. During initial node bring-up or

\subsection{The Five-Part Layer~6 Application Lens Developer Contract}
\label{subsec:ch1-developer-contract}

How does a developer build a new Application Lens---say, a *Beekeeping Apiary Fieldbook* or a
*Ceramic Kiln Firing Monitor*---so that it plugs seamlessly into any Collective node? Every
Layer~6 application implements a concise, language-agnostic **Five-Part Lens Developer Contract**:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Declare an \texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1}:} Ship a static, content-addressed
          JSON-LD manifest declaring the lens's human title, required RBAC bitmask
          (Table~\ref{tab:ch1-rbac-scopes}), subscribed semantic topic prefixes, and memory
          budget ($\le 512\,\mathrm{MiB}$ within the 20-Year Salvaged Hardware Mandate).
    \item \textbf{Pair via VLAN~30 TLS~1.3 \texttt{\#pair\_token} to Mint a Scoped \texttt{did:peer}:}
          Discover \texttt{\_collective-l6.\_tcp.local} on VLAN~30 (\texttt{:8443}), complete
          the URI-fragment QR pairing ceremony (never transmitting the bootstrap secret in HTTP
          headers or logs), and generate a non-exportable Web Crypto (\texttt{extractable: false})
          or \texttt{ATECC608A} \texttt{Ed25519} keypair bound strictly to that lens's manifest.
    \item \textbf{Project State via JSON-LD Framing and Topic-Scoped Delta Streams:}
          Open a persistent subscription to \texttt{/ws/v1/subscribe?topics=...} with an initial
          JSON-LD \texttt{@frame} specification. Render the initial framed graph $\mathcal{V}_a(t_0)$
          and apply incoming RFC~6902 JSON-Patch / CBOR deltas keyed by monotonic Hybrid Logical
          Clock (\texttt{HLC}) cursors---never polling full node snapshots in a timer loop.
    \item \textbf{Generate Input Controls Declaratively from \texttt{SHACL} Shapes:}
          Rather than hardcoding validation rules in client JavaScript, query the governed
          \texttt{sh:NodeShape} for any target intent class in \texttt{core/6\_intent/}. Bind UI
          sliders, selectors, and integer inputs directly to \texttt{sh:minInclusive},
          \texttt{sh:maxInclusive}, and QUDT fixed-point unit labels (\texttt{uJ}, \texttt{mK},
          \texttt{bp}, \texttt{mg}, \texttt{mL}).
    \item \textbf{Commit Intents via Two-Phase Deterministic Read-Back and Domain~B CRDT Outbox:}
          Submit draft JSON-LD intents to \texttt{POST /api/v1/intent/compile} to receive the
          canonical \texttt{Intent\_CID} and deterministic plain-language read-back prose. Only
          after the human steward completes a $500\,\mathrm{ms}$ confirmation gesture on the
          read-back card does the personal hardware key sign the commitment for
          \texttt{POST /api/v1/intent/commit} (or queue it inside the local IndexedDB
          **Domain~B Automerge CRDT Outbox** when operating offline).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{\texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1} and Least-Privilege Scoped \texttt{did:peer} RBAC}

If a mounted Shop-Floor BOM Lens in a shared neighborhood woodshop held the same authority as a
steward's personal Agora Deliberation Lens, anyone walking past the lathe could cast
constitutional ballots or inspect Layer~7 tax reserves. Conversely, if lenses relied on ambient
browser cookies, cross-site request forgery or a bug in an experimental visualization plugin
could silently escalate privileges \parencite{miller2006robust}.

Layer~6 enforces fine-grained object-capability containment through
\texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1} paired with ephemeral, hardware-bound \texttt{did:peer} session

orthogonal Layer~6 RBAC scopes detailed in Table~\ref{tab:ch1-rbac-scopes}.

\subsection{VLAN 30 TLS 1.3 (\texttt{:8443}) and \texttt{\#pair\_token} URI-Fragment QR Pairing}

To pair a new Application Lens without cloud identity providers, certificate-authority lock-in,
or password databases, \texttt{col-gatewayd} binds its listener strictly to local Civic
VLAN~30 (\texttt{:8443}) and advertises \texttt{\_collective-l6.\_tcp.local} via mDNS alongside
its SPKI SHA-256 certificate fingerprint. A steward displays a 60-second, single-use 256-bit
pairing QR code on an already-authenticated screen or E-Ink node faceplate:
\begin{center}
\small\texttt{https://node01.collective.local:8443/lens/\#pair\_token=<base64url\_256b>\&spki=<sha256>}
\end{center}
Placing \texttt{pair\_token} after the \texttt{\#} URI fragment delimiter exploits RFC~3986:
user agents strip the fragment locally and \textbf{never transmit it over the wire} in HTTP
request lines, \texttt{Referer} headers, or access logs. The client reads
\texttt{window.location.hash}, scrubs the URL bar via \texttt{history.replaceState()}, generates
a fresh \texttt{extractable: false} \texttt{Ed25519} keypair in IndexedDB, and completes an
HMAC-SHA256 challenge-response binding its new \texttt{did:peer} key and
\texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1} CID over TLS~1.3.

\subsection{1:1 Lock-Free SPSC Shared Memory Rings (\texttt{/uhai\_telemetry\_gateway})}

If twenty Application Lenses subscribe to live semantic deltas over courtyard Wi-Fi, TCP socket
backpressure in \texttt{col-gatewayd} must never stall \texttt{col-commonsd}'s 10~Hz Agora tick
or \texttt{col-peripheral}'s deterministic sensor loop. As implemented in
\texttt{uhai\_ring\_buffer.hpp} and \texttt{GatewayServer::PollRingBuffers()}, the stack
decouples gateway readers via dedicated 1:1 Lock-Free Single-Producer Single-Consumer
(\texttt{SPSC}) POSIX shared-memory segments (\texttt{"/uhai\_telemetry\_gateway"}):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{False-Sharing Elimination Across Three 64-Byte Cache Lines:} The 192-byte
      \texttt{SharedRingHeader} isolates the producer's \texttt{write\_index} and atomic
      \texttt{dropped\_frames} counter in Cache Line~0 (\texttt{0..63}), the consumer's
      \texttt{read\_index} in Cache Line~1 (\texttt{64..127}), and immutable ring geometry
      (\texttt{RING\_CAPACITY = 1024}, \texttt{UHAI\_MAGIC = 0x55484149}) in Cache Line~2
      (\texttt{128..191}).
\item \textbf{Wait-Free Overrun Safety:} Combined with $1{,}024 \times 64$-byte cache-aligned
      \texttt{TelemetrySlot} entries ($65{,}728$~bytes total), any transient HTTP backpressure
      simply increments \texttt{dropped\_frames} with \texttt{memory\_order\_relaxed} in $O(1)$
      nanoseconds while \texttt{col-gatewayd} drains the freshest state on its next poll cycle.
\end{itemize}
