\chapter{The Bilateral Membrane, Separated Concerns, and the Constellation of Applications}
\label{ch:constellation-membrane}

\epigraph{A convivial society should be designed to allow all its members the most autonomous action by means of tools least controlled by others. People feel joy, as opposed to mere pleasure, to the extent that their activities are creative; while the growth of tools beyond a certain point increases regimentation, dependence, exploitation, and impotence.}{---Adapted from \textcite{illich1973}}

Every human-facing interface encodes an implicit constitution. In conventional enterprise,
civic, and smart-home software, that constitution is overwhelmingly centralizing: a single
monolithic web portal or mobile ``super-app'' accumulates presentation logic, business rules,
session credentials, behavioral analytics, direct database write paths, and hardware actuator
overrides behind one administrative surface. What begins during early prototyping as developer
convenience hardens rapidly into both a security hazard and a political hierarchy. A single
compromised browser context, coerced administrator credential, or misconfigured API route
acquires simultaneous visibility into intimate household routines and unmediated authority over
physical infrastructure \parencite{saltzer1984end,rose2020zero,zuboff2019}. Worse still, when a
community's digital life is funneled through one proprietary dashboard, citizens cease to be
authors of their own tools and become passive operators of screens designed elsewhere
\parencite{illich1973,costanzachock2020design}.

Layer~6 of the Sovereign Stack---\textbf{The Semantic Commonwealth}, realized in POSIX user
space by the semantic engine \texttt{col-commonsd} and the local civic boundary mediator
\texttt{col-gatewayd}---inverts this architecture along three orthogonal axes:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Strict Bilateral Socket Containment:} Layer~6 possesses zero direct communication
      paths to physical hardware (Layers~1--2), thermodynamic ledgers (Layer~3), or the
      deterministic workflow virtual machine (Layer~4). Its entire capacity to affect or
      ingest authoritative state is bounded by two kernel-authenticated \texttt{AF\_UNIX}
      \texttt{SOCK\_SEQPACKET} membranes: inward across \texttt{l5\_l6.sock} to polycentric
      governance (\texttt{col-kmsd}) and outward across \texttt{l6\_l7.sock} to the legacy
      adversarial carapace (\texttt{col-adversaryd}).
\item \textbf{Clean Separation of Real Production Software from Simulation (\texttt{Layer 1b}):}
      Layer~6 is never conflated with the Oasis 3D digital twin (\texttt{Layer 1b}). Oasis is
      a zero-key, zero-socket spatial rehearsal engine whose synthetic frames carry the
      epistemic watermark \texttt{QUALITY\_SIMULATED = 0x01}. All human intent authoring,
      governance deliberation, workflow review, and mutual-credit clearing reside in dedicated
      Layer~6 Companion Applications engineered to drive genuine physical settlements.
\item \textbf{Unfolding from One Starter App to a Constellation of Convivial Applications:}
      Although a newly bootstrapped homestead on Lot~402 begins with a single four-tab
      Companion Application to minimize initial cognitive friction, Layer~6 is architecturally
      not a monolithic dashboard. As a settlement matures, Layer~6 unfolds into an open
      \textbf{Constellation of Specialized Convivial Applications and Screenless Modalities}---all
      acting as zero-business-logic ``Intent Builders'' over a uniform semantic and
      cryptographic foundation.
\end{enumerate}

In this opening chapter of Part~I, we formalize the socket topology, epistemic separation,
capability-scoped RBAC model, local-mesh pairing protocol, and lock-free memory isolation that
make this Semantic Commonwealth viable under real civic and adversarial pressure
\parencite{beer1972,beer1974,miller2006robust}.

\section{Strict Seven-Layer Adjacency for Layer 6 (\texttt{col-commonsd} and \texttt{col-gatewayd})}

Within the seven-layer linear topology of a Sovereign Node, Layer~6 occupies level $k = 6$,
situated immediately above Layer~5 (\texttt{col-kmsd}, Polycentric Governance and Web of

Carapace on isolated DMZ VLAN~40). Under the Sovereign Stack's \textbf{Law of Strict
Adjacency}, a daemon at Layer~$k$ may exchange mutating IPC frames solely with Layer~$k-1$ and
Layer~$k+1$.

Layer~6 splits its runtime responsibilities across two tightly coupled, privilege-separated
POSIX processes: \texttt{col-gatewayd}, which terminates TLS~1.3 civic device sessions on
VLAN~30 (\texttt{:8443}), and \texttt{col-commonsd}, which validates W3C JSON-LD semantic intents,
evaluates Ricardian treaty digests, clears bioregional Agora double-auctions, and drives the
two adjacent Unix-domain \texttt{SOCK\_SEQPACKET} membranes:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Inward / Downward Adjacency (\texttt{/run/collective/ipc/l5\_l6.sock}):}
      Point-to-point record socket connecting \texttt{col-commonsd} exclusively to Layer~5

\item \textbf{Outward / Upward Adjacency (\texttt{/run/collective/ipc/l6\_l7.sock}):}

\subsection{The Read-Only Diagnostic Hood and HTTP \texttt{405} Enforcement}

While Layer~6 is forbidden from mutating lower layers directly, human Stewards need transparent,
calm visibility into the health of their settlement: whether an automated drip-irrigation valve
in Layer~2 is open, whether Layer~3 mutual-credit channels are balanced, or where a BPMN~2.0
token in Layer~4 is currently waiting on a timer. To provide this observability without
introducing mutating backdoors, \texttt{col-gatewayd} maps lock-free Single-Producer
Single-Consumer (SPSC) shared-memory telemetry rings in strictly read-only consumer mode and
exposes diagnostic \texttt{GET} projections under \texttt{/api/v1/l1/*} through
\texttt{/api/v1/l7/*}.

In \texttt{GatewayServer::HandleRequest()} (\texttt{src/daemons/col-gatewayd/gateway\_server.hpp}),
any attempt by a client application, script, or compromised browser tab to issue a mutating
HTTP verb (\texttt{POST}, \texttt{PUT}, \texttt{PATCH}, or \texttt{DELETE}) against a
diagnostic layer namespace is intercepted by \texttt{IsDiagnosticLayerPath()} and rejected
with an explicit \texttt{HTTP 405 Method Not Allowed} response and \texttt{Allow: GET, OPTIONS}
header:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\forall m \in \{\texttt{POST}, \texttt{PUT}, \texttt{PATCH}, \texttt{DELETE}\}, \quad
\forall k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7\}: \\
\mathrm{Gateway}(m, \texttt{/api/v1/l}k\texttt{/*}) \longrightarrow \texttt{HTTP 405}
\; (\texttt{L4\_IS\_STRICTLY\_READ\_ONLY\_NO\_MUTATIONS})
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Crucially, notice that even \texttt{/api/v1/l5/*} rejects direct HTTP mutations with
\texttt{L5\_GOVERNANCE\_IS\_READ\_ONLY}. A Steward ratifying a pending task gate or participating
in a 2-of-3 FROST Steward vouch ceremony (\texttt{SCN-TAU-GENESIS}) never pokes raw RPC state
into \texttt{col-kmsd}'s diagnostic hood. Every human decision enters exclusively as a
canonical, human-signed JSON-LD semantic intent at \texttt{POST /api/v1/l6/intent}, where
Layer~6 verifies schema conformance and deterministic read-back provenance before forwarding the
signed frame downward across \texttt{l5\_l6.sock}.

\subsection{Layer 6 as the Apex of the Constitutional Intent U-Turn}

This structural asymmetry positions Layer~6 at the deliberative apex of the
\textbf{Constitutional Intent U-Turn} \parencite{beer1979}:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Downward Authorship Trajectory ($\mathrm{Steward} \to \mathrm{L6} \to \mathrm{L5} \to \mathrm{L4}$):}
      A Steward articulates a physical or civic objective inside a Layer~6 application,
      inspects the deterministic plain-language read-back of its exergy, ecological, and labor
      consequences, and signs the canonical digest with a hardware-bound \texttt{Ed25519} key.
      Layer~6 transmits the signed intent across \texttt{l5\_l6.sock} to \texttt{col-kmsd};
      only after Layer~5 verifies Web-of-Trust credentials, ecological floor headroom, and
      governance quorum does \texttt{col-kmsd} instruct \texttt{col-execd} across
      \texttt{l4\_l5.sock} to instantiate or advance the corresponding BPMN~2.0 workflow.
\item \textbf{Upward Escalation and Camouflaged Outward Trajectory ($\mathrm{L4} \to \mathrm{L5} \to \mathrm{L6} \to \mathrm{L7}$):}
      When a deterministic workflow in \texttt{col-execd} encounters a human verification gate,
      a local-and-mesh inventory cache miss, or an unresolvable resource contention, it emits a
      456-byte \texttt{UpwardYieldPacket} upward to Layer~5. \texttt{col-kmsd} filters the
      event against active policy and projects human-actionable holds across
      \texttt{l5\_l6.sock} into Layer~6. If and only if a Steward co-signs an external
      procurement fallback or surplus bill-offset directive does \texttt{col-commonsd} compile
      a camouflaged leaf instruction across \texttt{l6\_l7.sock} to \texttt{col-adversaryd}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Separating Layer 6 from Oasis (\texttt{Layer 1b})}
\label{sec:ch1-oasis-separation}

In early architectural drafts prior to the v3.1 ratification, casual shorthand occasionally
referred to Layer~6 as ``the Oasis interface.'' That conflation is permanently retired. A
viable cybernetic architecture must draw an uncompromising boundary between \textit{a virtual
world used to rehearse physical dynamics} and \textit{the civic instruments used by living
people to govern real land, food, energy, and mutual obligations}
\parencite{grieves2014digital,girard2013}.

\subsection{Why Oasis (\texttt{Layer 1b}) Holds Zero Keys and Zero Civic Authority}

Oasis (\texttt{Layer 1b}) is an outward-producing 3D WebGPU spatial simulation and
Software-in-the-Loop (SITL) proving ground. It models solar irradiance across rooftop
monocrystalline arrays, evapotranspiration in backyard keyline beds, thermal mass decay in
insulated compost drums, and avatar ergonomics on a simulated parcel such as Lot~402. To serve
safely as an experimental sandbox---where learners, architects, and adversarial red-teams can
fast-forward droughts, breaker trips, and demographic shocks at $100\times$ real time---Oasis
is structurally stripped of civic agency:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Zero Cryptographic Signing Keys:} The 3D simulation engine holds no personal
      \texttt{Ed25519} keys, no \texttt{ATECC608A} hardware enclave handles
      \parencite{microchip_atecc608a}, and no BBS+ selective-disclosure credentials
      \parencite{camenisch2004signature}. An avatar clicking a trellis in the 3D viewport
      cannot sign a binding Ricardian treaty or authorize an external GPO expenditure.
\item \textbf{Zero External Network Sockets:} Under WebAssembly and native sandbox builds alike,
      the simulation loop opens zero WAN sockets and speaks to the Sovereign Stack exclusively
      through bounded virtual pin bridges and lock-free UHAI ring buffers.
\item \textbf{Unforgeable Epistemic Provenance (\texttt{QUALITY\_SIMULATED = 0x01}):} Every
      telemetry frame emitted by Oasis carries \texttt{QualityFlags::QUALITY\_SIMULATED = 0x01}
      in \texttt{TelemetrySample::quality\_flags} (\texttt{core/1\_simulation/src/uhai/uhai\_ring\_buffer.hpp}),
      strictly segregated from physical transducer frames attested by hardware secure elements
      (\texttt{QUALITY\_HARDWARE\_ROOT = 0x02}). Layer~3 and Layer~5 reject any attempt to mix
      simulated \texttt{0x01} frames into production mutual-credit clearing or statutory compliance logs.
\end{itemize}

Separating simulation from governance also prevents a subtle sociological pathology identified
by \textcite{kucklich2005}: the gamification of civic labor into ``playbour,'' where playful
scoreboards blur seamlessly into coercive productivity tracking. In the Sovereign Stack, a 3D
twin is a pedagogical telescope and engineering wind tunnel---never a gamified boss.

\subsection{The Companion Application as Real Production Settlement Software}

To honor this separation in running code, all Layer~6 human-governance, semantic need
authoring, policy adaptation, and workflow inspection interactions live outside the 3D engine
in an independent \textbf{Layer~6 Commons Companion Application}
(\texttt{core/1\_simulation/src/web/companion/index.html}, paired with
\texttt{daemon\_client.js} and \texttt{companion\_l6.js}).

Two architectural properties of \texttt{companion\_l6.js} warrant particular emphasis:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Identical Execution Against Live POSIX Daemons:} Whether opened in a standalone
      mobile or tablet browser window (\texttt{body.l6-standalone-body}) or mounted inside a
      slide-out drawer alongside the Oasis viewport during SITL rehearsal, the exact same
      zero-dependency JavaScript module communicates exclusively over HTTP/JSON-RPC with live
      native POSIX daemons via \texttt{col-gatewayd}. When a Steward defines a new semantic
      need, selects an heirloom pea cultivar from \texttt{fabaceae\_cultivars.jsonld}, or
      ratifies a Layer~5 asset sourcing transition to \texttt{R0\_LOCAL\_WORKFLOWS}, the
      mutation executes inside the real C++20 Sovereign Stack runtime---not inside a mock game
      loop.
\item \textbf{Refusal to Synthesize Theatrical Telemetry:} Conventional prototypes mask missing
      backends with hardcoded demo numbers. By contrast, \texttt{companion\_l6.js} enforces a
      strict ``zero fake telemetry'' discipline: if the six native POSIX daemons are offline,
      \texttt{renderSnapshot()} refuses to fabricate balances, needs, or BPMN tokens, surfacing
      an explicit offline diagnostic state until \texttt{col-gatewayd} responds. Consequently,
      when a physical homestead or housing cooperative deploys Raspberry~Pi or x86 Sovereign
      Nodes wired to real \texttt{PT100} temperature probes, soil moisture sensors, and
      \texttt{LiFePO4} inverters, the Companion Application transitions directly into live
      production stewardship without rewriting a line of client code.
\end{enumerate}

\section{From One Starter App to a Constellation of Convivial Applications}
\label{sec:ch1-constellation}

During Phase~1 bootstrap on Lot~402---where a solo Steward or four-node household cluster is
taking its very first steps from legacy retail dependence toward backyard agroecology and solar
self-provisioning---presenting one cohesive four-tab Companion Application
(\textit{Needs \& Requests}, \textit{L5 Decisions}, \textit{L4 Workflows}, and
\textit{Cluster, L3 \& Bills}) is an intentional act of variety attenuation
\parencite{ashby1956}. A newcomer learning how a semantic need triggers a Layer~5 governance
proposal and instantiates a Layer~4 BPMN workflow benefits from seeing the entire causal arc
within four calm tabs.

Yet what serves a solo bootstrapper at $38$ workflows becomes an anti-convivial bottleneck in a
mature neighborhood or bioregional commonwealth running $500$ to $2{,}600+$ concurrent
workflows across twenty-five life domains. Stuffing culinary Hazard Analysis, perinatal care
check-ins, woodshop CNC lockouts, cargo-trike delay-tolerant routing, deliberative assembly
ballots, Ricardian legal drafting, and emergency medical triage into one ``everything app''
violates cognitive ergonomics \parencite{weiser1996calm}, erodes least-privilege security
\parencite{miller2006robust}, and mismatches physical work environments where hands are wet,
gloved, flour-dusted, or operating in bright field sunlight \parencite{ishii1997tangible,suchman1987plans}.

Layer~6 is therefore architected not as an application, but as an open \textbf{Semantic Commonwealth}
supporting a \textbf{Constellation of Specialized Convivial Applications}. Every application in the
Constellation is a stateless or local-first CRDT view \parencite{kleppmann2019localfirst}
holding \textbf{zero authoritative business logic}: its sole job is to render domain-appropriate
affordances, help a human Steward compose a valid JSON-LD semantic intent, present a
deterministic read-back, sign with a scoped personal key, and submit the signed envelope to
\texttt{col-gatewayd} \parencite{winograd1986understanding,hutchins1995cognition}.

\subsection{\texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1} and Least-Privilege Scoped \texttt{did:peer} RBAC}

If a mounted Shop-Floor BOM Terminal in a shared neighborhood woodshop held the same authority
as a Steward's personal Agora Assembly Reader, anyone walking past the lathe could cast
constitutional ballots or inspect Layer~7 tax reserves. Conversely, if applications relied on
ambient browser cookies, cross-site request forgery or local XSS in an experimental visualization
plugin could silently escalate privileges \parencite{miller2006robust}.

\texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1} paired with ephemeral, hardware-bound \texttt{did:peer}

content-addressed JSON-LD capability manifest declaring its exact required subset of eight

Table~\ref{tab:ch1-rbac-scopes}.

\subsection{VLAN 30 TLS 1.3 (\texttt{:8443}) and \texttt{\#pair\_token} URL-Fragment QR Pairing}

How does a wipeable Hearth Slate or an e-paper Fieldbook pair with a local Sovereign Node when
the public internet is unreachable or untrusted? Layer~6 eliminates cloud identity providers,
certificate-authority lock-in, and password databases through \textbf{Local Civic VLAN~30 Pairing}:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{mDNS Zero-Configuration Discovery on VLAN~30:} \texttt{col-gatewayd} binds its
      production listener strictly to the local Sovereign Civic Boundary (VLAN~30, port
      \texttt{8443}, enforced by \texttt{GatewaySecurityConfig}) and advertises
      \texttt{\_collective-l6.\_tcp.local} via multicast DNS alongside the SPKI SHA-256
      fingerprint of the node's local TLS~1.3 certificate. (During single-machine developer
      SITL rehearsal, \texttt{RunSocketServer()} optionally binds a loopback diagnostic port on
      \texttt{127.0.0.1:8090}.)
\item \textbf{RFC 3986 URI-Fragment \texttt{\#pair\_token} Secrecy:} To bind a new Constellation
      client, a Steward displays a short-lived (60-second), single-use 256-bit pairing QR code
      on an already-authenticated screen or E-Ink node faceplate formatted as:
      \begin{center}
      \small\texttt{https://node01.collective.local:8443/companion/\#pair\_token=<base64url\_256b>\&spki=<sha256>}
      \end{center}
      Placing \texttt{pair\_token} after the \texttt{\#} URI fragment delimiter rather than in a
      \texttt{?pair\_token=} query string exploits a foundational RFC~3986 browser invariant:
      user agents strip the fragment identifier locally and \textbf{never transmit it over the wire}
      in HTTP \texttt{GET} request lines, \texttt{Referer} headers, or server access logs.
\item \textbf{Challenge-Response DID Binding:} Inside the client's local execution context, the
      pairing script reads \texttt{window.location.hash}, immediately scrubs the URL bar via
      \texttt{history.replaceState()}, generates a fresh \texttt{extractable: false}
      \texttt{Ed25519} keypair in IndexedDB, computes an HMAC-SHA256 proof binding its new
      \texttt{did:peer} public key and \texttt{AppCapabilityManifestV1} CID to the one-time
      \texttt{pair\_token}, and completes mutual key confirmation over TLS~1.3. The raw
      bootstrap secret never crosses the network in cleartext or URL metadata.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{1:1 Lock-Free SPSC Shared Memory Rings (\texttt{/uhai\_telemetry\_gateway})}

A subtle availability hazard arises whenever human-facing web gateways share memory with
real-time control or clearing daemons: if twenty Constellation tablets subscribe to live node
snapshots over lossy courtyard Wi-Fi, TCP socket backpressure or JSON serialization stalls in
\texttt{col-gatewayd} must never block \texttt{col-commonsd}'s 10~Hz Agora tick or
\texttt{col-peripheral}'s deterministic sensor loop.

As implemented in \texttt{core/1\_simulation/src/uhai/uhai\_ring\_buffer.hpp} and
\texttt{GatewayServer::PollRingBuffers()}, the Sovereign Stack decouples gateway readers using
dedicated 1:1 Lock-Free Single-Producer Single-Consumer (SPSC) POSIX shared-memory segments,
anchored by \texttt{DEFAULT\_GATEWAY\_SHM\_NAME} (\texttt{"/uhai\_telemetry\_gateway"}):

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{False-Sharing Elimination Across Three 64-Byte Cache Lines:} The 192-byte
      \texttt{SharedRingHeader} places the producer's \texttt{write\_index} and atomic
      \texttt{dropped\_frames} counter in Cache Line~0 (offsets \texttt{0..63}), the consumer's
      \texttt{read\_index} in Cache Line~1 (offsets \texttt{64..127}), and immutable ring
      geometry (\texttt{RING\_CAPACITY = 1024}, \texttt{UHAI\_MAGIC = 0x55484149}) in Cache
      Line~2 (offsets \texttt{128..191}). Producer and consumer cores never contend on the same
      L1/L2 cache line.
\item \textbf{Wait-Free Overrun Safety:} Combined with $1{,}024 \times 64$-byte cache-aligned
      \texttt{TelemetrySlot} entries, the entire segment occupies a fixed, zero-allocation
      footprint of exactly $65{,}728$~bytes. If \texttt{col-gatewayd} experiences transient HTTP
      load, the producer increments \texttt{dropped\_frames} with \texttt{memory\_order\_relaxed}
      in $O(1)$ nanoseconds and continues uninterrupted, while \texttt{col-gatewayd} drains the
      freshest state on its next 50~ms \texttt{poll()} cycle.
\end{itemize}

\textit{maximal plurality of tools at the human edge, backed by unyielding constitutional
invariants at the core}.

Yet a pressing technical question immediately follows. Once dozens of independently designed
applications---a Hearth Slate, a Cultivar Fieldbook, a Treaty Studio, a paper ballot scanner,
and a POSIX shell script---coexist without a central monolithic backend dictating ad-hoc JSON
views, how do they agree on the exact meaning, physical units, and cryptographic hash of a
shared human need or facility blueprint? Answering that question is the task of
Chapter~\ref{ch:semantic-foundation}.
