\chapter{Wave D: Agroforestry, Five-Stream Biorefinery, Technospheric Foundry, Fiber Shed, and Open Building (\texttt{Eta}, \texttt{Mu}, \texttt{Theta}, \texttt{Nu}, \texttt{Xi})}
\label{ch:wave-d}

\epigraph{The economic process is entropic in all its material fibers\ldots{} Ecological sanity begins when human institutions treat low-entropy mineral stocks and living soils as finite funds to be cycled with reverence rather than mines and sinks to be exhausted.}{---Adapted from \textcite{georgescu1971}, \textit{The Entropy Law and the Economic Process}}

Waves~A through~C liberate a community's digital tools, daily meals, workshop repairs, care pods, and housing leases. Yet as long as timber, soil phosphorus, structural bronze/steel, footwear, and building joinery must still be imported from extractive global supply chains, physical dependency $D_t$ stalls halfway. \textbf{Horizontal Wave~D} closes the heavy material and ecological loops of the bioregion across five deeply coupled scenarios: \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Eta}} (\textit{Regenerative Agroforestry, rotational Coppice \& Botanical Apothecary}), \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Mu}} (\textit{Five-Stream Urban Unbuilding, Pyrolysis Biochar \& Nutrient Recovery}), \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Theta}} (\textit{Secondary Foundry, XRF Scrap Metallurgy \& Solar Crucible Batching}), \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Nu}} (\textit{Bioregional Fiber Shed, Zero-PFAS Textiles \& Ephemeral-RAM Footwear}), and \textbf{Scenario~\texttt{Xi}} (\textit{Two-Track Parametric Open Building \& Kigumi Barn-Raising}).

At Stage~3, Wave~D accounts for the largest share of long-horizon workflow concurrency---often \textbf{$850$ to $1{,}400$ simultaneous multi-season and multi-year Layer~4 state machines} (7-year chestnut coppice stools, 18-month solar timber kiln stacks, 72-hour biosolids holds, scrap-alloy lot pedigrees, and structural moisture cures). Layer~6 keeps stewards unhurried by holding $94\%$ of long-horizon phenological tokens in silent \texttt{DORMANT} state while projecting sharp, safety-critical Constellation tools at the exact moment of harvest, chemical assay, molten pour, or structural assembly.

\section{Scenario \texttt{Eta} (\texttt{SCN-ETA-COPPICE}): Ruggedized Agroforestry Fieldbooks and Mother-Tree Vetoes}
\label{sec:wave-d-eta}

\subsection{Offline GIS Sanctuary Polygons and Dual-Assay Apothecary Read-Back}

Scenario~\texttt{Eta} stewards silvopasture chestnuts, rotational hazel/black-locust coppice timber, mycological logs, and wild botanical medicines using two specialized Constellation surfaces:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{The Offline Domain-B Agroforestry Fieldbook (\texttt{agro:forestry}, \texttt{crdt:sync}):} Renders offline vector contour maps overlaid with \textbf{Mother-Tree Sanctuary Polygons}---ecologically sacred old-growth groves and riparian buffers carrying a hard \textbf{Ecological Protocol of Refusal} (\texttt{ERR\_MOTHER\_TREE\_SANCTUARY\_VETO}) that blocks any timber requisition regardless of workshop demand. Wild foraging intents (\texttt{ForagingQuotaIntent}) display visual gauges hard-capped at $\le 2{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($25\%$ of observed stand abundance, leaving $\ge 75\%$ for wildlife and regeneration), while rotational coppice cuts (\texttt{HeavyTimberAndCoppiceIntent}) unlock strictly during winter sap dormancy ($< 278{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ / $5.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$ multi-day root-zone average) and track solar kiln drying to $\le 1{,}200\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($12\%$ structural) or $\le 800\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($8\%$ luthiery tonewood) moisture content.
    \item \textbf{The Dual-Assay Botanical Apothecary Slate (\texttt{pharma:assay}):} Releasing any foraged mushroom or botanical tincture (\texttt{PharmacopoeiaBatchAttestation}) requires dual-steward hardware signatures after reading back verified assay bounds (\texttt{amatoxin == 0 ng}, heavy metals \texttt{< 100 ppb}). Across Layer~7, parcels enroll in Designated Forest Land and UCEA conservation easements, slashing property-tax drag by $70\text{--}90\%$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Scenario \texttt{Mu} (\texttt{SCN-MU-EXTRACTION}): Unbuilding Scanners and Cryptographic Taint Quarantine}
\label{sec:wave-d-mu}

\subsection{NIR/XRF Salvage Triage and Five-Stream Biorefining}

Scenario~\texttt{Mu} replaces municipal landfills and chemical fertilizer imports by treating urban demolition salvage and organic residues as five precision material streams (\texttt{EcologicalDeconstructionAndTriageIntent} achieving $\ge 9{,}800\,\mathrm{bp}$ / $98\%$ landfill diversion):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Handheld NIR/XRF Unbuilding Triage Scanner (\texttt{mu:triage}):} Pairs portable Near-Infrared (polymer) and X-Ray Fluorescence (heavy-metal) spectrometers with an offline Constellation slate to tag salvaged beams, fixtures, and bricks at deconstruction sites---immediately quarantining lead-painted or asbestos/CCA-arsenic lumber away from culinary or residential loops.
    \item \textbf{Biosolids, Struvite \& TLUD Biochar Inspector (\texttt{mu:biorefinery}):} Monitors \texttt{PyrolysisAndTerraPretaIntent} holds: EPA~503 Class~A thermophilic sanitation ($\ge 328{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ / $55.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$ continuous for $72\,\mathrm{h}$, resetting to zero on any thermal dip), urine phosphorus/nitrogen crystallization into pure struvite ($\mathrm{MgNH_4PO_4 \cdot 6H_2O}$ within $[8{,}500,\, 9{,}200]\,\mathrm{mpH}$, $\ge 9{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ recovery), and smokeless Top-Lit Updraft (\texttt{TLUD}) biochar pyrolysis ($[673{,}150,\, 823{,}150]\,\mathrm{mK}$).
    \item \textbf{Cryptographic Brownfield Phytomining Taint Lock \& Prohibited Export Register:} Hyperaccumulator biomass harvested from contaminated urban soils carries an immutable Layer~3/Layer~6 \texttt{TAINTED\_HEAVY\_METAL\_ASH} lineage tag blocking agricultural routing and directing ash solely to Scenario~\texttt{Theta} metallurgical smelting, while the \textbf{Prohibited Hazardous Export Register} forbids dumping any e-waste or chemical residue onto external Global South or municipal sinks (\texttt{WasteExemptionClaim} + municipal tipping-fee inversion).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Scenario \texttt{Theta} (\texttt{SCN-THETA-FOUNDRY}): Three-Tier Scrap Sourcing and Dead-Man Pour Consoles}
\label{sec:wave-d-theta}

\subsection{Solar Crucible Batching and Sub-250ms Two-Person Enclave Sign-Off}

Melting bronze, aluminum, or cast iron consumes orders of magnitude more microjoules than woodworking. Scenario~\texttt{Theta} embeds thermodynamic restraint directly into its \textbf{Foundry Metrology \& Crucible Console} (\texttt{theta:foundry}):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Strict Three-Tier Material Hierarchy:} Submitting any metal part need automatically searches \textbf{Tier~1 Direct Dimensional Reuse} (\texttt{Tier1RepurposeMatchIntent} from Scenario~\texttt{Mu} salvage stock) before permitting \textbf{Tier~2 Secondary Scrap Remelt} (\texttt{PyrometallurgyAndElectrowinIntent}, gated at $\ge 8{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ crucible fill efficiency and scheduled exclusively during peak solar/micro-hydro microgrid surplus). \textbf{Tier~3 Virgin Geospheric Extraction} (\texttt{LastResortGeosphericExtractionIntent}) is constitutionally locked behind a Ring~$R_4$ Watershed Assembly supermajority Read-Back imposing a $10\times$ Asymptotic Ecological Replacement Cost ($\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$) surcharge!
    \item \textbf{XRF Alloy Verification \& Two-Person Dead-Man Pour Gate:} Prior to tapping an induction furnace, optical/XRF spectrometry verifies tramp-element bounds (for instance, bismuth/lead/cadmium limits) on a high-contrast E-Ink foundry board; releasing the tilt-pour interlock requires simultaneous physical two-person hardware enclave confirmation within a $\le 250\,\mathrm{ms}$ coincidence window plus active fume-scrubber differential pressure verification.
\end{itemize}

\section{Scenario \texttt{Nu} (\texttt{SCN-NU-TEXTILES}) and Scenario \texttt{Xi} (\texttt{SCN-XI-ARCH}): Ephemeral-RAM Craft and Two-Track Open Building}
\label{sec:wave-d-nu-xi}

\subsection{Scenario \texttt{Nu}: Zero-Surveillance Tailoring (\texttt{OP\_PURGE\_R0\_LINEAGE}) and Visible Mending}

Industrial fast fashion poisons watersheds with perfluorinated (\texttt{PFAS}) coatings, hexavalent chromium (\texttt{Cr(VI)}) leather tanning, and synthetic microfibers while alienating wearers from repair. Scenario~\texttt{Nu} equips the textile and cobbler studio with two convivial tools:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{The Ephemeral-RAM Cloth \& Last Atelier (\texttt{nu:atelier}):} When tailoring bespoke garments or milling orthopedic Goodyear-welted shoe lasts (\texttt{PersonalGarmentCraftIntent}), 3D body or foot measurements live exclusively in volatile $R_0$ RAM and are cryptographically zeroized upon physical garment handoff via \texttt{OP\_PURGE\_R0\_LINEAGE}---preventing biometric body-shape databases and rejecting regimented institutional uniforms in favor of pluralistic personal expression.
    \item \textbf{The Visible Sashiko Mending \& PPE Board:} Prioritizes low-exergy Sashiko darning and cobbler resoling (\texttt{TextileRepairBounty}, $< 1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ of new-garment exergy), enforces $\ge 9{,}200\,\mathrm{bp}$ CNC pattern-nesting yield on bast/wool cloth, verifies \texttt{0 mg} PFAS and \texttt{0 mg} \texttt{Cr(VI)} across botanical dye/tanning vats, and requires $\ge 9{,}800\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($50\,\mu\mathrm{m}$) wash-water microfiber filtration.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Scenario \texttt{Xi}: Habraken Two-Track Open Building and Kigumi Barn-Raising}

Finally, Scenario~\texttt{Xi} synthesizes timber (\texttt{Eta}), salvage (\texttt{Mu}), and hardware connectors (\texttt{Theta}/\texttt{Alpha}) into living architecture governed by N.\ John Habraken's \textbf{Two-Track Open Building} doctrine \parencite{habraken1972supports}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Track 1 --- Communal Structural \texttt{Support} (\texttt{ParametricSupportBuildIntent}):} Engineered CNC \textit{Kigumi} (reversible nail-free mortise-and-tenon timber joinery), earthen/hemp-lime envelopes, seismic foundations, and fire-rated party walls undergo full integer Finite Element Analysis (\texttt{FEA}), $24$-hour timber moisture-equilibrium checks ($\le 1{,}200\,\mathrm{bp}$), LiDAR dimensional scan verification on the \textbf{Parametric Barn-Raising Tablet}, and licensed PE stamp compilation (\texttt{PragmaticL7PermitVariance} Read-Back using structural code-equivalence lenses).
    \item \textbf{Track 2 --- Sovereign Household \texttt{Infill} (\texttt{PersonalHearthInfillNotice}):} Non-load-bearing interior partitions, cabinetry, paint, and room layouts inside a resident's $R_0$ home belong strictly to the household---requiring \textbf{zero assembly vote, zero aesthetic committee approval, and zero interior digital twin inspection} so long as fire egress and wet-wall shutoffs remain untouched.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{invariant}[Taint Quarantine and Two-Track Architectural Autonomy Invariant]
\label{inv:wave-d-taint-habraken}
Across Wave~D: (a) no Constellation client may clear a \texttt{TAINTED\_HEAVY\_METAL\_ASH} or non-Class-A biosolids batch into agricultural/culinary workflows (\texttt{Eta}/\texttt{Gamma}), nor bypass the Ring-$R_4$ $10\times\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$ Read-Back for Tier-3 geospheric extraction; and (b) Track-2 interior \texttt{Infill} modifications inside $R_0$ dwellings (\texttt{Xi}) and personal garment geometry buffers (\texttt{Nu}) remain cryptographically exempt from collective telemetry retention.
\end{invariant}
