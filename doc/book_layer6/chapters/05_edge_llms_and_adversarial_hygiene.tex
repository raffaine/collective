\section{Salvaged-Hardware Edge Inference Physics and Model Governance}
\label{sec:salvaged-edge-inference}

Contemporary commercial artificial intelligence assumes rapid hardware obsolescence, megawatt data centers, and proprietary cloud tethering. A sovereign settlement bound by the \emph{20-Year Salvaged Hardware Longevity Mandate} operates under the opposite physical constraints: it must execute natural-language drafting, speech transcription, and retrieval-grounded explanation entirely on second-life laptops, refurbished mini-PCs, and reclaimed workstation GPUs without breaching domestic acoustic ceilings or winter solar budgets.

\subsection{Post-Training Quantization Within Salvaged Memory Envelopes}
\label{subsec:ptq-memory-physics}

In unquantized 16-bit floating-point (\texttt{FP16} or \texttt{BF16}) precision, a transformer with $P$ parameters requires $2P$~bytes solely to hold static weights in random-access memory, rendering even a 7-billion-parameter model ($14\,\mathrm{GiB}$ before activation buffers) unusable on a salvaged 8--16\,\textrm{GiB} ThinkPad. Layer~6 eliminates full-precision weight residency through second-order Hessian-aware post-training quantization---specifically 4-bit and 3-bit block-quantized formats such as GPTQ, AWQ, and GGUF \parencite{frantar2023gptq}. During offline model preparation, layer-wise weight matrices $\mathbf{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\mathrm{out}} \times d_{\mathrm{in}}}$ are quantized onto a discrete integer grid of bit-width $b_w \in \{3, 4\}$ in contiguous groups of $g \in \{64, 128\}$ weights by minimizing the squared layer-output reconstruction error weighted by the empirical inverse Hessian $\mathbf{H}^{-1} = (2 \mathbf{X}\mathbf{X}^\top + \lambda \mathbf{I})^{-1}$:
\begin{equation}
\widehat{\mathbf{W}} = \arg\min_{\mathbf{Q} \in \mathcal{Q}_{b_w, g}} \left\| \mathbf{W}\mathbf{X} - \mathbf{Q}\mathbf{X} \right\|_2^2.
\label{eq:gptq-objective}
\end{equation}
With each group of $g$ quantized integers accompanied by a 16-bit scale factor and zero-point offset ($b_s = 32$~bits per group), the static memory footprint $M_{\mathrm{weights}}$ in bytes is strictly bounded by:
\begin{equation}
M_{\mathrm{weights}}(P, b_w, g) = P \cdot \left( \frac{b_w}{8} + \frac{b_s}{8 \cdot g} \right).
\label{eq:weight-memory-bound}
\end{equation}
Evaluating Equation~\eqref{eq:weight-memory-bound} at $b_w = 4$ and $g = 128$ yields $0.53125$~bytes per parameter---a $3.76\times$ compression over \texttt{FP16} with negligible perplexity degradation on structured extraction and summarization tasks \parencite{frantar2023gptq}.

This compression enables a two-tier physical topology across a Node's local VLAN~30 runtime without violating the frugal baseline established in \texttt{bp\_zero\_stage1\_homelab\_workstation.jsonld}, where the deterministic Sovereign OS and all eight core POSIX daemons occupy $\le 240\,\mathrm{MiB}$ of idle RAM inside a $512\,\mathrm{MiB}$ constitutional ceiling:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Workstation Edge Tier (3B--7B Parameters):} Refurbished 10-to-15-year-old \texttt{x86\_64} or \texttt{ARM64} homestead workstations (\texttt{salvaged\_thinkpad\_or\_minipc\_host}, nominal compute envelope $65{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$) map a 3B to 7B 4-bit model ($1.65$--$3.85\,\mathrm{GiB}$ static weights) on demand into reclaimable user-space memory for local intent drafting, finite-state intake extraction, and offline speech-to-text (STT) transcription. When drafting concludes, \texttt{col-commonsd} issues \texttt{madvise(MADV\_DONTNEED)} or unmaps the arena cleanly.
  \item \textbf{Settlement Commons Server Tier (13B--30B Parameters):} At Ring~$R_1$ maturity, a single shared settlement host equipped with reclaimed second-life workstation accelerators (for example, dual salvaged 12--24\,\textrm{GiB} cards) hosts a 13B to 30B 3-bit/4-bit model ($6.9$--$15.9\,\mathrm{GiB}$ static weights) dedicated to citation-locked retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over ratified Layer~5 archives and multilingual cross-dialect translation.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Paged KV-Cache Arenas, Asynchronous Cadence, and Hydronic-Acoustic Limits}
\label{subsec:kv-cache-acoustics}

Unbounded context growth is the primary cause of out-of-memory faults in naive local LLM deployments. For a transformer with $n_{\mathrm{layers}}$ layers, $n_{\mathrm{kv}}$ grouped-query key-value heads of dimension $d_{\mathrm{head}}$, key-value quantization width $b_{\mathrm{kv}}$~bytes, and active context length $L_{\mathrm{ctx}}$ tokens, the transient key-value cache footprint satisfies:
\begin{equation}
M_{\mathrm{KV}}(L_{\mathrm{ctx}}) = 2 \cdot n_{\mathrm{layers}} \cdot n_{\mathrm{kv}} \cdot d_{\mathrm{head}} \cdot b_{\mathrm{kv}} \cdot L_{\mathrm{ctx}}.
\label{eq:kv-cache-arena}
\end{equation}
Under \texttt{col-commonsd}, neither the Intake Sandbox nor the Drafting Sandbox may invoke dynamic heap growth during token generation. At worker initialization, the parent supervisor pre-allocates a fixed, page-aligned slab of $N_{\mathrm{pages}}$ contiguous 256-token KV blocks ($L_{\mathrm{ctx,max}} = 4{,}096$ on workstations, requiring $\approx 256\,\mathrm{MiB}$ under 8-bit KV quantization for a 7B grouped-query model; $L_{\mathrm{ctx,max}} = 16{,}384$ on the Ring~$R_1$ RAG server) inside a strict Linux \texttt{cgroup v2} \texttt{memory.max} partition with swap disabled. Even under worst-case prompt saturation, an inference worker can never starve \texttt{col-execd}, \texttt{col-kmsd}, or \texttt{col-storaged}.

Equally decisive are the temporal and physical envelopes of salvaged inference:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Deliberative Latency by Design:} On salvaged AVX2 CPUs or older integrated GPUs, a 3B--7B 4-bit model generates $5$ to $18\,\mathrm{tokens/s}$, completing a structured JSON-LD proposal draft in $2{,}500$ to $8{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}$. Rather than disguising this latency behind streaming typing animations that mimic a sentient interlocutor, Constellation Companion interfaces present a calm, static progress bar labeled \texttt{COMPILING DRAFT PAYLOAD}. Multi-second turnaround harmonizes naturally with deliberative reflection rather than reactive instant-messaging loops \parencite{weiser1996calm,han2015}.
  \item \textbf{Bedroom Acoustic Cap ($\le 380\,\mathrm{cB}$) and Hydronic Heat Recovery:} As codified in the Stage-1 Homelab Blueprint (\texttt{perimeterAcousticCeilingCb: 380}), domestic workstations are governed by a hard acoustic ceiling of $380\,\mathrm{cB}$ ($38.0\,\mathrm{dBA}$) at one meter so that an autopoietic coding or drafting session never turns a living space into a server closet. Sustained high-wattage fan whine triggers automatic thread throttling in \texttt{col-commonsd}. Conversely, on the shared Ring~$R_1$ settlement server ($160{,}000$--$240{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$ under RAG load), waste Joule heat is not vented uselessly to outdoor air \parencite{georgescu1971}; liquid cold-plates route low-grade thermal reject ($308{,}150$--$315{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$) into the domestic greywater pre-heat manifold or winter seedling propagation bench.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Communal Model Governance, SHA-256 Pinning, and Dialect Sovereignty}
\label{subsec:model-governance-supply-chain}

Severing network cables to proprietary cloud providers solves data exfiltration, yet it does nothing to sanitize the ideological priors embedded inside pre-trained weight matrices. As \textcite{bender2021parrots} and \textcite{noble2018} demonstrate, large corpora scraped from commercial web platforms over-represent hegemonic English, corporate managerial idioms, and extractive property assumptions while distorting or erasing minority languages, regional dialects, and Indigenous oral traditions. Left ungoverned, a local drafting model subtly rewrites vernacular mutual-aid proposals into bureaucratic legalese or misinterprets colloquial dissent.

Layer~6 treats model weights, tokenizers, grammar definitions, system prompts, and local fine-tuning adapters (LoRAs) as constitutional public infrastructure governed through Layer~5:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Cryptographic Digest Pinning:} Every approved model component is registered in a Layer~5 \texttt{ModelArtifactManifest} signed by council ratification and accompanied by a public rationale record. Before \texttt{col-commonsd} maps any weight file, tokenizer table, or system prompt into memory, it streams the artifact through its internal NIST FIPS~180-4 \texttt{Sha256} engine (\texttt{src/daemons/col-commonsd/main.cpp}) and verifies bit-for-bit equality against the active Layer~5 ratification digest. Unratified or tampered weights trigger immediate load refusal.
  \item \textbf{Pre-Deployment Red-Teaming and Bias Audits:} Candidate models must pass a standardized Layer~6 offline evaluation suite before ratification: measuring translation fidelity across all languages spoken in the settlement, auditing demographic neutrality on restorative-justice and care scenarios, and surviving procedural indirect prompt-injection corpora generated by \texttt{col-adversaryd}.
  \item \textbf{Consent-Gated Local Dialect Adaptation:} Each Node maintains a governed JSON-LD controlled vocabulary mapping local idioms, agroecological terms, and Indigenous concepts to shared core schemas without flattening cultural nuance. Communities may fine-tune low-rank adapters on local council transcripts or oral histories \emph{exclusively} with the recorded, revocable consent of every speaker whose words appear in the training corpus.
  \item \textbf{Visible Provenance and Formal Translation Dispute:} Every rendered string in Layer~6 displays one of four immutable provenance badges---\texttt{human\_authored}, \texttt{machine\_drafted\_human\_approved}, \texttt{machine\_generated}, or \texttt{system\_authenticated}---alongside the short digest of the generating model. Any Steward may flag a \texttt{machine\_generated} summary or translation as disputed in a single action; \texttt{col-commonsd} immediately attaches a visible dispute banner across all clients, elevates the underlying structured Layer~5 record as authoritative, and logs the failure case into the next model audit cycle.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{invariant}[Cryptographic Weight-and-Prompt Supply-Chain Pinning]
\label{inv:model-supply-chain}
Let $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{active}}$ denote the set of \texttt{ModelArtifactManifest} tuples $\langle h_{\mathrm{weights}}, h_{\mathrm{tok}}, h_{\mathrm{prompt}}, h_{\mathrm{grammar}} \rangle \in (\{0,1\}^{256})^4$ holding unexpired Layer~5 ratification signatures from \texttt{col-kmsd}. The daemon \texttt{col-commonsd} spawns an inference worker over artifact tuple $A$ if and only if $\mathrm{SHA256}(A) \in \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{active}}$, and every \texttt{machine\_generated} or \texttt{machine\_drafted\_human\_approved} output envelope embeds the exact manifest digest $h_{\mathrm{manifest}} = \mathrm{SHA256}(A)$ that produced it.
\end{invariant}

\section{Dual-Sandbox Split-Model Isolation Against Indirect Prompt Injection}
\label{sec:split-sandbox-isolation}

A dangerous misconception in self-hosted software communities is equating ``offline local inference'' with immunity against adversarial manipulation. Because Layer~6 models execute without outbound internet sockets, no external server can eavesdrop on prompts directly. Yet Layer~6 is constitutionally required to communicate across \texttt{/run/collective/ipc/l6\_l7.sock} with \texttt{col-adversaryd} in the VLAN~40 DMZ, ingesting untrusted documents from the legacy external world: supplier invoices, municipal zoning notices, tax correspondence, statutory summonses, and unauthenticated public petitions.

\subsection{Refuting the Air-Gap Fallacy at the Layer 7 Boundary}
\label{subsec:airgap-fallacy}

In standard transformer architectures, system instructions and ingested data tokens occupy a single undifferentiated sequence $\mathbf{X} = [\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{sys}} \parallel \mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{user}} \parallel \mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{untrusted}}]$, interacting globally within every self-attention head:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Attention}(\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{K}, \mathbf{V}) = \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\frac{\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}} + \mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{causal}}\right)\mathbf{V}.
\label{eq:self-attention-mixing}
\end{equation}
Because natural language lacks a hardware privilege bit distinguishing ``instruction'' tokens from ``data'' tokens, an adversary who controls $\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{untrusted}}$---for instance, by embedding microscopic OCR text inside a PDF utility invoice or crafting a deceptive legal footnote---can steer the attention distribution to override $\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{sys}}$ \parencite{greshake2023injection,owasp2023llm}. If a single monolithic Layer~6 language model simultaneously read incoming Layer~7 vendor invoices and drafted Layer~5 treasury disbursements or Ricardian Treaty amendments, an indirect prompt injection could synthesize a plausible but unauthorized governance payload, manipulate a debate summary, or exfiltrate council context into an outward Layer~7 reply.

\subsection{Architectural Separation: Unprivileged Intake Sandbox vs.\ Authenticated Drafting Sandbox}
\label{subsec:intake-vs-drafting-sandboxes}

Following the principle of least authority \parencite{miller2006robust,saltzer1984end} and NIST zero-trust boundary segmentation \parencite{rose2020zero}, \texttt{col-commonsd} physically abolishes monolithic model execution. Reading untrusted external text and drafting sovereign governance records are partitioned across two mutually isolated OS sandboxes separated by a deterministic, fixed-schema binary pipe (Figure~\ref{fig:split-sandbox-hygiene}):

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Quarantine Pre-Normalizer (\texttt{col-utf8-scrub}):} Bytes arriving over \texttt{/run/collective/ipc/l6\_l7.sock} first pass through a deterministic C++20 state machine (zero neural weights) that strips invisible Unicode bidirectional overrides (\texttt{U+202A}--\texttt{U+202E}), zero-width joiners, homoglyph control sequences, and embedded markup tags, emitting canonical NFC UTF-8 plain text capped at a strict byte ceiling.
  \item \textbf{The Unprivileged Intake Sandbox (\texttt{col-intake-llm}):} Spawned inside an unprivileged Linux user, mount, PID, IPC, and network namespace (\texttt{CLONE\_NEWUSER | CLONE\_NEWNS | CLONE\_NEWPID | CLONE\_NEWIPC | CLONE\_NEWNET}) under a restrictive \texttt{seccomp-bpf} allowlist (\texttt{read}, \texttt{write}, \texttt{futex}, \texttt{exit\_group} only) with ephemeral KV-cache wiped cleanly via \texttt{explicit\_bzero} after every single document. The Intake Model possesses \emph{zero} file descriptors to \texttt{l5\_l6.sock}, \emph{zero} access to Layer~5 proposal schemas or BPMN templates, and \emph{zero} visibility into Steward identities or Agora histories. Crucially, its token sampler is locked at every decoding step $t$ by a compiled finite-state automaton / context-free grammar engine \parencite{willard2023outlines}. Given grammar state $\sigma_t \in \Sigma_{\mathrm{FSM}}$ and vocabulary $\mathcal{V}$, any token $v \notin \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{valid}}(\sigma_t)$ has its logit forced to $-\infty$ prior to argmax/sampling:
  \begin{equation}
  \tilde{z}_t(v) =
  \begin{cases}
  z_t(v), & \text{if } \delta_{\mathrm{FSM}}(\sigma_t, v) \neq \bot, \\
  -\infty, & \text{otherwise.}
  \end{cases}
  \label{eq:fsm-logit-mask}
  \end{equation}
  Consequently, \texttt{col-intake-llm} is mathematically incapable of emitting imperative prose, embedded instructions, or arbitrary JSON keys; it can output \emph{only} a fixed binary-convertible \texttt{TypedIntakeSlotFrame} containing strongly typed scalars (64-bit integer cent/exergy amounts, ISO-8601 calendar dates, enumerated NAICS or statutory clause IDs, and length-capped alphanumeric reference slugs).
  \item \textbf{The Authenticated Drafting Sandbox (\texttt{col-draft-llm}):} Operates in a separate namespace connected exclusively to authenticated Steward input streams (keyboard text, verified local STT transcripts, or Companion UI selections) and, when referencing a legacy filing, the sanitized scalar slots validated from \texttt{TypedIntakeSlotFrame}. It is architecturally barred from reading raw Layer~7 text buffers. Its output is validated against W3C SHACL shapes \parencite{w3c2017shacl}; if Steward intent is underspecified or ambiguous, \texttt{col-draft-llm} emits \texttt{CLARIFICATION\_REQUIRED} rather than guessing, escalating unresolved edge cases to a human interpreter.
  \item \textbf{Deterministic Template Read-Back and Personal Hardware Sign:} Neither sandbox holds signing keys, and neither can write to \texttt{l5\_l6.sock}. The candidate structured payload is canonicalized via RFC~8785 JCS \parencite{rfc8785} and rendered into plain human prose by a \emph{zero-LLM deterministic template engine} driven purely by the payload's canonical fields. Only after inspecting this deterministic read-back does the Steward trigger an Ed25519 signature inside their personal hardware token (\texttt{ATECC608A} \parencite{microchip_atecc608a} or \texttt{extractable: false} Web Crypto key \parencite{w3c2017webcrypto}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Why the Four-Stage Chain Breaks Arbitrary Indirect Prompt Injection}
\label{subsec:injection-proof}

We can now state precisely why an adversarial payload embedded inside an external Layer~7 invoice or petition cannot hijack governance execution. Under classical indirect prompt injection \parencite{greshake2023injection}, an attacker relies on three structural properties: (a)~unbounded expressive bandwidth between the untrusted document and downstream instruction-following context, (b)~implicit tool-execution privileges attached to model completions, and (c)~opaque post-processing where the human user approves a summary rather than the literal state mutation.

The Layer~6 pipeline severs all three links simultaneously:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Finite-Alphabet Channel Bottleneck:} Between \texttt{col-intake-llm} and \texttt{col-draft-llm}, raw natural language is physically unrepresentable. As shown in Listing~\ref{lst:typed-intake-slot-pipe}, the cross-sandbox wire struct \texttt{TypedIntakeSlotFrame} contains only fixed-width integers (\texttt{amount\_minor\_units}, \texttt{due\_epoch\_day}), strict C++20 \texttt{enum class} discriminators (\texttt{LegacyDocKind}, \texttt{StatutoryCodeId}), and a character-class-restricted ASCII invoice identifier (\texttt{[A-Z0-9-]\{1,24\}}). Even under total compromise of the Intake Model's internal activations, the maximum adversarial perturbation across the pipe is falsifying a bounded scalar slot (for example, misreporting an invoice date or integer figure)---never smuggling an imperative prompt into \texttt{col-draft-llm}.
  \item \textbf{Zero Ambient Authority:} Neither sandbox possesses network sockets, IPC handles to \texttt{col-kmsd}, or signing keys \parencite{miller2006robust}. A compromised model completion is an inert byte buffer in memory.
  \item \textbf{Deterministic Read-Back Completeness:} Suppose an adversarial PDF layout tricks \texttt{col-intake-llm} into extracting \texttt{950000} minor units instead of \texttt{9500}. Because the read-back string is synthesized deterministically from the JCS-canonicalized JSON-LD fields without touching any language model, the Steward's personal device explicitly displays the exact anomalous quantity alongside the original quarantined document hash prior to physical hardware button confirmation.
\end{itemize}

\section{Downstream Rendering Classes, Privacy-Scoped RAG, Exergy Shedding, and Graceful Degradation}

\subsection{The Three Constitutional Rendering Classes and Citation-Locked RAG}
\label{subsec:rendering-classes-rag}

\subsection{Privacy-by-Construction Exclusion from Vector Indices}
\label{subsec:privacy-scoped-rag}

Permitted retrieval shards are physically partitioned by council capability scope and cryptographically purged according to Layer~5 retention schedules; deliberation logs are never folded into model fine-tuning weights without recorded unanimous consent.

\subsection{Fixed-Point Microjoule (\texttt{uJ}) Token Metering and Winter SoC Load-Shedding}

\begin{equation}

\label{eq:exact-uj-inference}
