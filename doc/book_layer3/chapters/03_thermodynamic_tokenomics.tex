\section{Exergy-Backed Minting (Scenarios Alpha \& Theta)}
In the Oasis architecture, tokens represent actual thermodynamic capacity. When a citizen utilizes the community foundry (Scenario Theta) or 3D printers (Scenario Alpha) to refine raw biomass or metal, they are performing a physical transformation. The reduction in local entropy requires a verifiable injection of exergy (heat, electricity).

The Layer 3 smart contracts strictly enforce the equation of state for token minting. The issuance of a \texttt{FabricationToken} ($T_f$) is strictly proportional to the physical Joules dissipated by the Layer 2 hardware, verified by the Type-K thermocouples and closed-loop stepper motor encoders:
\begin{equation}
\Delta T_f = \frac{1}{\xi} \int_{t_0}^{t_1} \left( \dot{Q}(t) + \dot{W}(t) \right) dt
\end{equation}
where $\dot{Q}(t)$ is the rate of heat transfer, $\dot{W}(t)$ is the mechanical/electrical work rate, and $\xi$ is the network's thermodynamic difficulty parameter (a floating constant that adjusts based on the total exergy surplus of the microgrid). 

\subsection{Preventing Physical Double-Spending}
Because the token supply is directly tied to the physical limits of the universe, "double-spending" is not just a cryptographic attack; it is a physical impossibility. A node cannot simultaneously allocate the same 500 Watts of solar power to both the induction kiln and the cryptographic server. The Layer 3 ledger acts as the ultimate arbiter, rejecting any state transition that violates the Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL) matrix reported by the Layer 2 sensor mesh:
\begin{equation}
\sum_{k=1}^{n} I_k = 0
\end{equation}
If the ledger detects an electrical paradox—where the signed telemetry attempts to draw more current than the microgrid physically possesses—the transaction is dropped, and the offending node's stake is slashed \cite{kondepudi2014modern}.

\section{Automated Market Makers and the ERC Multiplier (Scenario Eta)}
While exergy-backed minting governs energy, the extraction of regenerative ecological resources (e.g., harvesting timber in Scenario Eta) is governed by the Ecological Replacement Cost (ERC). Legacy tokenomics allow resources to be extracted at a flat fiat rate until the ecosystem collapses. The Sovereign Stack employs an Automated Market Maker (AMM) governed directly by Layer 2 biological oracles.

When a citizen submits a \texttt{HarvestIntent} to the ledger, the smart contract queries the decentralized oracle network for the current ERC asymptotic curve. The token cost $P_{harvest}$ required to execute the extraction is dynamically multiplied by the $ERC_{cost}$:
\begin{equation}
P_{harvest} = P_{base} \times C_0 \left( \frac{1}{1 - \left( \frac{E_{current}}{C_{max}} \right)^\gamma} \right)
\end{equation}
Because $E_{current}$ and $C_{max}$ are continuously updated via Layer 2 NDVI drone photogrammetry and aquifer sensors, the AMM responds instantly to ecological stress. If a drought reduces the canopy's carrying capacity, $C_{max}$ shrinks, causing the ERC multiplier to spike exponentially. This non-linear pricing mathematically forces the network to halt extraction and pivot to regenerative labor before the substrate can be irreversibly degraded \\cite{odum1996environmental}.

\section{Adversarial Slashing and Game Theory (Scenario Rho)}
To maintain this rigid physical integrity against bad actors (Scenario Rho), the ledger employs rigorous game-theoretic slashing conditions. 

In a decentralized mesh, an attacker might attempt an Equivocation Attack: signing two conflicting telemetry payloads (e.g., claiming a battery is fully charged to Node A, but empty to Node B) to manipulate the token emission curve. When the BFT consensus network detects two valid signatures from the same public key on conflicting physical states, the smart contract automatically executes a slashing penalty.

The staked exergy tokens of the adversarial node are mathematically burned (deleted from the ledger). The magnitude of the burn $S_{burned}$ is proportional to the node's total stake $S_{total}$ and the severity factor $\alpha$:
\begin{equation}
S_{burned} = \alpha \cdot S_{total}
\end{equation}
This financial annihilation ensures that the cost of attacking the network always outweighs the potential fiat extraction, aligning the greedy behavior of individual nodes with the long-term survival of the cybernetic organism.
