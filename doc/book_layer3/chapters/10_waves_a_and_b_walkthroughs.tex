\subsection{1. Scenario Zero (\texttt{SCN-ZERO-SOFTWARE}): Khronos Lifecycle, Server Heat Recovery, and the TeX Asymptote}
As established in Dialogue~1 (\texttt{D1}) and Dialogue~2 (\texttt{D2}), Oasis (\texttt{Layer 1b}) and every piece of software in The Collective is a governed \textbf{Layer~6 Software Object} stewarded by a \textbf{Layer~5 Working Group} ($N_s = 1 \to 12$), analogous to the Khronos Group stewarding OpenGL. Inside Oasis, software never runs inner binary emulation; instead, Layer~3 (\texttt{col-storaged}) tracks its \textbf{mild Layer~1/2 physical footprint} alongside its \textbf{five-phase organizational lifecycle}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textit{Phase 1 (Friction to RFC):} When coordination waste or hardware drift surfaces as a Layer~3 $\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$ spike, affected nodes commit a content-addressed \texttt{SoftwareRequirementRfcIntent} CID into \texttt{col-storaged}.
    \item \textit{Phase 2 \& Phase 3 (Working Group Charter, Build \& Hydronic Heat Recovery):} As the chartered working group runs hermetic 3-architecture builds (\texttt{x86\_64}, \texttt{aarch64}, \texttt{riscv64}) and $10{,}000$-tick Oasis canary soaks, Layer~2 meters server rack power draw ($P_{\mathrm{rack}} \le 450{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$) and direct-to-chip copper water-block heat recovery. \texttt{col-storaged} verifies that $\ge 8{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($80\%$) of the server's dissipated electrical exergy ($\Delta E_{\mathrm{compute},\mathrm{uJ}}$) is captured into the domestic hot-water or greenhouse hydronic buffer at $318{,}150\text{--}328{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($45\text{--}55\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$).
    \item \textit{Phase 4 (Spec Ratification):} Once \texttt{VerifyMerkleArtifactConsensus} confirms $\texttt{builder\_consensus\_count} \ge 3$ and zero floating-point opcodes, \texttt{col-storaged} activates the 32-byte \texttt{MerkleArtifactRecord} and credits $\Delta R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}$ to the builders.
    \item \textit{Phase 5 (Dormant Equilibrium \& Substrate Drift Wake-Up):} When a mature software package reaches \textbf{Dormant Equilibrium (``The TeX Asymptote'')}, incidents drop to zero and every member's $R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t)$ decays via reputational entropy $\lambda$ toward $0\,\mathrm{bp}$. Because each working-group member retains their non-decaying civic baseline $B = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($V_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t) = 10{,}000 + R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t)$), the dormant package is mathematically immune to single-maintainer hijack. When a new Layer~2 sensor revision violates an old ABI assumption (\textbf{Substrate Drift}), Layer~4 hard-blocks and emits a structured yield frame that decompiles into a Layer~6 Semantic Diff, waking the working group to ratify an adapter patch before falling back asleep.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{2. Scenario Tau (\texttt{SCN-TAU-GENESIS}): Clean-Slate Lot 402 Boot and Stage I Scavenged Appliance Shims}
When a founding household ($N_s = 1 \to 4$) boots a Stage~1A node on Lot~402, \texttt{col-storaged} initializes with a clean-slate operational table (\texttt{workflows\_.empty()}), an initial external Layer~7 fiat reserve register $\Phi_0 = \texttt{2'000'000 cents}$ ($\$20{,}000$), and the Lot~402 House Enveloping Node boundary policy ($5{,}500\,\mathrm{cB}$ daytime / $3{,}800\,\mathrm{cB}$ nighttime acoustic cap, $9{,}600{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$ main breaker ceiling). Because Day-1 households bootstrap with scavenged Layer~7 appliances (\texttt{QUALITY\_SCAVENGED\_L7\_SHIM = 0x04}), \texttt{col-storaged} explicitly meters the parasitic shim standby overhead (\texttt{parasitic\_shim\_overhead\_mw = 45'000 mW}) and high diagnostic uncertainty ($\pm 1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$), making visible the exact thermodynamic cost of proprietary hardware lock-in.

\subsection{3. Scenario Sigma (\texttt{SCN-SIGMA-BURNOUT}): Somatic Homeostasis and Zero-Stat Liberation}
In \texttt{Scenario Sigma}, Layer~3 enforces two anti-burnout constitutional guarantees. First, \texttt{AttemptTokenizeBaselineCare()} unconditionally returns \texttt{false}: a steward activating a voluntary \texttt{RestShield} or recovering from exhaustion never suffers a deduction in baseline meals, hydronic warmth, or $B = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ civic standing. Second, \texttt{col-storaged} tracks the node's unscheduled fault interrupt rate (\texttt{unscheduled\_fault\_prob\_bp}). As the household upgrades its appliances from Stage~I scavenged shims (\texttt{350 bp} blind hard-block rate) to Stage~II hybrid retrofits (\texttt{40 bp}) and Stage~III native open-spec hardware (\texttt{5 bp} with 5-minute scheduled swaps), unplanned maintenance interrupts vanish---achieving \textbf{Zero-Stat Liberation}.

\subsection{4. Scenario Gamma (\texttt{SCN-GAMMA-KITCHEN}): The Dual-Loop Bakery, Weekend Detours, and GN 1/1 Convergence}
\texttt{Scenario Gamma} brings together four flagship Layer~3 mechanisms inside the communal hearth and neighborhood bakery ($N_s = 5$ active bakers serving $N_p = 500$ neighborhood residents):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Dual-Loop Demand \& Spontaneous Weekend Detours:} Household \textit{Pantry} lenses emit ZK-stripped 64-byte \texttt{AnonymousAppetiteLeaseV50} records into \texttt{col-storaged}. When twenty neighbors spontaneously head to the mountains on Friday afternoon, their local Layer~2 fridge/shelf drawdowns pause and their unrenewed pickup leases expire silently at \texttt{expiry\_hlc\_ms}. At the 02:00 dough-make cutoff, \texttt{AggregateActiveBakeryDemandV50()} schedules only the active non-expired staple mass ($8{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ / $85\%$ capacity) plus the $1{,}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($15\%$) Dr\_Sofia \textit{montage} experiment batch---zero wasted loaves and zero nagging phone alerts.
    \item \textbf{Bigelow $F_0$ Pasteurization \& Arrhenius Cold-Chain Ledgers:} For split-pea soup and canned broths, \texttt{col-storaged} records the piecewise-quadratic Bigelow thermal lethality integral ($T \ge 347{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ / $74.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ for $\ge 120{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}$), while cold-room walk-ins integrate \texttt{PantryPerishableBatchV50} Arrhenius decay at $275{,}150\text{--}277{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$.
    \item \textbf{Gastronorm GN 1/1 $\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$ Convergence \& Stage II Oven Retrofit:} By standardizing on $316$ stainless \textbf{Gastronorm GN~1/1 pans ($530 \times 325\,\mathrm{mm}$)} across prep, bake, and e-trike tiffin transport, the bakery slashes container $\lambda_{\mathrm{ERC}}$ by $76\times$ (Table~\ref{tab:ch2-erc-comparison}). When the crew retrofits their scavenged commercial deck oven with an open STM32/MAX31856 K-type thermocouple PID board, \texttt{col-storaged} commits a \texttt{ProofOfStewardshipRetrofitRecordV50}, minting $\Delta R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}$ and eliminating external vendor thermostat tribute.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{5. Scenario Alpha (\texttt{SCN-ALPHA-FAB}): \textit{The Workshop} Durable Ledger and Right-to-Repair CNC Retrofits}
In the solar-powered fabrication bay ($N_s = 1 \to 6$ machinists under an Industrial Bay Envelope lease of $8{,}500\,\mathrm{cB}$ daytime / $5{,}000\,\mathrm{cB}$ nighttime and $4{,}500{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$), \texttt{col-storaged} maintains \texttt{WorkshopDurableToolTwinV50} records for every lathe, 3D printer, and 5-axis CNC mill. When an imported Turnkey blueprint calls for laser seam welding that the local bay lacks, Layer~4 executes a \textbf{Tier~2 Macro-Activity Substitution} (\texttt{D8}), splicing an open \textbf{ISO metric \texttt{M6} bolted flange} sub-workflow from the Ontological Graph and logging the substitution in the batch's \texttt{ThermodynamicProofRecordV50}.

\subsection{6. Scenario Beta (\texttt{SCN-BETA-TRANSIT}): Five-Artery Kinetic Freight, ISO Euro-Totes, and BCC/HTLC Clearing}

    int64_t  hydronic_captured_heat_uj{0};    // Waste heat transferred to domestic/greenhouse loop (uJ)

[[nodiscard]] constexpr bool VerifyScenarioZeroHydronicRecoveryV50(
    ScenarioZeroBuildHeatReceiptV50& rec) noexcept {
    if (rec.rack_electrical_input_uj <= 0 || rec.hydronic_captured_heat_uj < 0 ||
        rec.hydronic_captured_heat_uj > rec.rack_electrical_input_uj) {
        return false; // First-Law conservation violation
    }
    rec.recovery_efficiency_bp = static_cast<uint16_t>(
        (rec.hydronic_captured_heat_uj * 10'000LL) / rec.rack_electrical_input_uj);
    return rec.recovery_efficiency_bp >= 8'000U;
}
