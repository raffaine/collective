\section{Wave E Walkthroughs: \texttt{Epsilon}, \texttt{Iota}, \texttt{Lambda}, and \texttt{Pi}}

\subsection{1. Scenario Epsilon (\texttt{SCN-EPSILON-COWORKING}): Eliminating $O(K^2)$ Horizontal Boundary Politics}
Inside a shared multi-guild coworking building ($N_s = 15 \to 80$), a dozen distinct child nodes---a $1$-person luthier bench, a $4$-person software pod, an electronics rework station, and a community reading room---share one floorplate and one local Layer~6 Semantic Daemon (\texttt{col-commonsd}). Rather than negotiating $\binom{12}{2} = 66$ bilateral treaties, every child node checks out domain-blind \texttt{EnvelopingBoundaryLeaseV50} slots (\texttt{cB}, \texttt{mW}, \texttt{mK}, \texttt{mm}, \texttt{ms}) from the Floorplate Enveloping Hypervisor in \texttt{col-storaged} ($O(K)$ linear scaling).

\subsection{2. Scenario Iota (\texttt{SCN-IOTA-EPISTEMIC}): Curating the Three Sovereign Libraries Under $B=1$ Dormant Protection}
\texttt{Scenario Iota} ($N_s = 5 \to 40$ metrologists, librarians, and research stewards) maintains the bioregional mirrors of the \textbf{Ontological Graph}, \textbf{Orchestration Registry}, and \textbf{Physics Baseline} in \texttt{col-storaged}. When a mature metrological standard or mathematical library enters Dormant Equilibrium ($R_i(t) \to 0$), the $N_s$ archivists' non-decaying $B = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ civic baseline prevents any external actor from pushing a malicious schema update to hijack the sleeping library (\texttt{D2}, \texttt{D3}).

\subsection{3. Scenario Lambda (\texttt{SCN-LAMBDA-CULTURAL}): Acoustic Studio Quiet Leases vs.\ Fast-Path Burst-Pipe Preemption}
\texttt{Scenario Lambda} ($N_s = 1 \to 20$ recording engineers, instrument makers, and performers) provides the Horizon Council's canonical stress test for \textbf{Two-Speed Jurisprudence (\texttt{D6}, \texttt{CCI-4})}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item From 20:00 to 23:00, the Acoustic Recording Studio holds an active Tier-\texttt{100} \texttt{EnvelopingBoundaryLeaseV50} imposing a strict partition acoustic cap of $\texttt{max\_acoustic\_cb} = 4{,}200\,\mathrm{cB}$ ($42.0\,\mathrm{dBA}$) on adjacent bays.
    \item At 21:15, a pressurized water pipe bursts in the adjacent utility chase. Layer~2 moisture and pressure transducers trip \texttt{HAZARD\_CLASS\_WATER\_INGRESS} (\texttt{0x01F4}).
    \item Under Fast-Path Jurisprudence, \texttt{EvaluateFastPathPreemptionV50()} (Listing~\ref{lst:ch8-envelope-lease}) immediately preempts the Studio's Tier-\texttt{100} quiet lease in under $5\,\mathrm{ms}$ of hardware time, grants a Tier-\texttt{990} emergency wet-vac extraction lease ($8{,}200\,\mathrm{cB}$, $2{,}200{,}000\,\mathrm{mW}$), and records an immutable \texttt{FastPathPreemptionReceiptV50} in \texttt{col-storaged}.
    \item The following morning (Slow Path), if the studio stewards propose requiring dual-sensor corroboration (moisture $\land$ flow-rate delta) before preempting master recording sessions, the Enveloping Node ratifies the Layer~6 Semantic Diff under $V_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$, compiling the new precedent rule directly into Layer~4 and notarizing its CID in Layer~3.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{7. Scenario Phi (\texttt{SCN-PHI-ELDER}): The Human Cornerstone of the Non-Decaying $B=1$ Civic Baseline}
\texttt{Scenario Phi} ($N_s = 6 \to 30$ elder residents and care stewards) reveals the deepest ethical purpose of the Unified Constitutional Voting Weight Law $V_i(t) = B + R_i(t) = 1 + R_i(t)$ (\texttt{D3}, \texttt{CCI-2}). As a senior resident ages and retires from active workshop or bakery labor, their physical task completion rate $\dot{r}_i(\tau)$ naturally drops to zero, and reputational entropy $\lambda$ decays their operational bonus $R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t) \to 0$. In a pure meritocracy ($B=0$), retirement would mean civic disenfranchisement. Under $V_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t) = 10{,}000 + R_{i,\mathrm{bp}}(t)$, every elder retains their full, non-decaying \textbf{$B = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($1.0$) constitutional voice} inside their Hearth, Building Envelope, and Elder Sanctuary forever, alongside Priority~\texttt{950} thermal ($295{,}150\,\mathrm{mK} = 22.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$) and acoustic ($4{,}000\,\mathrm{cB}$) care shields.

/**
 * @brief Verifies that an opportunistic actor accruing Delta R_attacker on a dormant node
 * (where all N_s members have decayed to R_j(t) == 0) cannot capture >= supermajority_bp
 * thanks to the non-decaying Universal Civic Baseline B = 10,000 bp (D2, D3, CCI-2).
 */
[[nodiscard]] constexpr bool VerifyDormantNodeAntiHijackImmunityV50(
    uint32_t active_node_members_ns,
    int64_t  attacker_delta_r_bp,
    int64_t  constitutional_supermajority_bp = 6'667) noexcept {
    if (active_node_members_ns == 0 || attacker_delta_r_bp < 0) {
        return false;
    }
    constexpr int64_t kCivicBaselineBp = 10'000;
    const int64_t attacker_voice_bp = kCivicBaselineBp + attacker_delta_r_bp;
    const int64_t total_pool_voice_bp =
        (static_cast<int64_t>(active_node_members_ns) * kCivicBaselineBp) + attacker_delta_r_bp;

    const int64_t attacker_share_bp = (attacker_voice_bp * 10'000LL) / total_pool_voice_bp;
    // Immune iff attacker share remains strictly below the constitutional supermajority threshold
    return attacker_share_bp < constitutional_supermajority_bp;
}

static_assert(VerifyDormantNodeAntiHijackImmunityV50(10, 5'000) == true,
              "10-member dormant node must block single-maintainer hijack (15,000 / 105,000 = 14.28% < 66.67%).");
