Every political economy rests upon an ontology of measurement. When a society decides what its ledgers count, what units those ledgers permit, and what realities they discard as unpriced ``externalities,'' it determines in advance whether its institutions will conserve the living biosphere or liquidate it \parencite{kapp1950social,polanyi1944}. Across the industrial and digital eras, two ostensibly rival accounting paradigms dominated institutional design: state-backed fiat finance and speculative blockchain tokenomics. Beneath their ideological antagonism, both systems shared the same foundational error. Both reduced the multi-dimensional metabolism of matter, energy, ecological regeneration, and human care to a single one-dimensional scalar currency.

In the legacy Layer~7 economy, a thousand dollars obtained by clear-cutting an old-growth watershed is mathematically indistinguishable on a balance sheet from a thousand dollars obtained by repairing neighborhood cargo trikes or baking sourdough loaves. Worse, when early decentralized ledger experiments attempted to escape central-bank debasement, they reproduced the most pathological features of the market inside cryptographic state machines: proof-of-work hash puzzles that burned megawatts of electrical exergy for artificial scarcity; automated market makers (AMMs) and continuous double auctions (CDAs) that priced community survival by speculative liquidity; stake-slashing penalties that weaponized financial ruin as a consensus mechanism; and panoptic ``Proof of Labor'' schemes that proposed strapping inertial measurement units and current clamps onto workers' tools to mint piece-rate tokens per physical calorie expended \parencite{zuboff2019,han2015}.

The Sovereign Stack rejects both fiat abstraction and crypto-tokenomic financialization at the root. Positioned between the hardware reflexes and edge digital twins of Layer~2 (\texttt{col-telemetryd}) and the deterministic BPMN~2.0 / WebAssembly state machines of Layer~4 (\texttt{col-execd}), \textbf{Layer~3: The Thermodynamic \& Ecological Ledger}---realized in POSIX silicon as the hardened C++20 storage and synchronization daemon \texttt{col-storaged} paired with the delay-tolerant mesh daemon \texttt{col-meshd}---does not mint a circulating currency, does not run double auctions, and never prices human care. Instead, Layer~3 maintains an immutable, local-first, conflict-free \textbf{multi-dimensional vector ledger} of physical reality expressed exclusively in canonical 64-bit fixed-point International System (SI) units \parencite{bipm2019si,neurath1919,odum1996environmental}.
