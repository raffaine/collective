\chapter{Smart Contracts and the Proof of Labor}

\section{Deterministic State Transitions}
In Layer 3, a Smart Contract is not a legal document, but a strictly deterministic state machine deployed across the gossip network. The contract holds custody of tokens (exergy reserves) and releases them only when mathematically satisfied by cryptographically signed external telemetry.

\section{The Autonomous Guild (Scenarios Pi \& Kappa)}
In the legacy world, human labor is verified subjectively by managers, leading to friction, bias, and wage theft (Scenario Pi). Apprenticeships (Scenario Kappa) require rigid institutional bureaucracy to verify hours. The Sovereign Stack eliminates human verification in favor of cryptographic \textit{Proof of Labor}.

When a citizen operates a tool (e.g., a CNC mill), the Layer 2 hardware (Hall-effect sensors and IMUs) samples the vibration and current draw. This telemetry is mathematically reduced via DSP into a unique "Labor Signature" hash, $\mathcal{H}_{labor}$. 

The Guild Smart Contract is deployed with a verification function $V$. To unlock their compensation, the citizen submits a transaction containing the time-bound labor signature $\mathcal{H}_{labor}$ and the signature of their own private key, $Sig_{citizen}$. The contract executes the following state transition logic:
\begin{equation}
S_{t+1} = 
\begin{cases} 
S_t + \Delta V_T & \text{if } V(PK_{tool}, \mathcal{H}_{labor}) = \text{TRUE} \\
S_t & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
where $S_t$ is the citizen's balance of Value Tokens, and $PK_{tool}$ is the public key embedded in the physical tool's secure enclave.

Because the tool itself is an autonomous signer on the ledger, it becomes a non-human, incorruptible witness to the citizen's physical exertion. The smart contract autonomously mints the exact token compensation proportional to the mechanical energy expended. There are no payroll delays, no HR departments, and no corporate extraction—only pure, mathematically verified thermodynamic exchange between the citizen and the mesh \cite{wood2014ethereum}.

\section{Spatial Smart Contracts and Non-Fungible Access (Scenarios Zeta \& Xi)}
While labor operates on fungible Value Tokens, physical space and architecture operate on Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs). In Scenario Zeta (Lodging) and Scenario Xi (Architecture), the access to a physical room is dictated by a Spatial Smart Contract.

When a citizen books a lodging node, the smart contract mints an ephemeral, time-bound NFT representing the access rights. This is executed using a Hash Time Locked Contract (HTLC). The cryptographic key to actuate the physical door's Layer 2 solenoid is locked behind a time parameter $T_{expiry}$.
\begin{equation}
\text{Access}(t) = 
\begin{cases} 
\text{Granted} & \text{if } t < T_{expiry} \text{ and } \text{Verify}(Sig_{citizen}) \\
\text{Denied} & \text{if } t \ge T_{expiry}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
Once $T_{expiry}$ passes, the cryptographic token mathematically dissolves on the ledger, and the citizen's access is atomically revoked, removing the need for legacy keycards or landlords.

\section{The Genesis Block (Scenario Tau)}
To bootstrap the Sovereign Stack (Scenario Tau), the architecture must initiate its physical reality. The Genesis Block is the absolute root of trust. It is generated by the first sovereign node once its Layer 2 hardware (JTAG provisioning) is locked. The Genesis Block hard-codes the physical coordinates, the foundational thermodynamic limits ($\eta_{sys}$), and the initial distribution of exergy tokens required to begin the simulation. From this cryptographic singularity, the entire cybernetic organism expands.
