\section{Why Scalar Money Fails Thermodynamic and Ecological Reality}
\label{sec:ch1-scalar-failure}

In conventional economics, the price mechanism is celebrated as a universal data-compression algorithm. By mapping every resource, machine, parcel of land, and hour of human labor onto a single scalar field $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ denominated in fiat currency (or an equivalent single-dimensional crypto token), market systems allow heterogeneous quantities to be added, subtracted, and cleared through a single scalar balance \parencite{polanyi1944}. From the standpoint of physical cybernetics and the Second Law of Thermodynamics, however, this projection from an $M$-dimensional physical phase space $\mathcal{X} \subset \mathbb{Z}^M$ onto a one-dimensional price line $\mathbb{R}$ is a catastrophic lossy compression \parencite{georgescu1971,ashby1956}.

Specifically, scalar price accounting violates three fundamental requirements of a viable cyber-physical commonwealth:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Dimensional Non-Substitutability (Violation of Conservation Laws):} A community bakery requires $750{,}000\,\mathrm{mg}$ of bread flour, $585{,}000\,\mu\mathrm{L}$ of potable water, $15{,}000\,\mathrm{mg}$ of salt, and $4{,}680{,}000{,}000{,}000\,\mu\mathrm{J}$ ($1.30\,\mathrm{kWh}$) of thermal exergy at $503{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($230.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$) to bake a batch of sourdough loaves. Excess thermal exergy cannot substitute for missing flour mass, nor can surplus acoustic headroom in centibels (\texttt{cB}) substitute for cold-chain refrigeration below $277{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($4.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$). A scalar token balance of ``$100$ Value Units'' conceals which physical coordinate is actually available or depleted until a physical process starves mid-batch.
    \item \textbf{Erasure of Exergy Grade and Irreversible Entropy Production:} Under the First Law of Thermodynamics, gross energy is conserved; under the Second Law, available work potential (\textit{exergy}) is irreversibly destroyed in every real transformation \parencite{kondepudi2014modern,jorgensen2001thermodynamics}. A scalar currency that prices electrical work, low-grade $305\,\mathrm{K}$ hydronic water, and virgin mineral extraction on a single monetary scale cannot distinguish a closed-loop regenerative cycle from irreversible ecological liquidation \parencite{kapp1950social,odum1996environmental}.
    \item \textbf{Vulnerability to Speculative Hoarding and Artificial Scarcity:} Whenever a ledger collapses physical stocks into a universal medium of exchange or runs a continuous double auction (CDA) to clear internal community needs, it rewards actors who withhold critical resources to drive up clearing prices.
\end{enumerate}

For these reasons, the Horizon Council permanently retired all early prototype references to ``Exergy Tokens ($E_T$),'' ``FabricationTokens ($T_f$),'' ``Value Tokens,'' and internal continuous double auctions. \subsection{The Exact Watt-Millisecond-Microjoule Identity and the $10^{12}$ Megajoule Law}

A remarkable mathematical property of the canonical SI unit set in Table~\ref{tab:ch1-si-metrology} is that integrating power in milliwatts (\texttt{mW}, $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{J/s}$) over time intervals in milliseconds (\texttt{ms}, $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s}$) yields energy in microjoules (\texttt{uJ}, $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{J}$) with \textbf{unit scaling factor equal to unity}:
\begin{equation}
1\,\mathrm{mW} \times 1\,\mathrm{ms} = 10^{-3}\,\frac{\mathrm{J}}{\mathrm{s}} \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s} = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{J} = 1\,\mu\mathrm{J}
\label{eq:ch1-mw-ms-uj-identity}
\end{equation}
Because of Equation~\eqref{eq:ch1-mw-ms-uj-identity}, integrating instantaneous sensor power $P_k\,\mathrm{mW}$ across a $100\,\mathrm{ms}$ tick requires zero division: $\Delta E_k = P_k \cdot \Delta t_{\mathrm{ms}}\,\mu\mathrm{J}$. Furthermore, a 64-bit signed integer (\texttt{int64\_t}, maximum value $\approx 9.22 \times 10^{18}\,\mu\mathrm{J}$) can represent up to $9.22\,\mathrm{Terajoules}$ ($2.56\,\mathrm{GWh}$) in a single register without overflow.

\begin{invariant}[The $10^{12}$ Microjoule-to-Megajoule Scaling Law]
\label{inv:ch1-mj-scaling}
Because $1\,\mathrm{J} = 10^6\,\mu\mathrm{J}$ and $1\,\mathrm{MJ} = 10^6\,\mathrm{J}$, one megajoule equals exactly one trillion microjoules:
\begin{equation}
1\,\mathrm{MJ} = 10^{12}\,\mu\mathrm{J} = \texttt{1'000'000'000'000LL uJ}, \qquad 1\,\mathrm{kWh} = 3.6\,\mathrm{MJ} = \texttt{3'600'000'000'000LL uJ}
\label{eq:ch1-mj-law}
\end{equation}
Dividing a microjoule register by $10^9$ (\texttt{1'000'000'000LL}) yields \textbf{kilojoules (\texttt{kJ})}, not megajoules (\texttt{MJ}), overstating megajoule quantities by $1{,}000\times$. Every Layer~2--4 wire structure and ledger calculation must preserve raw \texttt{int64\_t} microjoules (\texttt{\_uj}) and divide by \texttt{1'000'000'000'000LL} only when projecting whole megajoules for human display.
\end{invariant}

When integer division is required inside \texttt{col-storaged} (for instance, computing Carnot thermal exergy factors or efficiency-adjusted power), truncation drift is prevented via \textbf{Carry-Save Euclidean Remainder Accumulators}. Given dividend $N_t$ and divisor $D > 0$, the accumulator maintains persistent remainder state $r_{t-1} \in [0, D-1]$:
\begin{equation}
A_t = N_t + r_{t-1}, \qquad q_t = \left\lfloor \frac{A_t}{D} \right\rfloor, \qquad r_t = A_t \bmod D
\label{eq:ch1-carry-save}
\end{equation}
Summing over any number of ticks $T$ guarantees exact conservation: $\sum_{t=1}^{T} N_t = D \sum_{t=1}^{T} q_t + r_T - r_0$, bounding cumulative quantization error to strictly less than one physical quantum ($< 1\,\mu\mathrm{J}$) across decades of operation.
