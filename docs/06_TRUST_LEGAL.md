# Trust & Legal: The Layer 7 Proxy

The Sovereign Stack (Layers 1 through 6) operates purely on physics, cryptography, and peer consensus. However, physical land currently exists within the jurisdiction of the legacy nation-state. If The Collective ignores property taxes, zoning laws, or fiat liabilities, the state will execute a kinetic attack (foreclosure, eviction, or demolition) against the Genesis Node.

**Layer 7 (The Legacy Proxy)** is the API to the legacy world. It acts as an ablative shield. It speaks the language of fiat, courts, and bureaucracy so that the inner layers never have to. 

To achieve this without compromising the decentralized network, the architecture relies on two intersecting legal structures: The **Social Purpose Corporation (SPC)** and the **Perpetual Purpose Trust (PPT)**.

---

## 1. The Social Purpose Corporation (The Fiat Interface)

The local mesh cannot directly hold a fiat bank account or sign a legacy contract. It requires a legal wrapper. 

We utilize the **Social Purpose Corporation** (e.g., structured under Washington State law, RCW 23B.25), which legally permits the corporation's directors to prioritize a stated social/ecological mission over maximizing shareholder profit.

**Functions of the SPC:**
*   **Fiat Ingestion:** Acts as the merchant of record for "Trojan Services" (like the Fabrication Commons or Micro-Lodging). It ingests fiat via legacy payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal) and pays the legacy utility bills (The Legacy Tether).
*   **Liability Shield:** Absorbs all external legal liability for the Node's operations. 
*   **Legal Translation:** Takes the Layer 6 semantic credentials (like a student's apprenticeship transcript) and officially translates them into legally recognized, accredited formats for the legacy world.

---

## 2. The Perpetual Purpose Trust (The Sovereign Anchor)

Corporations can be bought, sold, or subjected to hostile takeovers by legacy capital. To prevent The Collective from being acquired, the SPC (and ideally the physical land itself) is wholly owned by a **Perpetual Purpose Trust (PPT)**.

Unlike a standard trust, a PPT has no human beneficiaries. It does not exist to enrich a family lineage. It exists solely to fulfill a legally binding *Purpose*.

**The Legally Binding Purpose:**
The Trust Deed is hardcoded to recognize only the thermodynamic integrity of the local biome and the cryptographic consensus of the mesh. Its legal mandate is to:
1. Ensure the Ecological Replacement Cost (ERC) of the land is never exceeded.
2. Defend the physical assets of the Genesis Node from external liquidation.

Because the PPT holds 100% of the voting shares of the SPC, legacy investors cannot buy a controlling stake. The Node is permanently removed from the speculative real estate market.

---

## 3. The Interface: Bridging Layer 7 to Layer 5

How does a decentralized, cryptographic mesh legally direct a state-recognized corporation?

The SPC requires human directors (a legacy requirement). However, the Trust Deed strictly limits the agency of these directors. The legal framework mandates that the human directors of the SPC must execute the decisions formulated by the **Citizens' Assembly (Layer 5)**. 

**The Execution Loop:**
1.  **Layer 6 (Intent):** The community proposes a physical expansion (e.g., adding a communal foundry).
2.  **Layer 5 (Sortition):** The decentralized assembly reaches cryptographic consensus to proceed.
3.  **Layer 7 (Translation):** The cryptographic payload is printed as a formal board resolution. The human directors of the SPC are legally compelled by the Trust to file the necessary municipal permits and pay the fiat fees to shield the construction from HOA or city injunctions (Scenario Tau).

---

## 4. The Terminal Decoupling (Scenario Omega)

The ultimate design of Layer 7 is planned obsolescence. 

As the Genesis Node progressively severs its reliance on municipal water, legacy grid power, and external supply chains, the fiat overhead of the SPC drops toward zero. If the legacy state undergoes a catastrophic failure or hyperinflation, the SPC can be legally dissolved or abandoned. 

Because the physical assets (Layer 1), automation (Layer 4), and economy (Layer 3) are completely decoupled from fiat, the Node survives the severance. Layer 7 drops away, and The Collective continues operating seamlessly on Layers 1 through 6.
