## 1. Intended Scope & Domain Vision

Scenario Upsilon governs **how the settlement prepares for, absorbs, and recovers from severe ecological, climatic, and infrastructure shocks**—wildfires, smoke domes, atmospheric-river floods, prolonged heatwaves, ice storms, earthquakes, and cascading Layer 7 utility grid collapses.

Against the grim cultural backdrop of "doomer bunker survivalism" (where elites hoard canned goods and rifles behind locked gates), Scenario Upsilon engineers **Regenerative Landscape Buffering & Open-Hearth Disaster Solidarity**:
* **Pre-Emptive Ecological Buffering:** Partnering with the living watershed *years ahead of the storm* via biochar keyline sponge swales (`Scenario Mu`/`Eta`), hydrated deciduous coppice firebreaks, and decentralized cistern/battery reserves.
* **Sub-16ms Microgrid Islanding & 0-mW Bistable E-Ink Continuity:** Dropping smoothly into 5-tier islanded survival operation where critical clinical cold chains (`Chi`), potable water (`Beta`), HEPA clean-air shelters, and 1-bit E-Ink/LoRa coordination stay $100\%$ alive across $72+$ hours of zero solar input.
* **The Open-Hearth Neighbor Refuge & 72-Hour Sunset:** Opening warm, HEPA-filtered commons halls and hot `Scenario Gamma` soup to surrounding Layer 7 neighbors at **zero cost (`0 uJ` / `$0`)**, while enforcing a hard 72-hour hardware sunset on emergency triage delegations and a **Shock-Verified Commons Jubilee** for damaged sister nodes.

## 2. The Current Landscape (Layer 7) & Its Structural Problems

When climate disasters or infrastructure blackouts strike legacy suburbs and cities (Layer 7), communities suffer four compounding failures:

1. **Paved Impervious Landscapes & Wildfire Tinderboxes:** Concrete sprawl turns heavy rain into catastrophic flash floods while neglected monoculture timber plantations and unburied utility lines ignite deadly wildfire smoke plumes.
2. **Grid-Tied Inverter Blindness & Total Blackout Paralysis:** Ironically, most commercial residential solar arrays automatically shut off during a municipal grid outage (because they lack islanding contactors and black-start battery inverters), leaving solar-panelled homes freezing in the dark with spoiling refrigerators and dead internet routers.
3. **Price Gouging vs. Armed Bunker Paranoia:** Commercial stores run out of bottled water and generators within hours (or price-gouge desperate families), while survivalist prepper culture breeds distrust and isolation at the exact moment neighbors need mutual aid most.
4. **Permanent "State of Emergency" Power Grabs:** In historical political systems, temporary emergency powers granted to crisis commanders routinely fail to sunset after the crisis passes, hardening into permanent authoritarian rule.

## 3. Wide Feature Horizon

Scenario Upsilon replaces brittle utilities and bunker paranoia with five resilience loops:

1. **Pre-Emptive Landscape Sponge & Firebreak Earthworks:** Keyline-plowed swales charged with `Scenario Mu` *Terra Preta* biochar infiltrate extreme storm runoff into deep groundwater aquifers (preventing downstream flash flooding while recharging summer springs), while irrigated, grazed `Scenario Eta` willow/poplar/alder coppice belts and roof-ridge water sprinklers form living green firebreaks around every settlement.
2. **Sub-16ms Autonomous Black-Start & Five-Tier Contention Shedding:** When Layer 2 grid-frequency/voltage sensors detect a municipal or regional bus fault, solid-state/mechanical islanding breakers open in $< 16\,\mathrm{ms}$ (less than one AC cycle) and transition the node into deterministic **Five-Tier Load Shedding**:
   * *Tier 1 (Inviolable Life & Ecological Safety):* `Scenario Chi` clinical refrigeration ($275{,}150\text{–}277{,}150\,\mathrm{mK}$), oxygen concentrators, positive-pressure MERV-16/HEPA wildfire clean-air rooms, potable water sterilization, and 0-mW E-Ink / LoRa emergency beacons.
   * *Tier 2 (Hearth Baseline):* `Scenario Gamma` soup stockpots, `Scenario Delta`/`Phi` nursery and elder heating/cooling sanctuary zones.
   * *Tier 3–5 (Automatically Shed Until Surplus Returns):* High-draw `Scenario Theta` induction furnaces, `Scenario Alpha` CNC spindles, `Scenario Zero` CI build clusters, and non-essential desktop/LLM loads.
3. **1-Bit Bistable Black-Start E-Ink & Offline LoRa Mesh:** Even if $100\%$ of desktop computers and Wi-Fi access points are powered down during a multi-day blizzard, wall-mounted 0-mW bistable E-Ink station boards and ultra-low-power $915\,\mathrm{MHz}$ LoRa Reticulum pagers remain active for weeks on tiny backup cells.
4. **Zero-ID Open-Hearth Neighbor Refuge (`0 uJ` / `$0` Charged):** The moment a regional disaster or AQI smoke emergency is declared, the `Scenario Epsilon` Municipal Neighbor Portal switches automatically to **Open-Hearth Refuge Mode**—inviting surrounding Layer 7 neighbors inside for warm shelter, HEPA-filtered air, device charging, first aid (`Chi`), and hot `Scenario Gamma` soup with zero payment, zero tokens, and zero ID required.
5. **Hardware 72-Hour Emergency Sunset & Shock-Verified Commons Jubilee:** Any fast-track emergency coordination authority expires automatically in hardware after $259{,}200\,\mathrm{s}$ ($72\text{ hours}$) unless actively re-ratified by assembly vote; after the storm passes, hardware-attested damage reports (`MicrogridIslandingAndLandscapeAttestation`) trigger a **Shock-Verified Commons Jubilee** forgiving asymmetrically damaged inter-node `BCC` debts back to $0\,\mu\mathrm{J}$ and dispatching `Scenario Pi` reconstruction swarms.

## 4. The Layer 6 Surface: Needs, Intents, Semantic Representations & Application Lenses

### A. Human Needs & Canonical Intents
* **`EcologicalShockAndRefugeIntent`:** Activating islanded storm/smoke/flood posture, opening HEPA clean-air halls to surrounding neighbors (`Open-Hearth Refuge`), and mobilizing `Scenario Chi` / `Scenario Beta` relief rosters.
* **`MicrogridIslandingAndLandscapeAttestation`:** Hardware-signed Layer 2 telemetry proof recording sub-$16\,\mathrm{ms}$ breaker opening, 5-tier load shedding, cistern/battery `SoC_bp` trajectories, and physical storm/fire impact metrics.
* **`ShockVerifiedJubileeRatification`:** Forgiving bilateral `BCC` clearing deficits for a sister node whose crops, solar arrays, or workshops suffered verified ecological shock damage.
* **`EmergencySunsetReaffirmation` (or Automatic Expiry at $259{,}200\,\mathrm{s}$):** Returning all node workflows to ordinary peacetime governance the instant the 72-hour timer expires.

### B. Semantic Representations & Deterministic Read-Backs
* **Hard-Coded $259{,}200\,\mathrm{s}$ (72-Hour) Constitutional Sunset (`col-kmsd` / `col-commonsd`):** In `https://w3id.org/collective/v1/`, no `EcologicalShockAndRefugeIntent` schema can be instantiated with `durationSeconds > 259200`. At $t = 259{,}200\,\mathrm{s}$, the monotonic hardware clock reverts all emergency triage permissions to standard peacetime thresholds automatically.
* **Mandatory Jubilee & Re-Ratification Read-Back:** Extending an emergency posture past 72 hours or ratifying a `ShockVerifiedJubileeRatification` requires a **Deterministic Read-Back** showing verified Layer 2 sensor proofs of the ongoing hazard or damage alongside confirmation that $100\%$ of $R_0$ civil liberties and Safe-Harbor rights remain untouched.

## 5. Governance Across Human Scales ($N = 1 \longrightarrow N_s \ge 1, N_p > 1 \longrightarrow N_s > 1, N_p > 1$)

1. **Solo Steward Scale ($N = 1 \implies N_s = 1\text{ operator}, N_p = 1\text{ homesteader}$):**
   * *Context:* A solo homesteader riding out a 3-day ice storm or wildfire smoke event on a $48\,\mathrm{V}$ battery, rainwater cistern, and HEPA bedroom filter.
   * *Governance Model:* **Zero panic, autonomic 72-hour survival hold.** Within $< 16\,\mathrm{ms}$ of grid failure, the node sheds workshop/server loads automatically, keeps the stove, fridge, water pump, and HEPA filter running for $72+$ hours, and broadcasts a periodic "All OK on Lot 402" LoRa heartbeat to peered neighbors.
2. **Single / Core Steward Serving a Wider Circle ($N_s = 1\text{ or small core}, N_p > 1\text{ neighborhood block}$):**
   * *Context:* A Stage 1/2 suburban seed household or 5-home microgrid pod opening its warm living room/commons hall, solar charging strip, and `Scenario Gamma` soup pot to $15\text{–}50$ dark, freezing suburban neighbors during a municipal power outage ($N_p > 1$).
   * *Governance Model:*
     * **Turning Crisis into Neighbor Solidarity:** Nothing dissolves suburban suspicion faster than sharing hot pea soup, clean air, and phone charging when the commercial grid fails. The Open-Hearth Refuge operates strictly inside the $R_1$ common hall/porch while keeping residents' private $R_0$ bedrooms latched and peaceful.
     * **Automated Elder & Infant Welfare Checks:** When an outage or heatwave hits, `col-commonsd` prompts the block stewards to check on any `Scenario Phi` elders or `Scenario Delta` infants on the street within the first 30 minutes.
3. **Multi-Steward Guild Serving a Community & Bioregion ($N_s > 1\text{ stewards}, N_p > 1\text{ community & peer nodes}$):**
   * *Context:* A watershed Resilience, Hydrology & Fire-Stewardship Guild ($N_s = 20\text{–}100+$ stewards) managing keyline flood basins, cultural prescribed burns, regional grain/medical reserves, and inter-node emergency relief convoys across $R_2 \dots R_5$.
   * *Governance Model:*
     * **Anti-Authoritarian 72-Hour Sunset:** Even during a major flood or wildfire, emergency dispatch coordinators hold **zero power to suspend the Commons Chamber, zero power to requisition personal $R_0$ property, and zero power to extend their mandate past $259{,}200\,\mathrm{s}$ (72 hours)** without a democratic assembly vote.
     * **Shock-Verified Commons Jubilee:** Prevents ecological disasters from turning damaged sister nodes into permanent debtors; verified flood or fire loss automatically zeroes out bilateral `BCC` deficits and mobilizes `Scenario Pi`/`Xi` rebuilding swarms.
