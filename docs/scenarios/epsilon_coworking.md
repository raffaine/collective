## 1. Problem Statement, Legacy Failure & Macro-Domain Vision

In late-stage financialized capitalism (Layer 7), cognitive work, business administration, computing infrastructure, and civic "Third Places" suffer from four structural pathologies:
1.  **Administrative & Legal Barrier to Entry for Grassroots Commons:** A neighborhood woodshop (Scenario Alpha), community kitchen (Scenario Gamma), or parent childcare circle (Scenario Delta) rarely fails for lack of physical skill—it fails because navigating municipal zoning codes, commercial general liability insurance, Subchapter T tax accounting, B2B wholesale purchasing, and property leases requires specialized legal and administrative overhead that small physical nodes cannot shoulder alone.
2.  **The Fiat Capture Bottleneck:** While transitioning toward bioregional autarky ($D_t \to 0$), every nascent settlement still faces mandatory external Layer 7 fiat obligations ($\Phi_t$): municipal property taxes, commercial leases, wholesale mineral/electronic imports, and ISP uplinks. Physical manufacturing and agricultural nodes have high material and land overhead per unit of external fiat earned, whereas knowledge work, engineering design, software development, media production, and shared workspace passes can harvest external fiat at high margins with minimal ecological draw.
3.  **Spatial Segregation & Atomization:** Legacy zoning isolates offices in sterile commuter business parks, separates parents from their children in distant daycares, and forces remote workers into lonely bedrooms or noisy commercial coffee shops that demand continuous purchases every two hours.
4.  **Surveillance Coworking & Wasted Compute Heat:** Corporate coworking chains deploy facial-recognition cameras, cloud-tethered smart locks, and network snooping, while corporate data centers burn megawatts of electricity on air-conditioning chillers to dump server heat into the atmosphere instead of heating neighboring homes and greenhouses.

### The Collective Macro-Domain Vision
Scenario Epsilon is **The Business, Administrative, Compute & Shared Layer 7 Carapace Commons of The Collective**. Far beyond a simple room of desks, Scenario Epsilon serves **six foundational macro-domain functions**:
1.  **The Cooperative Business & Administrative Back-Office (`Carapace-as-a-Service`):** Providing shared legal, tax, accounting, zoning compliance, insurance pooling, and Group Purchasing Organization (GPO) procurement for the entire local cluster of nodes (Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Eta, Nu).
2.  **The Primary Layer 7 Fiat Revenue-Capture Engine ($\Phi_t$):** Hosting outward-facing cooperative consultancies (software, CAD/structural engineering, ecological architecture, legal/CPA services, studio media) and external drop-in coworking memberships that cleanly and rapidly fund the settlement's bounded monthly fiat target $\Phi_t$ with minimal ecological footprint.
3.  **Multi-Domain Spatial Co-Location & Mixed-Use Symbiosis:** Architecturally integrating quiet deep-work and administrative studios with **Scenario Alpha** (fabrication/prototyping), **Scenario Gamma** (commons cafe & tiffin larder), **Scenario Delta** (on-site parent-proximate childcare pods), and **Scenario Iota** (epistemic libraries) under shared roofs and shared utility buses.
4.  **Sovereign Compute Vaults, CAD/Simulation Workstations & Hydronic Exergy Recapture:** Housing the settlement's high-density compute racks (running local AI/LLMs, OpenSCAD/FEA engineering, Oasis `L1b`, and `col-storaged` archival mirrors) with liquid-cooled heat exchangers feeding directly into the building's radiant heating and greenhouse loops.
5.  **Convivial Acoustic & Network Stewardship:** Zero-surveillance BLE access, hardware-enforced IEEE 802-1Q VLAN isolation, `SkillBeacon` serendipity matching, and restorative acoustic zoning.
6.  **Incremental Node Facility Blueprints (Bootstrap to Multi-Domain Guild Campus):** Scaling smoothly from a domestic home-office/server rack (`Stage 1`) to a leased mixed-use storefront (`Stage 2`) to a custom-built or ecologically deconstructed Multi-Domain Guild Campus (`Stage 3`).
