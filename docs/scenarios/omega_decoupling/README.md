## 1. Intended Scope & Domain Vision

Scenario Omega is the **twenty-fifth and culminating horizon** toward which all preceding twenty-four scenarios converge. It governs **how a mature bioregional federation tracks the obsolescence of its external Layer 7 scaffolding ($D_t \to 0$), lawfully dissolves its temporary corporate wrappers after permanently extinguishing property-tax foreclosure vectors, and graduates into Ecologically Sustainable Post-Scarcity**.

Crucially, Scenario Omega defines terminal decoupling **not as an isolated survivalist retreat from humanity, but as an open-armed graduation into bioregional federation and planetary solidarity**:
* **The Five-Domain Dependency Horizon ($D_t \le 200\text{–}500\,\mathrm{bp}$):** Measuring real physical autonomy across all five civilizational domains—Exergy/Grid (`Beta`/`Upsilon`), Nutrition/Water (`Gamma`/`Eta`/`Mu`), Materials/Tooling (`Alpha`/`Theta`/`Nu`/`Xi`), Medicine/Chemistry (`Chi`/`Phi`/`Sigma`), and Compute/Governance (`Zero`/`Iota`/`Kappa`/`Lambda`/`Pi`/`Rho`/`Psi`/`Tau`)—before retiring external commercial scaffolding.
* **Lawful Statutory Sunset & Eternal Land Lock:** Capitalizing an **Irrevocable Property Tax Escrow Endowment** at $10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($100\%$) and conveying all land into unencumbered **Community Land Trusts (`CLTs`) / Perpetual Purpose Trusts (`PPTs`)** before filing formal Articles of Dissolution and zeroizing `col-adversaryd`.
* **Ecologically Sustainable Post-Scarcity & The Planetary Lighthouse:** Dropping necessary maintenance stewardship to $\le 15\text{ hours/week}$ while keeping the **$R_6$ Planetary Lighthouse & Genesis Seedbed Bridge** permanently open—freely broadcasting medical cures, open-source software, hardware blueprints, and Mother-Machine seed kits to help the rest of humanity transition.

## 2. The Current Landscape (Layer 7) & Its Structural Problems

Communities attempting to build post-capitalist alternatives within or alongside Layer 7 historically hit four terminal traps:

1. **The Perpetual Debt & Tax Treadmill:** Even when a community builds its own debt-free housing and gardens, ongoing municipal property taxes and commercial bank reliance force residents to keep selling labor or commodities into the extractive market forever—or risk losing their land at a county tax-deed foreclosure auction.
2. **Post-Mortem Probate Liquidation:** When founding members of a conventional co-housing group or cooperative pass away, legacy probate courts, estate taxes, and estranged heirs can force the partition and commercial sale of the community's land and workshop tools.
3. **Premature Ideological Severance ("Leap Off a Cliff"):** Historical communes that severed ties with the outside world on day one—before establishing local metallurgy (`Theta`), analytical pharmaceutical synthesis (`Chi`), or multi-node tooling redundancy (`Alpha`/`Pi`)—collapsed as soon as a single critical transformer, antibiotic stock, or bearing wore out.
4. **Elitist "Lifeboat" Bunker Isolationism:** Conversely, wealthy survivalist compounds treat self-sufficiency as a private fortress to hoard resources while abandoning surrounding towns and the rest of humanity to ecological and economic collapse.

## 3. Wide Feature Horizon

Scenario Omega resolves all four traps through five graduation mechanisms:

1. **The Five-Domain Weakest-Link Dependency Observatory ($D_t$):** Layer 3 and Layer 6 continuously track the settlement's physical import-dependency ratio across all five vital domains. Terminal graduation cannot be proposed until **both** the weighted composite $D_t$ and **every single individual domain $D_d$** fall below the constitutional threshold ($\le 500\,\mathrm{bp}$ / $5.0\%$ for initial severance readiness, converging to $\le 200\,\mathrm{bp}$ / $2.0\%$ across 12 consecutive lunar cycles)—ensuring a $100\%$ solar energy surplus never masks a $40\%$ dependency on imported insulin or bearings.
2. **100% Capitalized Irrevocable Property Tax Escrow Endowment & `CLT`/`PPT` Lock:** Before retiring its commercial Subchapter T carapace (`Scenario Epsilon`), the node fully funds an actuarial **Irrevocable Property Tax Escrow Endowment** held by an independent regulated trust company. Its conservative annual yield pays county property tax assessments directly to the county treasurer in perpetuity—**permanently extinguishing the municipal tax-foreclosure vector** while all fee-simple land and buildings rest inside an unencumbered **Perpetual Purpose Trust (Uniform Trust Code Section 409) / Community Land Trust (`CLT`)**.
3. **Estate Liberation Protocol (Closing the Loop with `Tau`, `Omicron` & `Phi`):** When elders pass (`Scenario Phi`), zero probate courts liquidate the commons: personal $R_0$ heirlooms pass directly to chosen kin (`Scenario Omicron`), their DID becomes a permanent `Wisdom_Anchor`, and all $R_2$ guild tools return seamlessly to `Scenario Kappa` apprentices (`EstateLiberationIntent`).
4. **Lawful Carapace Sunset, `< 100 ms` Zeroization & Clean Socket Severance:** Following an **Entrenched Core $8000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($80\%$) double supermajority vote**, a **60-day islanded cooling-off drill**, and a final reaffirmation vote, the cooperative files formal Articles of Dissolution (e.g., Title 23B RCW / MBCA Section 14-05), terminates `col-adversaryd`, overwrites all Layer 7 database/WAL/SHM files with CSPRNG + zeroes in $< 100\,\mathrm{ms}$, and unlinks `/run/collective/ipc/l6_l7.sock` forever (`col-commonsd` and `col-execd` containing `EPIPE`/`EOF` cleanly).
5. **Ecologically Sustainable Post-Scarcity & The Planetary Lighthouse Seedbed:** With financial rent, compounding interest, and planned obsolescence eliminated, necessary maintenance stewardship stabilizes at **$\le 15\text{ hours/week}$ ($900\,\mathrm{minutes/week}$)** per steward with $B_{\mathrm{baseline}} = 10{,}000\,\mathrm{bp}$ guaranteed—liberating human life for STEAM science (`Iota`), arts and festivals (`Lambda`), open virtual worlds (`Zero`), and watershed rewilding (`Eta`). Meanwhile, the decoupled federation dedicates $\ge 500\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($5\%$) of its annual industrial surplus to fabricating **Genesis Seed Kits** (solar/water skids + Mother-Machine CNCs + heirloom seeds + `T4` mentors) and broadcasting all `Chi`/`Zero`/`Alpha`/`Iota` discoveries freely via the **$R_6$ Planetary Lighthouse** until the entire planet transitions.

## 5. Governance Across Human Scales ($N = 1 \longrightarrow N_s \ge 1, N_p > 1 \longrightarrow N_s > 1, N_p > 1$)

1. **Solo Steward Scale ($N = 1 \implies N_s = 1\text{ operator}, N_p = 1\text{ homesteader}$):**
   * *Context:* A solo homesteader at Stage 1A tracking their household dependency ratio $D_t$.
   * *Governance Model:* **Honest anti-autarky realism & progressive liberation.** Scenario Omega teaches a solo homesteader *why* $N = 1$ isolated autarky is a myth (a solo lot can reach $90\%$ solar and vegetable self-sufficiency, reducing monthly fiat bills dramatically, but can never solo-manufacture its own `HPLC` medicines or induction alloys). Instead, the Dependency Horizon Observatory shows the solo steward how each household improvement lowers their personal $\Phi_t$ bill while guiding them to peer with $R_1 / R_2$ neighbors (`Scenario Pi`).
2. **Neighborhood / Town Cluster Scale ($N_s = 10\text{–}50\text{ guild stewards}, N_p = 50\text{–}500\text{ residents}$):**
   * *Context:* A Stage 1B / Stage 2 neighborhood mesh and cooperative hub crossing the **$D_t \le 5000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($50\%$) Halfway Gate**.
   * *Governance Model:*
     * **The Halfway Gate Dividend:** Even before full Stage 3 decoupling, driving $D_t$ below $50\%$ across food (`Gamma`), shelter (`Zeta`), tools (`Alpha`), and childcare (`Delta`) liberates households from paycheck-to-paycheck wage coercion and funds the growing Property Tax Escrow Endowment.
     * **Targeted Import Substitution via `Pi` Beacons:** The cluster uses its 5-Domain Observatory to spot whichever domain lags behind (e.g., $D_{\mathrm{medicine}}$ or $D_{\mathrm{materials}}$) and emits `MeshGrowthBeacon` invitations to stand up the missing lab or foundry.
3. **Bioregional Federation Scale ($N_s > 1\text{ federated guilds}, N_p = 500\text{–}10{,}000+\text{ watershed residents}$):**
   * *Context:* A full multi-node watershed federation ($R_3 \dots R_5$) sustaining $D_t \le 200\text{–}500\,\mathrm{bp}$ across all 25 scenarios.
   * *Governance Model:*
     * **Entrenched Core $8000\,\mathrm{bp}$ Double Supermajority + 60-Day Islanded Drill:** Terminal dissolution of the corporate carapace requires an $80\%$ supermajority in both the Commons and Stewardship Chambers, a verified 60-day fully islanded operational drill, and a final reaffirmation vote.
     * **Constitutional Ban on Bunker Isolationism (`Planetary Lighthouse Duty`):** The federation's constitution permanently locks in the **Open-Hearth Refuge** (`Upsilon`), **Multi-Pathway Onboarding** (`Tau`), **Global Commons Broadcast**, and **$\ge 500\,\mathrm{bp}$ Genesis Seedbed Allocation**—ensuring that every decoupled bioregion acts as a catalyst to emancipate the next watershed.
