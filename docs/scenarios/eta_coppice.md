## 1. Problem Statement & Legacy Failure

In legacy industrial capitalism (Layer 7), forests are treated as dead standing lumber warehouses, while wild fungi, forest foods, and botanical medicines are either eradicated by herbicides or enclosed by pharmaceutical patents:

1.  **Clear-Cutting, Soil Compaction & Mycorrhizal Extinction:** Industrial logging clear-cuts entire watersheds with $30\text{-ton}$ diesel feller-bunchers, compacting rhizosphere soil pores, severing ancient ectomycorrhizal fungal networks, and spraying glyphosate to kill nitrogen-fixing deciduous "weed trees" like Red Alder and Birch so monoculture conifer plantations can be planted in sterile soil.
2.  **Destructive Slash Burning & Fossil Kilns:** Up to $4000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($40\%$) of felled tree biomass (branches, tops, bark) is piled and burned in open air, choking valleys with particulate smoke while timber is hauled hundreds of miles on diesel trucks to fossil-gas-fired kilns.
3.  **Loss of Forest Gastronomy & Synthetic Pharmacopoeia:** Legacy agriculture bulldozes complex forest ecosystems to plant annual grain monocultures, destroying native mast trees, wild berries, and symbiotic edible fungi. Simultaneously, legacy medicine isolates single synthetic molecules in petrochemical labs while discarding the synergistic immunomodulatory, neuro-regenerative, and antimicrobial phytochemistry of living forest ecosystems.
4.  **Land Speculation & Timber REITs:** Timber Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) liquidate mature stands on $25\text{-year}$ financialized discount cycles to pay Wall Street dividends, leaving rural communities with eroded hillsides, silted salmon rivers, and high wildfire risk.

## 2. Macro-Domain Architecture: The Six Pillars of the Forest, Mycological & Apothecary Commons

Scenario Eta transforms forestry into the **Silviculture, Heavy Structural Timber, Mycorrhizal Wild Ecosystems, Forest Gastronomy & Botanical/Mycological Apothecary Commons**. It operates across six tightly integrated pillars that chain directly to Scenarios Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Zeta, Mu, Nu, Xi, Phi, and Chi:

### Pillar I: Multi-Tier Silviculture & Heavy Structural Timber Sourcing (`Coppice-with-Standards` & Solar Milling)
Rather than choosing between thin firewood poles or destructive clear-cutting, Scenario Eta manages forests as multi-layered **Coppice-with-Standards** and **Continuous-Cover Selection Forests**:
*   **Understory Rotational Coppice ($5\text{–}12\,\mathrm{Year}$ Cycle):** Fast-growing nitrogen-fixing Red Alder (*Alnus rubra*), rot-resistant Black Locust (*Robinia pseudoacacia*), Hazel (*Corylus avellana*), Sweet Chestnut (*Castanea sativa*), and Willow (*Salix*) are coppiced at ground level during winter dormancy ($T_{\mathrm{ambient}} < 278150\,\mathrm{mK}$ / $5^\circ\mathrm{C}$). The established living root stool and its mycorrhizal fungal partners remain undisturbed, sending up vigorous straight poles for tool handles, woven wattle, mushroom logs, furniture stock, and seasoned hearth fuel.
*   **Overstory Heavy Structural "Standards" ($60\text{–}120\,\mathrm{Year}$ Selective & Windfall Harvest):** Rising above the coppice understory, spaced canopy standards—Douglas Fir (*Pseudotsuga menziesii*), Western Red Cedar (*Thuja plicata*), White Oak (*Quercus alba*), and mature Black Locust—grow into massive, clear-grained structural trunks. Standards are harvested strictly via **single-tree selection** or **storm windfall salvage** using low-impact portable electric winches, suspended skyline cables, or draft-animal skidding—**never** heavy tracked machinery that crushes soil structure.
*   **On-Site Electric Bandsaw Milling & Solar-Thermal Dehumidification Kilns:** Heavy logs are milled on-site via $48\,\mathrm{V}$ / $380\,\mathrm{V}$ DC electric horizontal bandsaw mills into heavy structural beams, mortise-and-tenon posts, live-edge slabs, and Cross-Laminated Timber (CLT) lamellas for [Scenario Alpha (`alpha_fabrication.md`)](file:///Users/raffaine/dev/collective/docs/scenarios/alpha_fabrication.md), [Scenario Zeta (`zeta_lodging.md`)](file:///Users/raffaine/dev/collective/docs/scenarios/zeta_lodging.md), and [Scenario Xi (`xi_architecture.md`)](file:///Users/raffaine/dev/collective/docs/scenarios/xi_architecture.md). Milled lumber is stacked in solar-thermal heat-pump dehumidification kilns (supplemented by pyrolysis syngas heat) until pin-type resistance probes confirm structural equilibrium moisture content $\le 1200\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($12\%$) for joinery/CLT timber and $< 2000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($20\%$) for thermal firewood.

Scenario Eta treats the subterranean **mycorrhizal fungal network** as the primary vital organ of the forest:
*   **Mother-Tree Hub Protection (`Protocol of Refusal`):** In a mature forest, ancient "Mother Trees" are linked to hundreds of neighboring trees and understory seedlings through shared ectomycorrhizal and arbuscular mycorrhizal fungal mycelium, shuttling fixed carbon, phosphorus, water, and insect-defense chemical warnings across species boundaries. Layer 5 cryptographically registers mapped Mother Trees and old-growth groves under the **Protocol of Refusal** (`is_mother_tree_sanctuary == true`), making it mathematically impossible for Layer 4 (`col-execd`) to schedule felling within their root-crown protection radius.

*   **Coarse Woody Debris Floor ($F_{\mathrm{eco}} \ge 3000\,\mathrm{bp}$):** At least $3000\,\mathrm{bp}$ ($30\%$) of natural deadfall, standing snags, and nurse logs are permanently left on the forest floor to shelter salamanders, cavity-nesting owls, nitrogen-fixing bacteria, and saprotrophic wood-decay fungi.
*   **Native Mycorrhizal Inoculant Propagation:** Forest stewards cultivate native ectomycorrhizal spore slurries and undisturbed forest leaf-mold blocks to inoculate new tree plantings and regenerate compacted agricultural soils in [Scenario Gamma (`gamma_kitchen.md`)](file:///Users/raffaine/dev/collective/docs/scenarios/gamma_kitchen.md).

### Pillar IV: Sovereign Forest Apothecary — Botanical & Mycological Pharmaceuticals (Chaining to Scenario Chi & Phi)
Plants and fungi synthesize complex secondary metabolites (terpenoids, alkaloids, phenolic glycosides, immunomodulatory polysaccharides) to defend against pathogens and communicate across the rhizosphere.     uint64_t coarse_woody_debris_floor_mg{3200000000ULL}; // >= 3000 bp (30%) deadwood floor
    uint64_t local_heating_and_build_baseline_mg{2000000000ULL};
    uint64_t requested_harvest_mg{1500000000ULL};
    uint16_t structural_beam_moisture_bp{1140};    // Must be <= 1200 bp (12%) for CLT/joinery

inline bool verify_silviculture_and_mycorrhizal_gates(const ForestQuadrantState& q,
                                                      uint64_t& export_surplus_avail_mg) {
    // Constitutional Protocol of Refusal: Mother-Tree Sanctuaries can NEVER be felled
    if (q.is_mother_tree_sanctuary) return false;

    // Winter dormancy (< 278,150 mK = 5 C), bio-acoustics (H' >= 28000), and mycorrhizal ergosterol (>= 150 ug/g)
    if (!q.winter_dormancy_active || q.ambient_temp_mk >= 278150) return false;
    if (q.shannon_biodiversity_scaled < 28000) return false;
    if (q.rhizosphere_ergosterol_ug_per_g < 150) return false;

    // Coarse woody debris floor must be >= 3000 bp (30%) of standing biomass for saprotrophic fungi & fauna
    if (q.standing_biomass_mg == 0) return false;
    uint64_t cwd_ratio_bp = (q.coarse_woody_debris_floor_mg * 10000ULL) / q.standing_biomass_mg;
    if (cwd_ratio_bp < 3000) return false;

    // Calculate regenerative surplus Q_avail = max(0, Q_inventory - F_eco - B_baseline)
    uint64_t protected_mass_mg = q.coarse_woody_debris_floor_mg + q.local_heating_and_build_baseline_mg;
    if (q.standing_biomass_mg <= protected_mass_mg) {
        export_surplus_avail_mg = 0;
        return false;
    }
    export_surplus_avail_mg = q.standing_biomass_mg - protected_mass_mg;
    if (q.requested_harvest_mg > export_surplus_avail_mg) return false;

    // Verify Solar-Kiln moisture bounds: <= 1200 bp for structural timber, < 2000 bp for firewood
    if (q.structural_beam_moisture_bp > 1200 || q.firewood_moisture_bp >= 2000) return false;
    return true;
}
