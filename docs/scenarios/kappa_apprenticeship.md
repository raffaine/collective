## 1. Problem Statement & Legacy Failure

In legacy capitalist infrastructure (Layer 7), the validation of human skill and preparedness is broken in two opposite, equally dangerous ways:

1.  **Paper Credentialism Disconnected from Embodied Preparedness:** Commercial universities and online certification mills issue degrees based on written exams and tuition payments without evaluating whether the graduate has the somatic composure, situational awareness, or tactile skill to safely handle high-energy machinery, clinical emergencies, or ecological interventions.
2.  **The Debt-Gated Licensing Cartel:** Where practical trade schools and professional boards do exist, access is rationed behind tens of thousands of dollars in compounding student debt, artificial guild quotas, and bureaucratic gatekeeping designed to inflate commercial prices rather than spread community self-reliance.
3.  **Deskilling, Gig-Economy Atomization & Workplace Surveillance:** Instead of investing in master-apprentice lineages that cultivate deep diagnostic mastery, legacy corporations deskill workers into disposable gig operators monitored by invasive AI workplace cameras, keystroke loggers, and posture trackers—leading to high injury rates and the extinction of tacit repair craft.
