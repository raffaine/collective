## 1. Intended Scope & Domain Vision

Scenario Psi governs **how the settlement prevents, de-escalates, mediates, and heals interpersonal harm, broken commitments, ecological damage, and irreconcilable philosophical divergence**.

It moves human society decisively **beyond the dominion and vengeance paradigm** (the belief that justice means punishment, cages, or public humiliation) while avoiding the naive commune trap of sweeping serious harm under the rug:
* **Immediate Safe-Harbor Protection Before Any Mediation (`< 500 ms` Abuse Bypass):** Never forcing a survivor of domestic intimidation, stalking, or violence into a room with an aggressor—routing safety threats immediately to unilateral `Scenario Omicron` Safe-Harbor separation and spatial `No-Transit` boundaries.
* **Transformative Root-Cause Inquiry (Systemic + Personal):** Asking not only *"Who did harm and how do they make restitution?"* but *"What underlying systemic condition in our workflows—exhaustion (`Sigma`), poor mentorship (`Kappa`), or noisy/scarce tools (`Alpha`)—contributed to this conflict, and how do we fix the system?"*
* **Standing for the Living Watershed ($F_{\mathrm{eco}}$ Restitution) & The Right of Graceful Fission:** Granting the living ecosystem standing for restorative healing when land or water is harmed, and guaranteeing that sub-communities with irreconcilable visions can fork and part as peaceful federated neighbors without civil war or asset theft.

## 2. The Current Landscape (Layer 7) & Its Structural Problems

Both state carceral systems (Layer 7) and informal community groups fail at conflict resolution in four predictable ways:

1. **Retributive Vengeance & Carceral Cages:** Legacy criminal courts equate "justice" with state vengeance—locking human beings in cages that traumatize perpetrators, bankrupt families, leave victims without material restitution, and do nothing to repair the root cause of harm.
2. **Adversarial Litigation & Lawyer Extortion:** Civil disputes between neighbors or cooperative partners are dragged into multi-year court battles where hourly legal fees consume the entire value of the shared enterprise.
3. **Public Shaming Pillories & Algorithmic Call-Out Mobs:** Online platforms and dysfunctional activist spaces replace due process with viral public shaming, permanent digital stigmas, and factional dogpiling that make honest accountability impossible.
4. **Forced "Peace-Washing" of Domestic Abuse or Factional Lock-In:** Conversely, informal communes without clear boundaries often pressure abuse survivors to "forgive and mediate" with an unsafe person, or trap dissenting philosophical factions inside a single deadlocked assembly until the community explodes.

## 3. Wide Feature Horizon

Scenario Psi replaces cages, courts, and call-out mobs with five transformative justice capabilities:

1. **Immediate Coercion/Abuse Safe-Harbor Bypass (`< 500 ms`):** The very first gate of Scenario Psi is a hard safety screen: if any party reports physical violence, sexual coercion, stalking, or intimidation, Layer 6 **forbids scheduling a mediation circle** and immediately triggers `Scenario Omicron`'s `< 500 ms` `SafeHarborSeveranceIntent`, rotates `ATECC608A` door locks, provisions a private refuge suite, and compiles a strict **Spatial `No-Transit` Separation Polygon** (`Scenario Beta`) barring the respondent from the survivor's residential and workshop zones.
2. **Cooling-Off Buffers & Unmediated Peace Hearths (Zero Audio Recording):** For ordinary neighbor, housemate, or guild disputes, Layer 6 inserts a calm somatic cooling-off hold (preventing hot-headed midnight message wars) before convening a restorative circle facilitated by sortition-selected (`VRF`), household-independent `Scenario Kappa` `T3_Mediator` stewards inside an **Unmediated Peace Hearth**—where the physical brass kill-switch severs all microphones and cameras (`0` audio/transcript logs) so people can speak honestly, apologize, and weep without permanent digital surveillance.
3. **Transformative Root-Cause Feedback (`SystemicRootCauseIntent`):** Alongside personal restitution (`RestorativeCompact`), every circle conducts a blame-free systems check: if two stewards clashed because a shared lathe was chronically double-booked or an unvented kitchen reached $310{,}000\,\mathrm{mK}$ ($37^\circ\mathrm{C}$), the circle emits a `SystemicRootCauseIntent` commissioning a `Scenario Alpha` tool build or `Scenario Xi` ventilation fix!
4. **Ecological Restitution for the Living Watershed ($F_{\mathrm{eco}}$ Standing):** When a reckless action harms the non-human commons (e.g., cutting inside a `Scenario Eta` Mother-Tree Sanctuary Polygon, discharging un-neutralized vat effluent in `Scenario Nu`, or bypassing a `Scenario Mu` taint lock), the watershed itself is represented by an Ecological Advocate; restitution takes the form of supervised tree planting, riparian restoration, and a temporary step-down of the steward's hazardous `Kappa` tool tier back to mentored `T1_Supervised` mode.
5. **The Constitutional Right of Graceful Fission (`GracefulFissionArchitect`):** When two sub-groups inside a node or guild discover a deep, good-faith philosophical divergence over lifestyle or priorities that no circle can bridge, neither side can purge or subjugate the other. Instead, the **Graceful Fission Architect** computes an equitable, deterministic partition of shared tool/exergy endowments and forks the CRDT/JSON-LD namespaces cleanly—allowing the departing group to seed an autonomous sister node (`Scenario Tau`/`Pi`) while remaining peaceful watershed peers.

## 4. The Layer 6 Surface: Needs, Intents, Semantic Representations & Application Lenses

### A. Human Needs & Canonical Intents
* **`TransformativeMediationIntent`:** Requesting a cooling-off hold and a sortition-selected restorative circle for a neighbor, guild, or treaty dispute (automatically routed to `< 500 ms` Safe-Harbor bypass if coercion/safety risk is flagged).
* **`MediationResolutionAttestation` (`RestorativeCompact`):** The 3-of-3 physical NFC co-sign (both parties + independent mediator) recording only the agreed forward-looking restitution actions and completion milestones—with **zero public shaming narrative or transcript**.
* **`SystemicRootCauseIntent`:** Routing an identified structural trigger (e.g., ergonomic strain, tool scarcity, acoustic spillover, or ambiguous guild scheduling) to `Scenario Sigma`, `Alpha`, `Xi`, or `Pi` for permanent engineering/policy repair.
* **`GracefulFissionProposal`:** Initiating a non-punitive, proportional fork of a guild or settlement pod into two sovereign, federated sibling nodes.

### B. Semantic Representations & Deterministic Read-Backs
* **Zero-Permanence of Personal Accusations (`No Scarlet Letter Graph`):** `https://w3id.org/collective/v1/` is constitutionally forbidden from storing public accusation walls, criminal rap sheets, or permanent social-credit demerits. While an active `RestorativeCompact` tracks specific temporary safety bounds (such as a `No-Transit` separation polygon or a `Kappa` `T1_Supervised` mentorship requirement) in `col-kmsd`, once restitution is completed, the dispute state closes cleanly.
* **Mandatory Restorative & Fission Read-Back:** Before parties tap their hardware keys on a `MediationResolutionAttestation` or `GracefulFissionProposal`, their slates present a **Deterministic Read-Back** of the exact restitution hours, `Kappa` supervision adjustments, or proportional asset/BCC partition ratios.

## 5. Governance Across Human Scales ($N = 1 \longrightarrow N_s \ge 1, N_p > 1 \longrightarrow N_s > 1, N_p > 1$)

1. **Solo / Dyadic Scale ($N = 1 \text{ or } 2 \implies$ Individual Boundary & Two-Neighbor Friction):**
   * *Context:* Two adjoining homesteaders or housemates experiencing friction over a shared tool return, garden boundary, or late-night workshop noise.
   * *Governance Model:* **Direct 1-on-1 conversation assisted by objective telemetry & cooling-off.** Before escalating to any formal circle, the stewards can inspect neutral hardware facts (e.g., `Scenario Lambda` lot-boundary decibel logs or `Scenario Alpha` tool-locker timestamps) after a 12-hour cooling-off pause, resolving $90\%$ of everyday misunderstandings over tea.
2. **Household / Pod Scale ($N_s = 1\text{–}3\text{ mediators}, N_p = 5\text{–}30\text{ pod members}$):**
   * *Context:* Interpersonal tension, unfulfilled Work-Bee shifts, or a safety shortcut inside a neighborhood co-housing pod or small guild ($N_p > 1$).
   * *Governance Model:*
     * **Independent Sortition Mediator (No Insider Bias):** If a small pod cannot resolve a rift internally, `col-kmsd`'s Verifiable Random Function (`VRF`) draws a trained `T3_Mediator` from an **adjacent** neighborhood ring ($R_2$) who has zero household or kinship overlap (`Scenario Omicron`) with anyone in the pod.
     * **Unconditional Right of Exit (`0` Outbound Sheriff Calls):** If a person repeatedly refuses safe community boundaries and rejects all restorative compacts, the community's ultimate boundary is **non-violent dissociation and dignified exit**—provisioning the departing person with their personal $R_0$ belongings and travel transition support, with **zero cages, zero prisons, and zero outbound punitive police calls**.
3. **Multi-Steward Community & Bioregional Federation ($N_s > 1\text{ stewards}, N_p > 1\text{ peer nodes}$):**
   * *Context:* Inter-guild resource contention or a treaty dispute between two watershed nodes ($R_2 \dots R_5$).
   * *Governance Model:*
     * **Cross-Watershed Restorative Arbitration Panels:** Inter-node `Treaty_CID` disputes convene a 3-node sortition panel drawn from neutral $R_3 / R_4$ peers, inspecting hardware-signed Layer 2/Layer 3 delivery and ecological telemetry.
     * **Peaceful Cellular Fission Over Factional Capture:** Rather than fighting a bitter 51%-vs.-49% political war for control of a large settlement, the **Right of Graceful Fission** guarantees that any coherent sub-community can fork peacefully with its proportional share of productive commons and establish a sister node in the patchwork (`Scenario Pi`).
