## 1. Problem Statement, Legacy Failure & Extending the Immune System to Humanity

While **Scenario Rho (`SCN-RHO-ADVERSARY`)** defends The Collective against technical, legal, and topological attacks, history proves that cooperative societies rarely die only from external sieges—they most often fracture from **internal human exhaustion, invisible caregiver burnout, and social resentment**. Legacy infrastructure (Layer 7) and naive communes fail across five human dimensions:

1.  **The "Hero / Martyr" Syndrome (The $k = 1$ Human Bottleneck):** In high-purpose communities, open-source projects (`Zero`), clinics (`Chi`), and workshops (`Alpha`), a small handful of hyper-conscientious stewards inevitably shoulder the hardest, most urgent tasks. Without a structural immune response, the community unconsciously relies on these "martyrs" until they suffer catastrophic burnout, chronic illness, or bitter departure—collapsing both the person and the node capability they held together.
2.  **The Gendered & Classist Exploitation of Invisible Labor:** Capitalist economies extract trillions of hours of unpaid domestic/reproductive care and underpaid custodial toil, treating the maintenance of life as low-status servitude.
3.  **The Dystopia of Gig-Economy Chore Auctions:** Naive decentralized platforms that introduce "task Dutch auctions" or dynamic token bounties for cleaning and care rapidly recreate class stratification: high-credentialed specialists buy their way out of scrubbing toilets while desperate newcomers take on hazardous or exhausting chores for tokens.
4.  **Compassion Fatigue & Unprocessed Secondary Trauma:** Emergency responders (`Chi`), crisis mediators (`Psi`), end-of-life companions (`Phi`), and childcare stewards (`Delta`) absorb intense emotional and nervous-system shocks that, if left unbuffered by mandatory decompression and respite, lead to secondary trauma and emotional numbness.
5.  **Digital Hyper-Connectivity & Taylorist Surveillance:** Corporate platforms monitor workers with cameras and biometric wearables while bombarding them with 24/7 algorithmic notifications, trapping the human nervous system in chronic sympathetic fight-or-flight.
