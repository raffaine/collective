# Oasis: Mission Statement & Design Pillars

## 🌍 The Vision: The Trojan Horse Simulator
Oasis is a high-performance, deterministic simulation engine masquerading as a video game. It is designed to bridge the psychological gap between legacy capitalist reliance and sovereign, localized resilience. 

While it borrows structural DNA from the greatest simulation games in history, it fundamentally rejects their "timid," sandbox-trapped nature. Oasis does not trap human creativity in a digital void; it weaponizes it. Every successful system designed in the game compiles down into actionable, cryptographically verified intents (BPMN) meant to be executed in the physical world.

---

## 🧬 The Three Pillars of Gameplay (The Sovereign Departure)

### 1. Dwarf Fortress: Thermodynamics & Emergent Psychology
*Dwarf Fortress* is legendary for simulating not just physical matter, but the deep, rippling consequences of psychology, memory, and emergent social behavior. Oasis adopts this dual-layer simulation entirely.
* **The Legacy Experience:** Procedural fantasy lore, magical beasts, and abstracted trauma.
* **The Oasis Departure:** Deep physics intertwined with Layer 6 Semantics. Physically, the engine tracks strict thermodynamics (thermal mass, material stress). Psychologically, every interaction creates a semantic trace. A localized failure (e.g., a ruined harvest) isn't just a lost resource; it ripples through the community mesh, altering Trust Rings, generating emergent social friction, and testing the community's resilience in highly unpredictable, systemic ways.

### 2. The Sims: Daily Routine & Visual Empathy
If DF provides the heavy underlying simulation, *The Sims* provides the framework for individual agency and the intuitive UI of daily life. It makes managing the micro-scale of human existence engaging rather than a spreadsheet chore.
* **The Legacy Experience:** Managing gamified "mood bars" (hunger, fun, social) by purchasing consumer goods for virtual dolls.
* **The Oasis Departure:** Accessible sovereignty. Oasis takes the deep sociological concepts of the Collective—Altruism Fatigue (Scenario Sigma), restorative justice, and Mutual Aid routines (childcare, cooking)—and grounds them in empathetic, individual routines. You aren't playing god; you are interacting with citizens navigating their daily limits, ensuring the local community doesn't burn out under ecological pressure.

### 3. Cities: Skylines: Macroscopic Flow & Infrastructure
Like *Cities: Skylines*, Oasis is a game about logistics, spatial zoning, and macro-infrastructure flow.
* **The Legacy Experience:** Painting zones, laying pipes, and magically hooking them up to an infinite, invisible municipal grid funded by fiat taxes.
* **The Oasis Departure:** The absolute rejection of the municipal umbilical cord. You must design brutal, closed-loop permaculture logistics. You manage the macro-flows of biochar processing, solar Exergy routing, and localized fabrication meshes. Every ounce of resource must be harvested, refined, and recycled entirely within the bounds of the Sovereign Node.

## 🌫️ The Fog of Legacy (Thermodynamic LoD)
In traditional engines, "Level of Detail" (LoD) is a graphical trick used to save GPU cycles by rendering fewer polygons in the distance. In Oasis, LoD is a core **simulation and narrative mechanic** representing *Legacy Pressure*. 

The engine only computes rigorous, deterministic physics (the 32-bit voxel thermodynamics) and deep psychological profiles for the territory and Citizens actively managed by the Sovereign Node's Trust Ring. 

The further an area or NPC is from the player's "range of control," the more the engine abstracts the simulation. Strict thermodynamics are replaced by statistical randomization and broad probabilistic inputs. 
* **The Mechanic:** This creates an unpredictable "Fog of War" pushing against the edges of your settlement. It represents the chaotic, unmanaged nature of the legacy fiat world. 
* **The Goal:** The player's objective is not just to survive, but to push back this "Fog of Legacy." By expanding your infrastructure and onboarding NPCs, you are literally bringing unmanaged, chaotic variables into a state of verifiable, localized thermodynamic truth.

## ⚡ The Defining Principle: Real Intent
The fundamental flaw of legacy simulation games is that they are **timid**. You spend hundreds of hours designing the perfect optimized city or resilient colony, only to turn off the computer and return to a fragile, inefficient physical reality. 

**Oasis replaces timid escapism with Real Intent.** 
When you successfully design an optimized solar-powered 3D printing mesh (The Fabrication Commons) in the Oasis simulation, the game does not just give you an achievement. It compiles your architecture into Layer 4 BPMN 2.0 schemas and Layer 6 Knowledge Artifacts. 

Once your physical Genesis Node is built, Oasis seamlessly transitions from a video game into a "Shadow Simulator." Your in-game commands actuate real-world IoT valves via MQTT (Layer 2), and your simulated resource trades mint real Value Tokens on the local ledger (Layer 3). 

**You do not just play Oasis. You use it to compile reality.**
